# Maya Stingless Bee

Melipona beecheii is a species of eusocial stingless bee, native to Central America from the Yucatán Peninsula in the north to Costa Rica in the south. It was cultivated in the Yucatán Peninsula starting in the pre-Columbian era by the ancient Maya civilization. The Mayan name for it is xunan kab, which translates roughly to regal lady bee. It has a golden-yellowish and brown striped body with translucent wings and small white hairs. It builds its nests inside hollow cavities of trees and has been seriously affected by deforestation. It has been kept for centuries almost exclusively in log nests, mainly by people in the Yucatán Peninsula, and is an important source of honey. In Mayan history the bee is the embodiment of a link to the spirit world and the subject of various religious ceremonies. According to the Maya tradition, a priest would harvest the honey during a religious ceremony held twice a year.
