# Central African Rock Python

The Central African rock python, Python sebae, is one of the pythons, a family of non-venomous snakes found in Africa, Asia and Australia that kill their prey by constriction. The family article says it has been known to eat antelope. In Cameroon bushmeat markets it is sold for meat; hunting, killing and selling pythons is illegal there under national wildlife law, but the article says there is little to no enforcement. The article says python bodies and blood are used in African traditional medicines and other belief uses. It reports a study of animals used by the Yoruba of Nigeria, which found that the African python is used to cure rheumatism and snake poison, to appease witches and to prevent accidents. In Benin, it says, Vodun practitioners believe pythons symbolize strength and the spirit of Dagbe, and each year people sacrifice animals and proclaim their sins to pythons kept inside temples.
