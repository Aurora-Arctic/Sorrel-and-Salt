# Grey Parrot

The grey parrot, Psittacus erithacus, also called the Congo grey parrot, is an African parrot in the family Psittacidae. It was previously also called the African grey parrot until the Timneh parrot was made a full species, having been treated as a subspecies. It is known for its intelligence and its speaking ability. It is predominantly grey with a black bill, and its tail feathers are red with occasional black touches. Wikipedia's article on West African Vodún says Vodun practitioners believe many natural materials contain supernatural powers, including the feathers of African grey parrots, and that shrines may include material from endangered species, including parrot feathers. It adds that foreign initiates leaving West Africa have found material intended for their shrines confiscated at airport customs. The species article says it is hunted for its meat and for its body parts, and was listed as Endangered on the IUCN Red List in 2016.
