# Cow Urine

Cow urine is a liquid by-product of cow metabolism. The page gives it a sacred role in Zoroastrianism and Hinduism. In Hinduism it goes into pancha-gavya for rituals, and the Mahabharata says Lakshmi was allowed to live in the cows' dung and urine. It was historically used as treatment in Indian Ayurvedic medicine, and as folk medicine in parts of India, Myanmar and Nigeria. The page says claims that it cures diseases or cancer have no scientific backing. It is also sold as a floor cleaner, and used as a fertiliser: jeevamrutha mixes it with cow dung, jaggery, pulse flour and soil.
