# Dracaena

Dracaena is the plant Wikipedia's article on Chagga religion names when it describes the beliefs of the Chagga people of the slopes of Kilimanjaro in Tanzania. The article gives the plant's Chagga name as masale. It says Chagga people guard against witchcraft materials placed near a homestead by planting dracaena boundary fences, which are associated with deceased ancestors and provide supernatural protection. It also says the dracaena plant symbolizes the dead and is often planted in skull groves and around banana groves. The article describes the religion as the pre-colonial Bantu religion of the Chagga. It names no species, so this row has no formal name; the nomenclature is recorded as unknown, which is an answer and not an absence. The article gives no medicinal or other use.
