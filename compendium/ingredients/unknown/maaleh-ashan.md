# Maaleh Ashan

Maaleh ashan is the traditional Hebrew name of an herb that, according to the Talmud, was an ingredient of the ketoret, the incense offered in the Temple in Jerusalem. It was said to make the smoke of the incense offering rise straight up. The Talmud says the House of Avtinas, which compounded the incense, kept the identity of maaleh ashan secret, and that the identity was lost after the destruction of the Second Temple in 70 CE. The page quotes the Temple Institute as saying that some have speculated it may be the plant Leptadenia pyrotechnica.
