# Sindoor

Sindoor is a traditional vermilion-red or orange-red cosmetic powder used by married women within Hindu communities across South Asia. It is applied along the central parting of the hair, called the maang, and marks marital status and auspiciousness. The word comes from the Sanskrit siṃdūra, a bright red pigment such as red lead or vermilion. The page says traditional sindoor was made by mixing Curcuma longa with slaked lime and alum, which react to give a red pigment, and that unlike red lead and vermilion these are not poisonous. Cinnabar-derived vermilion was used for ritual grade sindoor, and modern commercial products often use red lead or other synthetic pigments that may contain lead. Herbal versions use hibiscus, marigold and red sandalwood. The page links the custom to Puranic stories of Parvati and Shiva, and to Radha and Krishna.
