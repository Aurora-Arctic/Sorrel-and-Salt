# Shilajit

Shilajit, also called mumijo, is a thick, sticky, tar-like substance that appears as a blackish-brown powder or an exudate emerging between high mountain rocks. The page places it in the Altai Mountains, the Caucasus, the Himalayas, the Karakoram and the Pamirs, among other regions. People in these areas use it in folk and alternative medicine, including Ayurveda, Chinese and Tibetan systems. It is sold as a dry extract and in dietary supplements. The page describes it as a mineral tar or resin, highly viscous and very dark brown or black, containing more than twenty elements and composed of sixty to eighty per cent humic substances such as humic and fulvic acids. It says there is limited evidence that shilajit has beneficial effects on human health, and that some sources contain hazardous heavy metals, including lead.
