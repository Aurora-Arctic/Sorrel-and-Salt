# Cow Dung

Cow dung is the waste product of bovine animals: cattle, bison, yak and water buffalo. It is the undigested residue of plant matter with water, gut bacteria and digestive enzymes, rich in minerals, and its colour runs from greenish to blackish. Dried cakes called upla or kanda are burned for cooking fuel in India, and the dung makes biogas and fertiliser. In India and parts of Africa floors and walls are smeared with it to clean and smoothen them. In Hinduism it goes into pancha-gavya for rituals. The Mahabharata tells how Lakshmi came to live in cow dung, and Tantric Buddhist ritual manuals recommend it to purify mandala altars.
