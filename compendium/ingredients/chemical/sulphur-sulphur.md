# Sulphur

Sulphur is a chemical element (symbol S, atomic number 16), which Wikipedia describes as abundant and nonmetallic and notes is also spelled sulfur. Hall describes the mineral as yellow, found as a powder or as small translucent crystals on a host rock in volcanic regions, and obtainable from specialist shops. Hall associates it with soaking up negativity and with anything that erupts, including feelings and fevers, and with bringing latent psychic abilities to the surface and supporting creativity. Cunningham classes it as projective, ruled by the Sun and by Fire, with protection and healing as its powers. He records that it was burned in ceremonial magic to repel malign spirits, later burned as a fumigant to guard animals and houses, and until recently worn in a small red bag as a remedy for colds and pains. He also says pieces are set on an altar during protective rites or kept in the home as a ward.

#### Questions for the Curator

Spelling of the formal name: the roster gives 'Sulphur' and Hall prints SULPHUR, but Wikipedia's article is titled 'Sulfur' ('also spelled as sulphur') and Cunningham prints SULFUR. The owner should confirm which spelling is the formal name.
