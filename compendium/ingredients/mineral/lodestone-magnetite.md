# Lodestone

Lodestone is the name for naturally magnetised magnetite; Wikipedia says such pieces attract small pieces of iron. Cunningham lists many names for it, including Magnet, Way Stone, Loadstone, Heraclean Stone, Magnetis and the ancient Assyrian Shadanu Sabitu, gives its planet as Venus and its element as water, and links it to iron, copper, silver and gold. He uses it for attracting love, friendship and fidelity, for healing, for protection, for strengthening will, for drawing business success, and as a gambling talisman. Hall, under magnetite, gives black or brownish-grey, calls it dark, grainy and magnetic, and associates it with grounding, meditation, visualisation and attracting love, commitment and loyalty.

#### Questions for the Curator

Wikipedia (Magnetite, line 40) lists Lodestone as 'var1' of magnetite and line 71 says naturally magnetised pieces of magnetite are called lodestone, but the roster's formal name is plain 'Magnetite'. Lead to decide whether it should be 'Magnetite var. lodestone'.
