# Jade

Jade is the name Wikipedia gives to two different decorative rocks, nephrite and jadeite, and it says jade occurs in virtually all colours, mostly green. Hall's heading covers both, and Hall describes the stone as translucent when jadeite and creamy when nephrite, with a somewhat soapy feel. Hall associates jade with purity, serenity, increased love and nurturing, harmony, good luck, friendship and cleansing, and calls it a dream stone. Hall also says it assists fertility and childbirth, and that the Chinese believe holding jade transfers its virtues into the body. Cunningham gives its planet as Venus and its element as water, names Kwan Yin, Maat and Buddha among its deities, and uses it for love, healing, long life, wisdom, protection and prosperity.

#### Questions for the Curator

Wikipedia (Jade, line 43) calls jade 'an umbrella term for two different types of decorative rocks' (nephrite and jadeite) and line 45 says nephrite was deprecated as a mineral species name. Hall's JADE heading covers both ('also known as jadeite, nephrite'). The roster gives nomenclature 'mineral' for 'Jade'. Lead to decide the nomenclature.
