# Iolite

Iolite is the gemstone name for transparent cordierite; Wikipedia says cordierite is the mineralogical name and iolite the gemological one, and that the name comes from the Greek word for violet. Wikipedia adds that the mineral was formerly called dichroite and also nicknamed water-sapphire, and that it occurs in blue, smoky blue and bluish violet, and also in greenish, yellowish brown and grey shades. Hall gives its colours as grey, violet, blue or yellow and describes small, translucent stones whose colour changes with the angle of view. Hall calls it a vision stone and associates it with visualisation and intuitive insight. For healing, Hall lists the liver, malaria, fevers and migraine. Cunningham has no entry for iolite, so Hall is the only magical source here.

#### Questions for the Curator

Wikipedia (Cordierite, line 56) says iolite is 'the transparent variety' of cordierite, but the roster's formal name is the species 'Cordierite' with no variety. Hall prints only the label 'Iolite'. Lead to decide whether the formal name should be 'Cordierite var. iolite'.
