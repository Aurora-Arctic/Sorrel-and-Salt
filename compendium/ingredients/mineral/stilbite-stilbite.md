# Stilbite

Stilbite is the name of a series of zeolite tectosilicate minerals, according to Wikipedia; a 1997 reclassification by the International Mineralogical Association turned it from a species into a series name, with the species now called stilbite-Ca and stilbite-Na. Wikipedia says the colour is usually colourless or white, and sometimes yellow, brown, pink, orange, red, green, blue or black. Hall prints the heading as Stillbite, and describes small crystalline plates or pyramids in a cluster, in white, yellow, pink, orange, red and brown, from the United States. Hall associates it with creativity, opening the intuition, grounding spiritual energy and turning intuitive thought into action, and with spiritual journeying, guidance on the way and bringing back memories of the journey. She says clusters can be used for scrying and that it can be held or placed on the third eye. Her claims about the brain, ligaments and poisoning are her own and are not vouched for here.

#### Questions for the Curator

Hall prints the heading 'STILLBITE' (double l); Wikipedia's article is 'Stilbite' and does not mention that spelling. It is almost certainly a misspelling, but no source read says so.

Wikipedia (Stilbite, line 48) says stilbite is a series name, not a species; the species are stilbite-Ca and stilbite-Na. The roster nomenclature 'mineral' with canonicalName 'Stilbite' may need review.
