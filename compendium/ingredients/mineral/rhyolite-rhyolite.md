# Rhyolite

Rhyolite is a volcanic rock, formed when silica-rich lava cools quickly at the surface. Wikipedia describes it as fine-grained or glassy, built mainly of quartz, sanidine and plagioclase, and generally light in colour. Hall lists it as white, green, light grey or red, banded or spotted, found in Australia, Mexico and the United States and often sold shaped, polished or tumbled. Hall associates it with creativity and the soul's potential, with deep meditation, and with past-life work that settles the past so that a person can stay in the present. She also links it to self-worth, a calm strength in difficult circumstances, and gentle emotional release. She lists physical effects too, such as resistance to infection and skin complaints; these are her claims and this entry does not vouch for them. She says to wear it or place it where it is needed, for example on the forehead for past-life regression with a skilled therapist, or on the solar plexus for emotional release.
