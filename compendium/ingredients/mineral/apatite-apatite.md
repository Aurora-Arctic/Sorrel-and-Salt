# Apatite

Apatite is the name of a group of phosphate minerals, usually hydroxyapatite, fluorapatite and chlorapatite, which Abraham Gottlob Werner named in 1786. Wikipedia says it is most often green but also colourless, yellow, blue to violet, pink or brown, has a Mohs hardness of 5, and is occasionally cut as a gemstone. Hall lists a still wider range of colours and calls it the stone of manifestation. She says it develops psychic gifts, deepens meditation, aids communication, stimulates creativity and expands knowledge and truth. She also gives separate paragraphs for blue and yellow apatite. Cunningham has no entry for it. The uses named here are Hall's claims, not tested properties of the mineral.

#### Questions for the Curator

Wikipedia (Apatite) treats apatite as a group (hydroxyapatite, fluorapatite, chlorapatite), not a single species, and Hall's heading covers the same range. Please decide whether the formal name 'Apatite' should stand for the group.
