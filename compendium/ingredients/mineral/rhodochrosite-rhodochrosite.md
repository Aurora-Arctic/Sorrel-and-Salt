# Rhodochrosite

Rhodochrosite is a manganese carbonate. Wikipedia describes it as rose-red when pure, though it also occurs in pinks and pale browns, and says it is often banded. Cunningham gives it Mars, fire and a projective energy, with powers of energy, peace and love. He says it is carried or worn for extra energy during hard physical exertion, added to a bath or worn in it to soothe body and emotions, and carried to draw love. Hall says it stands for selfless love and compassion and is a stone for the heart and for relationships. Hall also describes it as a stone that brings buried feelings up for release and insists on facing the truth with loving awareness, and says it livens the mind, supporting dream states and creativity. Hall recommends wearing it on the wrist or placing it over the heart. These are the claims of those authors, not established fact.

#### Questions for the Curator

Cunningham prints the heading as "RHODOCROSITE", missing an h. It is taken as a misspelling of Rhodochrosite, since the entry describes a pink stone with the same uses; confirm.
