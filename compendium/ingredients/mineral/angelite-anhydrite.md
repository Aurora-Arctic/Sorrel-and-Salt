# Angelite

Angelite is the trade name, according to Wikipedia, for a semi-transparent, light blue-grey variety of anhydrite from Peru. Anhydrite is calcium sulphate, orthorhombic, usually white but sometimes greyish, bluish or purple. Hall describes angelite as blue and white, sometimes flecked with red, and associates it with peace, brotherhood, telepathic communication, protection, speaking one's truth, compassion and psychic channelling. Cunningham has no entry for it. Hall's account of how the stone forms differs from Wikipedia's, so the point is put to the curator; this entry follows Wikipedia for what the stone is. Everything said here about its uses is Hall's belief, not an established property of the mineral.

#### Questions for the Curator

Hall (s.v. Angelite) says angelite is formed from compressed celestite; Wikipedia (Anhydrite) calls angelite a trade name for a light blue-grey variety of anhydrite from Peru. The roster name Anhydrite rests on Wikipedia alone, and the heading 'Angelite' is a trade name, not a formal name. Please confirm the formal name.
