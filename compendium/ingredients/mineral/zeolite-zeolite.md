# Zeolite

Zeolite is the name for a group of microporous (full of tiny holes), crystalline aluminosilicate minerals, which Wikipedia says are used as adsorbents and catalysts. The word was coined in 1756 by the Swedish mineralogist Axel Fredrik Cronstedt, from Greek words meaning to boil and stone. Hall uses the name for a group of crystals often found together on a matrix, naming apophyllite, okenite, pectolite, prehnite and stilbite, in colourless, white, blue and peach. Hall associates zeolite with absorbing toxins and odours, and says it is a Reiki stone that helps attunement and the response to healing. She also lists goitre, bloating and addiction among its uses; these are her attributions, not established medicine.

#### Questions for the Curator

Zeolite is a group of minerals, not one species: Wikipedia says 'a group of several microporous, crystalline aluminosilicate minerals' and Hall says 'the generic name for a group of crystals'.
