# Unakite

Unakite is a coarse-grained metamorphic rock, an altered granite-type rock made up of pink orthoclase feldspar, yellow-green epidote and quartz, according to Wikipedia. It was first described in 1874 and named after the Unaka Range in the Great Smoky Mountains. Hall describes a mottled green-and-pink stone, often a small tumbled piece, easily obtained. Hall associates it with vision, with balancing emotions and spirituality, with grounding, and with psychic vision when placed on the third eye. She also describes using tumbled pieces as a casting set for scrying, and links it to past-life work, to recovery after illness and to the reproductive system. These are Hall's attributions.

#### Questions for the Curator
