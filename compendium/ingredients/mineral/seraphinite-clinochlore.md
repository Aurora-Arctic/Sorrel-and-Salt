# Seraphinite

Seraphinite is a trade name for a particular form of clinochlore, a member of the chlorite group, according to Wikipedia. The article says the name comes from its feather-like, shimmering look, like the wings of the seraphim, and that it is generally dark green to grey, with a Mohs hardness of 2 to 4. Hall describes it as green, with silvery feathers within the darker stone, often small and polished, from Siberia, and gives the other name Serafina. Hall associates it with spiritual enlightenment, self-healing, angelic connection and opening the crown chakras, with a gentle cleansing of the heart chakra and openness to love, and with safe journeys out of the body and a path toward peace. She suggests placing it on the third eye or heart, under the pillow, or wearing it round the neck. Her statements about the spine, chills and weight are her own claims and are not vouched for here.

#### Questions for the Curator

Wikipedia (Seraphinite, line 36) calls seraphinite 'a trade name for a particular form of clinochlore'. Neither source read prints 'Clinochlore var. seraphinite'; the roster name is the species, Clinochlore. Hall's heading is SERAPHINITE and she says 'See also Chlorite'.
