# Chlorite

Chlorite is the name of a group of green sheet-silicate minerals rather than a single species, taking its name from the Greek word for green. Wikipedia describes it as mostly green, with yellow, red or white examples uncommon, and notes that it can form blue-green crystals that resemble mica. Hall treats it as a generic name for a group, found in several forms, opaque, and sometimes enclosed within quartz, with sources in Russia, Germany and the United States. Hall calls it a strong, positive healing stone. She says that paired with amethyst it takes out energy implants and keeps off psychic attack, and that paired with carnelian and ruby it shields from such attack and assists earthbound spirits. She also suggests laying a grid of it around an area to guard against negative energies. These are Hall's claims, not established fact.

#### Questions for the Curator

Both Hall ('a generic name for a group') and Wikipedia (article 'Chlorite group') show Chlorite is a group of minerals, not one species. The roster name 'Chlorite' is kept; the curator should decide whether it should stay.
