# Chrysocolla

Chrysocolla is a blue to green hydrous copper silicate, a minor ore of copper. Wikipedia calls it a mineral and mineraloid, notes a 2006 study suggesting it may be a mixture of spertiniite and chalcedony, and says the name comes from Greek words for gold and glue and was used by Theophrastus. It is also cut as jewellery in place of turquoise. Cunningham gives it a receptive energy, rules it by Venus and water, and lists its powers as peace, wisdom and love; he describes holding it to drive off fear and illusion, and wearing it or using it in spells to draw love. Hall describes it as tranquil and sustaining, and associates it with meditation, communication, creativity, truth-telling and cleansing the chakras and the home. Cunningham lists turquoise as its substitute. These are the authors' claims, not established fact.
