# Peridot

Peridot is the gem-quality, yellow-green form of olivine. Wikipedia says it is the magnesium-rich end member of that series, called forsterite, that it is sometimes called chrysolite, and that few gemstones occur in just one colour. Hall gives its colour as olive green or yellowish green, sometimes honey, red or brownish. Cunningham attributes it to Venus and to the element earth, though he says it has long been linked with the Sun, and says it was once set in gold as a protective amulet against enchantments, night terrors and the evil eye. He uses it for healing, for attracting love, for calming anger, for sleep when worn to bed, and in wealth spells because of its deep green colour. Hall says it was believed in ancient times to keep evil spirits away, and associates it with cleansing, releasing old patterns, confidence and forgiving oneself. Hall also credits it with a tonic effect on the body. These are the claims of those authors.
