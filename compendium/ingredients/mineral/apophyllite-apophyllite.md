# Apophyllite

Apophyllite is a name for a group of layered silicate minerals (phyllosilicates) that includes fluorapophyllite-(K), according to Wikipedia, which gives the usual colour as white or colourless, with blue, green, brown, yellow, pink and violet also occurring. Hall describes its crystals as clear, white, green, yellowish or peach and calls it a stone of truth and an excellent aid to scrying. She says it calms and grounds the spirit and links it with the Akashic Record and Reiki. She also describes green apophyllite and apophyllite pyramids separately. Cunningham has no entry for it. These uses are Hall's claims, not established properties of the mineral.

#### Questions for the Curator

Wikipedia (Apophyllite) says the name refers to a group of minerals (the apophyllite group), not one species. Please decide whether the formal name 'Apophyllite' should stand for the group.
