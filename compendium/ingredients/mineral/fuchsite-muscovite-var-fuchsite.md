# Fuchsite

Fuchsite is the chromium-rich variety of muscovite, a mica. Wikipedia says trivalent chromium gives it an apple-green hue, that it is also called chrome mica, and that it is named after Johann Nepomuk von Fuchs. Hall also calls it green muscovite and describes green, plate-like, layered pieces from Brazil. Hall says it gives access to knowledge of great practical value and channels herbal and holistic information, and she associates it with releasing servitude and codependency and with true self-worth. She also says it boosts the power of other crystals, and her entry refers readers on to muscovite, the mica it belongs to. Wikipedia gives its hardness as two to two and a half, which makes it a soft stone. These are Hall's claims, not established fact.

#### Questions for the Curator

Wikipedia calls fuchsite a chromium-rich variety of muscovite, which supports the formal name. Hall's heading is FUCHSITE, also known as Green Muscovite; she does not print 'var.'.
