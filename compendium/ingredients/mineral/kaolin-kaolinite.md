# Kaolin

Kaolinite, also called kaolin, is a clay mineral with the chemical composition Al2Si2O5(OH)4. It is a soft, earthy, usually white mineral produced by the chemical weathering of aluminium silicate minerals such as feldspar, and it is described as a layered silicate. Rocks rich in kaolinite and halloysite are known as kaolin or china clay, and iron oxide colours kaolin pink, orange or red in many parts of the world. The article calls kaolin an important raw material in many industries. Wikipedia's article on West African Vodún says Vodun practitioners believe many natural materials contain supernatural powers, including kaolin, and that a person possessed by a Tchamba spirit may draw lines on the face with kaolin to symbolise the scarification markings of northern people. The Vodún article does not describe how the kaolin is prepared or who may use it.
