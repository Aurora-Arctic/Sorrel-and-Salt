# Lepidolite

Wikipedia describes lepidolite as the common name for a lilac-grey or rose-coloured series of minerals in the mica group, mainly pink, purple and red. Cunningham calls it a purplish type of mica rich in lithium, fragile, and records the folk names The Peace Stone and Stone of Peace. He gives its planets as Jupiter and Neptune and its element as water, and uses it as a calming stone for restful sleep free of nightmares, for psychic awareness, for luck and protection, and for reconciliation. Hall gives purple or pink, in plate-like layers or grainy masses, and associates it with clearing electromagnetic pollution, stabilising mood swings and soothing sleep disturbances.
