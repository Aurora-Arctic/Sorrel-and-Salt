# Lapis Lazuli

Wikipedia describes lapis lazuli as a deep-blue metamorphic rock composed of lazurite, pyrite, diopside and calcite. Cunningham calls it a royal blue stone with flecks of golden pyrite, and Hall describes it as dense and veined, deep blue flecked with gold, looking like the night sky. Cunningham gives its planet as Venus, its element as water and its metal as gold, and names Isis, Venus and Nuit as deities. He uses it for healing, as a fine meditative stone, for fidelity, to strengthen psychic awareness, for courage, and as a protective stone, especially for children. Hall associates it with dream work, psychic abilities, reversing curses, clarity, creativity, bonds of love and friendship, and revealing inner truth.

#### Questions for the Curator

Wikipedia (Lapis lazuli, lines 5–15) calls lapis lazuli a metamorphic rock composed of several minerals (lazurite, pyrite, diopside, calcite), not a mineral species. The roster gives nomenclature 'mineral'. Lead to decide the nomenclature.
