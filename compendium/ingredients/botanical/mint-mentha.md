# Mint

Mint, here the genus Mentha, is a group of aromatic, almost entirely perennial herbs of the mint family. Wikipedia says they have wide-spreading underground and overground stolons, erect square stems 10 to 120 cm tall, and opposite leaves, and that some mints are invasive because they spread unchecked. It estimates 18 to 24 species, with many hybrids and cultivars. Cunningham heads his entry Mint (Mentha spp.), calls it a general term for any of the Mentha family, and gives it as masculine, ruled by Mercury and Air, with Pluto and Hecate as deities. He says it is used in healing potions, travel and money spells, to provoke lust, and to protect the home. He sends the reader to the specific mint for cautions.
