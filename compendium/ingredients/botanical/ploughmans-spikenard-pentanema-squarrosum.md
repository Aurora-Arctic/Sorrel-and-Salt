# Ploughman's Spikenard

Ploughman's spikenard is an aromatic herb of the daisy family, found in Europe, North Africa and the Near East in calcareous grassland. Wikipedia says it was once harvested as a substitute for true spikenard, Nardostachys jatamansi, and that its leaves and roots smell faintly spicy, like cinnamon or cumin. It is a biennial or short-lived perennial with a purplish branching stem and yellow and purple flowerheads in late summer. Cunningham gives a spikenard entry for Inula conyza and Aralia racemosa together, feminine, with Venus and water, and the powers of fidelity and health; it is worn around the neck for luck and to ward off disease. Kynes gives three spikenards under one heading, with powers of balance, love and loyalty. Both citations are left for the curator.
