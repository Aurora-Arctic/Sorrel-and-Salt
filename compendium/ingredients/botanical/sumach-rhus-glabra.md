# Sumach

Smooth sumach is a deciduous shrub or small tree native to North America, found from southern Quebec to British Columbia and south to Florida, Arizona and north-eastern Mexico. Wikipedia says it bears dense, erect panicles of flowers in spring, followed by large clusters of edible crimson berries that last through winter, and that archaeologists in 2020 found chemical evidence that a Native American tribe smoked it, alone or blended with tobacco. Grieve describes a shrub six to fifteen feet high with pale-grey bark, says the leaves and bark are used in tanning and dyeing, calls the bark tonic and astringent, and warns that the berries of other sumachs should be carefully distinguished from poisonous species.
