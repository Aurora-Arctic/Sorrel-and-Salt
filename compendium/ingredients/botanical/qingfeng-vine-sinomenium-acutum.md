# Qingfeng Vine

Sinomenium acutum is a climbing plant of the moonseed family, Menispermaceae. The cited Wikipedia revision is the page for the genus Sinomenium, which Wikipedia says was first described as a genus in 1910 and contains only one known species, Sinomenium acutum. It says the species is native to China, northern India, Nepal, Japan, northern Thailand and Korea. The page lists the synonyms Menispermum acutum and Cocculus variiformis, among others, and a variety, Sinomenium acutum var. cinereum. It also records fossil Sinomenium remains from Pliocene sites in Italy and in western Georgia. The page says nothing about a use for the plant, so no form and no use is recorded here yet.
