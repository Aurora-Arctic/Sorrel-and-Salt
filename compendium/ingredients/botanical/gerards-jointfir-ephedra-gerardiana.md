# Gerard's Jointfir

Gerard's jointfir is a species of Ephedra, a seed-bearing shrub of the family Ephedraceae and order Ephedrales, described by Wallich and named in print by C. A. Meyer. It is endemic to the mountains of Afghanistan, Bhutan, northern India, Nepal, Pakistan, Sikkim, Tajikistan and Tibet, and its Chinese name is shan ling ma huang. It is a small perennial shrub made mostly of fibrous stalks, generally about 8 inches tall and sometimes up to 24 inches. It bears small yellow flowers, followed by round, red, edible fruits. The page says it is sometimes used as a stimulant, and that in Ayurvedic medicine its tea is used for colds, coughs, bronchitis, asthma and arthritis.
