# Citron

Citron is a large fragrant citrus fruit with a thick, coarse rind, resembling a lemon but larger. Wikipedia says it is one of the original citrus fruits from which the other kinds developed, and that it is used in cooking, traditional medicine, perfume and religious offerings. Cunningham, who misprints the genus as Citnus, calls it masculine, with the Sun and air, and gives psychic powers and healing. He says that eating the citron increases psychic powers, and that it is used in healing spells and incenses. He lists the folk names sukake, forbidden fruit and rough lemon. Kynes has no entry for citron.
