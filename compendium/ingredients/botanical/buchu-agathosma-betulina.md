# Buchu

Buchu, also called round buchu or short buchu, is an evergreen shrub of the rue family, native to the lower mountains of western South Africa, where it grows near streams in fynbos. Wikipedia describes opposite, rounded, glossy, fragrant leaves, white or pale pink five-petalled flowers, and a five-parted capsule. It was formerly called Barosma betulina. Cunningham calls it feminine, with the Moon and water, and lists psychic powers and prophetic dreams. He says an infusion, when drunk, enables one to foretell the future. Frankincense mixed with buchu is burned just before retiring to produce prophetic dreams, and only a small amount should be burned, in the bedroom. Kynes has no buchu entry. The American Herbal Products Association records cautions, set out in the safety notes.
