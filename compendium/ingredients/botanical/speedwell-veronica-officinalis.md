# Speedwell

Heath speedwell is a low perennial of the plantain family, Plantaginaceae, native to Europe and western Asia and naturalised in North America. Wikipedia describes hairy green stems 10 to 50 centimetres long that spread in mats and send up short shoots bearing soft violet flowers, and says it flowers from May to August. Its slightly bitter taste and tea-like smell made it a tea substitute in nineteenth-century France. Grieve names Veronica officinalis, gives the herb as the part used, and lists diaphoretic, alterative, diuretic, expectorant and tonic actions. Kynes has a speedwell entry that covers Veronica officinalis and germander speedwell (V. chamaedrys) together. It gives fire, Aries and Mercury, and powers of intuition, loyalty and fidelity, and protection, and says the plant was kept in Ireland to keep evil spirits away and tucked into clothing or a suitcase for travel. Cunningham has no speedwell entry. The Kynes entry is unresolved; see the questions.
