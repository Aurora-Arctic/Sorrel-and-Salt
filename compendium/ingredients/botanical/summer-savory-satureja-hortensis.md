# Summer Savory

Summer savory is an annual herb of the savory genus. Wikipedia says it is similar in use and flavour to the perennial winter savory but is used more often, because it is slightly less bitter. Cunningham gives it a masculine, Mercury, air character, the folk names Herbe de St. Julien and Garden Savory, and a single power, mental powers: carried or worn, it strengthens the mind. Kynes puts summer and winter savory under one heading, so her fire element, Virgo, the god Pan and her list of powers cannot be told apart between the two and are held for the owner to settle. Her healing category is kept for that reason with the same caution. Kynes also lists the herb for sale as essential oil, fresh or powdered herb, cut dried leaves and seeds.
