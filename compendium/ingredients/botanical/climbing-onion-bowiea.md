# Climbing-Onion

Bowiea, commonly known as climbing-onion, is a genus of extremely poisonous, bulbous, perennial succulent plants of dry regions of eastern and southern Africa, from Uganda to South Africa. The article says it is the 14th most commonly sold medicinal plant in South Africa, and that massive harvesting has significantly reduced its populations. Traditional healers in South Africa have long used it as a herbal remedy for skin diseases, eye irritation, palpitations, headaches, muscle pains, infertility, urinary tract infections and sexually transmitted diseases. The Xhosa use a decoction of the bulbs as a laxative, the Mfengu and Mpondo use ground roasted bulbs for the same purpose, and the Zulu use a water extract from the fresh outer scales for ascites, infertility, urinary tract infections, back pain and muscle pains. The Swazi use a decoction with Boophone disticha bulbs for scabies.
