# Cassia

Cassia is the bark of a tree in the laurel family, sold as Chinese cinnamon and as bastard cinnamon. Its naming is tangled: the American Herbal Products Association lists the bark under Cinnamomum aromaticum with Cinnamomum cassia as a synonym, while Wikipedia gives C. cassia and notes that sources disagree about which name is legitimate. Kynes files cassia under her Cinnamon heading together with Ceylon cinnamon, so her powers may not belong to cassia alone. She gives fire, with Aries, Capricorn and Leo, and Jupiter, Mercury and the Sun, and many gods of sun and love. Her powers include healing, love, prosperity, protection, psychic work, purification, lust, strength, success and transformation. She uses the oil in candle magic for psychic abilities.
