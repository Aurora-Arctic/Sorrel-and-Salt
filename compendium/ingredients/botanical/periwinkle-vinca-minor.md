# Periwinkle

Lesser periwinkle is a trailing evergreen subshrub of the dogbane family, native to central and southern Europe, with violet-purple flowers. Wikipedia says it spreads along the ground and roots along its stems. Cunningham calls it feminine, with Venus and water, and lists love, lust, mental powers, money and protection. He says the Pseudo-Apuleius gave strict rules for gathering it, and that the plant is then carried for grace, money, and protection against snakes, poison, wild beasts, the evil eye and spirits. Kynes places it under the Roman festival of Bacchanalia and adds Aphrodite and Bacchus. The American Herbal Products Association notes that lesser periwinkle contains small amounts of the compound vincamine. Cunningham marks periwinkle Poison, set out in the safety notes. Kynes puts greater and lesser periwinkle under one heading, so her citation is left for the curator.
