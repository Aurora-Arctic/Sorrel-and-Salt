# Wormwood

Wormwood is a silvery, strongly scented perennial of the daisy family, Asteraceae, native to North Africa and temperate Eurasia. Wikipedia says it flavours the spirit absinthe and some bitters and vermouths, and that its essential oil contains thujone. Cunningham calls it masculine, with Mars and fire, and lists psychic powers, protection, love and calling spirits. Kynes gives fire, Cancer, Gemini, Mars and Pluto, with Artemis, Diana and Isis, and lists banishing, binding, divination, purification and protection. Cunningham has it burned or worn for psychic powers, protecting vehicles and summoning spirits. Kynes has a sachet set in the highest place to banish a spirit, and writes of sprigs hung over doors and German funeral biers. Cunningham and Kynes mark the plant poisonous, and the American Herbal Products Association adds cautions, all set out in the safety notes.
