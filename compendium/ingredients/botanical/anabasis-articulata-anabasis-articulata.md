# Anabasis articulata

Anabasis articulata is a salt-tolerant desert plant of the Syrian Desert, a xerophyte, meaning a plant adapted to very dry ground. Wikipedia says Bedouin often use its ashes as a substitute for soap. The page also says the plant is known for medicinal properties, and that Algerian traditional medicine practitioners use its leaves to make an anti-diabetic decoction.
