# Opium Poppy

The opium poppy, also called the breadseed poppy, is an annual herb that Wikipedia names as the species from which both opium and poppy seeds come. It is native to the western Mediterranean and now grown across much of the world. Cunningham's entry is headed with Papaver spp., not this species alone, and Kynes treats the common poppy and the opium poppy under one heading, so both citations are left for the curator. Cunningham calls it feminine, with the Moon and water, and gives the deities Hypnos and Demeter and powers of fertility, love, sleep, money, luck and invisibility. Kynes adds fire, the signs Cancer and Capricorn, many deities, and powers including dream work and protection. She sells dried seedpods, whole or ground seeds and seed oil. Both sources record hazards, set out in the safety notes.
