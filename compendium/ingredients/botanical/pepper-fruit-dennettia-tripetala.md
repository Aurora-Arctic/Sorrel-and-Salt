# Pepper Fruit

Dennettia tripetala, known in English as pepper fruit, is a shrub or tree of the custard apple family, Annonaceae, native to western and west-central tropical Africa, including Guinea, Sierra Leone, Ghana, Nigeria and Cameroon. Wikipedia lists Uvariopsis tripetala as a synonym. It is called mmimmi by the Igbo, ata igebere or igberi by the Yoruba, imako by the Niger Deltans and Urhobo, ako in Benin and nkarika by the Efik. The fruit, leaves and roots are used in traditional medicine, and the fruit is used as food and to season dishes. The article says that in the eastern part of Nigeria the fruit is typically offered with palm wine, garden eggs, bitter kola and kola nuts at festivals, ceremonials, coronations, traditional weddings, naming rituals and new yam festivities. When chewed it is very peppery and stimulating. The article says the species is threatened by habitat loss.
