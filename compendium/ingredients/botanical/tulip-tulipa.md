# Tulip

Tulips are spring-blooming perennial bulb plants of the genus Tulipa, in the lily family, Liliaceae. Wikipedia says their flowers are usually large, showy and brightly coloured, and that tuliposides and tulipalins found in tulips are responsible for allergies. Cunningham calls the tulip feminine, with Venus and earth, and lists prosperity, love and protection. He has the flower worn against poverty and bad luck in general, notes that the word tulip means turban and that the flower is often worn in the turban in Middle Eastern countries for protection, and has tulips placed on the altar during love spells. Kynes also gives earth and Venus, and lists a long run of powers that include abundance, courage, fertility, love and peace. Both Cunningham and Kynes head the plant in a way that covers more than the genus alone, so their entries are cited with a question for the curator.
