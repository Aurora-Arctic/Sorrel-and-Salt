# Buckwheat

Common buckwheat is a flowering plant of the knotweed family, grown for its grain-like seeds and as a cover crop. Wikipedia says it is not closely related to wheat or to any grass, and that its name comes from the beech-nut shape of its three-sided seeds. Cunningham lists it under Fagopyrum, calling it feminine, with Venus and earth, and gives money and protection as its powers. He has the seeds ground to flour and sprinkled in a circle around the house to keep evil out, or used to form a magic circle on the floor around a person working magic. A few grains go into money incenses, and some kept in the kitchen guard against poverty. Cunningham is the only magical source. His heading covers the genus, so whether his entry describes common buckwheat alone is an open question for a curator.
