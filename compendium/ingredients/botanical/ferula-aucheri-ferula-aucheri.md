# Ferula aucheri

Ferula aucheri, a synonym of Dorema aucheri, is a plant that grows in the mountains of western Iran, in Sedeh and Kermanshah. In Persian it is called bilhar, or kandale kohi, and in Kermanshah zo. The page calls it the first umbelliferous plant found to produce exudate flavonoids. The young leaves and branches are harvested traditionally for cooking and make a well-known pickle, Bilhar pickle, or are used as a vegetable in soup, or steamed and served with butter. In parts of Iran it is used in traditional medicine, and is believed to work as a stimulant, antispasmodic, bronchodilator, expectorant, kidney-stone repellent, emmenagogue, and painkiller for visceral pain. The page says excessive harvesting for cooking and traditional medicine has made it endangered in local provinces. It also reports that, although it is eaten and used as medicine, an extract injected into albino mice caused liver damage.
