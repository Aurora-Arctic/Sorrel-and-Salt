# Pistachio

The pistachio is a small to medium tree of the cashew family whose seeds are widely eaten as food. Wikipedia says the trees are dioecious, the fruit is a drupe with a hard shell that splits open with an audible pop when ripe, and that the United States, Iran and Turkey together made 87 per cent of world production in 2024. Cunningham calls it masculine, with Mercury and air, and gives one power, breaking love spells. He says the Arabs believe eating the nuts is an antidote to love spells, and that nuts dyed red are given to zombies to bring them out of their trances. Kynes shares a heading with the mastic tree and adds Jupiter and the deities Artemis, Diana, Bacchus and Dionysus. Wikipedia records allergen hazards, set out in the safety notes.
