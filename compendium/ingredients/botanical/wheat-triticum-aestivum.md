# Wheat

Wheat here is common wheat, also called bread wheat, a cultivated grass grown for its grain. Cunningham, whose entry is headed Triticum without naming a species, gives it to Venus and earth, and names Ceres, Demeter and Ishtar as its deities. He lists its powers as fertility and money. A sheaf of wheat kept in the home is said to attract money, and the grains are carried in sachets. Kynes, in an entry covering six grains, records that the Egyptians gave wheat to the dead and that Greeks, Romans and Indians offered it to deities. Wikipedia supplies the species name and common names. Because both magical sources pool several kinds of wheat or grain, the entry is flagged for the curator.
