# Willow

Willow here is the white willow, a large deciduous tree of Europe and western and central Asia whose leaves are whitish beneath. Cunningham gives it the Moon and water and a feminine gender, and lists its powers as love, love divination, protection and healing. He says willow leaves are carried or used in mixtures to attract love, the wood is made into wands for Moon magic, and all parts guard against evil. Crushed bark with sandalwood is said to conjure spirits. Grieve names the bark as the part used and calls it tonic, antiperiodic and astringent. Kynes joins three willows under one heading and the American Herbal Products Association covers several Salix species together, so both are flagged. Wikipedia notes that its stems, called withies, are used for baskets.
