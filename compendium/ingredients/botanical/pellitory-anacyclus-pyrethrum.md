# Pellitory

Pellitory, also called Spanish chamomile or Mount Atlas daisy, is a perennial of the daisy family native to Mediterranean Europe and North Africa, resembling chamomile in habit, with one large white-rayed flower on each stem. Wikipedia says it does not contain pyrethrins, despite its species name, and that both pyrethrum and pellitory come from the ancient Greek for fire. It adds that it is used in Ayurveda and Unani medicine in India under names such as Akarkara. Grieve gives the pungent root as the medicinal part, chiefly for toothache and for promoting saliva, and says it was long exported from Algeria and Spain to India.
