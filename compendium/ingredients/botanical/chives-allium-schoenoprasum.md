# Chives

Chives are a perennial herb of the onion family with slender hollow leaves and round heads of pale purple flowers, and they are widespread across Eurasia and North America. Wikipedia says the leaves and flowers are edible and that chives are used to repel insects. Cunningham has no entry for them. Kynes puts Chinese chives (Allium tuberosum, also called garlic chives) and wild chives (A. schoenoprasum, also flowering onion or onion chives) under one heading, so her lore may not belong to one plant alone. She gives fire, Sagittarius, Mars and faeries, and lists protection. She hangs a dried bunch with its flowers where negative energy is sensed, grows chives in a pot or garden as a protective charm, and uses the leaves in knot magic.
