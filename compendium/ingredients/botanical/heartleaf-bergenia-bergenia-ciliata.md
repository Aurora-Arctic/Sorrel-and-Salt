# Heartleaf Bergenia

Bergenia ciliata is a plant of the genus Bergenia, called fringed elephant's ears, winter begonia or hairy bergenia, and Pashanbheda in Hindi and Sanskrit. The page says it is deciduous in USDA zones 5 to 7 and usually stays semi-evergreen south of zone 7. It is found in northern India, in Uttarakhand and Himachal Pradesh, in the mountains of West Bengal, and in Afghanistan, southern Tibet, northern Nepal and Bhutan. It grows mainly on rocks and flowers in February and March, and is related to the Phool Dei festival of Uttarakhand. It contains bergenin, catechin, gallic acid and beta-sitosterol. The page says it is used in Ayurveda, is highly cited as a medicinal plant in Sikkim, and is harvested from the wild as a medicine and sometimes as food.
