# Cattail

Cattail is a tall wetland plant, known also as bulrush and reedmace, whose brown flower spike gives it its name. Wikipedia describes a genus of about 30 species. Cunningham's heading names Typha capensis and other species, and Kynes names the common cattail, Typha latifolia, so the entry here stands for the genus. Cunningham calls it masculine, with Mars and fire, and gives lust: a woman who wishes to enjoy sex carries cat tail with her. Kynes gives water, Mars, Saturn and several gods, and lists abundance, ancestor work, confidence, home blessing, healing, inspiration, fidelity, protection and lust. She sprinkles the pollen in the four directions to consecrate an altar. Plants in polluted water take up lead and pesticide residues in the rhizomes.
