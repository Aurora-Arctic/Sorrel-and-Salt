# Celery

Celery is a plant of the carrot family grown as a vegetable, and its seeds are the part used in magic. Cunningham calls it masculine, with Mercury and fire, and gives lust and psychic powers. He has the seeds chewed for concentration, put in spell pillows to bring sleep, and burned with orris root for psychic powers, says the stalk with its seeds induces lust, and reports that witches ate the seeds before flying. Kynes treats garden celery and wild celery together under one heading, so her powers may not belong to one plant alone. She adds fire and water, Leo and the Moon, and lists creativity, divination, healing, fidelity, protection, psychic work and lust, with Greek funeral use and a bride's sprig for a faithful husband.
