# Primrose

The primrose, or common or English primrose, is a perennial of Eurasia that forms a rosette of wrinkled leaves and blooms in early spring with pale yellow flowers. Wikipedia says both flowers and leaves are edible and the young flowers can be made into primrose wine. Cunningham calls it feminine, with Venus and earth, gives the deity Freya, and lists protection and love. He says blue and red primroses growing in a garden protect it and attract fairies, women carry them to attract love, and they are sewn into children's pillows to gain respect and loyalty. He marks it Sk, set out in the safety notes. Kynes adds Libra and uses for dreams, healing and peace, and sells dried flowers and a flower essence. She puts the common and polyanthus primroses under one heading, so her citation is left for the curator.
