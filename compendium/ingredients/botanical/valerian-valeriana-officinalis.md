# Valerian

Valerian is an herbaceous perennial of the family Caprifoliaceae, native to Europe and southwestern Asia, grown for its root. Wikipedia says valerian is sold as capsules and standardised extracts, and discourages its use in pregnancy. Cunningham calls it feminine, with Venus and water, and lists love, sleep, purification and protection. He has the powdered root put in protective sachets and pillows, and urges against driving while under the effects of valerian taken internally. Kynes gives water, Scorpio and Virgo, Mercury and Venus, and the deities Epona and Kupala. Her powers include healing, peace, prosperity, protection, purification and truth, and she sells an essential oil, a flower essence, cut or ground root and seeds. The American Herbal Products Association's monograph names Valeriana officinalis among several species, so it is cited with a question for the curator. The hazards the sources record are set out in the safety notes.
