# Onion

The onion, also called the bulb onion or common onion, is the most widely cultivated species of the genus Allium, according to Wikipedia, which names garlic, scallion, leek and chives among its close relatives. Cunningham calls it masculine, with Mars and fire, gives the deity Isis, and lists powers of protection, exorcism, healing, money, prophetic dreams and lust. His other names are Oingnum, Onyoun, Unyoun and Yn-leac. Kynes agrees on fire, Mars and Isis, and adds banishing, courage, divination, dream work, fertility, healing, prosperity and protection, with strength and spirit guides. She has you braid a short length of leaves in a money spell, crumble onionskins into a cauldron to dispel negative energy, and use a sprouted onion in a spell for courage. She sells onions fresh, dried, flaked, powdered and braided into strings.
