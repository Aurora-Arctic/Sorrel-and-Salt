# Pokeweed

American pokeweed, also called poke or inkberry, is a large herbaceous perennial with a white taproot and berries that ripen from red to purple-black. Wikipedia says it is native to eastern North America and is poisonous to humans, dogs and livestock. Cunningham marks it Poison and calls it masculine, with Mars and fire, and the powers of courage and breaking hexes. He has an infusion made at the new Moon and sprinkled around the home to break hexes and curses, poke carried for courage, and the crushed berries used as magical ink. Kynes names the Algonquian word pocan as the source of the name, says hoodoo uses the seeds and roots for breaking jinxes and curses, and hangs a cluster of berries where a negative spirit is felt. Both authors warn of its toxicity, set out in the safety notes.
