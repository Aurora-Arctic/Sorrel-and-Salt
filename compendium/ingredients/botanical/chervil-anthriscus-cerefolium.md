# Chervil

Chervil is a delicate annual herb related to parsley, also called French parsley or garden chervil, and it is used to season mild dishes. Kynes puts garden chervil and sweet cicely under one heading, so her lore may not belong to chervil alone. She says the ancient Egyptians placed baskets of seeds in tombs, and that the Anglo-Saxons used it in healing charms against demons and sickness. Dried leaves were burned like incense after a death to ease grief and to reach the deceased, and it was used to soothe bad dreams. She gives air, Jupiter, Mercury and Ceres, and lists loss and sorrow, nightmares and spirit guides. Her spells sprinkle the leaves on a grave, or bury a handful to lay a burden to rest.
