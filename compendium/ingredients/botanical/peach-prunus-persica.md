# Peach

The peach is a deciduous tree with edible, juicy fruit, first domesticated in China in the Neolithic period. Wikipedia says the glossy-skinned kind is called a nectarine and is the same species. Cunningham calls it feminine, with Venus and water, and gives the powers of love, exorcism, longevity, fertility and wishes. He says the fruit eaten induces love, branches drive off evil spirits in China, and children there wear a peach pit about the neck against demons. Carrying a bit of peach wood is said to lengthen life. Kynes heads the peach and the nectarine together, and her citation is left for the curator. She gives the deities Aphrodite, Asherah, Gaia, Harpocrates, Hera, Isis and Venus. Hazards of the seed and leaf are set out in the safety notes.
