# Pecan

The pecan is a species of hickory native to the southern United States and northern Mexico, in the region of the Mississippi River. Cunningham spells it Carya illinoensis, calls it masculine, with Mercury and air, and gives the powers of money and employment. He says pecans are added to all money and prosperity spells, and gives a spell for keeping your job: eat shelled pecans slowly while picturing yourself at work, then wrap the shells in a bag and hide it at your workplace. Kynes heads the pecan with the shagbark and white hickories, and her citation is left for the curator. She says the Powhatan and other tribes made an intoxicating drink from the nut, and that her pecan is air. She advises keeping a pecan with financial papers as an amulet.
