# Oregon Grape

Oregon grape is a North American evergreen shrub with pinnate leaves of spiny leaflets, dense clusters of yellow flowers in early spring and dark bluish-black berries. Wikipedia says the berries are part of the traditional diet of some peoples of the Pacific Northwest and that the plant is the state flower of Oregon. Some botanists place it in the separate genus Mahonia. Cunningham calls it feminine and gives two powers, money and popularity, by carrying the root. His other names are California Barberry, Oregon Grape Root, Rocky Mountain Grape, Trailing Grape and Wild Oregon Grape. Kynes treats barberry and Oregon grape under one heading, so her citation is left for the curator. She gives the element earth, the signs Scorpio and Taurus, the planets Jupiter, Mars and Venus, and powers of abundance, clarity, healing, prosperity and protection. She wears a dried root as a protective talisman and holds berries while meditating on a financial decision, and says all parts of both plants are mildly toxic if ingested.
