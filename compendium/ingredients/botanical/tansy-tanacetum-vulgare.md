# Tansy

Tansy is a perennial herbaceous plant of the daisy family with finely divided leaves and yellow, button-like flowers, native to temperate Europe and Asia. Cunningham gives it a feminine, Venus, water character, the folk name Buttons, and the powers health and longevity: a bit in the shoes cures persistent fevers, and it is carried to lengthen life because it was given to Ganymede to make him immortal. Kynes adds Midsummer and powers including banishing, purification, release and defence, and the folk names bachelor's buttons, bitter buttons, golden buttons, hind heal and yellow buttons. Kynes says all parts, especially the flowers, are toxic if eaten. Wikipedia and the American Herbal Products Association also record hazards, set out in the safety notes, and the toxic flag is set.
