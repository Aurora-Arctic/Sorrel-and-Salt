# Hemp Agrimony

Hemp agrimony is a tall perennial herb of damp places, growing in clumps to about 1.75 metres by Wikipedia and with reddish, downy stems. Wikipedia describes opposite, deeply three-lobed leaves with serrated margins and mauve flower heads. Grieve describes a woody root-stock and stems of two to five feet. She says the herb is the part used, and that the leaves contain a volatile oil that acts on the kidneys, some tannin and a bitter principle said to cut short the chill of intermittent fever. She calls it alterative and febrifuge, and says it was used in Holland for jaundice and as a spring blood purifier. Country people laid its leaves on bread, thinking they stopped it going mouldy. Wikipedia notes tumorigenic pyrrolizidine alkaloids, which are a hazard to anyone taking it internally.
