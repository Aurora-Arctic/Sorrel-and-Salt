# Yerba Santa

Yerba santa is an evergreen aromatic shrub native to California and Oregon, in the family Namaceae according to Wikipedia. Wikipedia says its dark green leathery leaves are covered in shiny resin, that it sprouts from rhizomes after fire, and that the leaves have been used in traditional medicine. Cunningham calls it feminine and lists beauty, healing, psychic powers and protection. He has it carried or bathed in for beauty, its leaves put in healing incense and worn to ward off illness, and carried for spiritual strength and psychic powers. Kynes has no entry for it. Cunningham's heading names two species, so his entry is queried; see the questions. The American Herbal Products Association also covers it under a genus heading, which is not cited here.
