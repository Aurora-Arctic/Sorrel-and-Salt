# Tamarind

Tamarind is a leguminous tree bearing an edible fruit, indigenous to tropical Africa and naturalised in Asia. Wikipedia says the fruit is sour when young and sweeter as it ripens, and the young pulp is used in savoury dishes and as a pickling agent. Cunningham gives it a feminine, Saturn, water character, the folk names Tamarindo, Sampalok, Tchwa and Mkwayu, and one power, love: the tamarind is carried to attract love. Wikipedia also says tamarind is toxic to dogs, with potential for acute kidney injury, which is set out in the safety notes and is the reason for the toxic flag. Kynes has no entry of her own for it.
