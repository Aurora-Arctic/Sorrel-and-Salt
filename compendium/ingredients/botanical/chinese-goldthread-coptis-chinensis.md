# Chinese Goldthread

Coptis chinensis, the Chinese goldthread, is a flowering plant of the goldthread genus native to China. Its rhizomes, the underground stems used as the herb, contain the isoquinoline alkaloids berberine, palmatine and coptisine, among others. It is one of the 50 fundamental herbs of traditional Chinese medicine, where it is called duǎn è huánglián. The source says it has been shown to have anti-cancer, anti-inflammatory and antibacterial properties and to help improve cardiovascular conditions. Because berberine colours so strongly, the plant has also been used traditionally as a dye, especially for wool and other fibres. The source for this entry is a short article, so this description records the plant's range, its main compounds, its place in Chinese medicine and its use as a dye, and does not describe the living plant.
