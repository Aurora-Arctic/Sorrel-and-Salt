# Indian Atees

Indian atees, also called atish, atis, patis, ativish or atvika, is a flowering plant of the genus Aconitum. Indian systems of traditional medicine, including Ayurveda, use it, and Sanskrit calls it visha or ativisha. It grows on sub-alpine and alpine slopes between 2,500 and 3,500 metres in Jammu and Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand, and also in Pakistan and Nepal. The roots are used for dysentery, diarrhoea, fever, malarial fever, cough, cold, colic, headache, piles, hysteria, throat infection, indigestion with lost appetite after illness, vomiting, abdominal pain and diabetes, and it is said to check excessive menstrual flow. Fresh leaves are used for toothache.
