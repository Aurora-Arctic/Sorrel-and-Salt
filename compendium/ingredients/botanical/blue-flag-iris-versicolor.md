# Blue Flag

Blue flag is a North American iris of sedge meadows, marshes and streambanks in the eastern United States and Canada. Wikipedia lists harlequin blueflag, larger blue flag, northern blue flag and poison flag among its common names, and purple iris in Great Britain and Ireland. Cunningham calls it feminine, with Venus and water, and lists money. He has the root carried for financial gain and placed in cash registers to increase business. Kynes names blue flag within her Iris heading, which also covers the common flag iris and the yellow iris, and gives prosperity and money for blue flag alone. Her other powers, her deities and her water element belong to the whole heading, so the curator has been asked to settle them. Cunningham, Kynes, Wikipedia and the American Herbal Products Association all record hazards, set out in the safety notes.
