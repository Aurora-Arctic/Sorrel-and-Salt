# Cactus

Cacti are the plants of the family Cactaceae, which Wikipedia describes as succulents of dry places, usually with spines. Cunningham's entry names no species and says few cacti are poisonous, so only those used for food should be eaten. He gives protection and chastity as its powers. The spines protect: a cactus indoors guards against intrusion and absorbs negativity, one can stand outside facing each direction, and spines go into Witch bottles and are used to mark candles. Kynes lists Opuntia phaeacantha, Melocactus matanzanus and Carnegiea gigantea under her heading, with water and Mars, and gives abundance, healing and protection. She sells a flower essence and cactus juice, and uses needle-like spines in protection spells. Neither author names one species, so the file asks a curator to confirm the family reading.
