# Oak

Oak here means the genus Quercus, the trees and shrubs of the beech family that bear acorns. Cunningham heads his entry with white oak and Kynes with three species, black, English and white, and the AHPA handbook with the genus and the species white oak, sessile oak and English oak, so all three citations are left for the curator. Cunningham gives the planet as the Sun and the element as fire, with powers of protection, healing, fertility, luck and money, and names White Oak, Duir and Jove's Nuts as other names. Kynes ties oak to Jupiter, Thor, Zeus, Odin, Perun and Pan among others, and to the Ogham letter Dair, and sells oak bark pieces and a flower essence. Cunningham's cautions on external use of the bark are recorded as hazards. Wikipedia describes the genus and says oaks are widespread across the Northern Hemisphere.
