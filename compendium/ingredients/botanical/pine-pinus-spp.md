# Pine

Pines are conifers of the genus Pinus, trees or shrubs with needles in bunches of usually two to five and seeds carried on woody cones. Wikipedia says they grow across the Northern Hemisphere and are the source of timber, pine nuts and resin. Cunningham calls pine masculine, with Mars and air, and lists healing, fertility, protection, exorcism and money, with deities including Cybele, Pan, Attis, Dionysus and Astarte. He has cones carried for fertility, needles burned to purify a house and to reverse spells, and a branch over the door in Japan to keep joy in the home. Kynes names Scots pine and white pine, and adds Ishtar, Isis, Osiris and many others. Both headings cover more than one species, so each citation is left for the curator.
