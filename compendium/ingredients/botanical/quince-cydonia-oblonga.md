# Quince

The quince is a deciduous tree of the Rosaceae family, and the only member of its genus. Wikipedia says it bears hard, aromatic, golden-yellow pome fruit, similar in appearance to a pear, that is eaten processed into jam, quince cheese or alcoholic drinks. Cunningham calls it feminine, with Saturn, earth and Venus, and lists protection and love. Quince seed carried protects against evil, harm and accidents, a quince was shared by a bridal couple in Roman times, and serving quinces to loved ones ensures fidelity. Kynes adds the Moon, and says the Greeks and Romans decorated wedding tables and bedchambers with its fruit and flowers, and Pliny the Elder noted protection from the evil eye. Both headings cover more than the common quince, so their citations are left for the curator. Kynes and Wikipedia record a hazard of the seeds, set out in the safety notes.
