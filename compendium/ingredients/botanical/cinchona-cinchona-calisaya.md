# Cinchona

Cinchona is a tree of the Andean forests whose bark yields quinine and other alkaloids. Cunningham heads it with two species, Cinchona ledgeriana and C. succirubra, and the American Herbal Products Association heads its entry with the genus and several species. Cunningham gives luck and protection: a piece of the bark carried gives protection from bodily harm and evil. Wikipedia notes the trees are also called fever trees, because the bark was long the main treatment for malaria. The quinine in the bark can cause serious hypersensitivity reactions, and it should not be taken in pregnancy except under qualified supervision.
