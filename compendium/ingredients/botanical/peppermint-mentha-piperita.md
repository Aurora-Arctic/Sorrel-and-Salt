# Peppermint

Peppermint is a hybrid mint, a cross between watermint and spearmint, native to Europe and the Middle East and now grown across the world. Wikipedia names it as a source of menthol and menthone. Cunningham calls it masculine, with Mercury and fire, gives the deity Pluto, and lists purification, sleep, love, healing and psychic powers. He says fresh leaves on the altar call good spirits, a leaf under the pillow can bring dreams of the future, and it is rubbed on furniture, walls and floors to cleanse them. He notes Pliny's claim that peppermint excites love. Kynes shares a mint heading with spearmint and water mint and adds Demeter, Hecate and Persephone, and her citation is left for the curator. Wikipedia records skin and stomach effects, set out in the safety notes.
