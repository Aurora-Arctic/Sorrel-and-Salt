# Incense Grass

Cymbopogon commutatus is a perennial grass known as incense grass, aromatic rush, camel's hay or lemon grass. Its range runs from South Asia to parts of Africa and Arabia. Its foliage has a sweet, lemony smell when mashed. It is used for medicine in north-eastern Arabia. Because it yields citronella oil, it has been used as a component of soap, and it is also used to make insect repellent. It appeared on a four riyal Qatari stamp.
