# Mouse-Ear Hawkweed

Mouse-ear hawkweed is a low, hairy, yellow-flowered perennial of the daisy family, native to Europe and northern Asia, with a basal rosette of leaves and single lemon-coloured flower heads. Wikipedia says it is highly variable and an allelopathic plant, meaning its roots release substances that inhibit the growth of others. Grieve says it spreads by runners like a strawberry, that its juice is less bitter and more astringent than other milky plants, and that it was much used in the Middle Ages under the name Auricula muris. She records it as a sudorific, tonic and expectorant, a remedy for whooping cough and lung affections. Culpeper says the Moon owns it.

#### Questions for the Curator

(ACCEPTED). Do you accept the Grieve citation as _Pilosella officinarum_? My recommendation: yes.
