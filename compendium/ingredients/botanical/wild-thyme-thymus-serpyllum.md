# Wild Thyme

Wild thyme is a low, usually creeping subshrub of the mint family, with stems up to about 10 centimetres tall, small oval evergreen leaves and strongly scented lilac to magenta flowers in clusters. Wikipedia gives it the names Breckland thyme, creeping thyme and elfin thyme. Grieve, who uses the herb as the part, says it is found on dry downs and in barren soil, small and forming dense cushions, and that bees are fond of its blossoms. She adds that it was looked on as one of the fairies' flowers, tufts of thyme being a favourite playground of theirs. Kynes covers common thyme and wild thyme together and calls wild thyme a ground cover 2 to 4 inches tall, also known as mother-thyme and shepherd's thyme; the entry is flagged for the curator.
