# Chinaberry

Chinaberry is a deciduous tree of the mahogany family, native to Indomalaya and Australasia, and also called pride of India, bead tree, Persian lilac and Indian lilac. Cunningham, who spells the epithet "azederach", marks it Poison and uses the seeds as luck charms and carries them to bring a change into your life. Kynes gives earth, Taurus and Saturn, and the powers of banishing, change and healing. In India the tree was seen as the female counterpart to the Bodhi tree, and strings of the seeds hung in doorways to ward off illness. She has you hold a bead necklace while you meditate on healing. Every part of the plant is toxic, the fruit most of all.
