# Cabbage

Wild cabbage is a Eurasian plant whose cultivated forms include cabbage, broccoli and kale, according to Wikipedia. Cunningham calls cabbage feminine, with the Moon and water, and gives luck as its power. He has it planted in the garden right after a marriage, for luck in the marriage and in the garden. Kynes adds Cancer and Scorpio for green cabbage, Jupiter for red, and faeries, and lists fertility and luck. In her fertility spell a paper is wrapped in cabbage leaves, and an odd number of seeds is carried as a luck amulet. She records French newlyweds eating cabbage soup, Samhain love divination, and Anglo-Saxon medicinal use. Her folk names are colewort, purple cabbage and Dutch cabbage. Kynes groups several varieties under one heading, so the file asks a curator whether her entry describes the species alone.
