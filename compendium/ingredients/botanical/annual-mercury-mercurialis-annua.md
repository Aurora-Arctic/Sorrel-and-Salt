# Annual Mercury

Annual mercury is a branched, smooth, light green weed, taller than dog's mercury, with small leaves and male and female flowers sometimes on the same plant. It is native to the Middle East and the Mediterranean, where it grows on bare sandy soil, and in recent centuries it has spread across northern Europe and much of the world as a garden and farm weed. It flowers from July to October and sows itself freely by its rough seeds, which harvester ants carry off for their oily coating. Older writers called it Garden Mercury and French Mercury. It was once a mucilaginous emollient and the base of a purgative syrup, and its boiled leaves were eaten as a pot-herb in parts of Germany. Its name comes from the Roman god Mercury.
