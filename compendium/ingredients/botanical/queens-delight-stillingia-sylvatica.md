# Queen's Delight

Queen's delight, also called stillingia, queen's root, silver leaf or yaw root, is a herb or low shrub of the spurge family, Euphorbiaceae. Wikipedia says it is native to the south-central and south-eastern United States, where it grows in sandy places such as sandhills and pine flatwoods, and flowers between March and June. Cunningham, who prints the heading as Stillengia, gives it one power, psychic powers. The root is burned to develop them, and if something is lost the smoke of burning stillingia is followed to its hiding place. Kynes gives it no entry. The American Herbal Products Association lists the root as its part and records hazards, set out in the safety notes.
