# Strawberry Tree

The strawberry tree is an evergreen shrub or small tree of the heather family, native to the Mediterranean Basin and western Europe. Wikipedia says it is named for its red, rough-skinned berry, which resembles a strawberry but is edible only when fully ripe and then tasteless, and that Pliny the Elder is credited with the name unedo, meaning I eat only one. It also records a decoction of the leaves used in folk medicine. Cunningham, whose heading prints the Latin name with a spelling slip, gives it a masculine, Mars, fire character, links it to the goddess Cardea, and says the Romans used it to drive off evil and to protect children, and that it has been used in exorcisms since ancient Greece. Only protection among his powers matches a category. Cunningham's exorcism has no category of that word, so it is left out.
