# Centaury

Centaury is a small bitter herb with pink flowers, known as common or European centaury. Cunningham calls it masculine, with the Sun and fire, and says its smoke drives off snakes, though his heading groups several species. Kynes names Centaurium erythraea, with the synonyms C. minus and Erythraea centaurium, and gives fire, Leo, Sagittarius, Jupiter, Saturn and the Sun, with Adonis, Apollo, Horus and Ra. She lists banishing, healing, intuition, knowledge, protection, psychic work and strength. She places three flowering sprigs on the altar and keeps a jar of the powdered herb in the workspace while developing a skill. She tells how the centaur Chiron healed himself with it, and says it is strongest picked on Beltane.
