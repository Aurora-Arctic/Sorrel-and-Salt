# Green Hellebore

Green hellebore, also called American hellebore or Indian poke, is a species of Veratrum native to eastern and western North America. Wikipedia calls it extremely toxic and a pest plant to farmers with livestock. Grieve says it grows in swamps, low grounds and moist meadows of the United States, that the part used is the dried rhizome and roots, and that it closely resembles the German white hellebore, Veratrum album. She names its principal alkaloids as pseudojervine, rubijervine, jervine, cevadine, protoveratrine and protoveratridine, and calls it emetic, diaphoretic, sedative and highly poisonous. She says veratrine, got from related plants, was used externally as an anodyne counter-irritant for neuralgia. The AHPA handbook says it was used in the mid twentieth century for hypertension and dropped for its severe side effects.

#### Questions for the Curator

Grieve prints the picture caption "(Helleborus viridis LINN.)" beneath the title, which is a different plant (green hellebore of Europe), but her page heading and text are for _Veratrum viride_, so I cited the page as `stated` for _Veratrum viride_ only. Do you accept? My recommendation: yes.
