# Plantain

Plantains are the herbs of the genus Plantago, about 200 species of mostly herbaceous flowering plants found worldwide, many of them weeds of roadsides and wet ground. Wikipedia says the common name is shared with the unrelated cooking plantain. Cunningham names Plantago lanceolata, P. major and P. media, calls the plant feminine, with Venus and earth, and gives the powers of healing, strength, protection and snake repelling. He has the leaf bound to the head with red wool for headaches, hung in the car against evil spirits, and a piece of root carried against snakebite. His other names include Waybread, Snakeweed and Englishman's Foot. Kynes names greater plantain and ribwort and adds uses for dreams and success. Both headings cover more than one species, so each citation is left for the curator.
