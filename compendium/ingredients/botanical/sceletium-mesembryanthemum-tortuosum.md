# Sceletium

Mesembryanthemum tortuosum is a succulent of the family Aizoaceae, native to the Cape Provinces of South Africa. Wikipedia says it is often referred to by its synonym Sceletium tortuosum, and gives the common names kanna and kougoed. The article says plants of the genus Sceletium have likely been used for millennia by the San and Khoi peoples as masticatories and traditional medicines, for thirst, hunger, fatigue, and social and spiritual purposes. The plant contains more than 25 alkaloids, with mesembrine-type alkaloids predominating. The article says human toxicology data are limited. This entry rests on that one article. The row's name, Sceletium, is the genus name, so the species is entered under it as a question for the curator.
