# Stone Root

Stone root is a perennial of the mint family native to eastern North America, where it is the most widely distributed member of its genus and grows in damp, shaded woods from Quebec to Florida. Wikipedia says it bears lemon-scented flowers in mid-summer, when little else blooms in dense forest, and that Native Americans used it for a variety of ailments before it regained popularity as a medicinal herb in the mid-1800s. Grieve describes a four-sided stem one to four feet high, a hard, knobby, brown-grey rhizome about four inches long, and a strong odour and pungent, spicy taste. She names it for its discoverer, Peter Collinson, and calls the root its chief virtue.
