# Water Mint

Water mint is a spreading perennial of the mint family that grows in wet places, in the shallow margins and channels of streams, rivers, pools, ditches, canals, marshes and fens. Wikipedia describes square stems, green or purple and variably hairy, wide-spreading fleshy rhizomes and ovate leaves, and says it can reach about 90 centimetres tall; it rises above the surface if it grows in the water itself. Kynes, in a mint entry that covers peppermint, spearmint and water mint together, gives the element water for the group's water mints and records that the Druids regarded water mint as a sacred herb. He describes its leaves as tinged with purple, with pink to lilac flowers in rounded clusters. Because Kynes pools the mints, the entry is flagged for the curator.
