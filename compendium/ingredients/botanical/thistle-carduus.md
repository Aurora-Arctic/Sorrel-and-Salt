# Thistle

Thistle here is the genus Carduus, the plumeless thistles, 82 accepted species of prickly plants of the daisy family. Wikipedia says an unusually high share of them are noxious weeds. Cunningham, under the heading Carduus spp., calls thistle masculine, with Mars and fire, and gives Thor and Minerva as its deities. He lists strength, protection, healing, exorcism and hex-breaking: a bowl of thistles strengthens the spirits, a potted thistle by the door guards the house against evil, a thistle-fibre shirt breaks spells, and a blossom carried guards the bearer. Kynes heads her Thistle entry with the blessed, milk and cotton thistles, which she gives as Cnicus, Silybum and Onopordum, with Carduus only as a synonym of each. She adds Pan and Tyr among the deities, and the powers banish, clarity, courage, release, strength and truth. Neither heading names the genus alone, so both are marked unresolved.
