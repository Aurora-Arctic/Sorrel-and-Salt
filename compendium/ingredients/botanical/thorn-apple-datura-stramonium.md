# Thorn Apple

Thorn apple, also called jimsonweed, is a poisonous flowering plant of the nightshade family, Solanaceae, now a weed of warm and temperate regions. Wikipedia says all its parts hold the tropane alkaloids atropine, hyoscyamine and scopolamine, that it is used as a hallucinogen, and that overdoses are often fatal. Kynes gives it the element water, the sign Sagittarius, the planets Jupiter and Saturn, and Hades and Hecate as its deities. Her powers are banish, divination, dream work, hexes and protection. She has keywords written on paper, sprinkled with the flower essence and burned, and tells of its use at Delphi, in medieval witches' salves and as an Aztec and Navajo hallucinogen. Grieve heads her page with a warning of poison and lists leaves and seeds as the parts used. Every one of these sources records the hazard, which is set out in the safety notes.
