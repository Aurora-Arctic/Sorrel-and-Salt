# Catnip

Catnip is a mint-family herb native to southern and eastern Europe, the northern Middle East and Central Asia, and Wikipedia also calls it catmint. Cunningham calls it feminine, with Venus and water, and gives it to the cat goddess Bast. He uses it for cat magic and love, binds a psychic bond with a cat by it, puts it with rose petals in love sachets, and says it hung over the door attracts good spirits. Kynes adds air, Mercury, Sekhmet and Samhain, and lists abundance, courage, dream work, home blessing, fertility, healing, peace, protection, psychic work, spirit work and strength. She records a French tale of chewing the root for courage and a pillow stuffing for sleep.
