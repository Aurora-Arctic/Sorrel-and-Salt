# Pomegranate

The pomegranate is a deciduous shrub of the Lythraceae family whose fruit is full of juicy seeds. Wikipedia says it originated across the Caucasus and the Iranian plateau and was first domesticated by ancient Iranians about 5,000 years ago. Cunningham calls it masculine, with Mercury and fire, gives the deities Persephone and Ceres, and lists divination, fertility, wealth, luck and wishes. He says the seeds are eaten, and the skin carried, to increase fertility, a wish should be made before eating the fruit, a branch finds hidden wealth, and branches over doorways guard against evil. Kynes records its use by Assyrians, Greeks and Romans as a symbol of fertility, and of death and rebirth in the myth of Demeter and Persephone. The American Herbal Products Association records allergic and other hazards, set out in the safety notes.
