# Cat-Thorn

Scutia myrtina, commonly known as cat-thorn, is a plant in the buckthorn family, Rhamnaceae. It is a variable plant that may grow as a shrub or tree two to ten metres tall, often as a scandent liane that climbs by means of thorns. The older bark is dark, corky and fissured, and the thorns are sharp, recurved and paired at the nodes. The common name refers to the thorns, which look like a cat's claw. The leaves are opposite and notched at the apex, and the fruit is a berry with black skin. The Wikipedia article on muti, the traditional medicine of southern Africa, says that in the Eastern Cape an ointment made from the leaves of a plant it spells Scutia mytina is used to hasten the birth of the baby and the placenta. The plant article notes that several species of Scutia have been used in traditional medicine, such as in the Ayurvedic system of India.
