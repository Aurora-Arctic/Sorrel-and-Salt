# Pipsissewa

Pipsissewa, also called umbellate wintergreen or prince's pine, is a small perennial of dry woodlands and sandy soils, native across the cool temperate Northern Hemisphere. Wikipedia says it grows up to 35 centimetres tall, with one stem bearing shiny evergreen leaves in opposite pairs or whorls, and small umbels of white to pink flowers. Cunningham gives it two powers, money and spirit calling. His only uses are these: crush pipsissewa, blend it with rose hips and violet flowers, and burn the mixture to draw good spirits for magical aid, and carry it to attract money. His other names are False Wintergreen, Ground Holly, Price's Pine and Princess Pine. He gives it no gender, planet, element or deity. Kynes gives pipsissewa no entry, so the entry rests on Cunningham and Wikipedia alone.
