# Chestnut

Chestnut here is the sweet chestnut, a long-lived tree of the beech family whose brown nuts come in spiny husks. Wikipedia also calls it the Spanish or European chestnut. Cunningham's heading lists Castanea sativa, C. dentata and other species, and gives masculine, Jupiter, fire and love: use chestnuts in love spells or feed them to a loved one. Kynes puts the sweet chestnut and the horse chestnut under one heading, so her powers may not belong to one tree. She adds air, water, Jupiter, the Sun, Artemis, Boann and Diana, and lists abundance, home blessing, healing, justice, love, peace, prosperity and protection. In Wales chestnut stood in for hazelnut, and in Ireland branches were hung in cattle barns on Beltane.
