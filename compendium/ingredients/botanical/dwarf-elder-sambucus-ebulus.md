# Dwarf Elder

Dwarf elder, or danewort, is a herbaceous perennial one to two metres tall, spreading in large groups from a creeping underground stem. Wikipedia describes opposite pinnate leaves with a foetid smell, flat-topped clusters of white flowers and small glossy black berries giving a purple juice. Grieve says it favours waste places and the ruins of old buildings, and that the names Danewort and Walewort come from a belief that it sprang from the blood of slain Danes. She calls its action more drastic than the common elder: the leaves are the part chiefly used, the root decoction a drastic purgative, and the berry rob actively purgative. Wikipedia says all parts of the plant are toxic, especially the seeds. Culpeper puts both elders under Venus.

#### Questions for the Curator

Culpeper prints only the English heading "THE DWARF-ELDER" and no Latin name, so his page is cited with `nameBasis: unresolved`. The roster ties it to _Sambucus ebulus_; no authority I read maps Culpeper's heading to that formal name. He gives the planet: "Both Elder and Dwarf Tree are under the dominion of Venus", so the planet covers the common elder as well. Do you accept Culpeper's "THE DWARF-ELDER" as _Sambucus ebulus_? My recommendation: accept it only if you are content to rest the Culpeper-sourced fields on the common name; otherwise drop the Culpeper citation and the fields that rest on it.
