# Orchid

Orchid here means the genus Orchis, which Wikipedia describes as a genus in the orchid family found from Europe and north-west Africa to Tibet, Mongolia and Xinjiang, with a name from the Greek word for testicle that refers to its root tubers. Cunningham's entry is headed Orchis spp. and Kynes gives the Adam and Eve orchid and the early purple orchid under one heading, so both citations are left for the curator. Cunningham calls it feminine, with Venus and water, and gives one power, love, by carrying the root in a sachet. His other names include Salep, Sahlab, Saloop and Satyrion. Kynes adds the signs Libra and Scorpio, and the deities Bacchus, Dionysus, Pan and Saturn, with powers of fertility, love, loyalty, psychic abilities and success. She has you carry a piece of root as an amulet or split a root in half to strengthen commitment, and she sells flower essence and whole or powdered roots.
