# Wood Anemone

Wood anemone is a spring-flowering perennial of the buttercup family, Ranunculaceae, native to Europe and found in shady woods. Wikipedia describes a plant under 30 cm with palmate basal leaves from creeping rhizomes and a white flower with six petal-like sepals, which dies back by mid summer, and says it was also called windflower. Kynes, under a heading shared with the poppy anemone, gives wood anemone the names devil's bite, evening twilight, fairy windflower, lady's chemise and wood windflower. She records a German amulet made from it, an English legend that faeries paint its patterns, and a belief that picking it brings a thunderstorm. She has the first flower blown upon with a wish for luck. Kynes also records a hazard for anemones, set out in the safety notes. The questions note that her heading covers two plants.
