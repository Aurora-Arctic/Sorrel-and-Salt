# Globe Amaranth

Globe amaranth is a tropical annual native to Central America, including Panama and Guatemala, and now grown worldwide. Wikipedia says it blooms throughout summer and early autumn, is heat tolerant and fairly drought resistant, and reaches about 24 inches, with flower heads about 4 centimetres long, pollinated by butterflies, bees and other insects. The flowers keep their shape and colour when dried, so they go into leis in Hawaii and into the garland a sister gives her brother at Bhai Tika in Nepal, where it is called makhamali ful. It is common in cutting gardens, its betacyanins make a red-violet food dye, and Wikipedia says the edible plant has been used in herbal medicine.
