# Blueberry

Blueberries are a widespread group of perennial flowering plants with blue or purple berries, classed by Wikipedia in the section Cyanococcus of the genus Vaccinium. The commercial kinds, both wild lowbush and cultivated highbush, are native to North America. Wikipedia says the berry is covered in a powdery wax called the bloom, and is pale green at first, then reddish purple, and blue when ripe. Cunningham lists protection as its power and names four species under his heading. He has blueberries placed beneath the doormat to keep undesirables away from the property or out of the home, and pies or tarts eaten when under psychic attack to take the protection inside the body. Kynes has no blueberry entry. Her bilberry entry is for a different plant, Vaccinium myrtillus, and is not used here.
