# Emilia

Emilia, or lilac tasselflower, is a branching annual herb of the sunflower family, 20 to 70 centimetres tall, with lyrate leaves that sometimes turn purplish with age. One plant bears several pink or purplish flower heads, and the florets may be purple, scarlet, red, pink, orange, white or lilac. It completes its life cycle in about 90 days. It is widespread in the tropics, apparently native to Asia and naturalised in Africa, Australia, the Americas and on oceanic islands. The page says it is hepatotoxic, because it contains tumorigenic pyrrolizidine alkaloids, yet the leaves and young shoots are eaten as a vegetable, picked before flowering. In India it is one of the Dasapushpam, the Ten Sacred Flowers of Kerala.
