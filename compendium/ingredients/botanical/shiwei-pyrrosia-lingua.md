# Shiwei

Shiwei, written 石韋, is a fern named Pyrrosia lingua in the Wikipedia page on bigu, the Chinese practice of avoiding grain. The page says that the Quegu shiqi, Eliminating Grain and Eating Qi, a manuscript of 168 BCE found in 1973 among the Mawangdui Silk Texts and the oldest documented grain-avoidance diet, outlines a method for replacing grains with qi circulations while consuming medicinal herbs. It names shiwei as notable among them, taken as a diuretic to treat the urine retention that follows giving up grains. The text sets those who eat grain, who eat what is square, against those who eat qi, who eat what is round, using the Chinese model of round heaven and square earth. The page does not describe the plant or say which part is used.
