# Pomelo

The pomelo, also called the pummelo or shaddock, is the largest citrus fruit and an ancestor of several cultivated citrus, including the bitter orange and the grapefruit. Wikipedia says it is native to Southeast Asia, grows as a tree of 5 to 15 metres with winged leaf stalks and fragrant yellow-white flowers, and has at least sixty cultivars. It says the tree may have reached China around 100 BCE, and that seeds reached the Americas in the late 1600s. It also warns that the fruit can interact with many prescription drugs in the same way as grapefruit, though it is not itself toxic.
