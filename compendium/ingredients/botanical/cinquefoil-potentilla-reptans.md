# Cinquefoil

Cinquefoil here is creeping cinquefoil, a creeping perennial of the rose family with yellow flowers and leaves of five to seven leaflets, called five-fingers because of them. Cunningham heads the entry with three species, Potentilla canadensis, P. erecta and P. reptans, and Kynes groups creeping and dwarf cinquefoil, so the powers may not belong to one plant alone. Cunningham calls it masculine, with Jupiter and fire, and says the five leaflets stand for love, money, health, power and wisdom. He hangs it at the door or lays it on the bed for protection, and bathes the forehead and hands in an infusion nine times to wash away hexes. Kynes pours an infusion on the ground to return a hex to its sender.
