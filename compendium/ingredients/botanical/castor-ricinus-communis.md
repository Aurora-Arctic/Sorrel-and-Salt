# Castor

Castor is a tall plant of the spurge family, grown for its mottled seeds, from which castor oil is pressed. Cunningham, who prints the species name as Ricinus communes, marks it Poison and gives it one power, protection. He says castor beans guard against the evil eye and all negativity, and that they absorb evil. His folk names include Palma Christi, Mamona and Makula Kula. The seeds contain ricin and are highly poisonous, and ricin is present in lower amounts through the rest of the plant. The American Herbal Products Association notes that ricin is removed in extracting the oil.
