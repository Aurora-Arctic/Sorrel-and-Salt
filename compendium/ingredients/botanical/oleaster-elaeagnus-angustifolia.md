# Oleaster

Elaeagnus angustifolia, called Russian olive, silver berry, oleaster or wild olive, is a thorny tree native to Asia and limited parts of eastern Europe, with stems, buds and leaves covered in silvery to rusty scales. Its highly aromatic, creamy yellow flowers open in early summer and are followed by orange-red, cherry-like fruit about one centimetre wide, sweet but dry and mealy. It tolerates salt well. The page says the dried fruit, known as senjed, is one of the seven items of the Haft-sin table at Nowruz, the Iranian spring celebration, and is served in syrup in the fruit salad haft mēwa in Afghanistan. In Iran the dried fruit powder is mixed with milk for rheumatoid arthritis and joint pains. In folk herbalism the leaves, fruit, flowers and bark are all used, and the flowers are made into an infusion for fevers. The roots and shoots contain the alkaloids harmine, harmane and harmol.
