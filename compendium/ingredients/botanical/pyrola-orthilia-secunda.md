# Pyrola

Orthilia secunda is a low evergreen herb of the heather family with a circumboreal range, found across much of the Northern Hemisphere, and the only species of its genus. Wikipedia lists the common names sidebells wintergreen, one-sided wintergreen and serrated-wintergreen, and says it is a mixotroph, taking about half its carbon from mycorrhizal fungi. Grieve's page on the Pyrolas names it Pyrola secunda, explains the name Yevering Bells from its drooping bell-shaped flowers, and calls it very local in Britain, found in Monmouthshire and from Yorkshire northward. Wikipedia records a strong decoction of the root, used by the Southern Carrier of British Columbia as an eyewash.
