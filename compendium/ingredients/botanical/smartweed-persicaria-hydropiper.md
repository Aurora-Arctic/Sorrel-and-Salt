# Smartweed

Smartweed, or water pepper, is an annual herb of the knotweed family found in damp places and shallow water across Europe, temperate Asia, North America, Australia and New Zealand. Wikipedia says it grows 20 to 70 centimetres tall with narrow leaves, nodding spikes of tiny white-pink flowers and a pungent taste from the sesquiterpenoids polygodial and warburganal, and that wild plants produce oils that irritate the skin. Cultivated plants are eaten in Japan as tade, and the plant is used in traditional Chinese medicine. Grieve calls it stimulant, diuretic and diaphoretic, notes its irritant action and its 3 to 4 per cent tannin, and records the old belief that a handful under a horse's saddle prevents hunger and thirst.
