# Vervain

Common vervain is a perennial herb native to Europe, described by Linnaeus in 1753, with spikes of mauve flowers. Wikipedia says it has long been associated with divine and supernatural forces as well as with medicine. Cunningham calls it feminine, with Venus and earth, and lists love, protection, purification, peace, money, youth, chastity, sleep and healing. He has a crown of vervain protect the magician while invoking spirits, the herb placed in the home against lightning and storms, the infusion sprinkled to chase off evil spirits, and the dried herb scattered as a peace-bringer. Kynes adds air and fire, Capricorn, Gemini and Sagittarius, and the Sun, and lists a long run of deities and powers that include divination, dream work, prophecy and inspiration. She sells a flower essence, an essential oil and cut leaves and stems. The American Herbal Products Association's monograph covers more than one Verbena, so it is cited with a question for the curator.
