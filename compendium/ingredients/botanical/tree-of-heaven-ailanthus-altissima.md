# Tree of Heaven

Tree of heaven is a fast-growing deciduous tree native to China and Taiwan, widely planted elsewhere as a shade and street tree and now naturalised in many regions. Wikipedia lists Ailanthus glandulosa among its synonyms and notes it is sometimes called Chinese sumac, though that name is also applied to other species. Grieve describes a large, handsome tree of rapid growth with leaves one to two feet long and greenish flowers of a disagreeable odour, introduced to England in 1751. She says it is cultivated in France to feed the ailanthus silk moth, calls the bark antispasmodic, cardiac depressant and astringent, and notes its use in China for dysentery.

#### Questions for the Curator
