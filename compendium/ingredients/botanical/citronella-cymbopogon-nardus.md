# Citronella

Citronella grass is a perennial aromatic grass of the Poaceae family, originating in tropical Asia. Wikipedia says it cannot be eaten because it is unpalatable, and that it is invasive and renders pastureland useless, since cattle will starve even in its abundance. It is the source of citronella oil, an essential oil widely used for its natural insect-repelling properties. The oil is extracted from the aerial parts and applied to the skin or burned slowly, as in a citronella candle, to deter biting insects, particularly mosquitoes. Wikipedia gives the botanical name as Cymbopogon nardus (L.) Rendle, with Andropogon nardus L. (1753) as its older name, and the common name citronella grass.
