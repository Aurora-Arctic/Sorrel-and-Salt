# Vetch

Vetches are the plants of the genus Vicia, which Wikipedia describes as over 240 species of flowering plants in the legume family, Fabaceae, native to Eurasia, Africa and the Americas. Cunningham's entry is headed Vetch, Giant, with the genus given as Vicia spp., and lists fidelity. He has the root rubbed on the body and placed under the pillow when a loved one has strayed. Kynes has a Vetch entry for Vicia sativa, which she also calls fitches, poor man's peas, tare and thetch. She gives air, Gemini, Virgo and the Moon, and lists binding, love, loyalty and negativity. She sells a flower essence and seeds, and says a vine of vetch was wrapped around a handfasting broomstick. Both headings are narrower than the genus or name a different plant, so both are cited with a question for the curator. Cunningham's hazard mark is set out in the safety notes.
