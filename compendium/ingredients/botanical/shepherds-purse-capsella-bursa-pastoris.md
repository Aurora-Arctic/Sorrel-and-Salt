# Shepherd's Purse

Shepherd's purse is an annual or biennial weed of the mustard family, native to Eurasia and now common in many parts of the world, especially colder climates. Wikipedia says it grows from a rosette of lobed leaves, bears small white four-petalled flowers in any month in the British Isles, and sets flat, heart-shaped seed pods; it has culinary uses. Grieve names it for the pods' likeness to an old leather purse, lists many folk names, and calls it one of the most important drug plants of its family, used for bleeding. Culpeper says it is under the dominion of Saturn. AHPA lists it as a uterine stimulant and advises against use in pregnancy except under supervision.
