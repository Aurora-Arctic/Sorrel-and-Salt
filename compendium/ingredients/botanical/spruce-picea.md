# Spruce

Spruce is the genus Picea, about 37 species of evergreen conifers in the pine family found across the northern temperate and boreal regions. Wikipedia says they are told from other pines by four-sided needles, each set on a small peg that stays on the twig after the needle falls. They are resinous trees, and the name Picea comes from the Latin word for pitch. Wikipedia records that the fresh shoots of many spruces are a source of vitamin C, that Captain Cook brewed spruce beer against scurvy, and that Norway spruce resin has been used in Nordic folk medicine for wounds and skin disorders. Kynes writes about black spruce (Picea mariana), Norway spruce (P. abies) and white spruce (P. glauca) together. She gives earth and water, Cancer, Capricorn and Sagittarius, and powers including protection, healing, prosperity, intuition and inspiration, and an essential oil from the needles and twigs of black spruce. Cunningham has no spruce entry. The Kynes entry is unresolved; see the questions.
