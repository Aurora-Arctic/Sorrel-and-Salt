# Campion

Campions are plants of the genus Silene, which Wikipedia describes as the campions and catchflies. Kynes covers red campion (Silene dioica) and white campion (S. latifolia) under one heading and gives Earth, Mars, faeries and the deities Cernunnos and Pan. She lists clarity, courage, creativity, healing, inspiration, knowledge, love, manifestation, protection and wisdom. She sells a flower essence, seedpods and seeds, and says a pouch of seeds can be carried for support. Her folk names are bachelor's buttons, devil's flower, fairies' pinks, ragged Robin, Robin Hood, thunder flower and thunderbolts, and she records that picking the flowers brings bad luck. No source names a single species for the whole entry, so the file asks a curator whether the genus reading is right.
