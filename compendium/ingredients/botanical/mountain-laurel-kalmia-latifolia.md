# Mountain Laurel

Mountain laurel is an evergreen shrub of the eastern United States, 3 to 9 metres tall, with clusters of pentagonal light pink to white flowers in May and June. Wikipedia says it is the state flower of Connecticut and Pennsylvania, that Native Americans made spoons from the wood, and that all parts of the plant are poisonous because of grayanotoxin and arbutin. Grieve heads her page "POISON!" and says the leaves have narcotic poisoning properties, the flowers yield a deleterious honey, and the plant is dangerous to cattle. She records the powdered leaves as a local remedy in skin diseases, to be used with great care.
