# Tobacco

Tobacco here is the genus Nicotiana, 79 accepted species of herbs and shrubs of the nightshade family, Solanaceae, native to the Americas, Australia, south-western Africa and the South Pacific. Wikipedia says Nicotiana tabacum is the species grown worldwide for tobacco leaf. Cunningham, under the heading Nicotiana spp., calls it masculine, with Mars and fire, and lists healing and purification. He tells how shamanic candidates drink tobacco juice for visions, how it is smoked to converse with spirits, and how it is burned as incense to purify. Kynes heads her entry with common and wild tobacco, giving the elements earth and fire, the sign Virgo and the planets Mars and Moon, with the deity Yacatecuhtli and the powers healing, purification and release. Neither heading names the genus alone, so both are marked unresolved. Cunningham and Kynes both warn that the plant is poisonous, set out in the safety notes.
