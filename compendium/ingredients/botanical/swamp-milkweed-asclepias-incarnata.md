# Swamp Milkweed

Swamp milkweed is a herbaceous perennial native to North America, growing in damp to wet soils on the margins of flooded plains, lakes, ponds and marshes. Wikipedia says it is grown as a garden plant for flowers that attract butterflies and other pollinators with nectar, and that like most milkweeds it has latex containing toxic steroids, which repels many insects and mammals. Grieve describes a smooth, erect stem about two and a half feet high with fragrant rose-purple flowers in umbels, and a knotty yellow-brown rhizome that exudes a milky juice. She calls the root emetic and diuretic, and says it acts on the heart in the same way as digitalis.
