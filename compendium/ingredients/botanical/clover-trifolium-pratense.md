# Clover

Clover here is red clover, a herbaceous short-lived plant of the bean family, with dark pink flowers and leaves of three leaflets marked with a pale crescent. Cunningham heads the entry Trifolium spp. and Kynes groups red and white clover, so the lore may not belong to red clover alone. Cunningham calls it masculine, with Mercury and air. A two-leaved clover leads to a lover, a three-leaved one is a protective amulet, and a four-leaved one worn is said to ward off madness and strengthen psychic powers. Red clover in the bath is for financial dealings, and white clover is worn or scattered against hexes. Kynes makes a crown of flowers to attract love.
