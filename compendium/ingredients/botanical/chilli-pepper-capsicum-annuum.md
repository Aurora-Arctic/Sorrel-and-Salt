# Chilli Pepper

Chilli pepper here is the hot pepper of the nightshade family, grown for its pungent fruit, which Wikipedia says comes in many colours and cultivars. Cunningham, who spells it chili, calls it masculine, with Mars and fire, and gives fidelity, breaking hexes and love. Two crossed dried peppers tied with a red or pink ribbon under the pillow are meant to keep a mate faithful. Scattering red pepper around the house is meant to break a curse. Kynes lists cayenne, jalapeño and paprika as varieties under one heading and adds Aries, courage, strength, sex and protection. She hangs a strand as a talisman and scatters flakes at the corners of a property. Both books head the entry above the single species.
