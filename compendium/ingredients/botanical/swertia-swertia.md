# Swertia

Swertia is a genus in the gentian family whose plants are sometimes called felworts. Some species bear very showy purple and blue flowers, and many members of the genus have medicinal and cultural purposes. The page says the plants of the genus Frasera are sometimes considered part of it, sometimes a separate genus and sometimes the same. Plants of the World Online gives the genus 165 species, and the page names Swertia japonica, the Japanese felwort, and Swertia tibetica among them. The page lists several xanthones among its chemical constituents. Swerilactones from Swertia mileensis showed activity against the hepatitis B virus in laboratory tests. The page calls it one of the most widely used medicinal plants of Sikkim.
