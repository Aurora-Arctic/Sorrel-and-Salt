# Hairy Cumin

Ammodaucus leucotrichus is a flowering plant in the carrot family, Apiaceae, and the only member of the genus Ammodaucus. It is endemic to northern Africa, including the Canary Islands. In Morocco it is called kammūn ṣūfī, which the page translates as wooly cumin. There it is used as a medicine, in teas and in compresses, for a range of ailments that include snake bites and respiratory ailments.
