# Zygophyllum simplex

Zygophyllum simplex, known as hureim or simple-leaved bean caper, is a salt-tolerant flowering plant of arid regions of western Asia and Africa. It is an annual, highly branched succulent plant 8 to 20 centimetres tall, with fleshy, simple, oblong-cylindrical leaves and yellow petals, and it flowers from August to May. It is distributed throughout West Asia and Africa and as far east as India, and its most common habitats are shrub-steppes and deserts, where it grows best in salty conditions. In Qatar it is called daa or hureim and is a frequent sight on rocky desert plains. The page says it has a history of use in Arabic folk medicine as an anti-inflammatory.
