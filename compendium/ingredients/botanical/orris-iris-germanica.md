# Orris

Orris is the root of the bearded iris, a flowering plant of hybrid origin in the iris family. Wikipedia says it is commonly called the bearded iris or the German bearded iris. Cunningham's heading names the variety Iris germanica var. florentina, and Kynes treats three irises under one heading and the AHPA handbook the genus, so all three citations are left for the curator. Cunningham calls it feminine, with Venus and water, and gives the deities Aphrodite, Isis, Osiris, Hera and Iris and powers of love, protection and divination. He carries the whole root, adds the powder to sachets and sprinkles it, and records its use in Japan against evil spirits. His other names are Florentine Iris and Queen Elizabeth Root. Kynes adds the signs Aquarius and Gemini, with powers of communication, healing, inspiration, knowledge, love, protection, purification, release, success and wisdom, and sells dried cut root. Both Kynes and the AHPA handbook record hazards.
