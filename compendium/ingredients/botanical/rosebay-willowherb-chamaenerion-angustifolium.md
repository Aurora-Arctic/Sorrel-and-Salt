# Rosebay Willowherb

Rosebay willowherb is a tall perennial of the willow-herb family, Onagraceae, called fireweed in North America. Wikipedia says its reddish stems reach 0.5 to 2 metres, bearing a tapering spike of magenta to pink flowers, and that it is a pioneer of burnt and cleared ground, whose silky seeds (about 80,000 per plant) travel on the wind. It is native across the temperate Northern Hemisphere. Grieve describes it as one of the handsomest wild flowers, says its roots and leaves are demulcent, tonic and astringent, and records the leaves as a tea substitute in Russia. She adds that the young shoots are boiled and eaten like asparagus, though an infusion of the plant is said to be stupifying.
