# Turnip

The turnip is the swollen-rooted form of Brassica rapa, an annual to biennial plant of the mustard family, Brassicaceae, native to Eurasia. Wikipedia says the same species has also been grown into komatsuna, Chinese cabbage, pak choi and rapini. Cunningham calls the turnip feminine, with the Moon and earth, and lists protection and ending relationships. He says turnips are carved as lanterns at Samhain, that a dish of turnips set in front of an admirer who will not leave you alone makes the point, and that turnips placed in the home ward off every form of negativity. His heading marks the root with the note that it is good unless eaten in extreme amounts. Kynes adds water, Gemini, Scorpio and love, and tells the story of Stingy Jack, but heads her entry with a variety, so it is cited with a question for the curator.
