# Samphire

Samphire, or rock samphire, is a succulent, much-branched perennial of the carrot family that grows in crevices on sea cliffs, shingle and sea defences. Wikipedia says it grows to about 30 centimetres, has fleshy, deeply divided leaves that smell of furniture polish and taste hot and peppery, bears umbels of yellowish flowers, and ranges along the Atlantic, Mediterranean and Black Sea coasts, with the genus Crithmum holding only this species. Grieve says the whole plant is aromatic and calls it Sea Fennel, Crest Marine or Sampier, a name from Saint Pierre because it clings to sea cliffs. She gives the pickled young leaves as a condiment. Culpeper says it is an herb of Jupiter.
