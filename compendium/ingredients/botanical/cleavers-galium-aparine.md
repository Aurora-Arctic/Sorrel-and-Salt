# Cleavers

Cleavers is an annual herb of the madder family, also called bedstraw, goosegrass, sticky willy and velcro plant, whose stems cling to cloth and fur. Cunningham calls it feminine, with Saturn and fire, and gives protection. Because the plant cleaves to fabric, he says it can be used in binding spells. The American Herbal Products Association heads its entry with the genus, Galium spp., and covers cleavers together with lady's bedstraw. Kynes has no entry for cleavers. Wikipedia lists a long run of English names for it, among them catchweed, grip grass, hitchhikers, stickyback and sweetheart, most of them for the way it sticks to passers-by.
