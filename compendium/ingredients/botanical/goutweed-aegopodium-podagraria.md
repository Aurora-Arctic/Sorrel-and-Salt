# Goutweed

Goutweed, better known as ground elder, is a herbaceous perennial of the carrot family that grows in shady places and spreads by underground rhizomes to about a metre tall. Wikipedia says it is native to Europe and Asia and has been introduced widely as an ornamental, where it can become an invasive weed. Grieve says the name podagraria comes from the Latin for gout, for which the herb was long a specific, and that it was called Herb Gerard after St Gerard, invoked against gout. She names the herb and root as the parts used and calls it diuretic and sedative, taken for joint aches and sciatic pains, and the young leaves have been eaten boiled as a green vegetable. Culpeper puts it under Saturn.
