# Woad

Woad is a flowering plant of the mustard family, Brassicaceae, long grown in Europe for a blue dye and for medicine. Wikipedia says its name Isatis is linked to treating wounds and tinctoria to dyeing, that medieval woad regions included Languedoc around Toulouse, and that indigo replaced it from the sixteenth century. Kynes calls it fire, with Mars, Pluto and Saturn, and lists courage, strength, protection and prosperity. She writes of Roman accounts of Britons and Picts using it as war paint, and has the leaves used for courage and a pinch scattered to the four directions for prosperity. She sells dried leaves, seeds and powdered pigment. The American Herbal Products Association covers it within a genus entry and names the root as the part, and records cautions, set out in the safety notes. The questions note that the genus heading is not specific to this species.
