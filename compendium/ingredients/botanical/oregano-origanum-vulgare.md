# Oregano

Oregano, sometimes called wild marjoram, is a woody perennial of the mint family, native to the Mediterranean region and naturalised across the temperate Northern Hemisphere. Wikipedia says its flowers can be white, pink or light purple, and that it is widely used as a culinary herb. Cunningham has no entry for it. Kynes treats oregano and sweet marjoram under one heading, so her citation is left for the curator. She gives the elements air and fire, with Mars for oregano and Mercury, the signs Aries, Gemini, Libra and Virgo, and the deities Aphrodite and Venus. Her powers include healing, love, peace, prosperity, protection, psychic abilities, purification and release. She places either herb on a Samhain altar when a loved one dies, to comfort them on the journey through the afterlife, and sells essential oil, fresh leaves and stems, and cut dried leaves. The AHPA handbook records that allergic sensitivity to oregano has been reported.
