# Orange

The orange, or sweet orange, is the fruit of a tree in the citrus family. Wikipedia says it is a hybrid of the pomelo and the mandarin, that it originated in southern China, north-east India and Myanmar, and that the earliest mention is in Chinese literature of 314 BC. Cunningham heads his entry with the spelling Citrus sinesis, and gives the Sun and fire, a masculine nature, the other name Love Fruit, and powers of love, divination, luck and money. He uses the peel, seeds and flowers, and orange-flower water. Kynes lists bitter orange and sweet orange under one heading, so her citation is left for the curator. She gives all four elements, the signs Aries, Leo and Sagittarius, the Sun, and deities including Fortuna, Gaia, Hera and Juno. She puts an orange on the altar at the summer solstice, burns a piece of dried peel to boost a spell, and sells fresh and dried oranges, juice, peel, flower essence and essential oil.
