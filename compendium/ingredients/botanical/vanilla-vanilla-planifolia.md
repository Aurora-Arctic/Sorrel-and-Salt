# Vanilla

Vanilla planifolia is a vanilla orchid native to Mexico, Central America, Colombia and Brazil, and was first scientifically named in 1808, according to Wikipedia. Kynes calls it also Bourbon vanilla, flat-leaved vanilla and Mexican vanilla, and gives water, Libra, Scorpio, Taurus and Venus. Her powers include harmony, inspiration, love, peace and success, and she sells a flower essence, whole or powdered beans, an absolute and an extract. Cunningham calls the plant feminine, with Venus and water, and lists love, lust and mental powers. He gives the folk names Banilje and Tlilxochitl. Cunningham heads his entry with two names for the plant and a third species, so it is cited with a question for the curator. Kynes and Wikipedia both record that handling the plant can irritate the skin, as set out in the safety notes.
