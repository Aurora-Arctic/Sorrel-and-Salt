# Tamarisk

Tamarisk is the common name for the genus Tamarix, about 50 to 60 species of evergreen or deciduous shrubs and trees of drier Eurasia and Africa, also called salt cedar and taray. Wikipedia says they grow on saline soils, with slender branches, grey-green scale-like leaves and pink to white flowers. Cunningham gives a feminine, Saturn, water character and the powers exorcism and protection: a branch is held or its leaves scattered, the smoke drives away snakes, and the Chaldeans divined with it. Kynes adds Aphrodite, Apollo, Cernunnos, Osiris and Pan, Samhain, and powers including banishing, divination, prophecy and protection. Both put several tamarisks under one heading, so those lines are held for the owner to settle. Anu, Wepwawet and Yama are not in the deity list.
