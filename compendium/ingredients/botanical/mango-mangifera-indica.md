# Mango

Mangifera indica, commonly known as the mango tree after its fruit, is an evergreen flowering tree of the family Anacardiaceae. It is a large fruit tree that can grow to 30 metres, with simple, shiny, dark green leaves, and yellow-white fragrant flowers that appear at the end of winter. The article says there are two distinct genetic populations, the Indian type and the Southeast Asian type, and that 500 to 1000 varieties have been named or reported, mostly in India. The fruit is a fleshy drupe, greenish yellow, with bright orange flesh and a large flat pit. Wikipedia's article on Haitian Vodou says that in the religion different tree species are associated with different lwa, and that Oyu is linked with mango trees. The article does not say how the tree is used in practice.
