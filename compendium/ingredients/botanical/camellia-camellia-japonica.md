# Camellia

Common camellia is an evergreen shrub of the tea family, native to China and Japan, and Wikipedia says it is widely grown for its showy flowers. Cunningham calls it feminine, with the Moon and water, and gives riches as its power: fresh blossoms stand in water vessels on the altar during money and prosperity rituals. Kynes adds fire, Scorpio and Venus, the deities Kuan Yin and Parvati, and lists abundance, confidence, courage, love, manifestation, prosperity, strength and truth. She sells a flower essence, camellia (tsubaki) oil, green tea seed oil, teas and seeds. Her lore includes samurai graves marked with red camellias, a Victorian romance emblem, a cup of tea for confidence, a red flower in love spells and a white flower on the altar at esbats. Kynes groups Camellia sinensis with this species, so the file asks a curator to confirm the reading.
