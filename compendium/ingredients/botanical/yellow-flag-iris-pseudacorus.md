# Yellow Flag

Yellow flag is a wetland iris of the family Iridaceae, native to Europe, western Asia and north-west Africa, with bright yellow flowers and sword-like leaves. Wikipedia says its species name means false acorus, after the likeness of its leaves to sweet flag, and that the plant spreads fast by rhizome and by seed carried on water. It is reported as invasive in many American states. Wikipedia says the roots and leaves are toxic, noted in the safety notes. Kynes heads blue flag, common flag and yellow iris together under Iris, which she links to water, Aquarius, Gemini, the Moon, Venus and faeries. Her powers there include communication, healing, protection, purification and wisdom, with a flower essence and dried cut root on sale. The questions note that her heading covers several irises.
