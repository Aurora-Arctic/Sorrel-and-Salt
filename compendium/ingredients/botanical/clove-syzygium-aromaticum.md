# Clove

Clove is the dried, unopened flower bud of an evergreen tree of the myrtle family, native to Indonesia. Its name comes from the Latin for nail. Cunningham calls it masculine, with Jupiter and fire, and gives protection, exorcism, love and money. Burned as incense, cloves are meant to attract riches, drive off hostile forces, purify the area and stop others gossiping about you. Worn or carried, they draw the opposite sex and comfort the bereaved. Kynes adds air and earth, Apollo, Horus, Osiris and Ra, and says cloves were worn on children in northern India against evil and illness. She uses clove to purify sacred space and, in candle magic, to remove negativity.
