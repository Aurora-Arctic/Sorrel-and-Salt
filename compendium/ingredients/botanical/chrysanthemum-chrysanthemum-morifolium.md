# Chrysanthemum

Chrysanthemum here is the florist's chrysanthemum, a late-season flower of the daisy family that Wikipedia's genus page says blooms in autumn and is native mainly to East Asia. Cunningham marks it Poison and calls it masculine, with the Sun and fire. He gives protection: wearing the flowers guards against the wrath of the gods, and grown in the garden they guard it from evil spirits. Kynes groups the common chrysanthemum with the garland chrysanthemum (Glebionis coronaria) and lists Leo, Mercury, Amaterasu, Apollo, Athena, Hecate and Medea. She adds home, grief, inspiration, love, release and truth. She places a white chrysanthemum on the altar for truth, and sets petals on water to let go.
