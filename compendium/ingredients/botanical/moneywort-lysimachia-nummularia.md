# Moneywort

Moneywort, or creeping Jenny, is a prostrate evergreen perennial of Europe, spreading rapidly by rooting stems, with rounded leaves in opposite pairs and cup-shaped yellow flowers. Wikipedia says it prefers damp ground and is considered invasive in places in North America where it has been introduced. Grieve says the names allude to its trailing growth and to the coin-like leaves, and that it was once reputed one of the best wound-herbs, the bruised fresh leaves being applied to wounds and sores. Culpeper says Venus owns it and that it is good to stay fluxes, and to heal wounds and spreading ulcers, using the juice, the powder or a decoction of the green herb.
