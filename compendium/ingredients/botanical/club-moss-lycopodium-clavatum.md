# Club Moss

Club moss is not a true moss. It is a low plant with needlelike leaves that looks like tiny pine trees, with club-shaped spore cases on separate stems. Cunningham marks it X, not recommended for internal use, and calls it feminine, with the Moon and water. He gives protection and power, and says the herb must be gathered correctly: bathe in a running stream, offer bread and wine to the plant, then uproot it with the little finger or a silver blade. Kynes groups common club moss with fir club moss (Huperzia selago), so her lore may not belong to one plant alone. She cites Pliny on it as an amulet and hangs bundles tied with green yarn for prosperity.
