# Prickly Ash

Common prickly ash, also called northern prickly ash or toothache tree, is an aromatic shrub or small tree of the citrus family, native to central and eastern parts of the United States and Canada. Wikipedia says it is the northernmost New World member of its family and the type species of a genus that includes Sichuan pepper, and that oil extracts from its bark have been used in traditional medicine. Cunningham calls it masculine, with Mars and fire, and gives one power, love: the fruits are used as a perfume to attract love. His heading names the common and southern prickly ash together, and the American Herbal Products Association's heading covers the whole genus, so both citations are left for the curator. Kynes gives it no entry. The American Herbal Products Association records hazards for the bark, set out in the safety notes.
