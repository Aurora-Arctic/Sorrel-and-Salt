# Witch Hazel

Witch hazel is a shrub or small tree native to eastern North America, whose pale yellow flowers open in autumn. Wikipedia says its forked twigs are favoured as divining rods, and that Native Americans used the bark and leaves for external inflammations and boiled the stems into a decoction. Grieve names the bark and leaves as the parts used, and a distilled extract for external use as an astringent. Kynes gives it earth, fire and water, Libra, Saturn and the Sun, and a long list of powers including protection, healing, divination, release and peace. She has witch hazel water sprinkled to clear energy and a twig kept on the altar for focus. Cunningham adds that the bark and twigs protect against evil and that a carried piece is said to mend a broken heart. The American Herbal Products Association records hazards, set out in the safety notes. The questions note a misspelling in Cunningham's heading.
