# Delal a Kar

Phaleria nisidai is a species of Phaleria, a genus of about 25 flowering plants in the family Thymelaeaceae, which range from Sri Lanka to Malesia, Papuasia, northern and eastern Australia and the islands of the western Pacific. In Palau it is known locally as delal a kar, which means the mother of medicines, and Palauans use it as a panacea, a remedy for everything. The page on the genus says no more of this species, so nothing further is recorded here about its parts, preparation or traditions. The genus authority is the botanist William Jack, and another member, Phaleria macrocarpa, is known to produce agarwood.
