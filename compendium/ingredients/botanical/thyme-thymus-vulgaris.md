# Thyme

Thyme is a woody, evergreen subshrub of the mint family, Lamiaceae, native to the western Mediterranean and the main source of the culinary herb. Wikipedia lists common thyme, German thyme and garden thyme as its names. Cunningham calls it feminine, with Venus and water, and lists health, healing, sleep, psychic powers, love, purification and courage. He has it burned for health, slipped under the pillow for restful sleep, worn to develop psychic powers, worn in a woman's hair to make her irresistible, and smelled for courage and energy. Kynes adds the signs Aries, Capricorn, Libra and Taurus and the element air, but her heading covers both common and wild thyme, so it is marked unresolved. Grieve gives the botanical name and the American Herbal Products Association records cautions on pregnancy and allergy, set out in the safety notes.
