# Carob

The carob is an evergreen tree or shrub of the legume family, native to the Mediterranean region and the Middle East, and widely grown for its edible seed pods. Wikipedia says carob contains no theobromine or caffeine, which is why it is sometimes used for chocolate-like treats for dogs. Cunningham's entry lists protection and health, and says to carry or wear the fruit to maintain good health and guard against evil. His folk names are Caaroba, Caroba, Carobinha and Chocolate. His heading names three plants, Jacaranda procera, Prosopis dulcis and Ceratonia siliqua, so the file asks a curator whether his entry describes the carob alone. He is the only source for the magical use.
