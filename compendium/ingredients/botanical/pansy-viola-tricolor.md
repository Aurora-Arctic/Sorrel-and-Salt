# Pansy

The wild pansy, also called heartsease, is a common European wild flower that grows as an annual or short-lived perennial. Wikipedia says it is the parent of the cultivated garden pansy, and that before the garden forms were bred, pansy was another name for this wild plant. Its flowers can be purple, blue, yellow or white. Cunningham calls it feminine, with Saturn and water, and gives the powers of love, rain magic and love divination. He says to plant pansies in a heart shape and watch whether they prosper, as a sign for love, and that picking them with the dew still on them brings rain. His many other names include Heart's Ease, Love-in-Idleness, Johnny Jump-Ups and Kiss-Me-At-The-Garden-Gate. Kynes puts the wild pansy and the garden pansy under one heading, and her citation is left for the curator.
