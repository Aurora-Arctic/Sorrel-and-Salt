# Buttercup

The meadow buttercup, also called tall or common buttercup, is a herbaceous perennial found across Europe and temperate Eurasia, with glossy yellow flowers about 25 mm across, five overlapping petals and finely cut, hairy leaves. Kynes covers it with creeping buttercup under one heading. She gives water, Aries and Mars, faeries, and a long list of powers including abundance, divination, love, truth and wisdom. She says that in the English Midlands the smell of the meadow buttercup was believed to cause madness, and that on Midsummer's Eve garlands of buttercups were put on cows to bless the milk. The flowers were placed on doorsteps and windowsills on May Eve to guard against faery mischief. To deepen spiritual commitment she has a bowl of the flowers placed on the altar during meditation. Her heading pools two species, so her citation is an open question for a curator.
