# Oriental Water Plantain

Alisma orientale, commonly called Asian water plantain, is a flowering plant of the genus Alisma, found in Asia. The page notes that it is sometimes treated as a variety of Alisma plantago-aquatica. Its rhizomes are used in traditional Chinese medicine, where the herb is called ze xie, and the rhizome is also a herb in the Japanese medicine kampo. The page warns that the herb may have serious side effects or even toxic effects such as hepatotoxicity. The seed contains cis-aconitic anhydride ethyl ester and cis-2,4,5-trihydroxycinnamic acid. The page gives no description of the plant itself or its habitat beyond its occurring in Asia, and names the rhizome as the only part used in medicine.
