# Pea

The pea is a pulse or fodder crop, and the word often means the seed or the pod of the plant. Wikipedia says it is native to the Mediterranean region and south-west Asia, and that some sources still treat it as Pisum sativum. Cunningham files it under Pisum sativum, calls it feminine, with Venus and earth, and gives the powers of money and love. He says shelling peas brings fortune and business profits, and that a woman who finds a pod with exactly nine peas should hang it over the door, so that the first eligible man to walk under it becomes her husband. Kynes heads the garden pea, the snow pea and the sugar snap pea together, and her citation is left for the curator. She adds Thor and dwarfs as associations.
