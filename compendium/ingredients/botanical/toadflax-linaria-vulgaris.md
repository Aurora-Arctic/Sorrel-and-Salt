# Toadflax

Toadflax is a perennial of the plantain family, Plantaginaceae, native to Europe and northern Asia, with pale yellow snapdragon-like flowers. Wikipedia says it may be mildly toxic to livestock. Kynes names it yellow toadflax, giving the elements earth and fire, the planets Mars and Pluto, and the powers defence, hexes and protection. She has three seeds tied in white linen with black thread as an amulet, and dried leaves burned to break a hex, and says it is mildly toxic if ingested. Cunningham calls it masculine, with Mars and fire, lists protection and hex-breaking, and has it carried as an amulet against evil. His heading misspells the name as Linatia vulgaris, so it is marked unresolved. Grieve prints Linaria vulgaris and lists many folk names, and says its acrid oil is reputed to be poisonous though no harm from it has ever been recorded.
