# Carnation

The carnation, or clove pink, is a Dianthus species native to the Mediterranean region and long cultivated for its fringed, spicy-scented flowers, according to Wikipedia. Cunningham calls it masculine, with the Sun and fire, and lists protection, strength and healing. Worn in Elizabethan times to prevent an untimely death on the scaffold, it serves in protective spells, and red blossoms go on the altar in healing rituals and into sachets and incenses. Kynes adds Jupiter and Saturn, Aries, Capricorn and Sagittarius, and lists confidence, fertility, healing, love, nightmares, peace, truth and more. A few seeds carried help confidence when job hunting, a carnation worn stimulates love interest, and a white carnation held in meditation helps in seeking truth. Both authors print the epithet as carophyllus, and Kynes groups other pinks under the same heading.
