# Ti

Ti is a palm-like evergreen of the family Asparagaceae, with fans of red to green leaves, carried across the Pacific by the Austronesians. Wikipedia says it is of great cultural importance in the Pacific Islands, is planted on graves and used in magical and healing rituals, and that in the Philippines it is thought able to host spirits. Kynes gives it the element fire, the planet Jupiter and the powers healing, luck, protection and purification, with Laka, Lono and Pele as deities. She has the spiky end of a leafy branch used as a fan to purify a ritual space, and three leaves braided into a good luck charm. Cunningham calls it masculine, with Jupiter and fire, and has leaves kept on ships to turn storms away and planted round a house as a barrier. His heading misspells the name, so it is marked unresolved.
