# Oats

Oats are the seed of the common oat, a tall stout grass in the grass family grown for fodder and for its grain. Wikipedia says oats appear to have been domesticated as a secondary crop, because the seeds resembled those of other cereals closely enough to be gathered with them, and that they are eaten as oatmeal, including steel-cut and rolled oats. Cunningham gives oats the planet Venus and the element earth, calls them feminine, and offers one use, in prosperity and money spells. His other names are Groats, Oatmeal and Joulaf. Kynes does not give oats an entry of their own. She covers barley, maize, oat, rice, rye and wheat together under Grain, where she records that in Europe and the British Isles grains of barley, wheat, rye and oat were used for a wide range of predictions, including love divination. That entry is cited only for that lore, and it is left to the curator.
