# Cherry

Cherry here is the sweet cherry, a tree of the rose family native to western Eurasia, which Wikipedia also calls wild cherry or gean. Cunningham calls it feminine, with Venus and water, and gives love and divination. He ties a strand of hair to a blossoming cherry tree to find love, and threads cherry stones on a red or pink thread for a husband or wife. Kynes groups the sweet cherry with the black cherry and the European bird cherry, so her powers may not belong to one tree alone. She adds fire, Mercury, Artemis, Flora, Pan and Persephone, and lists abundance, creativity, divination, fertility, love, peace, wisdom and transformation. She uses two cherrystones as a charm for romance. Wikipedia says all parts but the ripe fruit are slightly toxic, from cyanogenic glycosides.
