# Pepper-Bark Tree

Warburgia salutaris, the pepper-bark tree, is a tree of the family Canellaceae. Its other names are peperbasboom in Afrikaans, molaka in Sotho, mulanga in Venda and isibaha in Zulu. It is native to southwestern Africa, including Mozambique, Zimbabwe, and KwaZulu-Natal and the northern provinces of South Africa. It is an erect tree up to about ten metres tall, sometimes reaching 20, with a thick canopy of aromatic, shiny green leaves and a leathery purple or black berry. The leaves add a peppery flavour to food and tea, and the bitter taste of the bark and leaves is due to iridoids. Wikipedia says the Maasai use it to treat malaria and it is used as a snuff or smoked for respiratory complaints. It is a popular medicinal plant that is overharvested in the wild, and it is a protected tree in South Africa.
