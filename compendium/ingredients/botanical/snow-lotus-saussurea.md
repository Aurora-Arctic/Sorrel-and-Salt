# Snow Lotus

Saussurea is a genus of about 300 species of flowering plants in the daisy family, native to cool temperate and arctic regions of East Asia, Europe and North America, with the greatest variety in alpine habitats in the Himalayas and East Asia. Common names include saw-wort and snow lotus, the second used for a number of high-altitude species in East Asia. They are perennial herbs from dwarf alpine species 5 to 10 centimetres tall to thistle-like plants up to 3 metres, with leaves in a basal rosette and dense heads of white to purple florets, often wrapped in woolly hairs that protect against frost and ultraviolet light. Several kinds of snow lotus are used in traditional Tibetan medicine. The page says Saussurea obvallata is among the most sacred species in India and is used in offerings to the goddess Nanda Devi.
