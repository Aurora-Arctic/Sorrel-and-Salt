# Ajuga orientalis

Ajuga orientalis, called oriental bugle or eastern bugle, is a herbaceous flowering plant native to the eastern Mediterranean. It grows in sandy, dry brushwood and lightly wooded country near the coast. It is usually evergreen, though it may lose its leaves briefly in a cold winter. The grey-green leaves are oblong and oval with scalloped edges. Flower stems appear in spring and summer, each carrying 4 to 12 tube-shaped flowers that are cream above and violet below. The page says that this species, like most Ajuga, is claimed to have medicinal effects and is used in traditional Turkish medicine, and it names no preparation or ailment.
