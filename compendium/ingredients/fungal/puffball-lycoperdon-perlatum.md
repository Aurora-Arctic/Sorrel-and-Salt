# Puffball

The common puffball, also called the warted or gem-studded puffball, is a mushroom of the Agaricaceae family with a round fruit body that tapers to a wide stalk and is covered in small spiny bumps. Wikipedia says it releases its spores in a burst when mature, and that it grows in fields, gardens and grassy clearings. Kynes gives it no entry of its own: it is named, with devil's snuffbox, pixy puff and wolf farts as folk names, under one mushroom heading that also holds the death cap and the fairy ring mushroom. She says puffballs were believed to grow where witches had gathered. Because that heading covers three mushrooms, her elements, sign, planets and powers are left for the curator to settle. Cunningham has no puffball entry. Kynes and Wikipedia both record hazards, set out in the safety notes.
