# Lungwort

Lungwort, here the lichen Lobaria pulmonaria, is a large lichen that grows on trees and is made of a fungus living with a green alga and a cyanobacterium. Wikipedia lists tree lungwort, lung lichen, lung moss, lungwort lichen, oak lungs and oak lungwort among its common names. Its leaf-like body is leathery and lobed, bright green when wet and brownish and papery when dry. Wikipedia says it has a history of use in herbal medicines, that research has corroborated some medicinal properties of lichen extracts, and that it is sensitive to air pollution. It is a different organism from the flowering borage-family plant also called lungwort.

#### Questions for the Curator

Her heading names the lichen, her body text describes another plant. I did not cite Grieve on this entry.
