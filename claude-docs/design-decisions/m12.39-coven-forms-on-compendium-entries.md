# M12.39 — A coven's own forms under a Compendium entry

**Status:** decided by the owner on 2026-10-20 (option A, with a per-form details table; see "Decided") · **Date:** 2026-10-20

The owner decided on 2026-10-20 that a coven may have forms of its own under a
Compendium entry, and that only that coven sees them. DESIGN.md §5 says the
opposite today: a variant "carries no workspace_id: it takes its ingredient's
tier" (`claude-docs/DESIGN.md`, `ingredient_variants`). This record sets out
where such a form could live, what each place costs against the code as it is,
and what the owner picked. The options and their reasons stay, so a later reader
can see what was turned down. Nothing here is built; the tasks are M12.39 to
M12.41, then M12.38.

## Why

M8.17's Link dialog turns a coven's own ingredient into one or more Compendium
entries (`splitLocalIntoCompendiumEntries`,
`src/modules/ingredients/services/cupboard-entries.ts`;
`claude-docs/db/workspace-compendium-entries.md`, "Matching forms"). A form of
the own entry that no pick has is today either dropped (when it holds nothing)
or blocks the link until the member assigns it (when it holds a jar or a spell
layer). The owner wants such a form to carry over as the coven's own form on
the pick, so a link loses nothing. M12.38 will also carry the member's ticked
details of the ingredient and of each matched form.

The cupboard row (`workspace_compendium_entries`) cannot hold a form. It is a
coven's presence plus seven null-means-Compendium overrides, none of them a
form, folk name, substitute, category or reference
(`src/modules/ingredients/validation/cupboard-merge.ts`,
`src/modules/ingredients/schema/workspace-compendium-entries.ts`).

## What the code does with a form today

Every reader of `ingredient_variants` decides who may see a form by looking at
its **parent ingredient**, never at the form:

- `readableVariantParent` and `inUseVariantParent`
  (`src/db/repository/ingredient-parent.ts`) are parent-only checks. The
  second is M12.24's `heldBy` hatch for a retired entry.
- `findReadableVariant`, `findManyOfIngredients` and `findManyOfVariants`
  (`src/db/repository/ingredients.ts`) take the `Unscoped` marker, meaning the
  table has no `workspaceId` column (`src/db/repository/types.ts`).
- `variantsOf` (`src/modules/ingredients/services/ingredient-children.ts`) and
  the loaders (`src/modules/ingredients/loaders/ingredient-children.ts`) key on
  the ingredient's tier alone. They hold no "which coven is reading" for a
  Compendium entry. M12.24's record lists the same gap under "Not yet
  covered".

How many places: `ingredientVariants` appears 113 times in 22 files; 68 times
in 12 files outside `src/db/seed/` and `schema/` (grep, 2026-10-20).
`src/db/repository/ingredients.ts` alone holds 31: the stock checks, the
same-form match, the search arms, the citation arm, the elements, form and
toxic filters. Others: `ingredient-facets.ts` (form facet), `references.ts`,
`stock.ts`, `vocabularies.ts` (the "form in use" test, `IN_USE`, and
`findCompendiumFormRenameClashes`), `write.ts`, `types.ts`, and in
`src/modules/ingredients/services/`: `ingredient-rows.ts` (`writeVariant`,
`replaceVariants`, `addVariants`), `ingredient-children.ts`,
`ingredient-substitutes.ts`, `cupboard-entries.ts`. Callers of `variantsOf`:
`src/app/compendium-reads.ts`, `src/app/entry-values.ts`,
`src/app/(shell)/coven/[slug]/ingredients/reads.ts`.

What hangs off a form, all by `variant_id`
(`src/db/migrations/0063_variant-children.sql` and the schema files):

| Child                                   | How it names the form                                                                      |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `inventory_items` (a jar)               | composite foreign key `(variant_id, ingredient_id)` to `ingredient_variants`               |
| `spell_ingredients` (a spell layer)     | the same composite key, with a CHECK that ingredient and variant are both null or both set |
| `ingredient_categories`                 | the same composite key                                                                     |
| `ingredient_substitutes`                | two composite keys: the form, and the substitute's form                                    |
| `reference_links.ingredient_variant_id` | a plain foreign key                                                                        |

The composite key target is the non-partial unique `(id, ingredient_id)` on
`ingredient_variants`, which is the schema's named exception to rule 4.

Two more facts matter. The form's uniqueness is
`ingredient_variants_form_unique`: live rows only, on
`(ingredient_id, lower(btrim(form)))`. And the public Compendium entry page is
held in the data cache whole, children included (`cachedEntry` in
`src/modules/ingredients/services/compendium.ts`, key `compendium-entry`,
tag `compendium`, `src/lib/compendium-cache.ts`). Rule 6 says that cache may
never hold a coven's data. `llms.txt` reads only slugs and names
(`src/app/llms.txt/route.ts`, `compendium-slugs.ts`), so it is not touched by a
form either way.

## Options

### A. A nullable `workspace_id` on `ingredient_variants`

A form with `workspace_id` null is the Compendium's. A form with it set belongs
to that coven alone: under a Compendium entry it is the coven's addition, and
under the coven's own entry it is one of that entry's forms (point 4 below).

| Concern                                                      | Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Schema                                                       | One column, one migration, plus the index change below. Forward-only. The column is additive, but the index change is a rebuild that `npm run check:destructive-ddl` flags, so it needs an ack sidecar (point 7 below).                                                                                                                          |
| Children (jars, layers, categories, substitutes, references) | **No change.** They name the form by `variant_id`, and a coven form is a normal row. Jars and layers on a coven form work with the existing keys.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Readers                                                      | All 68 non-seed references that read forms must add "the form's `workspace_id` is null, or is the reader's coven". Today they only look at the parent. This is the large cost, and the dangerous one: **a reader that forgets the predicate leaks a coven's form** to the Compendium or to another coven.                                        |
| Types                                                        | A nullable `workspaceId` column makes the table `TwoTier`, so the generic writers refuse it and every variant write stops compiling (point 3 below), and `findManyOfIngredients` and `findManyOfVariants` stop compiling until they take the reader. That is a forcing function for those, not for the direct `selectFrom` and `existsIn` uses.  |
| Partial unique index                                         | Split in two: `(ingredient_id, lower(btrim(form)))` where `workspace_id is null`, and `(ingredient_id, workspace_id, lower(btrim(form)))` where it is not. One index across all tiers would make a curator's insert fail on a coven's private form, which tells the curator that form exists (M6.6).                                             |
| `findReadableVariant`                                        | Gains the viewer's coven in its predicate. Its parent check stays.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Compendium cache (rule 6)                                    | `cachedEntry` reads children with no viewer, so every form read it makes must filter `workspace_id is null`. A missed one writes a coven's form into the shared cache. The cache would otherwise need no change.                                                                                                                                 |
| Public pages, `llms.txt`                                     | Same as the cache: public reads add the null filter. `llms.txt` is unaffected.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Curator and admin (M6.6)                                     | `carryFormRename` (`src/db/repository/write.ts`) renames every variant with that `formId` under a Compendium entry, so it would rename a coven's form. It needs `workspace_id is null`. The "form in use" test in `vocabularies.ts` counts any live variant, so it would show a coven's form to a curator as "in use". It needs the same filter. |
| Soft delete (rule 4)                                         | Unchanged: a coven form is soft-deleted like any form, and the finders already filter it. Deleting a workspace soft-deletes only the workspace row (`src/modules/coven/services/workspace-deletion.ts`), so its forms need no work.                                                                                                              |
| Membership proof (rule 5)                                    | A coven-form writer takes a `Membership` and writes its `workspace_id`; a reader takes the proof and ANDs it. The shape matches `insertInWorkspace`. The retired-entry hatch (`heldBy`) keeps working: the parent filter is unchanged.                                                                                                           |
| Enforcing "null under a coven's own entry"                   | **A CHECK cannot do it**: a CHECK sees one row and cannot see the parent's tier. See "Where the owner's wording meets the code".                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |

### B. A separate table of coven forms

A table such as `workspace_ingredient_forms (workspace_id, ingredient_id, form, ...)`,
leaving `ingredient_variants` as it is.

| Concern                 | Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Leak by omission        | **Impossible for the 68 existing readers, the cache and the public pages**: they never read the new table. A forgotten reader loses a feature; it leaks nothing. This is the option's one real advantage.                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Children                | **Large cost.** Jars, layers, categories, substitutes (twice) and references all name a form through a composite key into `ingredient_variants`. Each would gain a second nullable column for a coven form, a "exactly one of the two" CHECK, a second foreign key and second unique indexes. That is five tables, one of them with two keys, and every reader of those children. |
| Unique index            | The new table gets its own; the old one is untouched.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Everything a form shows | The form page, the facets, the search arms, the form-in-use test and the Link dialog's form map would each read both tables and merge. Two row types now stand for "a form" in code that treats it as one.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Curator and cache       | Safe by construction.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

### Per-form details for a matched form (M12.38)

A matched form is a Compendium form the coven keeps using, with the coven's own
wording over it. A form has the same six annotatable columns as the cupboard row
less the label (`description`, `safety_notes`, `elements`, `celestial_bodies`,
`zodiac_signs`, `colors`; `src/modules/ingredients/schema/ingredient-variants.ts`).

- **P1. A table beside the cupboard row**, one row per (coven, Compendium
  form), with the six overrides, null meaning the Compendium's value. It has the
  same merge rule as `applyCupboardEntry` (`entry?.x ?? row.x`). A foreign key
  to the form and to the workspace; a partial unique index on the live pair.
  Reads of a Compendium form for a coven merge it. The cache is unaffected,
  since the merge happens per viewer after the cached read.
- **P2. The cupboard row grows a JSON column keyed by form id.** One fewer
  table, but no foreign key, so a retired or deleted form leaves an orphan key
  nothing checks. The repository avoids this elsewhere.

P1 is the same mechanism as the cupboard row. It works the same under option A
or B.

## Where the owner's wording meets the code

1. **The CHECK cannot be written.** "A CHECK that `workspace_id` is null under a
   coven's own ingredient" asks one row to know its parent's tier. The cupboard
   table hit the same limit and enforces its tier rule in the service, the read
   filter and a db test (`claude-docs/db/workspace-compendium-entries.md`);
   the repo uses no trigger for a tier rule. A composite foreign key on
   `(ingredient_id, workspace_id)` to `ingredients` cannot express it either,
   because a Compendium parent has a null `workspace_id` and a coven form has a
   set one, so they never match. The rule is the service's, plus a db test that
   inserts the wrong row through the raw client and proves the readers hide it.
2. **DESIGN.md §5 and the union rule.** "A variant adds to its ingredient and
   never removes from it" (CLAUDE.md, MB.215) still holds: a coven form adds to
   what the coven sees. The sentence "carries no workspace_id: it takes its
   ingredient's tier" is what changes.
3. **Adding the column changes the repository's types.** The repository marks
   each table by whether it has a `workspaceId` column: none is `Unscoped`, a
   not-null one is `WorkspaceScoped`, a nullable one is `TwoTier`
   (`src/db/repository/types.ts`). The generic writers (`insert`, `update`,
   `updateById`, `softDelete`, `softDeleteByIds`) take only `Unscoped` tables.
   `ingredient_variants` is `Unscoped` today, so the moment it gains a nullable
   `workspace_id` every variant write stops compiling: `writeVariant`,
   `replaceVariants` and `addVariants` (`ingredient-rows.ts`) and
   `carryFormRename` (`src/db/repository/write.ts`). The compiler is doing its
   job here, and it lands in M12.39, which moves them to tier-aware writers with
   no change in behaviour. The two tier writers also differ in who they trust:
   `insertInCompendium` takes a `Curator`, `insertInWorkspace` a `Membership`.
   A coven's own entry's forms are written by a member, and they are neither.
   That is why M12.39 proposes the uniform reading below.
4. **A uniform reading, decided.** The owner's first words were that
   `workspace_id` is "null under a coven's own entry". The type rules and one
   index make it simpler to say instead: **null means the Compendium's form,
   set means that coven's form, wherever the form sits.** A coven's own entry's
   forms then carry their entry's coven (a backfill in M12.39's migration), and
   every reader asks one question, "is this form's audience the reader's coven,
   or everyone?", rather than also asking the parent's tier. The cost is a
   backfill of the own-entry forms and a column that repeats the parent's
   `workspace_id` on those rows. If the owner keeps null for own entries, the
   readers ask both questions and the variant writers need a third shape. The
   owner chose the uniform reading on 2026-10-20 (see "Decided").
5. **A coven form needs mutations of its own.** DESIGN.md writes variants
   inside the ingredient's save (MB.219). A Compendium entry's save belongs to a
   curator, who must not read, rename or delete a coven's form (M6.6). So a coven
   form is added, changed and deleted by mutations that take a `Membership`
   (M12.40), and a curator's save neither matches a coven form by its folded
   text nor soft-deletes one it was not sent. The reasons DESIGN.md gave against
   separate variant mutations (a second tier check, a second collision mapper, a
   cache expiry per variant) do not apply: the check is the `Membership`, and
   a coven form is never in the data cache.
6. **A coven form is a form row only.** A coven can give it the form's own
   columns, jars and spell layers, and cannot give it categories, substitutes or
   references. Those would need the curator's references and a tier check at
   each child table. This weakens the owner's "linking loses nothing" for a form
   that carried one of those. It is the smaller start, and the owner accepted it
   for now on 2026-10-20; a later task can add them (see "Follow-ups").
7. **Rebuilding the unique index is flagged as destructive.** Replacing
   `ingredient_variants_form_unique` with a narrower index is a drop and a
   create. `npm run check:destructive-ddl` flags it, so the migration needs a
   `<tag>.ack.md` sidecar (rule 10). Rule 10 also says a column or index drop is
   two PRs. The owner ruled on 2026-10-20 that this is not what that sentence is
   for: it covers removing something in use, not rebuilding it. The rebuild is
   one migration in one PR (see "Decided").

## Decided

**Decided by the owner, 2026-10-20.** Points 1 to 3 answer the three questions this record posed; points 4 to 6 answer the three it flagged:

1. **Model: option A**, a nullable `workspace_id` on `ingredient_variants`,
   with **per-form details in a table beside the cupboard row (P1)**.
2. **The same form name on both sides: keep both, with a merge.** If a
   curator adds a form a coven already has privately, that coven sees both. A
   coven member (an owner or a member, never a viewer) can merge their coven
   form into the shared one. The jars, spell layers and the form's own details
   move onto the shared form, and the coven form goes. The two unique indexes
   split below allow both to exist.
3. **An unmatched empty form during Link (M12.38):** the person linking decides
   in the form map, either to drop it or to carry it over as a coven form on a
   pick they name.
4. **Every form names its tier.** Null means the Compendium's form, and set
   means that coven's form, wherever the form sits. M12.39 backfills a coven's
   own entry's forms with their coven.
5. **A coven form carries no categories, substitutes or references, for now.**
   The coven form is a form row, with jars and spell layers. This is a
   follow-up (see "Follow-ups"), and no task is minted for it.
6. **The index rebuild is one PR.** M12.39 drops and rebuilds
   `ingredient_variants_form_unique` in one migration, with a `<tag>.ack.md`
   sidecar. Rule 10's two-PR rule is for removing something in use, not for
   rebuilding it.

Option B stays recorded above with its reasons. The mechanical guard in M12.40
is what makes A's one risk, a reader that forgets the predicate, impossible to
land unseen.

**Why the merge is its own task, M12.41.** M12.40 is complete without it: the
coven works with both forms, nothing is blocked, and a jar or spell on a coven
form works as on any other. The merge is a dialog with its own rules: which
details may move, what it refuses, and the shared move helper it lifts out of
the split service. Putting it in M12.40 would make a 12-hour task about 17 (both are
estimates).

## Follow-ups

**Categories, substitutes and references on a coven form.** A coven form carries
only the form's own columns, jars and spell layers (M12.40). A coven cannot give
it categories, substitutes or references, and the service refuses them. The owner
accepted this for now on 2026-10-20 and wants it kept as a follow-up. No task is
minted. Two places feel it:

- M12.40 names it as out of scope.
- M12.38 (Link) states the loss: a form of the coven's own entry that carried
  categories, substitutes or references reaches a pick as a coven form without
  them, and the Link dialog says so before the member confirms.

A later task would need a tier check at each child table, and the references a
coven may cite, which the Compendium keeps for curators.

### DESIGN.md §5, the index block

Replace the `ingredient_variants_form_unique` statement with:

```sql
CHECK (btrim(form) <> '');

-- A compendium form is unique per entry and folded form; a coven's, per
-- entry, coven and folded form. A compendium form and a coven's may share a
-- name, and so may two covens': one index across all of them would make a
-- curator's insert fail on a coven's private form and so tell the curator it
-- exists (M6.6).
CREATE UNIQUE INDEX ingredient_variants_form_unique
  ON ingredient_variants (ingredient_id, lower(btrim(form)))
  WHERE workspace_id IS NULL AND deleted_at IS NULL;

CREATE UNIQUE INDEX ingredient_variants_workspace_form_unique
  ON ingredient_variants (ingredient_id, workspace_id, lower(btrim(form)))
  WHERE workspace_id IS NOT NULL AND deleted_at IS NULL;

CREATE INDEX ON ingredient_variants (workspace_id)
  WHERE workspace_id IS NOT NULL AND deleted_at IS NULL;
```

The `(id, ingredient_id)` index, the `form_id` index and the trigram index stay
as they are. In the column list, add `workspaceId` (nullable) after `ingredientId`.

A compendium form and a coven's may share a folded name, and so may two covens': the coven sees both, and merges its own into the shared one (M12.41). Two ingredients may each have an Oil.
