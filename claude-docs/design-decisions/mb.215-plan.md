### Why those children (the research)

- **References and safety notes are per-form in the sources.** The EMA writes one herbal monograph per plant part and preparation; an essential oil is roughly a hundred times the strength of the dried herb and carries its own contraindications and routes.
- **Substitutes are per-form but not form-bound.** Cunningham's substitution lists are organised by use and property, and a formula swaps within its kind by convenience, not rule; the owner's examples (leaves for stems, a different ingredient) are ordinary. So the substitute hangs off the form it replaces and links anything; similarity of form is a ranking, not a constraint.
- **Categories are mostly plant-level, sometimes form-level.** Correspondence references attribute "powers" to the plant with parts and preparations beneath; "for anointing" is a real but occasional per-form use, and the join table is cheap to widen.
- **Deities are attributed to the plant.** Rose is Aphrodite's; the oil is the _use_. The deities table is ordered and linked, the costliest to split, for the weakest case. Folk names name the plant.

Sources: [EMA herbal monograph template](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/template-form/template-european-union-herbal-monograph_en.pdf-0), [ACHS: cautions for herbs vs oils](https://achs.edu/blog/cautions-contraindications-for-herbs-oils/), [Cunningham's Encyclopedia of Magical Herbs](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/145560), [Complete Book of Incense, Oils and Brews](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/13618404), [Fragrance in Ancient Greece](https://www.scento.com/blog/fragrance-ancient-greece-mythical-origins).

### Rejected (recorded by MB.215)

- **Self-referential `parent_id` in one table.** Cheapest migration, but two kinds of row in one table: a form row listed as an ingredient is only _absent_, never _impossible_, and `canonical_name` is duplicated and must be kept in sync.
- **Prefill on create.** Copies, does not share.
- **Override semantics.** Every read coalesces, join tables need an "explicitly none" flag, changing one element means re-entering the list.
- **Separate variant mutations.** The form saves the whole entry in one `withAudit`; separate mutations mean a second tier check, a second collision mapper, per-variant cache expiry and multi-round-trip saves.

## The model (what the entries describe)

**`ingredient_variants`** (`src/modules/ingredients/schema/ingredient-variants.ts`): `id: idColumn()`, `ingredientId` NOT NULL → `ingredients.id`, `form` text NOT NULL, `formId` nullable → `ingredient_forms.id`, `description`, `safetyNotes`, `elements ingredient_element[]`, `planets[]`, `zodiacSigns[]`, `colors[]`, `...auditColumns`. CHECK form not blank. `liveUnique('ingredient_variants_form_unique', t, t.ingredientId, sql\`lower(btrim(form))\`)`. UNIQUE `(id, ingredient_id)`, **not partial**: the composite FK's target, which a partial index cannot be, reserving nothing the PK does not (the one named exception to "every unique index is partial", classified in `partial-unique-indexes.test.ts`). INDEX `(form_id) WHERE deleted_at IS NULL`. GIN trigram on `unaccent_immutable(form)`and`unaccent_immutable(description)`. Own `set_updated_at`trigger line; listed in`table-metadata.ts`. `IngredientScoped`, read through `findManyOfIngredients` under the parent's tier (rule 5).

**`ingredients.identity_key`**: `GENERATED ALWAYS AS (lower(coalesce(canonical_name, name))) STORED`, `identityKeyOf(name, canonicalName)` beside `canonicalKeyOf`; unique per tier as `ingredients_compendium_identity_key_unique` / `ingredients_workspace_identity_key_unique`. `canonical_key` stays until MB.223.

**Composite FK shape** `(variant_id, ingredient_id) → ingredient_variants (id, ingredient_id)` (Drizzle `foreignKey`, MATCH SIMPLE) on `inventory_items`, `spell_ingredients`, `ingredient_categories`, `ingredient_substitutes`. MATCH SIMPLE skips when _either_ column is null, so a nullable `ingredient_id` beside it takes a CHECK. `foreignKeyFacts` in `table-metadata.ts` widened to multi-column keys (the drift test reads it).

- `inventory_items.variant_id` **NOT NULL** (the table is empty everywhere); `liveUnique` on `(workspace_id, variant_id)` replaces the ingredient pair.
- `spell_ingredients.variant_id` with CHECK `(ingredient_id IS NULL) = (variant_id IS NULL)`; `liveUnique` on `(spell_id, variant_id)` replaces the ingredient pair, so a jar may lay Rosemary herb and Rosemary oil; `form_only_on_custom` stays.
- `ingredient_categories`: `variant_id` nullable; pair PK → UNIQUE `(ingredient_id, category_id) WHERE variant_id IS NULL` and `(variant_id, category_id) WHERE variant_id IS NOT NULL`; still hard-deleted.
- `ingredient_substitutes`: `variant_id` (the form it is a substitute _for_) and `substitute_variant_id` (composite FK against `substitute_id`); CHECK `substitute_variant_id IS NULL OR substitute_id IS NOT NULL`; `not_itself` → refuse only `substitute_id = ingredient_id AND substitute_variant_id IS NOT DISTINCT FROM variant_id`; link/name uniqueness split by `variant_id IS NULL`; plain partial index on `variant_id`.
- `reference_links`: one `SOURCED` entry (above).

**Union rule as code.** `Ingredient.categories/substitutes/references` are the ingredient's own (`variant_id IS NULL`); `IngredientVariant.*` are that form's additions; nothing coalesces. The compendium category filter unions for free (a variant pair row also carries `ingredient_id`, so today's `categoryArms` already matches). `citesNothing()` counts an entry sourced if it or a live variant cites. A spell's derived categories = the ingredient's pairs plus the layer's variant's.
