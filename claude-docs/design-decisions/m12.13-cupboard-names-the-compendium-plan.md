# Plan: a coven's cupboard names the compendium's entry, rather than hiding it

**Status:** approved by the owner 2026-10-18 · **Raised during:** M12.12 · the plan behind M12.13–M12.28

## Context

A coven's cupboard holds jars; the compendium is the book that says what exists. Today a coven's own ingredient is a full row in the two-tier `ingredients` table, and a rule called "local beats compendium" (DESIGN.md §5) hides a compendium entry from the coven's lists and pickers when a coven row matches it — on the formal name, or on the label alone when the coven row has no formal name. The guess then drives actions: M12.8 moves the coven's jars onto the local on save, and `createStock` files a new jar of a hidden entry under the local.

Building M12.12 ("In the Compendium" on the coven entry page) showed what that costs in use:

- A quick stub "Mugwort" hides the compendium's Mugwort, with its correspondences, safety note and sources, from the coven's pickers; spells then derive worse categories and warnings.
- An unidentified "Cat's Claw" hides five different compendium Cat's Claws, and stocking one of them deliberately is redirected onto the unidentified entry.
- A curator adding a second entry of a name changes some coven's hiding silently.

The owner's decisions (2026-10-18, in conversation):

1. **Two kinds of cupboard item.** _Ours_: a full coven-tier row, as now, for what the compendium lacks or the coven cannot identify. _The compendium's_: no content row; a small per-coven row keyed on (coven, compendium entry) that **is** the entry's presence in the cupboard, holding the label the coven uses and the overrides decision 9 lists where it has any, everything else read from the compendium row, jars pointing at the compendium's variants directly.
2. **Nothing is hidden on a guess.** The hiding rule, the jar-follow on save and the `createStock` redirect go. A second copy of a compendium entry cannot exist by construction (one row per coven and entry, no content copied); an own row under a compendium entry's name still can (decision 4), and the Add flow asks instead of guessing.
3. **The Ingredients page is the cupboard only**, with a hand-off to search the compendium when a search finds nothing. **Pickers that need both tiers show two labelled groups**, the coven's then the compendium's — never one interleaved list.
4. **An unlinked stub beside a same-named compendium entry in the cupboard shows twice**, with a nudge to link, rather than being collapsed.
5. **"In the Compendium" holds exactly what the entry is.** Name-only matches move to a separate "Similar in the Compendium" nudge, shown only for a coven row with no formal name, exact-name matches only, with a "This Is It" action per entry.
6. **A soft-deleted compendium entry is never copied.** Covens whose cupboard names it go on reading it through a named `…IncludingSoftDeleted` finder, marked "No Longer in the Compendium"; it leaves the public compendium, search, pickers and new jars at once. The curator's confirm shows how many covens use it (a count, never names). Promotion into a full coven row happens only by the coven's own action, one at a time.
7. **M12.12 ships prepared for this**, with small adjustments only, and its PR carries this plan and the minted tasks.
8. **The word for the coven's edits is "annotated".** A compendium entry in the cupboard whose row overrides a field is _annotated_; the action that edits those fields is "Annotate This Entry". (Owner, 2026-10-18: "You can cross out and rewrite in a book." "Edited" was weighed and dropped for colliding with the app's Edit buttons.)
9. **Row-level overrides, inherit when null.** The row may override the label, description, elements, celestial bodies, zodiac signs, colours and safety notes; a null column shows the compendium's value, a set one the coven's, and an empty array means "cleared on purpose". Forms, folk names, substitutes, categories, references and the `toxic` flag always read from the compendium; to change those the coven converts the entry to "ours". (Limiting to the row is a cost line, not a principle: overriding child tables rebuilds a full entry.)
10. **No data migration.** Existing coven rows that shadow a compendium entry stay "ours"; the entry page's nudge offers a one-click conversion (re-pointing jars and spell layers to the compendium's forms, soft-deleting the local). The demo seed is rewritten to seed the rows directly.
11. **Every compendium entry in a coven's cupboard has a row** (owner, 2026-10-18, confirming decision 1 as written after the design section had drifted to "edited only"). "In the cupboard" means the row exists and nothing else: the cupboard is read from own rows ∪ these rows alone, a jar never joins it directly, and a first jar on a compendium variant inserts the row. Edits are optional on it; it stays until the coven removes it; an entry can be in the cupboard before it has a jar; M9.7's two-valued Source chip stands. A spell layer may name any live compendium variant without putting the entry in the cupboard, as it can today.
12. **Within the cupboard, rank is the score alone** (owner, 2026-10-18). A compendium entry at 0.9 sits above an annotated one at 0.8 and the reverse; kind is what the Source chip filters by, never a boost. Two headed groups exist only in a picker spanning both tiers (decision 3).

Intended outcome: the heaviest query in the area (`notHiddenByLocal`'s anti-join) and M12.8's merge machinery are removed; a coven's view of the compendium is a stated relationship, not an inference; and "our version" has a home that v2's notes can extend.

## Design

### 1. The table: `workspace_compendium_entries`

The row is "this compendium entry is in our cupboard, under this label, with these overrides". `src/modules/ingredients/schema/workspace-compendium-entries.ts`, migration `0078_workspace_compendium_entries.sql`.

**One noun in code, one in the schema.** The schema names the relationship, `workspace_compendium_entries`, the way the schema says `workspaces` where the screens say coven. Everywhere above it the row is a **cupboard entry**: row type `CupboardEntryRow`, GraphQL type `CupboardEntry`, field `Ingredient.cupboardEntry`, finders `findCupboardEntry…`, services `addCupboardEntry` / `annotateCupboardEntry` / `removeCupboardEntry`, all in one file `services/cupboard-entries.ts`. The curator's `deleteCompendiumEntry` retains its name: it acts on the compendium row itself.

| Column                                                                                             | Meaning                                            |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| `id`, `workspace_id` NOT NULL → workspaces, `ingredient_id` NOT NULL → ingredients                 | the row and the pair                               |
| `label` text, `description` text, `safety_notes` text                                              | null = the compendium's                            |
| `elements`, `celestial_bodies`, `zodiac_signs`, `colors` arrays, typed as `ingredients` types them | null = the compendium's; `[]` = cleared on purpose |
| `...auditColumns`                                                                                  | `set_updated_at` trigger in the migration          |

A row is _annotated_ when any of the seven is non-null. Indexes: `liveUnique` on `(workspace_id, ingredient_id)`; `liveUnique` on `(workspace_id, lower(label)) WHERE label IS NOT NULL`; a plain index on `ingredient_id` for the curator's count; an `unaccent_immutable` trigram expression index on `label` for the search arm. Housekeeping: `AUDITED_TABLES` in `tests/support/db/table-metadata.ts`, `CupboardEntryRow` in `src/modules/ingredients/types.ts`, repository marks `WorkspaceScoped` + `IngredientScoped` (writes via `insertInWorkspace` / `updateByIdInWorkspace` / `softDeleteByIdInWorkspace` in `src/db/repository/write.ts`), a shared test inserter in `tests/support/db/` so the e2e and db helpers that insert jars on compendium variants insert the row too. No `.ack.md`: nothing is dropped.

**"The ingredient is compendium-tier" is a service rule plus a read filter**, not a CHECK (cannot see another row) or a trigger (the schema has three, none for cross-row tier rules; every tier rule today is a service check or `readableIngredientParent`). The create path reads the entry through a live compendium finder; every read of the table joins `ingredients` with `workspace_id IS NULL`.

**A set label is unique per coven across both kinds.** Row-vs-row by the index; row-vs-own-row (`ingredients_workspace_label_unique` covers own rows only) by a read-before-write in both directions, refusing as `refuseCollision` in `services/workspace-ingredients.ts` does, `ValidationError` on `label` (or `name`). Only labels the coven set take part: a cupboard entry with no label reads under the compendium's name, and that name reserves nothing, so an own "Mugwort" and the compendium's Mugwort can share a cupboard (decision 4). The cross-table race is accepted and documented, as `refuseEndingARedirect` in `services/compendium.ts` accepts its own.

### 2. The read model

- **One `Ingredient` type, resolved.** A separate type would fork every fragment, loader and screen. For a compendium entry in a cupboard: `id` and `workspaceId` are the compendium row's, `isGlobal` stays `true` (`graphql/ingredient.ts:187` stays truthful), and a new field `cupboardEntry(workspaceId: ID!): CupboardEntry` — the same per-coven shape as `Ingredient.stock(workspaceId)` — returns `{ id, label, description, …, annotated: Boolean, compendiumName }` or null. Screens tell the three kinds apart: _ours_ = `!isGlobal`; _the compendium's, in the cupboard_ = `cupboardEntry != null`, marked "From the Compendium", and "Annotated" when `annotated`; _the compendium's, not in the cupboard_ = neither. A new `Ingredient.retired: Boolean` is true when the compendium row is soft-deleted ("No Longer in the Compendium"). The field resolves through one per-request DataLoader keyed by ingredient id within the coven (`ingredient-children.ts` style `cacheKeyFn`), batched by `findCupboardEntriesOf`; a cupboard list row, which arrives with the row already joined (below), primes that loader, so the list makes no second query and every other reader — the entry page, a picker node, a spell layer — takes the same path.
- **The merge is a pure function** `applyCupboardEntry(row, entry)` in `services/cupboard-entries.ts`, unit-tested per column for null / set / `[]`. `ingredientDetailOf` needs no change for children: its refs are the compendium row's, and forms, folk names, substitutes, categories, references and `toxic` inherit (decision 9). References inherit, which leaves `reference_links`' `SOURCED` registry and its `num_nonnulls = 1` CHECK untouched.
- **The coven entry page** takes the compendium id in the URL (the page already branches on `entry.workspaceId === null`, `page.tsx:75`); the row's id never appears in an address. The page reads the row and renders the merged entry with the mark, "Annotate This Entry" (the override fields) and "Remove from Cupboard"; a compendium entry with no row renders as today with "Add to Cupboard"; the Local badge shows only for _ours_.
- **The cupboard** (`workspaceListScope`, `repository/ingredients.ts:314`) becomes own live rows ∪ compendium rows with a live `workspace_compendium_entries` row for this coven — one keyed `EXISTS` in place of today's `heldBy` through two tables — with no `notHiddenByLocal`; sort and cursor on the effective name `coalesce(label, name)`; facets count the effective arrays, categories from the compendium row. M9.7's `source` is unchanged: `local` = own rows, `compendium` = rows of the new table. A retired entry with a row stays listed and marked.
- **How the two kinds merge in the list and the search score** (owner's question, 2026-10-18). They are never two lists. Every cupboard row is an `ingredients` row read by the one `findWorkspaceIngredientPage` statement. The cupboard row is a `LEFT JOIN workspace_compendium_entries e ON e.ingredient_id = ingredients.id AND e.workspace_id = $coven AND e.deleted_at IS NULL`, and the overrides enter that statement in three places: the sort and keyset cursor on `coalesce(e.label, ingredients.name)`; the facets on `coalesce(e.elements, ingredients.elements)` and the other three arrays; and the search rank through **one more arm** of `searchMatch`'s `UNION ALL` — `e.label` at the `name` weight 1, its `where` this coven's live rows, answered by the label's own trigram index. The rank is already `max(score) group by id`, so a relabelled entry ranks by the better of its coven label and its compendium name and still answers a search for the compendium name (Cronewort is found by "mugwort"). Rank is the score alone across kinds (decision 12): the own row "Mugwort" and the compendium's Mugwort tie on the name arm and sort by name then id, side by side (decision 4). The list row carries the joined cupboard row into the loader above, and the seven overridable fields resolve `entry?.x ?? row.x` through `applyCupboardEntry`, so a card, a picker entry and the entry page read the same effective values.
- **The substitute picker** (`findIngredientSuggestions`, :345) returns two groups through a `group` tag per node — the client maps them to Combobox `heading` "Your Ingredients" / "Compendium" (`Combobox/types.ts:19`). Yours = the cupboard less its retired entries (decision 6: a retired entry takes no new use), own rows and compendium rows with a cupboard row alike, under the effective label (matched on label _and_ compendium name); Compendium = live entries with no row here (`NOT EXISTS` on a keyed row, far cheaper than today's anti-join). An entry appears once. Each group ranked by score within itself; per-group limit, e.g. 8 and 8 (estimate, tuned in e2e).
- **Jars and spell layers change nothing**: `findManyStock` already reads jars on compendium variants through `readableVariantParent`; spell layers already name compendium variants, and a spell goes on reading a layer's retired entry through M5.3's existing hatch. The one gap, the cupboard row, jars and children of a _retired_ entry, is closed by the single new hatch `findCupboardEntryIncludingSoftDeleted(membership, ingredientId)` — the compendium row regardless of tombstone, only when this coven's cupboard names it — plus `inUseVariantParent`, a sibling of `readableVariantParent` that admits a retired parent only on that proof (open point 2). Registered in the four places: the repository file, `repository/index.ts`, `ESCAPE_HATCHES` in `tests/guards/soft-delete-finder-guard.test.ts`, the tier seam in `claude-docs/modules.md`.

New finders: `findCupboardEntry(membership, ingredientId)`, `findCupboardEntriesOf(membership, ids)`, `findCupboardEntryIncludingSoftDeleted`, `countCovensUsing(ingredientId)` (distinct workspaces with a live row; unscoped, curator only), `findCompendiumMatchesByName(name)` (live compendium rows on `lower(name)` or canonical name, exact).

### 4. The Add flow and the nudges

- `possibleDuplicates` (trigram similarity) holds on to its "did you mean" list, and each compendium match in it gains **Use It**; an exact `lower(name)` or identity match turns the warning into the question that holds the save: "The compendium has Rosemary. **Use It** · **Add Our Own**" (several matches: one Use It per entry, one Add Our Own; an entry already in the cupboard offers **Open It**). Use It calls `addCupboardEntry` with the typed name as the label when it differs and the typed description/arrays as overrides, so the entry is in the cupboard whether or not a jar follows.
- Entry page: **In the Compendium** shows exactly the same-identity entry for an own row with a formal name (and, for a compendium entry, its public page, as M12.12 built). **Similar in the Compendium** (new) shows only for an own entry with no formal name, exact `lower(name)` matches, **This Is It** per entry → `convertLocalToCompendiumEntry`, with the refusal shown inline; hidden from viewers. The two blocks share M12.12's component shape.
- GraphQL: mutations `addCupboardEntry`, `annotateCupboardEntry`, `removeCupboardEntry`, `convertLocalToCompendiumEntry` (payload lists the dropped folk names, substitutes, categories and references), `convertCompendiumEntryToLocal`; fields `Ingredient.cupboardEntry(workspaceId)`, `Ingredient.retired`; queries `compendiumMatches(name, workspaceId)`, `compendiumEntryUsers(id)`; `ingredientSuggestions` nodes gain `group`. Every one through a service on `/api/graphql`; SDL snapshot and `npm run codegen` in each task touching the schema.
