# M8.17: Editing a Coven's Own Ingredient

**Status:** decided and built. Only the decisions, the reasons and what they rule out are kept here.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-19)

- **Who gets Link:** every steward or practitioner on any of their coven's own entries, formal name or not. The page works out `rolePermits(role, CONVERT_PERMISSION)` itself, as it already does for Make It Ours, and passes the answer to the dialog. The search reaches every live compendium entry. `canConvert`, and the Similar block that reads it, stay unchanged.
- **After a save:** the dialog closes, and the page or list shows the new values.
- **Where Link sits:** in its own section at the foot of the dialog, below the form and outside it. `IngredientForm`'s props don't change for it.
- **Unsaved edits when Link is pressed:** the Link confirmation adds one sentence saying unsaved changes won't be kept.
- **Taken as recommended, not raised with the owner:**
  - The value read becomes `entryValues(entry, session)`, moved to `src/app/entry-values.ts`.
  - A form a keeper has renamed blocks the save, with an error at that variant's Form field, until the member picks a name.
  - The dialog has no Delete; that stays M9.10's. Reversed by the owner, 2026-10-19: see "Delete in the coven's edit" below.
  - On the list, a `?edit=<id>` that isn't the coven's own entry, or comes from an observer, opens nothing.
