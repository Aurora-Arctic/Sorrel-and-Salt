# M8.19 — Ingredient detail page shell: the plan

Approved 2026-10-17. The task's entry is in [`tasks/m8.md`](../tasks/m8.md); this is how it was built, kept beside the records so the choices are recoverable.

## What is built

One page body, `IngredientEntry` (`src/components/IngredientEntry/`), on two routes:

| Route                                               | Who                                              | Read                                                                                                |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/compendium/ingredients/[slug]` (`(public)` group) | anyone, signed out included                      | `readCompendiumEntry(slug)` — sessionless, wrapped in `cachedCompendiumRead('compendium-entry', …)` |
| `/coven/[slug]/ingredients/[id]` (`(shell)` group)  | the coven's members, by `requireCovenMembership` | `getIngredient(session, id, workspace.id)` then `ingredientDetailOf(session, entry)` — never cached |

Both reads end in the same assembler, `ingredientDetailOf`, which turns an `IngredientRow` into an `IngredientDetail` (`src/modules/ingredients/types.ts`): the row, its folk names, categories with their group's colours, deities, substitutes, and one `VariantDetail` per live variant, each with its form's icon, categories and substitutes. References are not read here: MB.155 adds them, beneath the substitutes, in the room this page leaves.

The page is the ingredient, then one `<section id="form-<slug>">` per variant in form order, each heading led by its `FormIcon` and marked with `ToxicMark` when that form is toxic. The ingredient's own safety note sits with its fields; each form's sits in its section. Nothing is drawn for safety outside `SafetyNote` and `ToxicMark` (M8.13a).

## The calls made here

- **Edit for a curator or admin on a compendium entry shows on both routes**, as a link to `/admin/compendium?edit=<slug>` (`compendiumHref`). On the public route the viewer is read with `viewerIfSignedIn`, which never redirects, so a visitor still reads the page with no session. A coven's owner or member sees no Edit yet: M8.17's modal is M8.17's.
- **A form that adds nothing still gets its section**, saying so in one line, so the anchor `#form-<slug>` and the jump link always exist and a reader learns the form is held rather than wondering whether the page is broken.
- **Local entries are badged** with a new `local` badge palette (tinted), the one word "Local", read by the e2e helper `localBadge`.
- **The notes slot** is the end of the page: variant sections are the last thing rendered, inside one wrapper, so v2's notes section is appended after it without moving anything.
- **The formal-name line** says the nomenclature in words ("Botanical name") and sets the name in italics only for `botanical`, `fungal` and `zoological`; `unknown` with a name is upright and marked unconfirmed; `none`, and `unknown` without a name, each get a sentence rather than a blank.
- **The 308** is `permanentRedirect` from the public page on a `moved` address. MB.84 pins its headers.
- **Old placeholders go**: `duplicates.tsx` loses its `/ingredients/<id>` default (every caller passes `duplicateHref`), the shell's Add Ingredient links a near match to the coven entry page and opens the saved entry's page on Save Ingredient, and the form's substitute summary links a compendium substitute to its public page.
