# M12.31: Build Design F, Click Feedback Outside Forms (approved build plan)

This is the approved plan for building Design F inside M12.31, stored beside the decision record it builds (`m12.31-click-feedback-outside-forms.md`). Where the two differ, the record wins.

## Context

M12.31 started as a scoping task. The owner chose **Design F** on 2026-10-20 and asked for its build to happen inside M12.31, in parallel agent lanes, rather than as new tasks. Design F covers what a click on a control outside any form or modal says back. On success, the result shows on the page and a polite status region says it in words. On failure, a floating callout appears under the control and leaves a warning badge behind that reopens it. The rule is in section 5 of `claude-docs/design-decisions/m12.31-click-feedback-outside-forms.md`, and the controls it covers are listed in section 2.

The owner made four decisions after commit 8ce094ab, and the record does not have them yet:

- **F replaces `ActionFailure` everywhere.** The record's "beside it above tables" goes.
- **The callout closes when focus or a click moves outside** the control, its badge and the callout. The reason is WCAG 2.4.11, Focus Not Obscured. That citation is from my own knowledge, not from the research sources, so it gets a footnote of its own.
- **The callout and the badge fade in and out**, over `$motion-short`, and reduced motion turns the fades off.
- **Focus-to-heading has moved to M8.17.** The owner confirmed it, and the M8.17 session accepted it and is building it. M12.31 does not touch focus after a removal.

**Ports:** the app runs on **8001** and the workshop on **61001**, as the owner asked. While the app holds 8001, e2e runs only with `E2E_PORT_BASE` set away from it.

### Lane A: the core (about 10h)

This lane builds a new component directory (working name `src/components/ClickFeedback/`) with its doc, tests and Ladle stories.

**Status region**

- One visually hidden polite `<output>`, mounted early in `src/app/layout.tsx` beside `Providers`.
- `announce(text)` reaches it through context.
- Saying the same text twice uses the alternating no-break space from `Modal/discard.tsx`.
- With no provider in place, `announce` does nothing, so tests and Ladle don't break.

**`useClickFeedback({ name })`**

- It returns `{ anchorRef, report(message | undefined), announce, feedback }`.
- The caller puts `ref={anchorRef}` on its control and renders `{feedback}` directly after it.
- `feedback` is the badge followed by the callout.
- `report(undefined)` clears everything at the control's next press: badge, callout and `aria-describedby`.

**Callout**

- It is `role="alert"` and carries the service's own sentence.
- **Position:** Floating UI, as `InfoTip/use-tip.ts` uses it.
  - Under the control, left-aligned; else right-aligned to it; else pinned to the `.app` box.
  - The arrow points at the middle of the control, with its tip about 11.5px from it.
  - The callout sits at `z-index` between `floating` (20) and `dialog` (30).
  - It never moves the layout.
- **Closing:**
  - an X button labelled "Dismiss", 24×24, at the top right, using `ClearIcon` or `CrossIcon`, with ink that inverts per theme;
  - Escape;
  - focus or a pointer press moving outside the control, the badge and the callout (the pattern in `CategoryChips/category-more.tsx`).
- **Focus return:** closing returns focus to whatever opened the callout, the badge or the control.
- **One per screen:** a second failure, anywhere, closes the first callout. The first badge stays.

**Badge**

- A toggletip `<button>` with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, labelled "Show the error for <name>".
- It is 19.2px visible, with a 24×24 hit area that grows only back over its own control.
- **Position:** top right on a bordered button; just right of the text, vertically centred, on `.btn--quiet`.
- It uses a shared warning-triangle mask taken from `.notice--error::before` in `_primitives.scss`.
- The control's `aria-describedby` points at a visually hidden "Last try failed: <msg>".

**Fades**

- Fade in with `arrived($motion-short)`, and fade out with the same keyframe reversed, as `SkeletonFade` does.
- The element stays mounted until `animationend`, with a fallback timer as in `MenuButton`.
- Focus return and `aria-expanded` don't wait for the fade-out.
- `reduced-motion` turns the fades off.

**Tests**

- **Vitest (role and label queries):** report, then reopen, then dismiss; Escape; closing on focus out and on a press outside; focus return to the badge or the control; one per screen; `aria-describedby` set, then cleared at the next press; the status region announces repeated text.
- **Playwright** at 1280px and 375px, in both themes:
  - placement and the three fallbacks;
  - zero layout shift;
  - the badge's 24px hit area overlaps no neighbour;
  - reduced motion;
  - axe.
- Extend `tests/guards/tip-placement.test.ts` if the callout falls outside what it watches.
