# Fewer, quieter tooltips

## Context

The owner finds the app cluttered with tooltips. Two kinds are involved and they need different fixes.

- **InfoTip** (`src/components/InfoTip/`): the ⓘ beside a field label holding its hint. About 55 across the forms; roughly half restate the label ("Zodiac Signs: The signs it answers to"). The research (NN/g Tooltip Guidelines; NN/g's mobile tutorial and coach-mark studies) says a tutorial would not help and that tips should be few, user-triggered and never carry essential instructions. So: cull, not replace, and no "dismissed" state.
- **IconTip** (`src/components/IconTip/`): the one-word label over an icon-only control (Edit, Quick Look, +). NN/g says icons need a label and a short tooltip is the minimum, so these stay. The clutter comes from their timing: they open at once on hover, fade over 500ms (`$tip-fade`), hold for 150ms after the pointer leaves, and sweeping a toolbar leaves a trail of fading words. The owner wants them to come and go quickly and not linger when hovered.

The owner said yes to the triage and asked for the faster icon tips. No tutorial, no hide-tips toggle.

## Part A: IconTip timing

Files: `src/components/IconTip/icon-tip.tsx`, `src/components/InfoTip/use-tip.ts`, `src/components/IconTip/index.scss`, `src/scss/_variables.scss`, `tests/components/IconTip/index.test.tsx`, `claude-docs/components/icon-tip.md`, `claude-docs/components/info-tip.md`.

1. **Give `useTip` an options bag** for timing: `{ openDelayMs, closeDelayMs }`. Hover waits `openDelayMs` before opening (a timer cleared by leave, so a sweep past opens nothing); focus and tap open at once, since a keyboard or touch user should not wait. `hideSoon` uses `closeDelayMs`. Every hover-opened tip gets a delay, the owner's call: **InfoTip, Combobox, UserList and TipMark default to `openDelayMs: 300`** and keep `closeDelayMs: 150` and their hoverable bubble, since their text can be long and a pointer may want to rest on it.
2. **IconTip passes `{ openDelayMs: 300, closeDelayMs: 0 }` and its bubble no longer holds the tip open.** 300ms is the common desktop figure (Fluent 250, Material 500). On leave it closes at once. The bubble takes `pointer-events: none` and hover is heard on the control rather than the wrapper, so resting on the word does nothing and it goes the moment the pointer leaves the icon. This is the owner's call against WCAG 1.4.13's "hoverable" clause; the cost is small because the bubble is decoration (`aria-hidden`) repeating the control's own accessible name, and a low-vision user who zooms still has the name. Record the call in `icon-tip.md`.
3. **A short fade for IconTip only**: add `$icon-tip-fade: 120ms` in `_variables.scss` and use it in `IconTip/index.scss` in place of `$tip-fade`; `TIP_FADE_MS` in `icon-tip.tsx` matches. InfoTip keeps its 500ms, the owner's pace.
4. **Optional, ask at review**: a "warm" window as Material and Fluent do, where once one IconTip has opened, a neighbour opens with no delay for ~1s, so scanning a toolbar is still quick. Small: a module-level timestamp in `icon-tip.tsx`. Leave out unless the owner wants it.
5. Tests (fake timers, as `tests/components/InfoTip/index.test.tsx` does). IconTip: hover does not open before 300ms and does after; a hover that leaves before 300ms opens nothing; focus opens at once; leaving closes at once with no grace. InfoTip: its hover test gains the 300ms wait; the "kept open while the pointer is on the tip" test stays. Combobox, UserList and TipMark tests that hover a tip gain the wait.
6. Docs: `icon-tip.md` "Behaviour" and the `useTip` paragraph in `info-tip.md` record the options and why IconTip differs.

## Part B: InfoTip triage

One rule, written into `claude-docs/components/info-tip.md` as a "When a field gets a tip" section: **a field keeps its ⓘ only when the tip states something the label cannot carry: a rule, a flow, or a format with a trap.** A tip that restates the label is removed. A tip that only gives a format moves into an example placeholder (one or two words, within the short-placeholder rule).

Call sites: `hint=` props in `src/components/IngredientForm/{fields,index,variants,categories,suggestions}.tsx`, `references.tsx`, `reference-panel.tsx`, `CupboardEntryForm/index.tsx`, `GroupedValueForm/index.tsx`, `GroupForm/index.tsx`, `FacetPanel/index.tsx`; `<InfoTip>` in `IngredientEntry/` and `app/.../[id]/page.tsx`.

**Keep** (rule or flow): Classification; Variants; References; Locator; Colours (corresponds to, not is); Cupboard Name (unique per coven); the Cupboard "empty list says none" four, folded into one wording; GroupForm theme colours (4.5:1, light filled from dark); GroupedValueForm Group and Tradition; FacetPanel Source and Match plus the "stuck" tips; all IngredientEntry nomenclature and compendium tips.

**Remove** (restates the label): Name (both forms), Form suggestion, Formal Name, Folk Names, Safety Notes (both), Toxic (both), Categories, Celestial Bodies, Zodiac Signs, Deities, Substitute Ingredients, Cupboard Description and Safety notes, GroupedValueForm Description (three kinds), Reason, Invite Note, Create Coven Name (its one fact, "its address is made from it", stays as a `.field__hint` line if the owner wants it kept in view).

**Reference panel, 13 to 1**: one InfoTip at the panel's top, "How to Cite", holding the Chicago conventions (author inverted, underscores italicise, as printed). Each field's format becomes an example placeholder: Authors `Shelley, Mary`, Edition `2nd ed.`, Volume `Vol. 2`, Place `London`, Published `1985`, Pages `112–30`, Address `https://`, Read Through `Perseus Digital Library`. Container hints ("The book this chapter is in") go, the label says it.

A removed hint also comes off the field's `aria-describedby` through the field components (`FieldShell` and friends handle a missing `hint` already; confirm). Update `tests/components/**` that assert a removed hint's text; `tests/e2e/tip-placement.spec.ts` measures the Add Ingredient dialog's tips and needs its count or list refreshed.

**Quick Look** keeps its IconTip only; the owner does not want a visible word on it (2026-10-20).

## Out of scope
