# M9.3 — Starting a jar of a form the coven holds is refused

**Status:** decided · **Date:** 2026-10-16

The owner's call, made while M9.3 was being built. The stock service starts a
coven's jar of a form with `createStock`, and refuses a form the coven
already holds. DESIGN.md §5 (stock rows) and §7 (`addIngredientToWorkspace`)
carry the result.

## What the task said

M9.3's entry named the function `addStock` and made it idempotent: adding a
form the coven already held returned the live jar and wrote nothing. If two
adds raced, the loser caught the unique-index error and returned the
winner's jar. M9.4's mutation, `addIngredientToWorkspace`, repeated it, and
so did §5 and §7.

## Why it changed

"Add" with an amount reads as adding that amount. Someone who holds 5 g and
sends 10 g would expect 15 g back. Under the idempotent rule they got the
5 g jar, as a success, with nothing to say their 10 g was dropped. The race
was worse: the loser's whole input vanished the same way.

Three answers were weighed:

- **Top up the held jar** — convert the amount into the jar's unit and add
  it. That is a restock operation, not a create. It has to refuse a
  different dimension, a count, and a jar with no amount or unit, and it
  leaves open what a different source or date means. It is new scope.
- **Return the held jar with a flag saying so** — the caller can tell, but
  the name still says "add", and a client that ignores the flag repeats the
  original problem.
- **Refuse** — chosen. Nothing is written, and the caller is told why.

## What it does now

- `createStock(session, workspaceId, variantId, input)` throws a
  `ValidationError` with one issue at `['variantId']` when the coven holds a
  live jar of that form. The input's amounts are never applied to the held
  jar. Changing a jar is `updateStock`'s.
- The check is the insert breaking
  `inventory_items_workspace_id_variant_id_unique`, with no read before it.
  A create and a racing create are refused the same way, and
  `findStockOfVariant`, which only served the read, is gone.
- After a soft delete there is no live jar, so a create inserts a fresh row,
  as before.
- `addIngredientToWorkspace` keeps its name: from the compendium page, the
  action is adding an ingredient to the coven, which does not read as adding
  an amount. It passes the refusal on as a field error (M9.4), and M9.12's
  flow tells the member the coven holds the form and links to that jar.

A top-up — adding an amount to a jar — is not part of v1. If one is wanted,
it is its own task and its own operation on a jar's id.
