# M9.6 — The Coven's Ingredients Page

**Status:** decided and built. Only the decisions, the reasons and what they rule out are kept here.

## Decision

`/coven/[slug]/ingredients` doesn't exist yet. Only the single-entry page, `ingredients/[id]`, from M8.19, does. M9.6 builds the coven's list of everything in its cupboard: its own entries plus the compendium entries it has added. The page is the coven twin of M8.18's public `/compendium` cards page. Most of the stories about a coven's ingredients walk this page, so it also turns them on.
When M8.18's CI fixes land, both branches get merged in again. M8.18's worktree has uncommitted changes in about ten files, including `CompendiumList/index.scss` and its stories, and they are not in this branch yet.

- The type-ahead suggests from the coven's cupboard only.
  | Facet counts | The `workspaceIngredientFacets` service and GraphQL field; `covenFacetCounts` in `src/components/CompendiumList/facets.ts:58`, written for this page and never called yet. |

1. **A count of the coven's list.** `findWorkspaceIngredientCount(membership, filter, start)` in the repository is a twin of `findCompendiumCount`, over `and(list.arms, workspaceListScope(...))`. The service `countWorkspaceIngredients(session, workspaceId, filter, start)` asserts the same `ingredient:['read']` permission as the list and is **not cached**, because the data is the coven's (rule 6). Nothing in GraphQL changes: only the server-rendered page counts.
2. **The filter gets a coven mode.** `CompendiumList/filter.tsx` is a client component, so its props must be plain data rather than functions. Today it chooses between the admin and public lists with `audience: 'admin' | 'public'`. This adds `audience: 'coven'` with `covenSlug` and `workspaceId` props. In coven mode the filter:
   - carries `source`, `inStock` and `lowStockOnly` in `panelOf`, `NO_FACETS`, `heldFilter` and `hrefFor`, so FacetPanel's Source, In Stock and Low Stock controls appear;
3. **Type-ahead from the cupboard.** `IngredientSearchBox` and `IngredientSearchForm` take an optional `workspaceId`. When it's set, the box queries `workspaceIngredients(workspaceId:, query:, first:)` instead of `compendium`, and shows the coven's label where one is set. The match shape stays the same. Run `npm run codegen` and commit its output. Suggestions never come from another coven, and that includes the suggested names themselves (CLAUDE.md).
4. **The search sentence covers the coven filters.** `buildSearchSentence` gains clauses for Source, In Stock and Low Stock Only (`sentence.ts` and `types.ts`), and the coven filter uses the existing `noun` option.
   - A fourth script in `src/app/pre-paint-scripts.tsx` reads `localStorage['card-density']`. When the stored choice is regular, it sets `data-card-density="regular"` on `<html>` before the first paint, so the compact look never flashes.
   - A new gate spec, `tests/e2e/coven-ingredients.spec.ts`, proves only what no story walks:
     - the type-ahead suggests this coven's cupboard only, never another coven's entry or a compendium entry the coven hasn't added;
