# M12.8 — A jar follows a local that hides its compendium entry

**Status:** decided · **Date:** 2026-10-16 (the owner's three calls, made when the task was scoped)

**Decided:** when a coven saves a local ingredient that hides a compendium entry, the coven's live jars of that entry move to the local's variant of the same form. This supersedes M9.4, whose entry in [`tasks/m9.md`](../tasks/m9.md) said: "a stock row whose compendium entry a local entry later suppresses (M8.3) stays in the table. The list shows the local entry, and that jar reaches no read until the local entry is deleted or relabelled so it no longer matches. Nothing is moved or deleted behind the member's back."

## Why M9.4's choice was replaced

Leaving the jar made stock the coven owned vanish from every screen, with nothing to say where it went. Moving it keeps the figure visible on the entry the coven now uses. The move is not behind the member's back: it is the effect of a save they made, and a clash they must settle is refused (below).

## The owner's three calls

- **A typed error.** A clash of units raises `JarMergeNeeded`, a subclass of `ValidationError`, rather than a result union or a plain `ValidationError`. It maps as VALIDATION with no transport change, and carries `conflicts` (both jars, whole) for a caller that wants to show them.
- **Copy the form's own facts, not child rows.** A form made for the local copies the compendium form's description, safety notes, toxic flag, elements, celestial bodies, zodiac signs, colours and curated form. It copies no references, substitutes or categories: those are cited or chosen, not facts of the form.
- **`jarMerges` is service-only.** `LocalIngredientInput` takes `jarMerges: { jarId, quantityOnHand, unit }[]`, where `jarId` is the moving jar. The SDL gains it in M8.17.

## Other rules

- **Grams against kilograms is a conflict.** Units must be equal (none against none counts), or one jar must hold no amount at all. Nothing is converted, as everywhere else in the module.
- **A threshold follows a changed unit.** The local jar keeps its threshold, source and acquired date, but a threshold is a number in the jar's unit. Where a merge leaves the jar in a different unit (a chosen amount in another unit, or a bare local jar taking the moving jar's), the threshold is converted with `unitConvert` if both units are of one dimension (10 g becomes 0.01 kg), and cleared if either unit is none or the pair crosses dimensions or involves a count. It is cleared rather than guessed, because 10 left unchanged would silently become 10 kg, and no density table exists to convert across dimensions. A merge that keeps the unit leaves the threshold untouched.
- **Promoting a folk name is a relabel.** It can make the row hide an entry, so it moves jars too. It has no `jarMerges`, so a unit clash refuses it. Its refusal is worded for what the member can do there: both jars are still visible before the promote, so it says to put the two in one unit and promote again, and its issue has an empty path (no field), not `jarMerges`. `carryJarsToLocal` takes a `fix` parameter (`'amount'` or `'unit'`) rather than the caller catching and rewording, so the error is built once.
- **The permission now includes `inventory: update`.** The three saves assert it with `ingredient`, in one call. Owner and member hold both; the viewer holds neither write; no role holds one without the other.
- **`createStock` follows too.** A jar started on a form of a hidden entry is stored on the local's form, made if absent. Of two locals hiding one entry, the one sharing the entry's identity wins, then the older. The indexes allow at most two (one by identity, one by label), so the age rule is only a deterministic fallback.
- **Sums are exact.** Quantities add as thousandths in `BigInt`; a sum above 999,999,999.999 is a `ValidationError` on `jarMerges`, not a database overflow.

## Known limits

- Jars already sitting on a hidden entry from the M9.4 days move on the local's next save.

## The save-versus-add race, and its lock

Member A saves a local Rosemary while member B adds a jar of the compendium's Rosemary. Without more, A's save could read the hidden jars before B's jar commits, and B's `createStock` could look for a hiding local before A's save commits, leaving the jar stranded on a hidden entry. The four callers (the create, the update and the promote, and `createStock`) therefore take one transaction-scoped advisory lock keyed on the coven, `write.lockCovenStock`, as the first statement of their `withAudit`. Whichever action comes second waits for the first and then sees it.

- **Why advisory, not a row lock.** There is no row both sides share: the jar being added does not exist yet, and the local being saved may not either.
- **Why first.** Taking it before any other read or write keeps the lock order the same everywhere, ahead of the row locks `stockWrites` takes later, so the two cannot deadlock.
- **What it costs.** Saves and stock creates within one coven queue behind each other for the milliseconds a transaction takes. Other covens are not held up: the key is a fixed namespace plus a hash of the coven's id.
