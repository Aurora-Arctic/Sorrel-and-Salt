# M12.53 — The component workshop moves from Ladle to Storybook

**Decided 2026-10-21.** Built in M12.53.

## Why

The Claude Design sync reads a Storybook. Ladle has no equivalent, so the sync
needs the workshop to be a Storybook. Nothing else about what the workshop is
for changed: one place to see each component, against the real tokens, with no
page routed to it.

## What was chosen

- **`@storybook/nextjs-vite`** (Storybook 10.6.3, Vite 8). Its framework
  supplies the `next/link` and `next/navigation` handling that `.ladle/` wrote by
  hand, so the workshop keeps no alias for them. The one alias left is `@` to
  `src/`, commented in `.storybook/main.ts`.
- **Helpers live in `.storybook/`**, along with the four stories the app never
  imports (`foundations`, `main-body`, `navigation-bar`, `pending-link`).
- **All 85 story files are CSF3** (Storybook's story file format): a default
  `meta` with the title, one named export per story.
- **Autodocs are off.** The workshop shows components, not generated docs.
- **Port 61000 and `allowedHosts: ['devcontainer']` are unchanged**, as is the
  never-answered `/api/auth` request that holds the two loading stories
  (`sign-in-sign-in-methods--pending-add`, `sign-in-sign-in-panel--pending`) in their waiting state.
- **`npm run workshop` asks for no sign-in.** `npm run workshop:build` goes through
  `scripts/build-workshop.ts`, which relays Storybook's exit code and accepts
  `--outDir` (`deploy.yml` passes `public/workshop`). No `--base` is needed.
- **Ladle is removed** (`.ladle/`, `@ladle/react`, `npm run workshop:ladle`). The
  owner dropped the Ladle-versus-Storybook comparison on 2026-10-21, so nothing
  keeps it installed.
- **Stories and `.storybook/` are type-checked.** The owner approved bringing
  them under `tsc` in this task (`tsconfig.json` includes `.storybook/**/*.ts(x)`
  and excludes only `node_modules`). Fixtures take realistic values of their real
  type, with no casts. One helper holds a cast: `route()` in `.storybook/route.ts`,
  because typed routes make `Route` exclude dynamic addresses, and the app does
  the same in `src/app/invite/[token]/page.tsx`. `scripts/` stays unchecked.

## Mapping from Ladle

| Ladle                                 | Storybook                                                        |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `meta.title`                          | `meta.title`, unchanged; ids are the kebab title + `--` + export |
| `storyOrder` in `.ladle/config.mjs`   | `storySort` in `preview.tsx`, with `Default` first               |
| `meta.theme` / `Dark` + `Light` pairs | story-level `globals: { theme }`, only where the theme is shown  |
| `meta.width`                          | the `viewport` global (presets `w375`, `w382`, `w600`, `w700`)   |
| `iframed`                             | dropped: every story already has its own iframe                  |
| `reducedMotion`                       | `parameters: { reducedMotion: true }`                            |
| `?story=<id>&theme=<t>&width=<w>`     | `/?path=/story/<id>&globals=theme:<t>;viewport:w<w>`             |
| `.ladle/components.tsx` provider      | the `withWorkshopFrame` decorator in `.storybook/preview.tsx`    |

## Phone stories

There are no `Phone` stories. A phone view is any story opened at a narrow
viewport (see "Opening a story at phone width" in `claude-docs/workshop.md`).
Every Ladle story pinned to 375px was removed, unless it was the only story
showing its state. Those stay with the pin dropped: `DetailsLongName`,
`DetailsLongWord` (IngredientEntry), `PrivilegeChangesTab` (TabbedPage,
formerly `PhoneScrolled`), `LongWorkspaceName` (ContextRow, formerly
`PhoneLongName`), `LongHeadingWithAction` (PageHeader), `EveryRowState`
(SignInMethods), `CovenRow` and `CovenRowFiltered` (CardDensitySwitch),
`OverTheShell` (ImpersonationBanner).

The other pinned widths:

- **`SectionNav` `AdminOnAHiddenPage` (700px)** keeps its width as the story's
  starting setting. Its More-menu state exists only at tablet width, so the width
  is the thing the story shows. The other two 700px stories (`AdminTablet`,
  `AccountMenu` `OpenAtSevenHundred`) show nothing a viewport change does not,
  and were removed.
- **`TabbedPage` `AddAsPlus` (600px) and `AddBeneathAtThreeFifty` (382px)** were
  two identical stories with different pins. Both drew `AddAction`, the list

## The story pass (2026-10-21)

Once the stories were on Storybook, the owner approved one pass over them to
remove stories that repeated another, and to make the sidebar and the story
names say the same thing everywhere. A story that only repeated another
shows nothing a reader could not see already, and costs a review each time a
component changes.

- **Every story file has a `Default` story**, the state at rest, and it sorts
  first. `AtRest` became `Default`; where a file had no resting state, its
  first or simplest story took the name.
- **One name for one state**: `Empty`, `NoMatches`, `Loading` (data on its way),
  `Pending` (an action on its way), `FirstPage`, `MiddlePage`, `LastPage`,
  `Failed`. Renamed: `Busy` to `Pending` (AddIcon, Field, MenuButton),
  `LoadingLink` to `Pending` (Pending Link), `LoadingSignIn` and
  `FailedSignIn` to `Pending` and `Failed` (SignInPanel), `LoadingAdd` to
  `PendingAdd` (SignInMethods), `CouldNotLoad` to `Failed` (CategoryPicker),
  `WithError` to `Failed` (EmailForm, SignInMethods), `Failure` to `Failed`
  (Notice), `NoMatch` to `NoMatches` (AdminInvitations, CompendiumList,
  UserList, GroupList), `EmptyForASearch` to `NoMatches` (PrivilegeLedger),
  `NoInvitations` and `NoneWaiting` to `Empty`, `OnePageOfSeveral` and
  `MidList` to `MiddlePage`, `SecondPage` to `LastPage` (ReceivedInvitations),
  and the Combobox `List` and ChipColorField `DarkTheme` to `Default`
  (`LightTheme` to `LightColour`).
- **Removed as repeats**: GroupForm (7 to 5), GroupedValueForm (9 to 6),
  GroupedValueList (21 to 10: one kind carries the list states, the other kinds
  keep one story each), the IngredientForm `InModal` story, IngredientCard
  stories merged into their neighbours, CompendiumCards `CanAddAndEdit`
  (the `withEdit` helper covers it), and IngredientEntry `CovenEntryLastEdited`
  folded into `CovenEntry`.
- **Shared fixtures** moved into `.storybook/`: `FIXTURE_EDIT`, `FIXTURE_COVEN`,
  `FIXTURE_WORKSPACE_ID` (`fixtures.ts`), `NO_FILTER` and `NO_FACETS`
  (`compendium-fixtures.ts`), `InModal` (`in-modal.tsx`) and `setInputValue`
  (`press.ts`).
- **New sidebar**: Foundations; Pages; Sign In (Panel, Sign-In Methods, Provider
  Mark); Shell; Account (Invitations Received); Coven (Invitations Sent); one
  flat Ingredients group (no Lists group); Admin (Invitations, Nav, User List);
  Emails; Forms (Icon Tip moved here); Feedback (Notice, Skeleton, Skeleton Fade,
  Spinner, Main Body, Navigation Bar, Pending Link; the "Loading" subgroup is
  gone); Layout; Navigation; Surfaces. Theme Toggle stays in Layout and Click
  Feedback in Surfaces. Why: a group per area meets a person where they look
  for a piece, and the ingredient pieces were split across Admin, Lists and
  Surfaces by which page drew them first.

### Follow-up edits (same day)

- **CovenCards**: `WithEdit`, `WithAnnotate` and `WithMore` folded into
  `WithStock`, whose member foot shows all three. `WithLoneBin` stays: its DOM
  differs.
- **IngredientEntry**: `NoFormalNameWithEdit` folded into `NoFormalName`.
  Kept apart because the DOM differs: `NothingRecorded` (a page) and
  `DetailsNothingRecorded` (a modal); `DetailsLongName`, `DetailsLongWord` and
  `DetailsNoFormalName` (three different wrapping cases);
  `LocalReplacingCompendiumEntry` and `LocalReplacingSeveral` (the count
  differs, and See All appears past three).
- **IngredientForm**: the `useCannedApi` call moved to a meta decorator
  (`parameters.cannedApi: false` turns it off for `LoadingSubstituteSummary`);
  the `XView` wrapper pairs were inlined; a `CompendiumForm` helper carries the
  repeated compendium props in place of `meta.args`, since the stories render
  their own JSX around the form. The Promote and CompendiumEntry open-state
  stories are unchanged.
- **Read in detail, nothing removed**: Field, Modal, UserList, MenuButton,
  SignInPanel, AccountMenu, AddIcon, ContextRow, AppShell, InvitationAcceptance
  and IngredientSearch. Each story shows a state with its own DOM.
