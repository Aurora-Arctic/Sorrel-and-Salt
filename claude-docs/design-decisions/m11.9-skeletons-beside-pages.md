# M11.9 — Skeletons beside each page, chosen by address

**Decided:** 2026-11-06, by the owner. The skeleton a navigation draws is now chosen by the address
being navigated to, and each skeleton sits beside its page and is built from that page's own
components. This **supersedes M12.33's link targets**
([`m12.33-loading-standard.md`](m12.33-loading-standard.md)): the `target` option, the
`navigationTarget` prop, `siteLinkTarget`, `adminTarget`, `covenTarget` and `ingredientLinkTarget` are
gone. The rest of M12.33 (the three parts, the delays, the bar, the provider) stands. The standard is
[`../loading.md`](../loading.md); the measurements that led here are in
[`../loading-audit.md`](../loading-audit.md); the plan the work followed is
[`m11.9-plan.md`](m11.9-plan.md).

## Why

The audit measured the skeletons in a browser and found them off by up to 148px: the entry page's side
menu was 140px in the skeleton and 288.5px on the page, the cards 212px against 226 to 260px. The
cause was that no skeleton reused its page's components. A link named a target, and `MainBody` and
`SectionBody` drew a generic `Skeleton` that copied only the page's class names. A copy drifts the
first time the page changes, and 41 link targets in 24 files had to be kept in step with the pages by
hand.

## The calls

- **The destination address chooses the skeleton.** `src/app/skeletons.ts` maps each route pattern to
  a host (`main`, `coven` or `admin`) and a component. `NavigationProvider` keeps the destination
  `href` in the active navigation; `skeletonFor` matches it. A link carries nothing but its `href`.
- **Nothing draws on the same address, on a refresh, or when the address is unmatched.** A pager or
  filter keeps the skeleton its `ListFrame` already draws.
- **A section host draws only while it stands.** The coven host draws for any coven slug to any coven
  slug, so a switch between covens keeps the header and switcher up and busy (the owner chose this over
  the first rule, "the same coven slug", on 2026-11-07, because the loading standard's spec says the
  shell stays usable in a wait). The admin host draws for the same area (`curator` or `keepers`).
  Otherwise `MainBody` draws the whole frame. `MainBody` and `SectionBody` add no heading of their
  own: every registered skeleton draws its page's `PageHeader` `h1`, which `tests/guards/skeleton-heading.test.tsx`
  checks (the owner replaced the hidden `<h1>Loading</h1>` on 2026-11-07).
- **Each skeleton is a `skeleton.tsx` beside its page** (or beside the layout that stays up during the
  wait), built from `PageHeader`, `TabbedPage`, `PageNav`, `ListFrame`, `StackTable` and the card frames.
  `.page-column`, `.page-frame` and `.page-with-nav` became the components `PageColumn`, `PageFrame`
  and `PageWithNav`, which a page and its skeleton both draw.
- **`Skeleton` keeps two shapes, `line` and `block`.** The page shapes (`table`, `cards`, `form`,
  `page`, `contents`, `recipe`, `detail`, `requests`, `document`) were removed with the target files and
  their tests.
- **A guard enforces the pairing.** `tests/guards/skeletons.test.ts` fails when a `page.tsx` has no
  registry entry, or an entry names no page. The pages with no entry on purpose are listed in the test
  by name, each with its reason. This is the sweep-task rule's mechanism plus a mechanical guard.
- **Two lint rules close the gaps the audit found around the edges.**
  `in-app-link-through-pending-link` stops a bare `next/link` `Link` from being added, and
  `full-page-load-reports-leaving` stops a `location.assign`, `replace` or `reload` without a
  `reportLeaving()` before it. Deleting the target type already makes a link-named skeleton impossible
  to write, so no lint rule is needed for that.
- **Still no `loading.tsx` and no route-level `<Suspense>`.** Streaming sends a `200` before the guards
  have run, which loses the `404`, `403` and `308` they answer with, and breaks pages without
  JavaScript. The skeleton is drawn by the client from the registry instead.
- **A skeleton matches its page where the page is static.** Static parts stand within 2px of the real
  ones and the frame does not move; dynamic content may be a little off. `tests/e2e/skeleton-layout.spec.ts`
  is the gate, and `claude-docs/loading.md` ("How closely a skeleton matches its page") says how it
  measures. `framed()` supplies the context row and column for a skeleton the layout does not frame,
  and a skeleton runs no query.
- **The window scrolls to the top as the skeleton appears.** The owner chose this on 2026-11-07, so a
  click made on a scrolled page moves the page once, at the tap, and nothing moves on arrival. It is an
  instant scroll, made when a host draws a destination skeleton and not for a same-address navigation or
  a route modal.

## `PageTab.skeleton` stays optional

`PageTab.skeleton` is optional and `ListBody`'s `skeleton` is required. A `ListBody` always swaps in a
skeleton while a pager or filter is pending, so a missing one is always a gap. A tab is different: a
`TabbedPage` can be built with tabs that have no wait of their own to cover (the registry already
draws a whole page's skeleton when the address changes pages), and a required prop would force such a
tab to carry a placeholder that stands for nothing. A tab without a
skeleton shows an empty panel while it is on its way (`tab-switch.tsx` draws `null`), and the page
bar still shows. That is a plain absence the reader can see, where a required prop would be satisfied
with a stub.

## Deviations

- The `target="_blank"` links in `TermsAgreement` are plain `<a>` rather than `PendingLink`, because
  their tests mount no app router and a new tab navigates nothing in this one.
- The Grimoire's spell skeleton is five `line` bars inside the spell card's frame, so it follows the
  card density (`data-density`, `html[data-card-density]`) like the card does.
- The component-test setup resets a reported `leaving` flag after every test
  (`tests/support/setup-dom.ts`), because the flag is module state that a real page leaves only by
  unloading.
