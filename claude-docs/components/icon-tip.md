# IconTip

`src/components/IconTip/` shows a short word beside an icon control, such as "Edit" beside a pencil, for a sighted reader who cannot tell what the mark does. The directory also holds what every Edit control shares: the link and button controls built on the tip, the set's line icons, and the Quick Look eye (`lidded-eye.tsx`) drawn open or shut. Props are in `types.ts`. Used by the admin lists, the ingredient form, the coven and compendium cards, the entry page and Quick Look window, the spell builder and recipe page, the account menu, and the radio strip. The tip's hook and the ⓘ tip are [`info-tip.md`](info-tip.md)'s; the menu is [`menu-button.md`](menu-button.md)'s.

## Contracts

**Props and links**

- `IconTip` wraps one icon control, a `<button>` or a link, which keeps its own name, role and attributes.
- `EditLink` is a client component. A plain press opens the row's modal at once under `heading`, over a skeleton, and the form arrives in the same dialog ([`modal.md`](modal.md), "Opening at once"). It is still a link: a press with a modifier key, a new tab, or one before the script has loaded follows `href`. `heading` is built on the server with the builder the loaded modal uses.
- Each control built on the tip is named "<verb> <entry>" in hidden text, with the verb as the tip ("Edit Aluminium, Aluminium"), and takes the entry's name as the card names it (`cardName`, "Name, Formal").
- `QuickLookLink` takes `EditLink`'s props and a `place`, the modal place its press shows in ([`modal.md`](modal.md), "Two places on one page"). It opens the entry's details modal over its list ([`ingredient-entry.md`](ingredient-entry.md), "The details modal"). `QuickLookButton` serves where the details open in the page, not at an address, such as over the Add Ingredient form's held duplicates ([`ingredient-form.md`](ingredient-form.md), "Quick Look in the warning").
- `LinkIcon` inside a link is drawn through `LinkIconHint` ([`link-hint.md`](link-hint.md)), so the chain turns into the loading ring while the link is pending; the bare `LinkIcon` is the glyph alone.

**Behaviour** (on the same [`useTip`](info-tip.md) hook as InfoTip, but quicker)

- It opens once the pointer has rested on the control for 300ms, and at once on the control's focus, so a pointer passing over a row of pencils opens nothing. Both are heard on the wrapper, which is no larger than the control (the bubble is fixed, outside its box). It is the wrapper and not the control, so any control can sit inside without IconTip taking over its props.
- `opensOnPress` opens the tip on a press, for a status mark whose button does nothing else (the card's In Every Cupboard check, the entry page's In Your Cupboard shelf, the Beta Tester sprout). On touch there is no hover, and a tap focuses no button in Safari, so without it the word never shows. It opens rather than toggles, since emulated hover may have opened it already. A control with a press of its own (a pencil, the plus) does not take it.
- It is shut while the control's menu is open, so every caller gets it and `MenuButton` knows nothing of tips (a `MenuButton` icon: the card's plus, the entry page's pencil). `useTip`'s `canOpen` asks as it opens, so the 300ms wait is covered; a `MutationObserver` on `aria-expanded` takes away a tip already showing, so a menu opened by a press or ArrowDown with the pointer resting loses it too. With the menu closed the next hover or focus shows it again; an Escape that closes the menu and hands focus back does not, until the next hover. A focus inside the control's own open menu bubbles to the wrapper and is ignored: the menu says what the control does.
- It closes the moment the pointer leaves, with no wait, and fades out over `$icon-tip-fade` (120ms, not every other tip's 500ms). It also closes on Escape or blur. Escape is heard on the document and marked handled while a tip is open, so it closes the tip alone: inside a modal the first Escape shuts the tip and the next reaches the modal.
- The bubble takes no pointer (`pointer-events: none`), against WCAG 1.4.13's clause that hover content be hoverable. The tip repeats one word the control already says to a sighted reader by its shape and to assistive technology by its name, it is `aria-hidden`, and a tip that held while the pointer crossed onto it would cover the row beside the control for no gain. Dismissible (Escape) and persistent (it stays while the pointer rests on the control) still hold.
- It is in the page only while open and for its fade after, so an open tip is one that exists.

**Accessibility**

- The tip is decoration. The control's own name already says Edit and what. No control lists the tip in `aria-describedby`, since it would read "Edit" a second time. A link stays a link and a button a button, so tests and e2e specs find them by their names.
- A status mark's meaning that the tip would carry alone is also written out of sight as the button's description (the stock badge's Low statement, [`stock-badge.md`](stock-badge.md)), since the bubble is hidden from a screen reader.

**Placement**

- **Side (default): to the left of the control, level with it.** Every Edit stands at the end of its line, so the room to its left is the line's own. Not above, as InfoTip's: a `.data-table-frame` scrolls sideways, and a box that scrolls one way clips both ways, so a tip above the first row was cut off. The tip covers the text beside the control while open.
- **In a table row: centred above the control** (`.data-table .icon-tip__bubble`), so it covers nothing of the row's own. Above the first row is the header row, inside the frame, so nothing is clipped. A phone card (a `.data-table--stack` table under `$table-stack-width`) keeps the tip to the left, since above the first card's top-right Edit is only the card's padding and the frame would cut it off. The placement is a flat property on the wrapper, not JavaScript's guess, because only the container query knows which the table is showing.
- **Below (`placement="below"`): its right edge on the control's**, for a mark at the right end of a row with rows beneath it. The account menu's invitation count uses it ([`account-menu.md`](account-menu.md)), where a tip to the left would cover the row's words and one centred under it would run past the menu panel's edge. The panel does not clip, so the tip can hang past it. The spell preview's Screen Awake eye uses it too: it stands at the top edge of a full-screen pane, where a tip beside it fell level with a phone's camera cutout.
- **Above (`placement="above"`): centred over the control**, for a control in a row of its kind, a segment of the radio strip, where a tip to the side would cover the next segment and one below would cover the chosen name printed under the bar.
- **The admin pages' shared Add** (`add-action.tsx`): the tip is its label ("Add Category"), drawn only at the page header's narrow width where the label is hidden and the link is a plus alone, and on a tabbed page at or under 680px of the header's own width; never at or under 350px (`$add-below-width`), where the Add is labelled again ([`page-header.md`](page-header.md), "Styling"). It stands over the plus at `layer('floating')`. The action's wrapper carries no `translate` (it is brought down to the heading's letters with `position: relative; top`): a `translate` makes the wrapper a stacking context, which would rank the tip's layer only inside it and hold the tip under the shell's bar once scrolled.

**Controls built on it** (shared by the coven cards' foot, the entry page's icons and the Quick Look window, so the places cannot differ)

- `RecordButton` (tipped "Record a Working", named "Record a Working of" and the spell), `BinButton` (tipped with its verb, "Delete Ingredient" or "Remove"), `AnnotateButton` (where a press asks which coven first), `QuickLookButton`, `PreviewButton` (named "Preview Spell <title>") and `MoreMenu` (a [`MenuButton`](menu-button.md) of flat items or labelled sections, named "More for <entry>").
- `PreviewButton` wears `icon-tip__edit--plain`, the text colour, as the page's pencil does; `RecordButton` keeps the accent green. Print is an item of the page's `MoreMenu`, not a button, except as the recipe page's lone Print for a role whose menu would hold nothing else.
- `CompendiumEditLink` is named and tipped "Edit in Compendium" so it cannot be taken for a coven's own choice.
- `ViewLink` takes `EditLinkProps` without `name` and is named and tipped View alone, with the open `LiddedEyeIcon` in the `.icon-tip__look` box: a link to the reader page of the row an editor is open on.
- A Delete or Remove of something that has an edit dialog lives in that dialog and is not drawn beside More. Only a thing with no edit dialog draws a lone bin (`.icon-tip__remove`), which asks first ([`.claude/rules/components.md`](../../.claude/rules/components.md), "Destructive controls"). A More drawn with words keeps its chevron. The coven card's `CardMore` draws `DotsIcon` in a `MenuButton` tipped "More" where the card holds two or more choices.
- **Edit Stock** ([`stock-dialog.md`](stock-dialog.md)) wears `StockIcon`, a jar with a cloth tied over its mouth, not the pencil, which Edit beside it already wears. It is first in the foot, tipped "Edit Stock" through `ModalButton`'s `tip` so the tip wraps the button and never the dialog, and named "Edit Stock, <ingredient>". Edit and Annotate beside it wear `icon-tip__edit--plain`, passed as `className` by the coven card's foot only. Staged removal in the dialog uses `BinButton` (verb "Remove", named by the form).
- **Annotate** (`AnnotateLink`) wears `AnnotateIcon`, not Edit's pencil, since it writes the coven's notes over a compendium entry.
- **Add and held on a compendium card** (`AddIcon`, `HeldMark`): a button tipped and named "Add to My Ingredients" and then the entry, `.add-icon` (styles in [`add-icon.md`](add-icon.md)). `.add-icon--held` is a status mark tipped and named "In Every Cupboard" and then the entry, on a button that does nothing else (so the keyboard reaches its tip; `opensOnPress`), at the card's bottom right ahead of a curator's pencil. The add button shows the shared spinner in the plus's place while it works.
- **A beta tester on the admin Support page** ([`support-requests.md`](support-requests.md)): `SproutIcon` after the sender's name, on a button tipped and named "Beta Tester" that does nothing else (`opensOnPress`); the Sender filter's Beta Testers option shows it too.
- **A jar line's stock badge** (`StockBadge`) is tipped "Low Stock at 10 g or less" or "Out of Stock": an icon alone is an icon tip, not an InfoTip.
- **Quick Look on entry lists**: left of each name on the admin Compendium list, and at each card's bottom left on a coven's Ingredients and the public compendium. `.icon-tip__look` is Edit's box in accent ink with the eye drawn a third bigger than the pencil, so a card foot's height and the eye's line with the pencil do not move. `size="small"` is the mark at `$glyph-size` in a `$icon-size` (24px) box, for the spell builder's step rows and inventory lines and the Add Ingredient form's duplicates.
- **The admin lists' Edit**: `GroupList`, `GroupedValueList` (every value page) and `CompendiumList`, each through `EditLink`.
- **The ingredient form's three Edit buttons**, each wrapped in `IconTip` around its existing button: a variant's pencil and a reference row's pencil ("Edit"), and an open reference row's Edit Source ("Edit Source"), drawn as a page with a pencil (`SourceEditIcon`, the form's own `icons.tsx`) so it does not look like the row's pencil ([`ingredient-form.md`](ingredient-form.md), "Editing a source").
- **Each segment of a radio strip** (`RadioGroupField`'s `layout="strip"`) is tipped with its option's label, above, because the form group's Icon picker draws icons alone. The segment is a `label` round a hidden native radio, which keeps its own name, so the tip stays decoration ([`field.md`](field.md)).

**Icons**

- Every line icon on a 16-unit box is drawn at stroke 1.5 unless its drawing needs another, and then the icon's comment says why. The exceptions are the eye's outline at 1, the jar at 1.2, and Annotate and the cauldron at 1.25. Icons on a 24-unit box (the section nav's and the form icons, at 1.6) and the shelf mark on a 32-unit box (1.2) keep their own strokes.
- The undo and redo icons are mirror images on one box, sized by attribute since a bare `svg` in a `.btn` has no size. `CompleteIcon` is the jar shelf's round flask (read from `SHAPES.flask`) with a tick on its bulb.

## Styling

Tokens and three mixins in `_mixins.scss` (`icon-button`, `icon-button-remove`, `icon-button-edit`), so every Edit matches.

- **`icon-button`**: a button takes the shared focus ring; an icon link keeps the accent ring the typography gives every link, so the rule names buttons alone, through `:where(button)`, to keep its weight a class's. The default 1.5rem box around a 1rem mark is WCAG 2.2's 24px target.
- **Sizes**: the ingredient form keeps the 24px box and 1rem icon beside its fields' text. A list row's `EditLink` (`.icon-tip__edit`) and a lone bin take `$icon-size-large` around `$glyph-size-large`. The action icons of a card's foot, an entry page's head and the Quick Look window's head are 13% larger (`$action-icon-size`); there the eye's mark is 11% past the pencil's and Edit Stock's jar 11% past the others, each in the same box. The same classes in a list row, table or form stay at `$icon-size-large`. Each place that works a gap out from the box (the stock lines' badge inset, the entry's title room, the window's controls row) reads `$action-icon-size` too.
- **Ink**: a coven card foot's Edit and Annotate use `$text-primary` (pure white would vanish on a light card) in place of the accent green, which its Edit Stock pencil keeps; the hover and focus tint stays the pencil's. The Edit pencil beside Complete on a draft working's row (`EditButton`'s `plain`) uses `$text-primary` while Complete keeps the green. The three dots use `$card-more-ink` wherever they stand. The compendium card's Edit in Compendium wears the same ink (`icon-tip__edit--ink`), its focus ring the link's own; the foot's eye takes it too, set after the green so it wins.
- **Contrast**: the accent on the page, on the card (banded rows, stacked cards, the modal) and on its own tint each clear 3:1, a control's floor (`tests/scss/tokens.test.ts`, "an Edit pencil").
- **The bubble** is the `tip-bubble` mixin's, InfoTip's look, fixed so a table frame's sideways scroll clips nothing. The wrapper is middle-aligned, so a mark taller than its row's text sits level with it, not on its baseline.
- **At phone width** a list's Edit cell carries `.data-table__card-action`, standing it at its card's top right ([`../styling.md`](../styling.md), "Tables at phone width").
- **Forced colours.** An open button (`aria-expanded`) takes a `Highlight` fill with `HighlightText` ink, which the pencil and eye take from `icon-button-edit`; the ink rules for the dots, the plain pencil and the eye repeat it.
