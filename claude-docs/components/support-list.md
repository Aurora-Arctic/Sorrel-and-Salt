# SupportList

`src/components/SupportList/` — the sender's own requests on `/account/support`, newest first, as a compact list of two-line rows. Line 1 is the **subject**, the row's one link, stretched over the row (`card-link`) and cut with an ellipsis on one line. Line 2 is the meta row, in this order: the status badge, the kind (as the form names it), the sent day (`30 Oct 2026`) and the reply count ("1 reply", "2 replies" or "No reply yet"), the last three muted. The row shows no page path. It never draws a link to a bug's issue: the address is staff-only and the sender's reads do not carry it. The page passes only rows the services returned for the signed-in sender, with each row's count read from `repliesForRequests`; the component filters nothing. Paged with the shared pager.

## Contracts

- **The status badge** is `statusBadgeClass` from `SupportRequests/status-badge.ts`, the one the admin list and dialog use: `open` is `badge badge--local`, `waiting_on_sender` is `badge badge--caution`, and `resolved` and `closed` are `badge badge--muted`. The words are `STATUS_LABELS`.
- **A row opens its request** in a dialog over the list, a `RouteModal` at `?request=<id>` that keeps the page's cursor, with the same `listed` size and phone sheet as the staff dialog. The page reads the request through `getMySupportRequest` and `repliesForRequests` (`account/support/reads.ts`, cached), so another sender's id reads as absent and the dialog stays closed. Closing it drops only `request`. The body is [`SupportSenderRequest`](support-sender-request.md).
- **The page is the Support Requests tab** of the account page (`/account/support`, last in `ACCOUNT_TABS`), inside the tab layout, so it has no heading or frame of its own.
- **The head line** has a muted count line at the left, in the small size: `<n> Open Requests / <n> Closed Requests` (singular at 1, `No Requests Yet` with none). Open counts the statuses open and waiting on the sender, closed counts resolved and closed, over all the sender's requests and not the page, from `countMySupportRequestsByStatus` (the sender's own session; `findOwnSupportStatusCounts` ANDs `created_by`). **New Request** is at the right: a [`SupportLink`](support-link.md) styled `btn btn--quiet btn--small`, borderless and on the page's ground at rest with the page ink, the support glyph inline at the text's size before the words. The line is flex, space-between and centred, and the count line wraps beneath the button only when they cannot share it. The gap below the line is the column's space(4). On hover and focus it takes the quiet button's border and fill; the icon's cut-out takes the button's ground (the page at rest, the quiet fill on hover and focus).
- **A row is lit like an ingredient card** on hover (pointers that can hover) and focus: `$surface-card-hover` and `$shadow-lifted`. Rows sit `space(3)` apart, the list's gap the one contributor and the row margin zero.
- **Spacing.** The list is a column with a gap of 4 between the rows' list and the pager; the rows are 2 apart. Each row is 3 above and below its two lines, which are 2 apart, and 4 either side. The meta row's items are 3 apart and, when they wrap on a phone, 1 between lines. The `ul` default margin, the empty message's paragraph margin and the row's margin are zeroed, and the row's `::before` (the site's list marker) is emptied; no global `section` is used. The dialog is outside the flow.

## Loading

The tab's wait is the [`Skeleton`](skeleton.md) `requests` shape, set by the tab's `shape` in `ACCOUNT_TABS`: the head line (a short muted-line bar and a small button box), then three two-line rows at the list's padding and gap, so nothing moves when the list arrives.
