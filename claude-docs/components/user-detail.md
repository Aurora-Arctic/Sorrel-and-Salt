# UserDetail

`src/components/UserDetail/` is the body of the modal that opens over `/keepers/users` when a keeper presses a user's name, and over `/keepers/support` (`?user=<id>`, on top of an open request) when a keeper presses a sender's name ([`support-requests.md`](support-requests.md)). It shows the facts the users table holds about the user, read-only, then the covens they belong to, then the support time-out and site access panels, and, where impersonation is registered, Impersonate. It is a render-only server component: the page reads the memberships through the coven service and builds the modal around it, and this draws only the body.

## Contracts

- **Laid out as the ingredient modal is, not as a table.** There are two sections, Account and Covens, each under a small title in the heading face and parted by hairlines, with Impersonate under the last one, where the other modals stand their actions. A table at a modal's width stacks into cards that repeat every label on every coven.
- **The facts come first**, as a `<dl>`: Email, Role, Coven Creation, Sign-In Methods, Terms (the version agreed to most recently, or a red cross read as "Not agreed") and Signed Up. The address is not repeated: the heading carries it.
- **The role and creation controls sit beside the facts they change, at the right edge.** Change Role is beside Role, and Approve or Revoke of coven creation beside Coven Creation (none for a curator or a keeper, who each hold the flag). This modal is the one place a keeper changes either: the users table's rows hold the facts alone, so a row is an index into this modal.
  - They are `UserList`'s `RoleControl` and `CreationControl`, keyed by their action, and locked by `changesPaused` while keeper changes are paused.
  - With `inModal`, `ConfirmedAction` asks over this modal's body through `ConfirmOverlay`, its warning and Reason field included, rather than opening a second modal. A success reads the page again, so the modal shows the new value and the other action.
  - A refusal is the callout under the control (`refusalInModal="callout"`). Escape closes it and leaves the modal open.
- **The marks are the list's own.** Sign-In Methods is `SignInMarks`, the Logins column's marks, Email & Password and Passkey included ([`user-list.md`](user-list.md)); it reads None only when the user has neither a provider nor a passkey. `SignInMarks` lives in `UserList/marks.tsx` beside the table's `Mark` and imports the list's stylesheet, so both draw the same. The check and cross are the list's icons in its `user-list__mark` colours. `PrimaryAdminMark` is `UserList/primary-admin-mark.tsx`.
- **A coven's name is text, never a link.** A keeper is no member of the covens they read about, and `/coven/[slug]` answers a non-member 404.
- **The covens are a list, ordered here**: stewards, then members, then observers, each by coven name, because the service's order is unspecified. The list is bounded by the user's own memberships. Each item is the coven's name, its role and the UTC day they joined. Role labels live in `MemberList/roles.ts`.
- **Support Time-Out.** When the page passes `timeouts`, a section after Covens and before Site Access holds the same `TimeoutPanel` as the support request dialog (card, ledger and form), keyed by the user. It is a wrapper over `PausePanel`, with the fields, end and ledger the site access pause's parts (`access-pause-parts.tsx`; [`support.md`, "The support feature, revised after review"](../design-decisions/support.md#the-support-feature-revised-after-review), "Open-Ended Time-Outs"). The page reads them with `readTimeoutsOf`, which returns nothing for a non-keeper.
  - **This is the one place the ledger of earlier time-outs is drawn**, titled Earlier Time-Outs, and the request dialog links here instead. The lifter shown is the row's `updated_by`, the lift being the only write after insert.
- **Message to User.** The support time-out form and Site Access each have a Message to User field after Note (Coven Access has Message to Member), drawn by `PauseFields`. It is required on the time-out form and Site Access, as Note is, and optional on Coven Access. It is limited to 500 characters and sent as `message`, trimmed (null when a blank Coven Access message is left out). The email carries it and the end date, never the note.
- **Site Access.** When the page passes `pauses`, a section after Support Time-Out and before Impersonate holds `AccessPausePanel` (`access-pause-panel.tsx`). It is a thin wrapper giving the site's words and writes to `PausePanel` (`pause-panel.tsx`), which the member modal's Coven Access is too ([`member-detail.md`](member-detail.md), "Coven Access"). The panel's fields, end and Earlier Pauses list are drawn from `access-pause-parts.tsx`, its shared words are `PAUSE_WORDS` there, and a wrapper overrides any of them in its `PausePanelCopy`; the styles stay in `SupportRequests/timeout-panel.scss`.
  - **With no pause standing** it shows a form, and Pause Access asks before it sends `pauseUserAccess` (`until` is null for No End Date, the note trimmed). It is held while Until a Date has no future day, by `pauseReady`, the rule the member modal's Pause shares, and while the note is blank. `AccessPausePanel` passes `noteRequired` through `PausePanel` to `PauseFields`; the member modal's Coven Access leaves it off.
  - **With a pause standing** it shows a card with Lift Pause, which asks before it sends `liftUserAccessPause`. Pauses lifted or run out are listed beneath as Earlier Pauses.
  - **Who is offered it.** The page (`readOpenedUser`) reads the newest 25 through `listUserAccessPauses` and passes none for the primary keeper, for the admin's own account and for a non-keeper, so the action is absent there ([`auth/admin-bootstrap.md`](../auth/admin-bootstrap.md), "Pausing a user's access").
- **Impersonate shows only for a non-keeper, where `canImpersonate`**, the list's own rule: Better Auth refuses a keeper target whatever this shows.
- **It asks first inside the modal**, through `ConfirmQuestion` ([`modal.md`](modal.md)) rather than `ConfirmedAction`, which would open a second modal on top. `impersonate-action.tsx` is the one client file. The wording, landing and send are `UserList/impersonation.tsx`, shared with the list's row button, so the two cannot drift.
