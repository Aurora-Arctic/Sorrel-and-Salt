# UserDetail

`src/components/UserDetail/` is the body of the modal that opens over `/keepers/users` when a keeper presses a user's name, and over `/keepers/support` (`?user=<id>`, on top of an open request) when a keeper presses a sender's name ([`support-requests.md`](support-requests.md)). It shows the facts the users table holds about the user, read-only, then the covens they belong to, then the support time-out and site access panels, and, where impersonation is registered, Impersonate. It is a render-only server component: the page reads the memberships through the coven service and builds the modal around it, and this draws only the body. Props are in `types.ts` (`UserDetailProps`). `user` is a `Pick` of the list's `UserListEntry`, `memberships` are the user's live covens in any order, `pauses` are the access pauses newest first, and `canImpersonate` is off by default.

## Contracts

- **Laid out as the ingredient modal is, not as a table.** There are two sections, Account and Covens, each under a small title in the heading face and parted by hairlines, with Impersonate under the last one, where the other modals stand their actions. A table at a modal's width stacks into cards that repeat every label on every coven.
- **The facts come first**, as a `<dl>` with the terms in one column and their values in the next: Email (verified or not, the word with the check or cross to its right), Role (bold for a keeper or a curator, with the primary-admin crown), Coven Creation (Yes or No, the mark to its right), Sign-In Methods, Terms (the version agreed to most recently, or a red cross read as "Not agreed" by a screen reader) and Signed Up. The address itself is not repeated: the heading carries it, under the name.
- **The role and creation controls sit beside the facts they change, at the right edge.** Change Role is beside Role, and Approve or Revoke of coven creation beside Coven Creation (none for a curator or a keeper, who each hold the flag). This modal is the one place a keeper changes either: the users table's rows hold the facts alone, so a row is an index into this modal.
  - They are `UserList`'s `RoleControl` and `CreationControl`, keyed by their action, and locked by `changesPaused` while keeper changes are paused.
  - With `inModal`, `ConfirmedAction` asks over this modal's body through `ConfirmOverlay`, its warning and Reason field included, rather than opening a second modal. A success reads the page again, so the modal shows the new value and the other action.
  - A refusal is the callout under the control, with its badge, as on the row, rather than a notice in the modal (`refusalInModal="callout"`). It floats over the rows so nothing below moves, and Escape closes it and leaves the modal open.
- **The marks are the list's own.** `ProviderLogo` lives in `UserList/marks.tsx` beside the table's `Mark` and imports the list's stylesheet, so both draw the same. The check and cross are the list's icons in its `user-list__mark` colours. `PrimaryAdminMark` is `UserList/primary-admin-mark.tsx`.
- **A coven's name is text, never a link.** A keeper is no member of the covens they read about, and `/coven/[slug]` answers a non-member 404.
- **The covens are a list, ordered here**: stewards, then members, then observers, each by coven name, because the service's order is unspecified. The list is bounded by the user's own memberships. Each item is the coven's name, then its role and the day they joined (a `<time>` with the UTC date, as the list's Signed Up is) in the muted ink at the caption size. Role labels live in `roles.ts`. With no memberships a sentence stands in place of the list.
- **Support Time-Out.** When the page passes `timeouts`, a section after Covens and before Site Access holds the same `TimeoutPanel` as the support request dialog (card, ledger and form), keyed by the user. The form offers No End Date beside the dated spans and marks Note required, and the card says "with no end date" for an open-ended one. Its fields, end and ledger are the site access pause's parts (`access-pause-parts.tsx`; [`../design-decisions/m15-support-review.md`](../design-decisions/m15-support-review.md), "Open-Ended Time-Outs"). The page reads them with `readTimeoutsOf`, which returns nothing for a non-keeper.
  - **This is the one place the ledger of earlier time-outs is drawn**, titled Earlier Time-Outs, and the request dialog links here instead. Each entry shows the day it was set and its end day (or "No End Date"), a "Lifted {date}" badge where lifted, the note, then "Set by {name}" and, where lifted, " · Lifted by {name}". The lifter is the row's `updated_by`, the lift being the only write after insert. There is no list marker.
- **Message to User.** The support time-out form and Site Access each have a Message to User field after Note (Coven Access has Message to Member), drawn by `PauseFields`. It is required on the time-out form and Site Access, as Note is, and optional on Coven Access. Pause Access and Time Out Sender stay held until it has text. It says "Emailed to them, up to 500 characters." beneath it and is sent as `message`, trimmed (null when a blank Coven Access message is left out). The email carries it and the end date, never the note.
- **Site Access.** When the page passes `pauses`, a section after Support Time-Out and before Impersonate holds `AccessPausePanel` (`access-pause-panel.tsx`). It is a thin wrapper giving the site's words and writes to `PausePanel` (`pause-panel.tsx`), which the member modal's Coven Access is too ([`member-detail.md`](member-detail.md), "Coven Access"). The panel is laid out on the time-out panel's classes, its fields, end and Earlier Pauses list drawn from `access-pause-parts.tsx`.
  - **With no pause standing** it shows a form: For (1 Day, 1 Week, 1 Month, Until a Date, No End Date), Until when a date is chosen, and Note. Pause Access asks before it sends `pauseUserAccess` (`until` is null for No End Date, the note trimmed). It is held while Until a Date has no future day, by `pauseReady`, the rule the member modal's Pause shares, and while the note is blank. `AccessPausePanel` passes `noteRequired` to `PausePanel`, which hands it to `PauseFields`; the member modal's Coven Access leaves it off, so a coven pause's note stays optional.
  - **With a pause standing** it shows a card: a Paused badge, the end or "with no end date", the note, who set it and when, and Lift Pause, which asks before it sends `liftUserAccessPause`. Pauses lifted or run out are listed beneath as Earlier Pauses, each with its days, the note, "Set by {name}" and, where lifted, " · Lifted by {name}".
  - **Who is offered it.** The page (`readOpenedUser`) reads the newest 25 through `listUserAccessPauses` and passes none for the primary keeper, for the admin's own account and for a non-keeper, so the action is absent there ([`auth/admin-bootstrap.md`](../auth/admin-bootstrap.md), "Pausing a user's access").
- **Impersonate shows only for a non-keeper, where `canImpersonate`**, the list's own rule: Better Auth refuses a keeper target whatever this shows.
- **It asks first inside the modal**, through `ConfirmQuestion` ([`modal.md`](modal.md)) rather than `ConfirmedAction`, which would open a second modal on top. `impersonate-action.tsx` is the one client file. The wording, landing and send are `UserList/impersonation.tsx`, shared with the list's row button, so the two cannot drift. A success does a full load of `/`, the question busy until it opens. A refusal closes the question and shows an error notice.
- **Tokens only**: spacing from `space()`, and the button row is the page's own `btn btn--destructive`.

## Not here

Stories: [`index.stories.tsx`](../../src/components/UserDetail/index.stories.tsx), `Admin / User Detail`, on invented people. `States` chooses Default (three covens, each role once), No Covens, Several Providers, Admin (the primary keeper, with no Impersonate), Curator, Other Admin, Without Impersonation, Changes Paused, No Providers, May Create Covens, Access Open and Access Paused. `InAModal` draws the detail inside a `Modal` in its own iframe, so Impersonate's question draws over it. Impersonate reaches Better Auth, which the workshop does not serve: the question can be seen, its answer cannot.
