# ChipColorField

`src/components/ChipColorField/` sets one of a category group's two chip colours, for [`GroupForm`](group-form.md). It is Ark UI's colour picker, inline: an area of saturation against brightness or lightness, a hue slider, and the channels of a format the keeper chooses (HSB, HSL or RGB), then the hex box the form validates after a divider, and a Match button. The picker is kept in step with the hex box. Over the area it veils the colours that fall short of 4.5:1 on the field's own ground. Beneath it are a sample chip on that ground, the ratio, and a warning when another group's colour in the same theme stands too close. The ground is the dark card for `colorDark` and the light page for `colorLight`, the harder of each theme's two surfaces. Ark is chosen for its built-in format switch, with `@colordx/core` for colour distance ([`ingredients.md`, "The admin group pages: where a deleted group's rows go"](../design-decisions/ingredients.md#the-admin-group-pages-where-a-deleted-groups-rows-go), "The picker, and a warning for a colour too close").

## Contracts

- **The hex box is the field.** It is the control the label names (`id`) and the one a refusal describes. The picker is a second way to set it: a drag, a hue, or a channel typed and committed with Enter or by leaving it writes the colour's lower-cased hex into the box. A hex typed is kept as typed; the schema lower-cases it on save.
- **The hex is tidied when the box is left.** `hexColour` (`src/lib/contrast.ts`) turns a loose hex, such as ` 4E8BC2` or `abc`, into `#4e8bc2` or `#aabbcc`; text it cannot read stays as typed for the schema to refuse. The box stays a raw `<input>` because `PlainTextField` draws a label and an error of its own and takes neither this `id`, `ref` nor the ratio's description. The same function runs in the server schema.
- **A drag is one undo step.** The picker writes the hex on every pointer move; the form's history joins them while the pointer is held ([`field.md`](field.md#undo-and-redo)).
- **The picker keeps its own colour.** It re-reads the hex only when the hex is whole and differs from its own, so half a hex leaves it be, and a drag through the grey edge of the area keeps its hue rather than snapping to the red a hex would give a grey.
- **The parts sit inside `ColorPicker.Content`.** Inline, the content is always open, and the picker finds its channel inputs there to keep them in step with its value; outside it, they would show the colour they started with.
- **The format is the keeper's, toggled as Chrome toggles it.** Each press of the format button moves to the next format: HSB, HSL, RGB and round again.
  - Its accessible name says both formats: "Dark Theme Colour Format: HSB. Switch to HSL".
  - Ark's tooltip under it uses the site's `tip-bubble`, and stays open through a press. A closed tooltip stays laid out, overriding Ark's `hidden`, so the fade can run.
  - The area follows the format: lightness for HSL, brightness otherwise, as Ark lays it.
- **The veil marks the floor on the colour's own ground.** `failingRegion` (`src/lib/group-colors.ts`) is the area's low-contrast side at the current hue, in the area's own space: below the 4.5:1 boundary on the dark card, where dark colours vanish, above it on the light page, where pale ones do.
  - The field clips a layer of the ground's own colour to it at 60%, hatched, so a failing colour visibly sinks into its ground, with the boundary as a fine solid line.
  - A label sits inside the veil at `failingLabelPoint`, moving out only where the veil is too shallow to hold it.
  - A `.field__hint` describes the area.
  - A hue with nothing failing draws no veil, line, label or hint. The floor is still the schema's: the veil shows it, never enforces it.
- **Match sets the colour from its partner.** "Match Light" on the dark field and "Match Dark" on the light field, named in full for a screen reader as "Match Light Theme Colour" and "Match Dark Theme Colour", which begin with what they show. It sets the colour to `pairedColor` of the other, the same rule the form's auto-fill uses. The button is disabled until `partner` is a whole hex.
- **The channels and the hex share one line at every width.** Under 30rem of the field's own width (a container query), the gaps close, the figures drop to caption size and the captions to single letters, so the values still show whole.
- **The ratio is shown, never enforced, here.** For a whole hex, a `.field__hint` reads `4.64:1 on the dark card` (or `on the light page`). It is cut to two places by `formatRatio`, as the refusal is, so a failing 4.499 never reads 4.50. Its id joins the box's `aria-describedby` after any error. The ratio and the refusal measure with the same `chipContrast` (`src/lib/contrast.ts`).
- **A refusal of the colour is an error notice in the field.** `error` and `errorId`, which GroupForm passes from its own form state, show the schema's or the server's refusal (the contrast floor missed, or the pair's hues too far apart) in the site's `.notice.notice--error`, above any near-colour warning. Its id is the one the hex box's `aria-describedby` already names. The markup is written by hand rather than through [`Notice`](notice.md), whose error carries `role="alert"`: the box already reads it as its description, so an alert would have it read twice.
- **A colour too near another group's is warned of, never refused.** When the hex lies within `NEAR_COLOR_DELTA` (0.05 in OKLab, through `colorDistance` in `src/lib/near-color.ts`, kept out of `group-colors.ts` so the schema reaches only zod) of another group's colour in the same theme (`others`), a [`Notice`](notice.md) in the site's `.notice.notice--warn` (an `<output>`, a live region by its tag) names the nearest and says the save still goes through. Its id joins the box's description. The element is always rendered, empty and hidden when there is nothing to say, so a warning that appears is announced. Save stays as it was, because two close groups may be meant.
- **The hex box is masked while typed.** `hexMask` (`src/lib/contrast.ts`, through `useMask`; see [Field](field.md), "Masks") keeps one leading `#`, drops spaces, and keeps a non-hex character on screen so the refusal can name it. When only the `#` is left the box is emptied, so Backspace clears it and the caret is never trapped behind the `#`. Case and `#abc` expansion stay with `hexColour` on blur.
- **The sample shows the full chip level on the colour's own ground**, whatever the page's theme, because the full level is the only one a category wears. The ground is wrapped in the `theme-dark` or `theme-light` mixin, so `light-dark()` in `chip()` picks this colour. Both halves of the chip's pair are set to it. The sample is `aria-hidden`: the ratio says what it shows.

## Styling

The stylesheet holds the picker's own layout and geometry, each length on no scale commented where it is set; everything else is a token.

- Both grounds lift off the modal on a drop shadow in their own theme, which sets the dark card apart from a dark modal whose surface it shares.
- Each ground is `var(--surface-card)` under `theme-dark` or `var(--surface-page)` under `theme-light`, since that surface is what the ratio is measured on.
