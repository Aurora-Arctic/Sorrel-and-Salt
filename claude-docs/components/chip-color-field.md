# ChipColorField

`src/components/ChipColorField/` is one of a category group's two chip colours (M5.6b), used by [`GroupForm`](group-form.md). It is Ark UI's colour picker, inline: an area of saturation against brightness or lightness, a hue slider, and the channels of a format the admin chooses, HSB, HSL or RGB, then the hex box the form validates after a divider, and a Match button. Over the area it veils the colours that fall short of 4.5:1 on the field's own ground, edged with a fine line and labelled "◐ < 4.5:1" inside. Beneath them are a sample chip on that ground, the ratio the colour reads there, and a warning when another group's colour in the same theme stands too close. The ground is the dark card for `colorDark` and the light page for `colorLight`, the harder of each theme's two surfaces (MB.36).

Ark was the owner's choice of picker, for its built-in format switch, with `@colordx/core` for the colour distance ([`m5.6b-admin-groups.md`](../design-decisions/m5.6b-admin-groups.md), "The picker, and a warning for a colour too close").

## Props

`ChipColorFieldProps` (`types.ts`):

| Prop                                                            | What it is                                                                                    |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `id`                                                            | The hex box's id, which the form's `<label>` names                                            |
| `label`                                                         | The field's name, which also names the picker's parts: "Dark Theme Colour hue"                |
| `column`                                                        | `colorDark` or `colorLight`: which ground the veil, the sample and the ratio use              |
| `value`, `onChange`, `onBlur`                                   | The hex as typed, whole or not, and its handlers, which react-hook-form's `Controller` passes |
| `sample`                                                        | The sample chip's text                                                                        |
| `others`                                                        | Every other group's colour in this theme, `{ name, hex }`, which the warning compares against |
| `partner`                                                       | The pair's other colour as typed, which Match sets this one from                              |
| `inputRef`, `aria-describedby`, `aria-invalid`, `aria-required` | Passed to the hex box                                                                         |

## Contracts

- **The hex box is the field.** It is the control the label names and the one a refusal describes. The picker is a second way to set it: a drag, a hue, or a channel typed and committed with Enter or by leaving it writes the colour's lower-cased hex into the box. A hex typed is kept as typed; the schema lower-cases it on save.
- **The picker keeps its own colour.** It re-reads the hex only when the hex is whole and differs from its own, so half a hex leaves it be, and a drag through the grey edge of the area keeps its hue rather than snapping to the red a hex would give a grey.
- **The parts sit inside `ColorPicker.Content`.** Inline, the content is always open, and the picker finds its channel inputs there to keep them in step with its value; outside it, they would show the colour they started with.
- **The format is the admin's, toggled as Chrome toggles it** (the owner's calls). Each channel's caption sits under its box and centred, as Chrome sets them: hue, saturation and brightness; hue, saturation and lightness; or red, green and blue. Under the inputs, across the three channels' full span, a button shows the current format at its left and Chrome's up-and-down chevrons at its right edge, as a select draws its arrow, with Match beside it under the hex; each press moves to the next, HSB, HSL, RGB and round again. Its accessible name says both, "Dark Theme Colour format: HSB. Switch to HSL", and Ark's tooltip under it, in the site's `tip-bubble`, InfoTip's, fading in and out as InfoTip's does (a closed tooltip stays laid out, overriding Ark's `hidden`, so the fade can run), says "Format: HSB. Press for HSL." on hover and on keyboard focus, staying open through a press. The area follows the format too, laid out in lightness for HSL and in brightness otherwise, as Ark lays it.
- **The veil marks the floor on the colour's own ground** (the owner's calls). `failingRegion` (`src/lib/group-colors.ts`) is the area's low-contrast side at the current hue, in the area's own space: below the 4.5:1 boundary on the dark card, where dark colours vanish, above it on the light page, where pale ones do. The field clips a layer of the ground's own colour to it at 60%, finely hatched a shade off the ground in its own direction (darker on the dark card, lighter on the light page), so a failing colour visibly sinks into its ground, and draws the boundary over it as a fine solid line, iron-gall on the dark card's veil and chalk on the light page's. A label, the half-filled contrast glyph and "< 4.5:1", sits inside the veil at `failingLabelPoint`: across at the veiled side's own centre of area, down at the middle of the veil in that column, moving out only where the veil is too shallow to hold it. A `.field__hint` says "Colours in the dark veil fall short of 4.5:1 on the dark card" (or "the light page"), and the area is described by it. A hue with nothing failing draws no veil, line, label or hint. The floor is still the schema's: the veil shows it, never enforces it.
- **Match sets the colour from its partner.** "Match Light" on the dark field and "Match Dark" on the light field, named in full for a screen reader as "Match Light Theme Colour" and "Match Dark Theme Colour", which begin with what they show, set the colour to `pairedColor` of the other, the same rule the form's auto-fill uses. The button sits on its own line directly under the hex, at the hex's width, and is disabled until the partner is a whole hex.
- **The channels and the hex share one line at every width.** The controls are one grid: three channel columns sharing what the divider and a fixed-width hex column leave, captions under their boxes, then the format button across the channel columns and Match in the hex's. Under 30rem of the field's own width, a container query, the gaps close, the figures drop to caption size and the captions to Chrome's single letters, H, S and B, so the values still show whole.
- **The ratio is shown, never enforced, here.** For a whole hex, a `.field__hint` reads `4.64:1 on the dark card` (or `on the light page`). At the floor or over it the line turns the theme's accent green (the owner's call); under it, it keeps the hint's colour. It is cut to two places by `formatRatio`, as the refusal is, so a failing 4.499 never reads 4.50. Its id joins the box's `aria-describedby` after any error. The ratio and the refusal measure with the same `chipContrast` (`src/lib/contrast.ts`).
- **A refusal of the colour is an error notice in the field.** `error` and `errorId`, which GroupForm passes from its own form state, show the schema's or the server's refusal, the contrast floor missed or the pair's hues too far apart, in the site's `.notice.notice--error`, which leads with its warning triangle, above any near-colour warning (the owner's call). Its id is the one the hex box's `aria-describedby` already names.
- **A colour too near another group's is warned of, never refused.** When the hex lies within `NEAR_COLOR_DELTA` (0.05 in OKLab, through `colorDistance`) of another group's colour in the same theme, an `<output>`, a live region by its tag, in the site's `.notice.notice--warn`, the plain box with the red triangle, seen but short of an error since the save still goes through (the owner's call), names the nearest: `Close to the dark theme colour of "Wellbeing", so their chips may be hard to tell apart.` Its id joins the box's description. The element is always rendered, empty and hidden when there is nothing to say, so a warning that appears is announced. Save stays as it was: two close groups may be meant.
- **The sample shows both chip states on the colour's own ground**, whatever the page's theme. The ground is wrapped in the `theme-dark` or `theme-light` mixin, so `light-dark()` in `chip()` picks this colour. Both halves of the chip's pair are set to it. The sample is `aria-hidden`: the ratio says what it shows.

## Styling

Layout and tokens only before MB.115, bar the grounds, the veil and its label. The area is 9rem tall at the field's width, with a crosshair cursor; the label is a body-size line on a `$shadow-dark` pill, so it reads over any colour; the thumbs are rings in `$chalk` with a `$shadow-dark` edge, the hue thumb centred on its track; the edge line is 1.5px with `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke`, so it keeps its width however the area stretches. The samples are centred on their ground. Both grounds keep `space(4)` inside, `space(5)` above and `space(2)` below, close over the ratio that captions them, and both lift off the modal on a drop shadow in their own theme, which is what sets the dark card apart from a dark modal whose surface it shares. The ratio is centred under its panel (the owner's calls). Each ground is `var(--surface-card)` under `theme-dark` or `var(--surface-page)` under `theme-light`, since that surface is what the ratio is measured on.

## Stories

[`index.stories.tsx`](../../src/components/ChipColorField/index.stories.tsx) has four stories: `DarkTheme` and `LightTheme` on a seeded pair, `UnderTheFloor` (a hex fit for the light page, read on the dark card), and `NearAnotherGroup`, a shade off another group's colour.

## Testing

`tests/components/ChipColorField/index.test.tsx` covers the hex box, the picker's named parts, the three formats and their channels, a channel committed by Enter and by leaving it, the channels following a whole hex and not half of one, the veil in each ground's colour with its label, edge and hint, Match from the partner and waiting for a whole one, the ratio on each ground and in the box's description, no ratio for half a hex, the sample's text, the warning naming the nearest group, staying silent and following the colour, and a passed error described first. The picker answers a channel a moment after the key, so those two tests wait for it.
