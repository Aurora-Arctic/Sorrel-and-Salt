# CategoryPicker

`src/components/CategoryPicker/` — the categories picked from one box, as a list field's entries are: typing narrows the live categories, listed under their groups, and a pick is a chip inside the control in its group's colour. It is the ingredient form's Categories field, the spell builder's (`SpellBuilder/categories-field.tsx`), and the compendium list's Categories filter (`CompendiumList/filter.tsx`). A client component, for the box's text. Props are in `src/components/CategoryPicker/types.ts`.

## Contracts

- **It is the deities field's box**, not a wall of chips: the same [`Combobox`](combobox.md), its entries inside the control with their x and a Clear, the chevron to list everything, and the list under headings. What differs is that nothing typed is a category: there is no Add, no "Use what you typed" row (`offerTyped` off), and Enter with nothing highlighted adds only the one category the text names whole.
- **Data-free and controlled.** Ids in through `value`, ids out through `onChange`; no fetching and no react-hook-form, so the filter reuses it unchanged. A pick appends its id and empties the box, and the list stays open for the next, the focus in the box (`keepOpen`), in the Categories facet and in the ingredient form's categories field alike, since each pick adds a chip. Escape, Tab or a press outside closes it; an x, or Backspace in the empty box, takes an id out and keeps the rest in order; Clear takes them all. Each is announced through a labelled status region, "Categories changes", as a list field's are.
- **`boxName` is the box's `name` attribute**, which a form that moves the focus by field name (an undo) finds it by. `action` is a control at the legend's end, handed the picker's announcer (`LegendAction`): an ingredient variant's Copy From Ingredient.
- **The rows come from the categories.** The list offers every category not yet picked whose name or whose group's name holds the text, case set aside (typing a group lists all its categories), under its group's heading — the Combobox's `heading` on each row — the groups and the names both in the order the read gives them (alphabetical in SQL), which the picker keeps without sorting. No code names a group, so one an admin adds is a heading without a change here. A category's description reads beneath its row, and each row is marked by a bar in its group's colour, the Combobox option's `colors`, so the colour a pick's chip will wear is seen before the pick.
- **An entry's colour is its group's row.** Each pick is a `ComboboxEntry` given its group as `colors`, so it wears the row's `color` through `chipColors` as a solid fill (claude-docs/styling.md, "Chips, badges and the solid-fill rule"). Never a Sass token by slug. Its tooltip reads the name with its group in brackets on its first line, the entry's `qualifier`, as a picked deity reads with its tradition, since the colour alone cannot name the group, and its description beneath, the entry's `detail` — "Testward (Wards & Fixtures)" over "Keeps a fixture from harm." — both read as its x's description. The chip itself reads the bare name.
- **An error is the box's, and names its entries.** While `errorId` is set the box is `aria-invalid` and described by the error, so the form's focus after a failed save lands on it, as on every list field; each id in `invalid` gives its entry the `errorId`, which edges the chip and describes its x. The `status` (the categories could not be read) is read with the box the same way, and marks nothing invalid.
- **A pick the categories do not name** — an edit's, arriving before the read, or one since retired — waits while `pending`, and is then drawn by its id, uncoloured, so that it can be taken out. While `pending` the box says it is looking and shows the Combobox's spinner with `aria-busy`.
- **The fieldset is named by the legend's text alone** (`aria-labelledby`, as `ListField` does, so the tip's button stays out of the name).
- **Counts are the filter's.** A category with a `count` (how many entries picking it would leave) reads "Testward (4)" on its row and on its chip, name and count together. With `hideZeros` a zero-count row is left out of the list; a category already picked keeps its chip, so it can always be removed. Without `hideZeros`, zero rows stay under their group, in the order given: the read already sorts them alphabetically.
- **While `loading`, a chip draws a placeholder in the count's place**, hidden from assistive technology, and its x is named by `removeNoun` the same whether or not the count has arrived: "Remove Category: Testward" with a noun, "Remove Testward" without.
- **Styling is layout only**: the legend's row with its tip, and the legend, the entries and the box a step further apart than a single field's label and control. The box, its entries and its list are the Combobox's; an entry's colour is the `is-coloured` entry's. The chips wrap inside the control, so a 375px screen scrolls nothing sideways.
