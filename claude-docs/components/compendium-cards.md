# CompendiumCards

`src/components/CompendiumCards/` is the public compendium's list: the search and its filters, one `IngredientCard` per entry in an `IngredientCardList`, and the pager. A page holds `CARD_PAGE_SIZE` (24) entries, which the page asks its read for, so the grid ends on a full row at one to four columns. It is the card-shaped sibling of the keeper's `CompendiumList` table. Both are render-only: the page reads one page of entries under the address's filter and hands it over. The filter props are the admin list's ([`compendium-list.md`](compendium-list.md)).

## Contracts

- Each card is a plain link to `/compendium/ingredients/<slug>`: a press anywhere on the card off its controls opens the entry, the gaps between the foot's buttons and the New or Updated mark included (the stretched link is [`ingredient-card.md`](ingredient-card.md), "The whole card is the link"; proved in `tests/e2e/card-click.spec.ts`). The +N button, Quick Look, the plus or check, Edit in Compendium and the dots keep their own action and never open the entry. Text on the card cannot be selected by dragging, since the drag starts on the link. Two entries sharing a label are told apart by the formal-name line the card draws.
- The list is regular density with no Compact switch: the public compendium shows full cards and the coven's Ingredients list shows compact ones. A page on its way draws the list's own skeleton, `CompendiumListBodySkeleton` (`CompendiumList/skeleton.tsx`).
- The filter is `CompendiumListFilter audience="public"`: no Classification facet and no No Refs or No Form control, which are the curators' to-do lists. `?withoutReferences=1` and `?withoutForms=1` are dropped before the address is read.
- The foot is up to three icons at the right end (`actions`), in this order: the plus or check, a curator's pencil (Edit in Compendium), the three dots. Each shows only where the viewer may do it. The left end (`startActions`) is the Quick Look eye, for everyone, which opens the entry's details modal over the results. Where each sits is layout, so no test asserts it.
- `action: 'none'`, or `add` with no `workspaceId`, draws neither the plus nor the check: an account that only views and a visitor with no account, who signs in through the header.
- Each `CompendiumCardEntry` carries `recency` (`new`, `updated` or null), worked out by the page with `entryRecency` and shown by `EntryMarks`.

### The plus and the check

The plus is `AddIcon` (`src/components/AddIcon/`, a client component shared with the entry page, so the cards stay a server component); its behaviour is in [`add-icon.md`](add-icon.md), "Contracts". What the cards add:

- **The plus** shows for an entry not held, with `add` and a `workspaceId`. With one writable coven (`covens` of length one or none) it adds there directly, with no stock; a form and a quantity are given afterwards in Edit Stock ([`stock-dialog.md`](stock-dialog.md)).
- **The menu.** With two or more writable covens the plus is always a [`MenuButton`](menu-button.md) of every one by name, however many hold the entry: the plus adds at once only where there is one coven, not one coven left. A coven in `heldIn` is a disabled item (`aria-disabled`, still reached by the arrow keys, doing nothing when chosen) with a green check, named "<coven>, in its cupboard" (`mark`), so the menu keeps its shape as covens take the entry. Choosing a coven adds at once, with no question. The coven just added to counts as holding the entry, a disabled item, before the page has been read again. A refusal is said in a callout under the plus and focus returns to the plus, since the item that had it went with the menu.
- The entry pages (public and a coven's) and the Quick Look window draw the same `AddIcon` just left of the pencil, with `HeldMark` in its place once every coven that may add holds the entry ([`ingredient-entry.md`](ingredient-entry.md), "On the public page", "The plus", "The details modal").
- **The held mark.** For an entry every writable coven holds (`held`), the card draws `HeldMark` (`held-mark.tsx`): a status mark, not a link. It is a button only to carry its tip, which opens on hover, on focus (WCAG 2.1.1) and on a press, as InfoTip's ⓘ does, so a tap shows it on touch ([`icon-tip.md`](icon-tip.md), "Behaviour"). The press does nothing else. The details modal's In Your Cupboards list carries the links to each coven's copy.
- **Names.** The plus and the check carry the entry as the card names it (`cardName`: its name, then its formal name) after their words, in the accessible name and the visible tip alike: **Add to My Ingredients: Testwort, Formalis testis** and **In Every Cupboard: Testwort, Formalis testis**. Without it a screen reader's list of buttons reads the same name once per card. The tip says the whole name, so the words on screen are the name (WCAG 2.5.3). The colon is there because a formal name holds a comma of its own. A refusal's badge follows the name: Show the error for Add to My Ingredients: <entry>. The entry pages and the Quick Look window pass their own name for the entry to `PlusMark`.
- **Cupboard** is the compendium's one noun for "you hold this", said four ways (`AddIcon/wording.ts`): **In Every Cupboard** on the card's check, **In Your Cupboard** on the entry page's shelf, **<coven>, in its cupboard** for a held coven in the plus's menu, and **In Your Cupboards**, the title of the details modal's list of holding covens, with **See in Your Cupboard** for its link to a coven's page.
- **The holding count.** The page asks `heldCompendiumEntryIdsIn` over every membership, observers included, not only the writable ones, bar one whose seat is paused (its read is refused, which would blank the count for every coven), and gives each entry `holding`, the names of the holding covens in the switcher's order. `held` and `heldIn` still count the writable covens alone, so the check and the menu's disabled items are unchanged. The page asks nothing when the viewer has no coven they may write in. `HoldingCount` (`AddIcon/holding.tsx`) is the number at the mark's top right, `aria-hidden` since the name says it in words, nothing at zero; `holdingText` adds ". In 2 covens: Hearthside, Moonwell" (". In 1 coven: Hearthside") to the accessible name and the tip alike.

### The three dots

- `CompendiumCardMore` (`card-more.tsx`) is the shared `MoreMenu` ([`icon-tip.md`](icon-tip.md)), drawn through `choicesFor` ([`menu-button.md`](menu-button.md), "Sections"): **Edit Stock** first, then **Annotate**, **Make It Ours** and **Remove**, each from the dialogs the entry page uses. Remove is here rather than a bin of its own, so the foot stays at three icons; a remove leaves the viewer on the list (the Quick Look window passes `leaveHref`).
- The add-to-coven choice its dialogs ask for (`IngredientEntry/coven-choice.tsx`) is not in the page's first scripts. It starts loading once the page is idle and when More is pointed at, focused or pressed, before the menu opens ([`../design-decisions/graphql.md`](../design-decisions/graphql.md), "The date field, the sortable list and the coven choice load apart").
- A choice shows only where the viewer's role in a holding coven permits it, and the dots show only when they would hold at least one; the service still checks on the write. The page reads the roles per request (`card-places.ts`, `readCardCovens` and `cardPlacesOf`); none of it enters the cached compendium reads (rule 6). `card-places.ts` derives Annotate (`ingredient:update`), Make It Ours and Remove (`ingredient:delete`) and Edit Stock (`inventory:update`) from the role, so an observer has no dots and a practitioner or steward has all four.
- Edit Stock shows for a coven that holds the entry and lets the viewer update stock (`CardCoven.canStock`). `src/app/entry-stock.ts` reads the forms and jars for the viewer, one `stockOf` and one `variantsOf` call for the page and the open Quick Look entry, outside the cached compendium read (rule 6); a coven the service refuses is left without Edit Stock. The coven's own Ingredients page does not gain this item. The item is words only, because `MenuButtonItem` draws no icon; the owner wants the coven card's `StockIcon` on it, which is open (an optional `icon` on the menu item drawing the existing glyph unchanged would do it).
- Edit in Compendium is the icon beside the dots, so `choicesFor`'s Compendium section is empty and left out; the menu is one **Coven** section, labelled though it is alone. It has no bin, unlike the coven card's `CardMore`, because a lone choice cannot arise from the roles above; were one to, the app-wide rule that a lone choice is its own icon would apply, and nothing is built for that.
- A keeper or curator has the pencil, and the dots only if they are also a member of a holding coven. The Quick Look window mirrors the cards.

### Edit in Compendium

A `CompendiumEditLink` sits at the right when the entry carries `editHref` and `editHeading`. The page gives them to a curator alone.

## The page

`src/app/(public)/compendium/page.tsx` draws the list per request over the cached, sessionless reads in `src/app/compendium-reads.ts`, which the admin page shares, so every card and link is in the server HTML, keyed by the address alone, whoever asks (rule 6).

- **Action and coven.** `add` when any membership `canWrite` (the per-role statement in `viewerFor`, never a comparison of role names). `workspaceId` is the first such membership in the switcher's order: `/compendium` names no coven in its address, so the page takes the choice Add Ingredient makes for a page without one (`destinationsOf`).
- **Held.** `heldCompendiumEntryIdsIn` reads `workspace_compendium_entries` once per coven, scoped by that coven's membership proof. It is per viewer and sits beside the cached reads, never inside them. A visitor, an account that only views and one in no coven are asked nothing and get neither `held` nor `heldIn`. A refusal reads as nothing held, so the plus shows and the add itself says no.
- **A curator's Edit.** `?edit=<slug>` on the search's own address opens the admin Compendium's edit dialog (`CompendiumDialog`, `path="/compendium"`) over the results; close or a save comes back to the same search, query and page kept. `?edit=` is read only once the viewer is known to curate, through the admin page's `readEditPlace` and `readCompendiumEntry`: an old slug redirects to the new one, and a slug naming nothing shows `MissingNotice`. For anyone else it opens and reads nothing.
- **Quick Look.** `?entry=<id>` is read by `readCompendiumDetails` with no session ([`ingredient-entry.md`](ingredient-entry.md), "The details modal"). An id that names no compendium entry, a coven's own included, opens nothing and says nothing, so the address tells nobody a coven's entry exists. On a coven's Ingredients, a member's own entry shows the page's Edit menu in its details instead (Edit Ingredient and Delete Ingredient); a delete there closes the details onto the list with the row gone.
- **Indexing.** `generateMetadata` marks a filtered view `noindex` with the canonical address `/compendium`. On production the proxy marks a pager page (`?after=`, `?before=`) and the list with Quick Look open (`?entry=`) `noindex, follow` by header, and the page leaves the list's JSON-LD off those addresses as it does off a filtered view.
- **In My Covens.** The page passes `myCovens`, every coven the viewer belongs to, an observer's included, in the switcher's order ([`compendium-list.md`](compendium-list.md), "In My Covens"); a paused one is listed disabled, saying when the pause ends ([`facet-panel.md`](facet-panel.md), `covens`). A visitor and an account in no coven get none, and the field is not drawn.
  - With no coven in the address and no `covensNot`, the page reads the cached `readCompendium` and `readFacets`. Otherwise it reads the session and calls `readCompendiumHeldIn` and `readFacetsHeldIn`, which prove each `covenIds` entry by membership and drop any the viewer is not in, so a foreign coven's id edited into the address filters nothing; with no id proved they are the cached reads.
  - Under Not In (`covensNot=1`) the list holds the entries none of the proved covens holds; with no coven in the address the proved set is every coven the viewer belongs to. If every named coven is dropped, the coven filter applies nothing under Not In as under In.
  - The narrowed reads are per request and never cached (rule 6); the coven condition is part of the list's SQL (rule 7), so the facet counts count only the narrowed entries. The field shows no count per coven, because those would be per viewer.
  - "Held" means a live `workspace_compendium_entries` row and nothing else. Stock adds nothing: `createStock` and `saveStock` insert the row beside a coven's first jar of a compendium form, and `removeCupboardEntry` refuses while the coven holds a jar of it, so a coven with stock of an entry always holds it.

## Styling

The cards have no stylesheet of their own: the card is [`IngredientCard`](ingredient-card.md)'s, the grid [`IngredientCardList`](ingredient-card-list.md)'s, the plus and the check [`AddIcon`](add-icon.md)'s. `index.tsx` imports `CompendiumList/index.scss`, because the filter and the empty sentence are `CompendiumList`'s own.
