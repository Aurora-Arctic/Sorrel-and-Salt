# CompendiumCards

`src/components/CompendiumCards/` is the public compendium's list (M8.18; MB.80): the search and its filters, one `IngredientCard` per entry in an `IngredientCardList`, and the pager beneath. A page holds `CARD_PAGE_SIZE` (24) entries, which the page asks its read for, so the grid ends on a full row at one to four columns. It is the card-shaped sibling of the admin's `CompendiumList` table. Both are render-only: the page reads one page of entries under the address's filter and hands it over.

## Props

| Prop                                                    | What it does                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `entries`                                               | One `CompendiumCardEntry` per card: name, slug, formal name, categories, forms, safety notes                    |
| `filter`, `facets`, `categories`, `vocabulary`, `total` | The same filter state the admin list takes; see [`compendium-list.md`](compendium-list.md)                      |
| `previousHref`, `nextHref`, `position`                  | The pager's links and "Page X of Y"                                                                             |
| `action`                                                | The foot of each card: `add` or `none`                                                                          |
| `workspaceId`                                           | The default coven, which the plus adds to; `add` with none draws no plus and no check                           |
| `covens`                                                | The covens the viewer may write in, as `{ id, name }`, for the menu when there are two or more                  |
| `myCovens`                                              | Every coven the viewer is a member of, viewers included, as `{ id, name }`: In My Covens offers them (M12.55)   |
| `entries[].holding`                                     | The names of the viewer's covens that hold the entry, any role: the count and tip on the plus or check (M12.55) |
| `entries[].held`                                        | Every writable coven holds the entry, and the check shows (M9.12)                                               |
| `entries[].heldIn`                                      | The ids of the writable covens that hold the entry, which the menu shows disabled                               |
| `entries[].editHref`, `entries[].editHeading`           | A curator's Edit in Compendium on the card, and the head of the dialog it opens (M8.17)                         |
| `entries[].places`                                      | The covens that hold the entry and let the viewer do something about it, for the three dots                     |

## Contracts

- Each card is a plain link to `/compendium/ingredients/<slug>`: a press anywhere on the card off its controls opens the entry, the gaps between the foot's buttons and the New or Updated mark included (MB.272; the stretched link and what passes a click through to it are [`ingredient-card.md`](ingredient-card.md), "The whole card is the link"). The +N button, Quick Look, the plus or check, Edit in Compendium and the dots keep their own action and never open the entry. Text on the card cannot be selected by dragging, since the drag starts on the link. Proved in `tests/e2e/card-click.spec.ts`. The Quick Look eye at its bottom left, when the entry carries `details`, opens the entry's details modal over the results instead (M12.5). Two entries sharing a label are told apart by the formal-name line the card draws.
- The list is regular density, never compact. That is deliberate (the owner’s call, 2026-10-20): the public compendium shows full cards and the coven’s Ingredients list shows compact ones, so the public compendium has no Compact switch. It sits in `ListFrame` through `IngredientSearch shape="cards"`, so a page on its way draws `ListBody shape="cards"`.
- The filter is `CompendiumListFilter audience="public"`: no Classification facet and no No Refs or No Form control. Those are the curators' to-do lists ([`compendium-list.md`](compendium-list.md)).
- The foot of a card is one row, chosen by the page from the visitor. Up to three icons share the card's right end (`actions` on `IngredientCard`), in this order (the owner's call, 2026-10-20): the plus or check, a curator's pencil (Edit in Compendium), and the three dots (More, below). Each shows only where the viewer may do it, and a card with none has no foot on the right. The left end (`startActions`) is left to the Quick Look eye (M12.5). Where each sits is layout, so no test asserts it.
  - `add` with a `workspaceId` draws, for an entry not held, a **plus** (`PlusIcon`) button tipped and named Add to My Ingredients and then the entry (`AddIcon`, `src/components/AddIcon/`, a client component shared with the entry page; the cards stay a server component). With one writable coven at all (`covens` of length one or none) it adds there directly, with no stock (the owner's call of 2026-10-21 retired the quantity dialog M9.12's second PR was to put behind it; a form and a quantity are given afterwards in Edit Stock, [`stock-dialog.md`](stock-dialog.md)): it sends `addCupboardEntry(workspaceId, ingredientId)` through `/api/graphql`, then reads the page again, which draws the check in its place. It is busy (the shared spinner in the icon's place, `aria-busy`) from the press until the page has been read again, and uses `aria-disabled` rather than `disabled` so the focus stays; a refusal is said in a callout under it ([`click-feedback.md`](click-feedback.md), M12.31). With two or more writable covens it is always a menu instead (below), however many hold the entry: the owner's call of 2026-10-20 that the plus adds at once only where there is one coven, not one coven left. For an entry every writable coven holds (`held`), it draws a **check** (`CheckIcon`, `held-mark.tsx`'s `HeldMark`): a status mark, not a link, the owner’s call of 2026-10-20. It is tipped and named In Every Cupboard and then the entry (MB.120, the owner’s call of 2026-10-20): a button there to carry its tip, as the entry page’s shelf is: the tip opens on hover, on focus, so the keyboard reaches it (WCAG 2.1.1), and on a press, as InfoTip’s ⓘ does, so a tap shows it on touch (the owner’s call, 2026-10-20; [`icon-tip.md`](icon-tip.md), "Behaviour"). The press does nothing else. The details modal’s In Your Cupboards list carries the links to each coven’s copy.
  - **The menu** (the owner's call, 2026-10-20, over the modal picker it first had): with two or more writable covens, the plus is a standard [`MenuButton`](menu-button.md) with the plus as its `icon`, with the same tip and name, which name its menu too. Its items are every writable coven, by name; a coven in `heldIn` is shown, not left off, as a disabled item (`aria-disabled`, still reached by the arrow keys, doing nothing when chosen) with a green check after its name and named <coven>, in its cupboard ([`menu-button.md`](menu-button.md), `mark`; the owner's call, 2026-10-20, in place of the note Already in My Ingredients; the words MB.120's, "Names" below). The menu therefore keeps its shape as covens take the entry. Choosing one sends `addCupboardEntry` for that coven at once, with no question after it. The button is busy, its name Adding, from the choice until the page has been read again; a refusal is said in the same callout under the plus, and the focus is handed back to the plus, since the item that had it went with the menu. The entry pages, public and a coven's alike, draw the same `AddIcon` just left of the pencil, the same menu, animation and announcement, and `HeldMark` in its place once every coven that may add holds the entry, and so does the Quick Look window beside its pencil (the owner's calls, 2026-10-20; [`ingredient-entry.md`](ingredient-entry.md), "On the public page", "The plus", "The details modal"). Tests: `tests/components/CompendiumCards/add-icon.test.tsx`; e2e: `tests/e2e/compendium-add.spec.ts`.
  - **After an add** (the owner's call, 2026-10-20): when the add succeeds, the spinner in the plus draws into a check, which stays for `ADDED_HOLD_MS` (1s) and then cross-fades back to the plus (`useFade`, [`click-feedback.md`](click-feedback.md)). The check also stays while the page is still being read again. If the add left every writable coven holding the entry, the check stays and does not fade, since the read again draws `HeldMark` in its place. A refusal shows no check. The success is said once through the page's status region, "Added to <coven>.", and the button's name stays the same throughout. While the check shows, the button is `aria-disabled` and does nothing when pressed. Its `data-state` is `added` while the check holds and `returning` during the fade; the motion is `$arrive-duration` and none under reduced motion, which swaps the glyphs at once. The coven just added to counts as holding the entry, a disabled item in the menu, before the page has been read again.
  - **Names** (MB.120). The plus and the check carry the entry, as the card names it (`cardName`: its name, then its formal name), after their words, in the accessible name and the visible tip alike: **Add to My Ingredients: Testwort, Formalis testis** and **In Every Cupboard: Testwort, Formalis testis**. Without it a screen reader's list of buttons read the same name once per card, while the pencil and the dots beside them already said whose they were (Edit in Compendium <entry>, More for <entry>). The tip says the whole name, so the words on screen are the name (WCAG 2.5.3, Label in Name), and a sighted reader sees which entry the mark is for, as a screen reader hears it (the owner's call, 2026-10-20, that the tip says it too). The colon is there because a formal name holds a comma of its own: "Add Testwort, Formalis testis to My Ingredients" reads the formal name as the place. A refusal's badge follows the name, Show the error for Add to My Ingredients: <entry>. The **cupboard** is the compendium's one noun for "you hold this" (the owner's call, 2026-10-20), said four ways for where it stands: **In Every Cupboard** on the card's check, which speaks for every coven the viewer may add in; **In Your Cupboard** on the entry page's shelf; **<coven>, in its cupboard** for a held coven in the plus's menu, which follows one coven's name; and **In Your Cupboards**, the title of the details modal's list of the covens that hold the entry, with **See in Your Cupboard** for its link to a coven's page. The entry pages and the Quick Look window pass their own name for the entry to `PlusMark`, the one the icons beside it use. Story: `In Several Covens`. Tests: `tests/components/AddIcon/index.test.tsx`, and the cards' in `tests/components/CompendiumCards/index.test.tsx`.
  - `none`, or `add` with no `workspaceId`, draws neither, for an account that only views and for a visitor with no account, who signs in through the header (the owner's call, 2026-10-19).
  - **The three dots** (`card-more.tsx`, `CompendiumCardMore`; the owner's call, 2026-10-20) are the shared `MoreMenu` ([`icon-tip.md`](icon-tip.md)), named More for <entry>, drawn through `choicesFor` ([`menu-button.md`](menu-button.md), "Sections"), which always draws the labelled sections, the Compendium side first where it has anything. They hold what the viewer can do about the entry in the covens that hold it, each from the same dialogs the entry page uses: **Edit Stock** first (M9.12, the owner's call, 2026-10-21), then **Annotate** (a link to the entry's page with that coven's dialog open where one coven applies, a coven picker where several do), **Make It Ours** and **Remove**. Edit Stock opens M9.9's dialog ([`stock-dialog.md`](stock-dialog.md)) as a modal (`StockDialogModal`) at once where one coven applies, and asks which coven first (`edit-stock.tsx`, `EditStock`) where several do. It shows for a coven that holds the entry and lets the viewer update stock (`rolePermits(role, { inventory: ['update'] })`; `CardCoven.canStock`), and the place carries the forms and jars (`CompendiumCardPlace.stock`). `src/app/entry-stock.ts` reads them for the viewer, one `stockOf` and one `variantsOf` call for the whole page and the open Quick Look entry, outside the cached compendium read (rule 6); a coven the service refuses is left without Edit Stock. The item is words only, because `MenuButtonItem` draws no icon; the owner asked that it wear the coven card's `StockIcon` pencil, and that is open: an optional `icon` on the menu item drawing the existing glyph unchanged would do it. A save reads the page again, so the card and the check show the new stock. The coven's own Ingredients page does not gain this item. A choice shows only where the viewer's role in a holding coven permits it (`MAKE_IT_OURS_PERMISSION` for Make It Ours; the service still checks on the write), and the dots show only when they would hold at least one. The page reads the roles per request (`card-places.ts`, `readCardCovens` and `cardPlacesOf`) and hands each card its `places`; none of it enters the cached compendium reads (rule 6). Remove is here rather than a bin of its own, so the foot stays at three icons; a remove leaves the viewer on the list, and a refusal is said in a callout under the dots. There is no Compendium-side choice yet, since Edit in Compendium is the icon beside the dots, so the Compendium section is left out and the menu is one **Coven** section, labelled though it is alone (the owner's call, 2026-10-20). The Quick Look window mirrors the cards, so its dots are drawn the same way. Unlike the coven card's `CardMore`, this one has no bin, because a lone choice cannot arise today: `card-places.ts` derives Annotate (`ingredient:update`), Make It Ours (`MAKE_IT_OURS_PERMISSION`) and Remove (`ingredient:delete`) from the viewer's role, and a viewer has none of them while a member or an owner has all three, so the dots hold four choices with Edit Stock (`inventory:update`, which a member and an owner have and a viewer lacks) or do not show. Were a future role to leave only one, it would follow the app-wide rule that a lone choice is its own icon, not a menu of one; nothing is built for that yet.
    - **What the dots hold, by viewer:** a visitor with no account has no plus, no pencil and no dots, only the Quick Look eye. An account in no coven, a member of no coven that holds the entry, or a viewer (who writes nothing) has no dots. An owner or member of a coven that holds the entry has Edit Stock, Annotate, Make It Ours and Remove. An admin or curator has the pencil, and the dots only if they are also a member of a holding coven.
  - Edit, a `CompendiumEditLink` (the entry page's, named Edit in Compendium, then the entry's name and formal name), sits at the right when the entry carries an `editHref` and an `editHeading` (M8.17). The page gives them to a curator alone.
- With no entries it draws the filter's empty sentence (`emptyText`) instead of a list.

## The page

`src/app/(public)/compendium/page.tsx` draws it per request over the cached reads in `src/app/compendium-reads.ts`, which the admin page shares. The reads take no session, so every card and link is in the server HTML. The page asks for the viewer to choose `action` and the coven: `add` when any of the viewer's coven memberships `canWrite` (the per-role statement in `viewerFor`, never a comparison of role names), `none` otherwise, with no session included. The coven, passed as `workspaceId`, is the first such membership in the switcher's order: `/compendium` names no coven in its address, so the page takes M8.16's choice for a page without one (`destinationsOf`).

**Held (M9.12).** For every coven the viewer may write in the page also asks which of the page's entries each already holds, through `heldCompendiumEntryIdsIn` (the ingredients module), which runs `heldCompendiumEntryIds` once per coven, one after another: each is one read of `workspace_compendium_entries` for the page's ids, scoped by that coven's membership proof. It gives each entry `held` (every writable coven holds it) and `heldIn`. The read is per viewer and sits beside the cached ones, never inside them (rule 6). A visitor, an account that only views and one in no coven are asked nothing and get neither field. A refusal reads as nothing held, so the plus shows and the add itself says no.

**A curator's Edit (M8.17).** An admin or curator (`canCurate`) gets Edit on each card: the search's own address with `?edit=<slug>`, which opens the admin Compendium's edit dialog (`CompendiumDialog`, given `path="/compendium"`) over the results, headed Edit Compendium Entry. Close or a save comes back to the same search, the query and page kept, inside a `RouteModalProvider` so a press opens the dialog at once. `?edit=` is read only once the viewer is known to curate, through the admin page's own `readEditPlace` and `readCompendiumEntry`: an old slug redirects to the new one, and a slug naming nothing shows `MissingNotice` over the list. For a visitor or any other account there is no Edit, and `?edit=` opens nothing and reads nothing. The list's reads are unchanged, cached and sessionless, keyed by the address alone, whoever asks (rule 6).

**Quick Look (M12.5).** Every card, for anyone, a visitor with no account included, carries `details`: the search's own address with `?entry=<id>`, which opens the entry's details modal over the results ([`ingredient-entry.md`](ingredient-entry.md), "The details modal"), read by `readCompendiumDetails` with no session. An id that names no compendium entry, a coven's own included, opens nothing and says nothing: the page is the page without it, so the address tells nobody a coven's entry exists. The window mirrors the card it was opened from (the owner's call, 2026-10-20): the same plus or check, Edit in Compendium and three dots, in that order, from the same page data (`more` and `editAction.inCompendium` on the opened entry). A curator's Edit pressed inside the details opens the edit dialog over them. On a coven's Ingredients, a member's own entry shows the page's Edit menu in its details instead (Edit Ingredient and Delete Ingredient); a delete there closes the details onto the list with the row gone.

`?withoutReferences=1` and `?withoutForms=1` are dropped before the address is read, so it lists what the unfiltered page lists. `generateMetadata` marks a filtered view `noindex` with the canonical address `/compendium`; the unfiltered list and its pages stay indexable.

**In My Covens (M12.55).** The page passes `myCovens`, every coven the viewer is a member of, a viewer's included, in the switcher's order, and the cards hand it to the filter, which draws In My Covens and its choice in the right half of the line Forms and Toxic hold ([`compendium-list.md`](compendium-list.md), "In My Covens"). A visitor and an account in no coven get none, and the field is not drawn. With no coven in the address and no `covensNot`, the page reads the cached `readCompendium` and `readFacets`, sessionless, exactly as before. With one or more, or with `covensNot=1` (Not In), it reads the session and calls `readCompendiumHeldIn` and `readFacetsHeldIn` (`src/app/compendium-reads.ts`), which pass `covenIds` to the ingredients module's `listCompendiumHeldIn`, `countCompendiumHeldIn` and `compendiumFacetsHeldIn`. Those prove each id by membership and drop any the viewer is not in, so a foreign coven's id, edited into the address, filters nothing; with no id proved they are the cached reads. Under Not In (`covensNot`) the list holds the entries none of the proved covens holds, and with no coven in the address at all the proved set is every coven the viewer is a member of, viewers included. If the address names covens and every one is dropped, the coven filter applies nothing under Not In as under In, so a foreign id never changes the result. The narrowed reads are per request and never cached (rule 6), and the coven condition is part of the list's SQL (rule 7), so the facet counts count only the narrowed entries. The field itself shows no count per coven: those would be per viewer, and the rest of the panel's counts come from the cached read (the owner's call, 2026-10-23). "Held" means a live `workspace_compendium_entries` row and nothing else. Stock adds nothing to that: `createStock` and `saveStock` insert the row beside a coven's first jar of a compendium form, and `removeCupboardEntry` refuses while the coven holds a jar of it, so a coven with stock of an entry always holds it.

## The holding count

The plus and the check show how many of the viewer's covens hold the entry (M12.55). The page asks `heldCompendiumEntryIdsIn` over every membership, viewers included, not only the writable ones, and gives each entry `holding`, the names of the covens whose cupboard holds it, in the switcher's order. `held` and `heldIn` still count the writable covens alone, so the check and the menu's disabled items are unchanged, and the menu still lists only the covens the viewer may write in. The page asks nothing when the viewer has no coven they may write in, since then there is no plus or check to carry the count.

`AddIcon` and `HeldMark` take `holding` and draw it through `src/components/AddIcon/holding.tsx`:

- **The badge.** `HoldingCount` draws the number at the mark's top right (`.add-icon__count`), `aria-hidden`, since the name says it in words. At zero it draws nothing.
- **The words.** `holdingText` adds ". In 2 covens: Hearthside, Moonwell" (". In 1 coven: Hearthside" for one) to the accessible name and the tip alike, after the entry's name, so the words on screen are the name (WCAG 2.5.3). At zero it adds nothing, and the tip is as before (the owner's call, 2026-10-23).

The badge is drawn with tokens only (`$font-body`, the caption size, weight 500) and has had no design review. Tests: `tests/components/AddIcon/index.test.tsx` ("the holding covens (M12.55)"), `tests/components/CompendiumCards/index.test.tsx` ("the viewer’s covens (M12.55)") and `tests/app/(public)/compendium/held.test.tsx` ("for the holding count and In My Covens").

## Styling

The cards have no stylesheet of their own. The card is [`IngredientCard`](ingredient-card.md)'s and the grid [`IngredientCardList`](ingredient-card-list.md)'s; the plus and the check are [`AddIcon`](add-icon.md)'s (`.add-icon`, `.add-icon--held`), the pencil and the dots [`IconTip`](icon-tip.md)'s. `index.tsx` imports `CompendiumList/index.scss`, because the filter and the empty sentence (`emptyText`, `.compendium-list__empty`) are [`CompendiumList`](compendium-list.md)'s own and bring their look with them (MB.120).

**On a phone** and **under forced colours**, the cards are what [`IngredientCard`](ingredient-card.md), [`IngredientCardList`](ingredient-card-list.md) and [`AddIcon`](add-icon.md) make them: one card across, and no rule of their own.
