# FacetPanel

`src/components/FacetPanel/` — the Filters button and the panel of facets it opens
(M12.4). The button reads "Filters (3)" once something is chosen, with a quiet Clear
All link beside it. The panel holds every facet of the compendium search: categories,
forms, celestial bodies, zodiac, deities, elements, toxic, and for an admin the No Refs and No Form switches and the Reviewed choice chip.
A client component. The layout was signed off in
[`m12.4-filter-layout`](../design-decisions/m12.4-filter-layout.md).

## Its parts (M10.11)

The panel is two parts, so a list with other facets can reuse them:

- **`FacetShell`** (`shell.tsx`, `FacetShellProps`): the Filters button with its count, the quiet Clear All, and the dialog with its head and foot (Apply Filters, Close, Clear All), every contract below on opening, closing and applying. Its facets are its `children`.
- **`CategoryLine`** (`category-line.tsx`, `CategoryLineProps`): the Categories line, the picker and its Match chip, which every list's filters share. Its `noun` ("an ingredient", "a spell") names what Match's tip counts. A choice without a `count` shows with no number, and is never hidden as a zero.

`FacetPanel` itself is the ingredient content inside `FacetShell`: the Categories line and every facet after it. The grimoire's filter is `FacetShell` holding `CategoryLine` alone, without counts ([`grimoire-list.md`](grimoire-list.md)).

The component returns a fragment: the Filters control, the Clear All link and the
dialog. The parent lays the first two out and must be `position: relative`, because
the dialog opens just under it. The parent also owns the `<form>`: the panel draws
fields and hidden inputs only, and its Apply Filters button is the form's submit.

## Contracts

- **Sort rule (M12.52).** The panel sorts nothing: every facet lists its values in the order the repository read gives, never by count. Names are alphabetical in natural-language order, categories under their group and deities under their tradition; signs and bodies by position; Toxic, Source and Elements are fixed. See `claude-docs/design-decisions/m12.52-facet-sort-order.md`.

- **Under a stuck row (2026-10-19).** The search row sticks to the top once scrolled to
  ([`ingredient-search.md`](ingredient-search.md)), and the panel opens inside its form, so it
  travels with the row and always opens under it. At the tablet width and up, an open panel
  taller than the room below the row is capped at the window's height less the offset and the
  row, and scrolls inside itself. The phone sheet is full height already and is unchanged.

- **Count.** `n` is the number of chosen values: every category, form, deity, zodiac
  sign, celestial body and element counts one, plus one for a Toxic setting other than Any and
  one each for No Refs and No Form when on. Two categories and two forms read 4. Match counts nothing:
  it is a mode of the categories, not a filter (owner, 2026-10-17).
- **Two ways to open.** The width is read when the button is pressed, against the
  shell's tablet width (`56rem`, the same as `$shell-tablet-width`). At that width and
  up it opens with `show()`: over the results, no scrim, the page still usable, sliding
  down by `useSlide` (`src/lib/use-slide.ts`; grid rows 0fr to 1fr, and reduced motion
  snaps). Below it, `showModal()`: a right-hand drawer with the browser's backdrop,
  inert page and Escape. The sheet slides in from the right edge and back out to it
  (`transform`, `$sheet-slide` in the component's scss), while the backdrop fades as a
  `Modal`'s does (`$modal-fade` and `$modal-scrim` in `_variables.scss`). In is CSS
  alone, `@starting-style` as `showModal()` shows it; out is `.is-closing`, which the
  component sets and waits on (the dialog's `transitionend` for `transform`, or a 400ms
  net) before it calls `close()`. The backdrop's `opacity` end reaches the dialog too, and
  is not the one it waits on. Reduced motion, or no `matchMedia`, closes at
  once, and so does the width crossing the tablet width.
- **Focus.** Opening focuses the dialog. Closing returns focus to the button when it
  was inside the panel.
- **Escape.** A non-modal panel closes on Escape, except an Escape that a combobox
  popup used (`defaultPrevented`), which closes only the popup. A modal sheet's Escape
  (`cancel`) is held back and taken through the slide; the dialog's `close` event, which
  the browser raises on any other close, snaps the state shut.
- **Held, then applied.** A change in the open panel goes to `onChange` and nowhere else, since the panel covers the results. Every shut calls `onApply` once, as it starts (a shut that follows a shut, or comes during the sheet's slide out, calls nothing). Apply Filters is the form's submit, which applies everything, so it only shuts the panel without calling `onApply` again. A modified click on a Clear All link (new tab) still follows the link.
- **A press outside shuts it.** While the non-modal panel is open, a `pointerdown` anywhere outside it (the search box, the page) shuts it and calls `onApply`, the same path as Escape. The Filters button and the bar's Clear All are left to their own handlers, so nothing applies twice. On the modal sheet the page is inert and the backdrop is the dialog's own box, so a press whose target is the dialog itself (not anything in it) shuts it. The sheet fills the screen below 32rem, so there is no backdrop to press on a narrow phone; Close and Escape remain.
- **Crossing the tablet width** closes the panel at once, since it would otherwise
  change from an overlay to a sheet under the person's hands.
- **No scrollbar while it slides (M12.46).** Once open, `.facet-panel__inner` scrolls (`overflow: auto`, capped to the room under the sticky row). The `--open` class arrives as the slide starts, though, and a box still shorter than its content showed a scrollbar for the whole opening. A `facet-panel-rolling` animation, as long as the slide (`--slide-ms`), holds `overflow: clip` and the scroll applies when it ends. Closing drops the class, so the box is clipped again from the first frame. Reduced motion has no slide and no animation.
- **The panel's top never moves while it opens.** Opening a dialog runs the browser's focus step, which scrolls its first control into view, and a box with `overflow: hidden` can be scrolled that way: while the panel was still short, its content slid up and then fell back as it grew. `.facet-panel__inner` is therefore `overflow: clip` (with `hidden` declared just before it as the fallback), which cannot scroll at all; `onScroll` on the clip still puts `scrollTop` back to 0 as a net. `tests/e2e/admin-compendium.spec.ts` samples the top every frame of a real slide.
- **Before hydration** the dialog renders with `open`, inline, at every width, with
  no Close, and the Filters control is a link to it. The controls submit through the
  surrounding GET form: each combobox as its native select, Match and Toxic as radios,
  No Refs as a checkbox valued `1` (the value the parameter table reads), and categories as
  a native `<select multiple name="categories">` in optgroups by group (`NativeCategories`;
  a zero-count option is left out unless chosen). Once hydrated, `CategoryPicker` replaces
  it and a chosen category travels as a hidden `categories` input. Once the script runs, React removes `open`, so the
  panel collapses. It is not seen opening first: the root layout's pre-paint script sets `data-js` on `<html>` before first paint, and `[data-js] .facet-panel--static` is `display: none`. Hidden is not removed, so the controls still submit if the form is sent before hydration. Without JavaScript the attribute never lands and the panel stays. The mark rides in `src/app/pre-paint-scripts.tsx` beside the theme script rather than in a mechanism of its own: it has the same job, setting something on `<html>` that first paint must already see, and the same rule for existing only in the server's HTML.
- **Source order is visual order**, with no CSS `order`. The rows are Categories with
  Match beside it; the small controls on one wrapping row (`.facet-panel__small`: Toxic, then Source, In Stock and Low Stock Only where the caller offers them, then No Refs for the admin); Forms, with room beside it for the next dropdown facet (nothing is drawn there until one exists); Celestial Bodies and Zodiac; Deities and Elements; then the foot. Each small control is there only where it is offered, tops level, and the row wraps on the phone sheet, which stacks the same order. Match always shows. With two or more categories it is enabled and its InfoTip says what All and Any mean; with fewer it rests on All (the held `categoryMode` is kept, and the address drops it below two, M8.12), draws dashed with no hover like the stuck Toxic, and its hint says Match applies once two or more categories are chosen. Match's hint has no ⓘ: it shows in the tip bubble when the pointer is on the chip, or its summary has focus while enabled (the owner's ask: a hover on the field, not beside it), and stays hidden while the chip's menu is open. Match and Toxic stand at the top of their neighbour's field and are a control's height: the group's own margin is zeroed in a row, the legend takes the label's weight, and the bar is a control tall. A tip in the panel opens below its label, not above as elsewhere (`.facet-panel { --tip-placement: bottom }`), and the panel is its `data-tip-boundary`, so `useTip` shifts a tip at the row's right-hand end back inside the panel. An e2e test checks the open tip lies inside the panel at 1280 and 375. A chip's tip is anchored on `.facet-chip__bar`, so it is placed against the chip. The chip's name ("Match:") is set at the caption size, smaller than its value.
- **The Reviewed chip (M14.8)** is the admin's: a `ChoiceChip` named `reviewed` with Any, Reviewed and Needs Review, on the small-controls row after No Form. It is drawn only when `admin` is set, `filter.reviewed` is defined (`''`, `'reviewed'` or `'needs_review'`) and `facets.reviewed` holds the counts, which the service gives a curator or an admin alone. Its radios submit the address's own spelling, `1` and `0`, so the form works before hydration. A chosen setting counts 1 on the Filters button. Like Toxic, when either count is 0 it rests on Any, is disabled, and its hint says every matching entry has the same review mark.
- **The coven's controls (M9.7)** are optional parts of the filter, shown only when the caller sets them: `filter.source` (`''` for both, `local` or `compendium`) draws the Source choice chip (All, Local and Compendium, radios named `source`), `filter.inStock` draws the In Stock switch, named `inStock` and valued `1` as No Refs is, and `filter.lowStockOnly` (M9.8) draws the Low Stock Only switch, named `lowStockOnly` and valued `1` the same way. They sit on the row with Forms and Toxic, and each chosen one counts 1 on the Filters button. A compendium panel sets neither and draws neither. The options show their counts from `facets.source`, `facets.inStock` and `facets.lowStockOnly`, both optional on `CompendiumFacetCounts`: a count missing leaves the label bare. `covenFacetCounts` (`CompendiumList/facets.ts`) maps a coven's answer to them. The coven's live counts come from `useLiveFacets` given a `workspaceId`. The parameter table that reads them is `COVEN_PARAMS` in `IngredientSearch/coven-params.ts`; the page that mounts them is the coven's Ingredients page ([`coven-cards.md`](coven-cards.md)).
- **The compact scale (M9.7).** Every input in the panel is shorter than the app's forms: `.facet-panel--compact` (always on) sets the height, in `--facet-control` (2.5rem, `$compact-control` in the panel's scss), on the comboboxes' boxes, native selects, choice chips and the switch's row; the rows' gap is space 3 rather than 4. Labels, option text and counts keep their size, colours and focus rings are the primitives' own, and every target stays over 24px (WCAG 2.5.8). Chips keep their size: they already fit a box this tall. The rules sit only under the panel, so the ingredient form and every other form keep `$control-height`.
- **Layout and the choice chip (M9.7).** Match stands to the right of Categories; Forms, Toxic, Source and In Stock (and No Refs on the admin panel) share the next line, and the compendium panels use the same arrangement. On the public compendium, where Toxic is the line's only chip, the line is halved from the tablet width up for a viewer with covens (the owner's calls, 2026-10-23): the left half holds Forms growing beside Toxic, and the right half In My Covens growing beside its In / Not In choice (each half a `.facet-panel__line` that grows as a `.facet-panel__grow`). With no covens there is no right half, and Forms and Toxic take the line as on the other panels. On a phone the line stacks Forms, Toxic, In My Covens. The admin and coven panels keep Forms growing beside their chips. Match, Toxic and Source are `ChoiceChip`s (`FacetPanel/choice-chip.tsx`): a pill reading "Match: All" that opens a menu of radios. It is a native `<details>`, so it opens before the script runs; once hydrated it fades in and out as a Modal does (`$modal-fade`, instant under reduced motion), closes on Escape, on a press outside and when focus leaves, and closes after a pick. Before hydration the pill's text does not follow a pick; the radios still submit. A chip that cannot narrow (Match below two categories, Toxic or Source when every entry shares one answer) is a disabled button with no radios, inside a disabled fieldset, so it takes no focus and submits nothing. Its hint (`chipHint`, no ⓘ) shows in the tip bubble when the pointer is on the chip: a disabled button may swallow mouse events, so the hover is taken on `.facet-chip__bar` around it (the disabled button has `pointer-events: none`), and the fieldset's `aria-describedby` is how assistive tech reads it. Match's hint when not stuck also shows on focus of its summary. Source's ordinary hint (not stuck) is still an InfoTip beside the chip. In Stock and Low Stock Only are the panel's own `FacetSwitch`es (`SwitchField` is untouched).
- **A chip menu is never clipped (M9.7).** The panel's slide needs its clip (`overflow: clip`), and a chip menu is wider than its chip, so a chip at the end of a row (Match, Toxic, No Refs, Source) opened its left-aligned menu past the panel's right edge and the clip cut it. Which chip ends a row changes with the panel (admin, coven, public) and the width, so CSS alone cannot say. `ChoiceChip` measures each time the menu opens: if the chip's left edge plus the menu's width passes the right edge of the nearest clipping ancestor, it adds `facet-chip__menu--end`, and the scss hangs the menu from the chip's right edge (`inset-inline: auto 0`). Before hydration the menu stays left-aligned. `tests/e2e/admin-compendium.spec.ts` opens the public panel's Toxic menu at 1280 and 390 and checks it lies inside the panel.
- **Counts** are written `<FacetCount>`: a space and the number in small muted type, with no brackets, on every option, chip and switch label ("Local 18").
- **Counts** show on every option, on the Toxic options, on each chosen chip and on
  No Refs' label. A value with a count of 0 is hidden in every facet except Toxic, whose three options always show with their counts; when Toxic or Not Toxic is 0 the Toxic chip selects Any, is disabled and carries a hover and focus hint saying every matching entry shares the same toxicity, so it cannot narrow further. The Source chip follows the same rule: when Local or Compendium counts 0 it rests on All, is disabled, submits no `source`, and its hint (which replaces the ordinary one while stuck) says every matching entry comes from the same source. A held `source` is still counted on the Filters button, as a held Toxic is. Elsewhere a zero is hidden unless it is chosen: a chosen one stays as a removable chip, and a facet with no value left keeps its field with an empty list (`FacetCombobox`).
- **The sheet's foot is sticky**, with scroll padding so a focused control is never
  hidden behind it (WCAG 2.4.11).
- **Chips come out only by their cross**, inside the panel.

## Styling

Tokens only, as signed off in MB.120 (2026-10-20). `FacetPanel/index.scss` is laid out in the order
the panel is built: the Filters button and Clear All, the dialog and its slide, the static block
before script, the rows (`__line`, `__quick`), then the choice chip (`.facet-chip__*`) and the foot. The
sizes are `space()` steps and tokens. Four measurements stay as named, commented locals because no
token fits: the categories floor and wide width, the compact control height, the native select's
cap and the form's quarter-em gap.

The `WIDE` constant, the width from which the panel stays beside the results with no scrim, is
exported from `index.tsx`, and a test guards it against the Sass `$shell-tablet-width` it must equal.

**On a phone**, below `$shell-tablet-width`, the panel is a modal sheet (`showModal()`) that slides in from the right edge over a fading backdrop, full height, with its foot stuck to the bottom and room kept above the foot so a focused control is never hidden behind it (WCAG 2.4.11). Each row's controls stack in source order, and the chips and switches wrap. From the tablet width up, the panel is an overlay that slides down under the search row with no scrim: its title and Close are hidden (the title stays for assistive technology), and once open it scrolls inside itself, under the sticky row. The Filters button's label never wraps. Under reduced motion neither the slide nor the chip menu's fade runs.

**Forced colours.** No rule of its own. The switches and the chip menu's radios take the `.switch` and `.radio` primitives' forced-colours rules ([`styling.md`](../styling.md)), and the chips keep their 1px border, which forced colours repaints in the system's ink. The forced-colours pass over the compendium section is M14.3, a Wave 14 task (minted from MB.120).

## Not here

The GET form, the address state and the debounce that applies a change are the
caller's (`CompendiumList`'s filter). The panel does not read the address.

## Layout of the Forms line

For a viewer with covens, the public compendium's Forms line is two lines of its own inside the outer one. Forms grows beside Toxic in the left half, and In My Covens grows beside its In / Not In choice in the right. Both halves grow alike, so each takes half, and they stack below the tablet width as every line does. They need no rule of their own.

## Compact scale

Every input in the panel is a step shorter than the app's forms (`$control-height`), so the panel takes less room. The scale is scoped to the panel: the primitives, Field and Combobox stay as the ingredient form draws them. Only heights and the strip's side padding change. Type stays at the body and small sizes, and colours and focus rings are the primitives' own. The height is a custom property so the workshop can compare scales. Chips keep their own size because they already fit inside a box this tall.

## Menus and tips inside the panel

A chip at the end of a row would open a menu wider than itself past the panel's right edge, where the slide's clip cuts it. `choice-chip.tsx` measures on open and adds `facet-chip__menu--end` when that would happen, and the menu then hangs from the chip's right edge and opens leftward. Which chip ends a row varies (Match, Toxic, No Refs, Source), so the CSS alone cannot say.

A tip in the panel opens below its label, not above it as everywhere else (`--tip-placement`, read by `useTip` and inherited by every tip in the panel). The panel's clip ends at the first row's top edge. A placed tip is fixed and so never clipped, but one above Match would cover the panel's own header. Where there is no room below, the tip flips. Where it would run past a side of the screen it shifts back, so a chip at a row's end needs no rule of its own.

## Opening and clipping

The search row sticks (`IngredientSearch`), so the tray opens under a row that may be at the window's top. When taller than the room below it, the tray scrolls inside itself rather than running off the window. This applies only once open. The `--open` class arrives as the slide starts, and a short box with `overflow: auto` shows a scrollbar for the whole opening. So the animation holds the clip for the slide's length and the scroll comes after it. A browser that cannot animate `overflow` shows the bar through the slide.

The slide clip uses `overflow: clip` where known, not `hidden`. A hidden box can still be scrolled by the browser's focus step, which moves a dialog's first control into view as it opens and slid the content up inside a still-short panel, so its top rose and then fell back. A clipped box cannot scroll at all. `hidden` is the older browsers' fallback.

## The modal sheet

Below the tablet width the dialog is a full-height drawer from the right. Its foot sticks to the bottom, and the sheet scrolls with room kept above it so that a focused control is never hidden behind it (WCAG 2.4.11). It is `position: fixed`, as a modal dialog is by default, not the overlay's absolute, because an absolute box translated past the right edge widens the page, which then scrolled sideways.

It slides in from the right edge and back out to it while the backdrop fades as a Modal's does. A slide rather than a fade, since a drawer comes from its edge. The way in is CSS alone, from `@starting-style` as `showModal()` shows it. The way out is `.is-closing`, which the component sets and waits on (the sheet's `transform`) before it closes the dialog. `$sheet-slide` is longer than a dialog's fade because the sheet travels its own width. `shell.tsx` waits up to `LEAVE_LIMIT_MS` for it, which must stay longer.
