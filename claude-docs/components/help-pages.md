# Help pages

The public help centre and its staff counterpart. Components: `HelpSearchForm`, `HelpArticleView`, `HelpIndex`, `HelpGlossary` and `HelpSearchResults`, each in `src/components/<Name>/`. Pages: `src/app/(public)/help/` and `src/app/(shell)/help/staff/`. Data: [`graphql.md`, "Help articles"](../design/graphql.md#help-articles).

## Addresses

`helpPath()` in `src/lib/help-paths.ts` is the one place an address is built.

- A term is `/help/glossary#slug`.
- An FAQ entry is `/help#slug` when public, `/help/staff#slug` otherwise.
- An article is `/help/[parent/]slug` when public, `/help/staff/[parent/]slug` otherwise.

`glossary`, `staff` and `search` are reserved slugs, so no article takes those addresses.

## Public routes

`/help`, `/help/glossary`, `/help/[slug]` and `/help/[slug]/[child]` read the cached public finders (tag `help`) and are in the sitemap. `/help/search` is public, reads the session, is never cached and is `noindex`. The proxy admits `/help`, `/help/glossary`, `/help/search` and one or two segments beneath `/help` except `staff` (`HELP_ARTICLE` in `src/proxy.ts`); robots allows the `/help` prefix.

An article page answers 404 for a row that is not a public article, and for a child requested at the wrong parent. A child asked for at its top-level address gets a 308 to its nested address.

## Staff routes

`/help/staff`, `/help/staff/[slug]` and `/help/staff/[slug]/[child]` call `requireCuratorSession()` before any read, are never cached, and are `noindex`. They answer 404 for a public row, so a public row has one address. The `/curator` and `/keepers` layouts link to `/help/staff`.

## Layout

Every help page is one centred reading column: each component's root carries `.help-page` (`_primitives.scss`), `max-width: $measure` and `margin-inline: auto`, so the heading, the search box, the lists and the text share the text's edges. It stands inside the public layout's `.page-column` and inside the staff layout's `.page-frame`, so the two areas cannot differ. A primitive rather than a component style, because a route loads a component's stylesheet only when it draws the component. An article whose body has three or more `##` sections also carries `.page-with-nav`, which takes the document pages' pair (`$document-menu-column`, `$measure`) and centres on its own.

## Ids

Only the FAQ entries (`HelpIndex`) and the terms (`HelpGlossary`) carry an id, their slug, besides the front page's two sections (`guides` and `faq`, which `HelpIndex` reserves too). `renderMarkdown` gives each h2 and h3 an id from its text, so `HelpIndex` and `HelpGlossary` pass every slug, and the ids earlier answers took, as its `reserved` argument; no heading claims an anchor.

## Headings

Every help page but the front page (`/help`, and the staff index, whose h1 is Staff Help) is headed by a level-one "Help" ("Staff Help" on a staff article), then the search box, then the page's own title as a level-two heading: the article's title, "Glossary" or "Search Help". The document `<title>` and metadata keep the page's own title. The Markdown body is drawn with `renderMarkdown(body, placeholders, reserved, true)`, whose last argument moves every heading down a level (h2 becomes h3), so the outline stays h1, h2, h3; the seed text is unchanged. The glossary does the same for its definitions. The h2 stands `space(6)` (32px) below the search box, a step clear of it, on every such page and on the staff index, whose first section takes the margin instead (a section is a flex stack, so its heading's margin would add rather than collapse); the rule lives with the box (`HelpSearchForm/index.scss`). A back link sits in that gap at its own spacing (`HelpBackLink`, `HelpArticleView/back-link.tsx`), 94px from the box to the h2 on a guide, on a sub page, on the glossary and on the staff index, the last two linking back to Help, alike; under it the staff index's Guides heading takes no top margin of its own.

- **A keeper sees Edit Article** as a pencil, `EditLink` named Edit Article and tipped Edit, at the right end of the title row, flush with the article column's right edge, as the help index's Staff Help link ends its title row (`.help-article__title`, a grid of the title, taking the room left, and the pencil). The row holds at every width: a title that wraps keeps the pencil on its first line at the right, centred on that line. The rules live in `HelpArticleView/title.scss`, which every page with a pencil imports. It links the row's edit page (`keeperHelpEditPath`, `/keepers/help/<id>`), on the public and the staff articles alike. It comes from the same `helpViewerOffers()` read, `edit` true for a verified keeper only; a curator sees none. The edit page checks the keeper itself.
- **An article links the page above it** between the search box and its title: a sub page its guide ("Back to Your Coven"), a guide the help index ("Back to Help", or "Back to Staff Help" to `/help/staff`), drawn as the entry page's muted chevron back link (`.back-link` and `BackChevron` from `IngredientEntry/back-chevron`, without its history behaviour), spaced as the entry page spaces its own: 37px from the search box to the chevron, and about 45px from the chevron to the title (43px, the nearest the spacing scale gives); the chevron hangs 4px from its text, outside the column. Nothing more: the tree is two levels below the index, so one link stands in for a trail. A sub page reads its parent for the title, by the same finder that answered the sub page, so a parent the reader may not open is a 404.
- **An article with three or more `##` sections draws a contents menu**, `PageNav` labelled "On This Page", collapsible and non-sticky, as the document pages do; fewer sections are in view without one. The entries are read before the headings move down, so each jumps to an `h3`.
- **The pages beneath an article** are a numbered list (`ol`) with no frame, on the article column's edges and a body section's step (`space(8)`) below the text, the `nav` labelled "In This Guide" with the same words as a visible eyebrow hidden from assistive technology, each number in the heading face and each summary muted.
- **A guide with sub pages is one sequence**, the guide first and then its sub pages in the order of that list, and every page of it ends with a `nav` named "Guide Pages": a Previous link to the page before and a Next link to the page after, each a `PendingLink` showing a "Previous" or "Next" label above the page's title, drawn as the parent link (`.back-link`), Previous with the chevron pointing back and Next with it after the text pointing forward (`.back-link--forward`), held to the row's end. The guide has no Previous and the last sub page no Next; a guide with no sub pages has neither. `guideSteps` (`src/lib/help-pages.ts`) builds the pair from the guide and the sub pages the page already read, which the finder has cut to the reader's reach, so a curator's steps never name a keeper-only page. The row stands `space(7)` below the content, over a hairline, in two equal halves, Previous in the first and Next always in the second, so a long title wraps within its own half; on a phone (`$shell-phone-width`) each takes the full width, Next still aligned to the end. The chevron sits on the label's line, not the middle of a wrapped title.
- **The index lists the glossary among the guides**, as a described item ("The words Sorrel & Salt uses, and what each one means."), so the Guides section is always drawn. The FAQ is open text with no accordion, and the glossary has no A–Z bar.
- **Search results** open with a count ("7 results for “steward”", an `output`), then each result as a quiet card (`.card--quiet`, as the front page's guides are): the title, in the body ink, whose link covers the card (`card-link`), with a chip of its kind at the top of the row's end (Question, Guide or Term, the `muted` badge palette) and, for a row whose audience is not `public`, a chip naming that audience, "Staff" or "Keeper", from `HELP_AUDIENCE_LABELS` in `src/lib/help-audience.ts` (the `local` palette for both), and the summary beneath, muted and small, at the card's full width. The staff search is the only one that returns rows that are not public. Each searched word is wrapped in a `<mark>` wherever the title or summary holds it, in any case, and drawn by weight with no fill. A query that finds nothing draws the site's No Matches state, as the coven's Ingredients list does: a `role="status"` region named "No Matches" holding a muted sentence, then quiet buttons to the guides and the glossary. An empty query draws neither results nor links.

## The front page

`HelpIndex` draws, beneath the h1, a band (`.help-index__band`, on the page itself, with no surface or edge) holding a lede, the search box at its `hero` size and the quick links; then the guides and the FAQ.

- **Staff Help, for staff only.** A curator or keeper sees a forward link, "Staff Help" with the chevron turned onward (`.back-link--forward`), to `/help/staff`, as the page header's action: at the end of the Help title row, aligned right. It stays on that row at every width (`HelpIndex/index.scss`), where the site's header rule would drop an action under a narrow title. No one else sees it, signed out included. The page reads `helpViewerOffers()` (`src/lib/help-viewer.ts`) per request beside the cached reads, never inside them, so nothing keyed by the viewer enters the `help` cache (rule 6); the `(public)` layout already reads the session, so the page renders per request and needs no client island.

- **The jump menu** is the site's [`PageNav`](page-nav.md), named "On This Page", with two entries: Guides (`#guides`) and Frequently Asked Questions (`#faq`). The page takes `.page-with-nav`, as an article with a menu does: the menu stands in a column beside the band and the sections from 40rem, and above the guides, open with no fold, on a phone. Both ids are reserved before the answers are rendered, so no answer heading takes one; a question slug would clash only if a question were titled "Guides" or "FAQ". The menu is drawn only when there are questions, so the staff index, which has none, has no menu; its skeleton is drawn without one. The public index's skeleton draws the two labels as text, and the questions beneath them (`HelpIndexSkeleton menu`). The grid's gap alone, `space(6)` (32px), stands between the band's quick links and the Guides heading, whose own top margin is 0, as the search box stands above a page title elsewhere; on a phone the menu sits there, a gap above it and one below. The FAQ heading keeps a further `space(6)`.

- **The search box** (`HelpSearchForm`) is the same on every help page, public and staff: a magnifier drawn inside the box and a "Search Help" placeholder, and the button drops beneath the box below 20rem. The front page's hero box (`size="hero"`) is `$hero-control-height` tall at the lead size; every other page draws the standard height. On the staff index, which has no band, the box stands `space(6)` above the Guides heading and nothing more: the section takes the step and its first heading no margin.
- **Quick links** are a list named "Quick Links", each link a pill on the guide cards' surface and hover pair (`$surface-card`, a hairline edge, `$edge-highlight` and `$fill-highlight-field` on hover), read on each request by `quickLinks(HELP_QUICK_LINKS, readHelpArticle)` (`src/lib/help-quick-links.ts`). `HELP_QUICK_LINKS` names articles by their shipped titles, which the skeleton draws while the page is on its way; each is read at its `slugify` slug, the title and the address shown come from the finder, and a slug that no longer answers as a public article drops out. `tests/lib/help-quick-links.test.ts` fails when a named title is not a public article's title in the shipped seed. The staff index passes none.
- **Each guide is a card** (`.card--quiet`) with a left bar of `$rule-heavy` that turns accent on hover and focus, a kind eyebrow ("Guide", or "Reference" for the glossary), the title as an h3 whose link covers the card (`card-link`), and the muted summary. One column on a phone, two from 40rem.
- **The FAQ** rules each entry with a hairline above it and sets the question at the h4 size; a "Back to Top" link to `#main` (the skip target both frames draw) follows the last entry, drawn as the parent link is (`.back-link`) with its chevron pointing up, its chevron 38px below the last answer, as the parent link's stands 37px below the search box. The question an address names (`/help#slug`) is marked as a glossary term is (`target-highlight`), its tint reaching `space(5)` below the answer, as far as the padding stands above the question, so it stops short of the next rule and of Back to Top.

## The glossary page

The page links back to Help (`HelpBackLink` to `/help`, for every reader) between the search box and its title, as a guide does, and its skeleton draws the same line. A keeper sees **Edit Glossary** at the end of the title row, the article's pencil in the same `.help-article__title` row: `EditLink` named Edit Glossary and tipped Edit, linking Keepers › Help opened on the glossary (`keeperHelpViewPath('glossary')`, `/keepers/help?view=glossary`). The page reads `helpViewerOffers()` per request beside the cached terms, `edit` true for a verified keeper only; a curator and a signed-out reader see none.

One row per term, ruled above, and the last below too: the term beside its definition from 40rem (11rem and the rest), stacked on a phone. The term is in the heading face at the h4 size, and holds a link to its own anchor, named "Link to <Term>" and drawn as a small, light "#" by CSS so the term's text stays its title; it shows on hovering the row or focusing the link.

The row an address names (`/help/glossary#steward`) is marked by CSS `:target`, through the `target-highlight` mixin (`_mixins.scss`), which the FAQ shares: a `::before` behind the row, reaching `space(3)` past each side so the rules stay their width, holds a 1px `$edge-highlight` edge on its start and a faint tint (`$accent` at 5% over `$surface-card`): a quiet mark of the row, not a callout. The tint arrives at `$fill-highlight-field` and settles over `$motion-long` (`target-arrive`, in the primitives), and stays still under reduced motion. The row's `scroll-margin-top` keeps it clear of the shell bar. `:target` is set by loading the address or by a plain anchor on the page, but not by a client navigation (a `PendingLink` from another help page), which moves the address with `history.pushState`. So the glossary and the index each mount `HashTarget` (`src/components/HashTarget`), which, when the page arrives with a hash and nothing is the target, replaces the address with its own hash, setting the target without a history entry, then puts back the router's history state so Back still works.

## Callouts

A help body marks a callout as a blockquote whose first words are a bold label: `> **Note:** …`, `> **Tip:** …` or `> **Take Care:** …`. `renderMarkdown` draws it as a `.notice` (plain, `.notice--tip` or `.notice--warn`) with `role="note"`, named by its label, the label shown above the text as `.eyebrow.notice__label` and hidden from assistive technology. Every paragraph of the blockquote stays inside. Any other blockquote, and every blockquote in a site document (which passes no terms), stays a blockquote. `tests/components/DocumentPage/help-callouts.test.tsx` holds the cases.

## Look

A section heading in an article body (an h3) has a short hairline beneath it (`short-hairline`). A screenshot or a widget in the text sits in a `.figure` frame with its caption beneath (`_primitives.scss`).

## Glossary marks

A body marks a glossary term as `[[Term]]`, or `[[Term|shown text]]` to show other words. The part before the bar matches a term row's title in any case, or its slug through `slugify`. Nothing is matched without a mark.

- **Where they render.** `renderMarkdown` takes the terms as its last argument and the help views pass them (`HelpArticleView`, `HelpIndex`, `HelpGlossary`), so the site documents, which pass none, are unchanged. The terms come from the cached public glossary read (tag `help`, `readGlossaryTerms` in `src/lib/help-glossary.ts`), once per page, never per mark. Staff pages read the same public terms.
- **What a mark becomes.** A [HelpTerm](help-term.md): a link to `/help/glossary#<slug>` with a tooltip of the term's title and its definition as plain text, drawn as a lifted card (`$surface-card`, a hairline, `$shadow-floating`) with the title in the heading face above the definition. A mouse click or Enter follows the link; a touch or pen tap opens the tooltip instead, whose "See in Glossary" link goes to the anchor, and a second tap, a tap outside or Escape closes it. A mark naming no row shows its shown text and no link.
- **Where they do not.** A heading, a link and a code span or block hold no mark: in a heading or link the shown text is plain, and in code the mark stays as typed so the syntax can be written down. The keeper's editor shows marks as typed ([`rich-text-editor.md`](rich-text-editor.md), "Help syntax").
- **The glossary page** links a mark in a definition to its anchor with no tooltip, since every definition is on the page.
- **Summaries** (search results, index and sub page lists, the page description) show the shown text as plain text.
- **Saving.** `createHelpArticle` and `updateHelpArticle` refuse a body that marks a term no live glossary row holds, as a `ValidationError` on `body` naming each missing term; an update that leaves the body alone is not checked. A term being saved counts as present in its own body. The seed test asserts every mark in the shipped prose names a seeded term.

## Widgets

A help body places a live widget with a paragraph that is one tag, `::widget[name]`. The names are a fixed list, `HELP_WIDGETS` in `src/lib/help-widgets.ts`:

| Name                    | What it draws                                                                                                                                                                                  | Where its rule comes from                                                        |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `unit-converter`        | An amount and two units, each a `ComboboxSelect` sized to its longest unit as a form's closed set is; the result, or the refusal for a conversion across dimensions or involving a count unit  | `unitConvert` and `UNITS` (`src/modules/ingredients`)                            |
| `invitation-refusals`   | A picker, The Message You Saw, that finds the message typed loosely and draws it as the accept page does, with who can fix it and what to do now; beneath it, a disclosure listing all of them | `INVITATION_REFUSAL_MESSAGES` and `CARD_REFUSAL_MESSAGES` (`@/modules/identity`) |
| `role-permission-table` | A table of each workspace action against Steward, Practitioner and Observer, a check or a cross in each cell                                                                                   | `rolePermits` and `WORKSPACE_STATEMENTS` (`@/modules/coven`)                     |
| `autosave-status`       | The real `SaveStatus` in each of its six states, each in its own framed sample under its name, and the retry delays                                                                            | `AutosaveStatus` and `RETRY_DELAYS_MS` (`src/lib/autosave`)                      |

- **The role table leaves out what no screen offers.** `HIDDEN_STATEMENTS` in `role-permission-table.tsx` lists the statements it skips, now `workspace.update` (renaming a coven, which has no screen); the task that builds a rename screen takes it off the list. A test holds every entry to a statement in `WORKSPACE_STATEMENTS` and every other statement to a row.
- **The invitation widget is a server shell round a client picker.** `invitation-refusals.tsx` reads the service's two tables and hands every message to `refusal-finder.tsx` as props; the finder draws only once hydrated, so before the script runs and without one the reader has the shell's own disclosure, "Every Message the Page Can Give". Each printed card wording sits straight after the link message it replaces, and the two card-only reasons keep the service table's place. Who can fix each message (You, Whoever Sent It, A Keeper, No One Needs To) and what to do now are the widget's own words, in one table keyed by reason, `REFUSAL_FIXES` in `invitation-refusal-fixes.ts`, beside the accept page's button for the two reasons that have one. The picker is `ComboboxTypeahead` with `match` set to `fuzzyMatcher` (`src/lib/fuzzy-match.ts`, on Fuse.js), because a reader half-remembers the sentence. The answer's error is the message alone, as the accept page's is; a printed card's mark sits above it, outside the error. The list is a native `<details>` wearing `CollapsibleSection`'s classes, so it reads as the app's other expanding sections and opens with no script; the widget adds only what a summary needs and the section's button does not: no marker, its own focus ring, the chevron turned by `[open]`, and a closing hairline.
- **The autosave widget draws examples, not live lines.** Each state is an `example` `SaveStatus`, so the widget holds no live region or alert, with what the state means beneath it. Its time is a fixed date built from local parts (2:02 pm), so the server and the browser draw the same text. Nothing to Save shows no time; A Change Is Waiting shows the last save's.
- **Each widget imports its rule, never a copy**, so a reason, a role or a delay added in the code shows in the widget. The invitation and role widgets are server components, since their modules are server-only, and the invitation widget's picker is a client island inside it; the converter and the autosave widget are client components. All four live in `src/components/HelpWidget/`, and `HELP_WIDGET_ELEMENTS` maps each name to its element.
- **The converter's answer is an equation**, "1 tbsp = 3 tsp", under a hairline at `heading-size(4)` with tabular figures and the sign muted; the left side and the sign hold together, so a narrow figure wraps after the sign. A refusal keeps the line with a muted dash for the answer and adds a notice beneath, a warning edge, the lead "Cannot convert" and the app's sentence word for word. The drawn line and the notice are `aria-hidden`: the status reads the sentence, "1 tbsp is 3 tsp." or the refusal, since a spoken "equals" is harder to follow.
- **Where it renders.** `HelpArticleView` hands `HELP_WIDGET_ELEMENTS` to `renderMarkdown` as its last argument; a root paragraph that is one tag naming a handed widget is swapped for it. A tag inside a sentence, a tag for a widget not handed over, and every tag in a site document stays text.
- **Saving.** `createHelpArticle` and `updateHelpArticle` refuse a body that places a name not in the list, as a `ValidationError` on `body` naming it and listing the widgets, the same way as an unknown term mark; an update that leaves the body alone is not checked.
- **Tests.** `tests/components/HelpWidget/` checks each widget against its rule (a reason added to the invitation tables and missing from the widget fails there); `tests/e2e/help-widgets.spec.ts` reads one article per widget on the built server with axe.

## Screenshots

## Links to help

`Welcome`, the footer nav (`SiteFooter`, after Privacy), the 404 screen, the account menu (`AccountMenu`, a "Help" row before Support), the `/support` page and the Support dialog (each a "Search Help" line before the form) link to `/help`. The top bar (`PublicFrame`, `AppShell`) does not. An empty Ingredients list and an empty Grimoire tab each end with a "Learn More" link to their guide, built by `publicGuidePath(slug)` (`src/lib/help-paths.ts`) from the guide's slug, which is its title's. The Curator and Keepers navs each end with a "Staff Help" entry linking `/help/staff` ([`admin-nav.md`](admin-nav.md)).

Every link from a help page to another page, the term marks and their "See in Glossary" link among them, is a `PendingLink`, so a press shows the destination's skeleton at once. A link to an anchor on the same page (the front page's "Back to Top", a glossary term's own "#") stays an `<a>`, since it loads nothing.

## Skeletons

Each help page's skeleton sits beside its component (`skeleton.tsx` in `HelpIndex`, `HelpArticleView`, `HelpGlossary` and `HelpSearchResults`) and draws the page in the page's own classes, as [loading.md](../loading.md) sets out. Each draws the heading, the search box at its real size and the h2, so nothing moves when the page arrives. Words still to come are `SkeletonWords` (`src/components/Skeleton/text.tsx`): transparent filler of a given word count behind `SkeletonText`'s bars, so a bar wraps and takes its line height as the real text would. A skeleton's links are spans, as it links nowhere.

- **The index** draws the band with the quick links' shipped titles, the jump menu, six guide cards with their bar and "Guide" kind, and the glossary's card from `HELP_GLOSSARY_CARD` (`src/lib/help-pages.ts`), which the page reads too. With `menu` it also draws three questions and Back to Top.
- **An article** draws the back link ("Back to Help", or the staff heading, on a guide; a bar for the guide's title on a sub page), the title, three paragraphs under a section heading, the In This Guide list with three entries, and the guide's steps: Next alone on a guide; on a sub page (`parent`), both Previous and Next.
- **The glossary** draws six ruled two-column rows. **The search page** draws the count and four result cards with their kind.
