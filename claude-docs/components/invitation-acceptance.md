# Invitation Acceptance

`src/components/InvitationAcceptance/` is the panel of `/invite/[token]`, the route an invitation's mailed link opens, for both the site tier and the workspace's. The server component `src/app/invite/[token]/page.tsx` reads where the visitor stands and hands down one of the states in `types.ts`; this component shows it and sends the accept. The rules it shows are in [`auth/admin-users.md`](../auth/admin-users.md), "Inviting a keeper".

## Contracts

- **`signed-out`**: no session. The page reads nothing of the invitation, so a stranger holding the link learns nothing of it. It offers **Sign In**, `signInPath` of the link itself, so the sign-in comes back here.
- **`refused`**: the accept service's own reason, in its words, from `invitationStanding`: a dead link, a different address, a pause, or an address not yet verified. Only the last carries `emailHref`, the email page with the link as its `next`, offered as **Confirm Your Email**. An account that already used a card carries `startCovenHref` (`/coven/new`), offered as **Start a Coven**. No message names a workspace.
- **`acceptable`**: what the invitation grants, by tier, and **Accept Invitation**. A site invitation names its role, `siteRole`, curator or admin. The sentence names the coven (`covenName`) only in this state: the standing reaches it after the address checks, so no refusal can say it.
- **The click sends `acceptInvitation`**, which checks everything again and decides. The button stays busy, with a spinner, until `router.push` reaches `landing`: `/keepers` for a keeper's site invitation, `/curator` for a curator's, `/coven/[slug]` for a coven's (the slug comes from the standing). Accept is `aria-disabled` while it sends, not `disabled`, so the browser does not take the focus off it, and `accept()` ignores a second press itself. A success says nothing of its own, since the landing is the answer, so the hook's `announce` is not called.
- **A refusal on accept is `useClickFeedback`'s callout** ([`click-feedback.md`](click-feedback.md)): an alert floating under Accept, which keeps the focus and is described by "Last try failed: …". A dismissed callout comes back from the badge beside it, and the next press clears it. `readGraphqlFailure` (`src/lib/graphql-failure.ts`) reads the failure, and `GENERIC_FAILURE` is the sentence when the server sent none.
- **The page uses `getSession()`, not `requireSession()`:** an unverified account would otherwise be redirected to the email page without being told why, and the signed-out state is the page's to show.
- **A refused link has one sentence per reason**, from `MESSAGES` in `src/modules/identity/services/invitation-acceptance.ts`, and none names a coven. Two are worth knowing:
  - A declined link says "This invitation was declined. If you change your mind, ask whoever sent it for a new one." The invitee's answer outranks a steward's Remove that may follow.
  - A link into a deleted coven has a reason of its own, `closed`: "The coven this invitation was for has been deleted, so it can't be accepted." It sits after the dead-link and address checks, so a link in two states reports the earlier (an accepted one still says accepted) and another address learns nothing of the coven.

## Styling

A `.panel`, as sign-in's, the email page's and the create page's are: the root is `panel invitation-acceptance`, and its `PageHeader` ([`page-header.md`](page-header.md)) holds the `h1` and the grant sentence as its `lede`. A refusal has no grant, so its head is the heading alone, its row's margin dropped so the panel's gap spaces it, and the alert says the rest. The page has no AppShell, so its `main` is `.page-frame.page-frame--narrow` ([`styling.md`](../styling.md), "The page frame"): it centres the panel, holds it clear of the top inset and caps it at `$narrow-column` (28rem), so the panel sets no width of its own. `.invitation-acceptance` only stacks the header, the alert and the button a `space(3)` apart.

A refused link's reason is a `Notice` ([`notice.md`](notice.md)) as an error, an alert the server renders. Accept's wrapper is a `div`, not the other states' `p`, because the callout is a `div`. Accept is drawn through `BusyButton` ([`busy-button.md`](busy-button.md)).
