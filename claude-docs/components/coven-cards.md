# CovenCards

`src/components/CovenCards/` is a coven's Ingredients list: the search and its filters, one compact `IngredientCard` per entry of the coven's cupboard in an `IngredientCardList`, and the pager beneath. It is the coven's twin of [`CompendiumCards`](compendium-cards.md) and, like it, render-only: the page reads one page of the cupboard under the address's filter and hands it over. A page holds `CARD_PAGE_SIZE` (24) entries. Props are in `types.ts`; `filter`, `facets`, `categories`, `vocabulary` and `total` are the filter state of [`compendium-list.md`](compendium-list.md), with Source, In Stock and Low Stock Only added.

## Contracts

- **Each card is a plain link** to `/coven/<slug>/ingredients/<entry id>`, with no modal. `label` is what the coven calls the entry (its cupboard row's label where one is set), so the card shows the coven's name, not the compendium's.
- **`kind` is `own` for an entry the coven made, `cupboard` for a compendium entry its cupboard names.** `retired` (the curators retired the entry) draws its mark on the card. The card shows neither Compendium nor Annotated, so `CovenCardEntry` carries no `annotated` and the list page does not work it out; the entry page and its head still show both ([`entry-marks.md`](entry-marks.md)).
- **The list is `density="compact"`.** The Compact switch that lets a member turn it off is [`card-density-switch.md`](card-density-switch.md); the coven filter row draws it (`CompendiumListFilter`, `audience="coven"`) inside the search landmark. The admin and public lists have none.
- **The card's `children` hold the entry's jars** as [`JarLines`](jar-shelf.md#jar-lines), one line per jar, each stock badge centred on the same vertical line as the card's rightmost action icon below it. The card draws no forms line (`IngredientCard`'s `forms={false}`): the jar lines name each form held, and the Quick Look window lists every form.
- **The foot is built by `covenCardActions`** (`actions.tsx`), in this order:
  1. The Edit Stock pencil, a [`StockDialog`](stock-dialog.md), when the entry carries `stock` (its forms and jars, given only to a role that may update stock). One dialog serves all the entry's jars, in which a form is edited, added or removed.
  2. Edit, an `EditLink` named for the entry's label and formal name (two entries can share a label), when the entry carries an `editHref` (the list's own address with `?edit=<id>`) and an `editHeading` (the dialog's head, shown at once over a skeleton while the form loads).
  3. Annotate, an `AnnotateLink` named the same way, on a compendium entry the coven holds, when the entry carries an `annotateHref` (`?annotate=<id>`). Its icon, `AnnotateIcon` in `src/components/IconTip/icons.tsx`, is an open book with a pencil, drawn at stroke width 1.25 rather than the set's 1.5.
  4. More (below).
     At the foot's left, in the card's `startActions`, the Quick Look eye (`DetailsLink`) opens the entry's details modal over the list at `?entry=<id>`. Edit and Annotate wear the plain text colour (`icon-tip__edit icon-tip__edit--plain`, passed as `className`; the admin's uses keep the green), so the stock pencil is the foot's one green control.
- **More** (`card-more.tsx`, `CardMore`) is the card's further writes: three vertical dots tipped "More" beside Edit or Annotate, a `MenuButton` with `icon` named "More for {label}", opening downward. The dots are an icon on the card only, never words. It is drawn on a compendium entry the cupboard holds, offering Make It Ours (`mayMakeOurs`) and Remove (`mayRemove`), each on the permission its service checks.
  - The coven's own entry has no More. Its Delete Ingredient is in its Edit Ingredient dialog, and nowhere on the card or in its Quick Look ([`.claude/rules/components.md`](../../.claude/rules/components.md), "Destructive controls").
  - Each choice asks in place, in the dialogs the entry page shares (`MakeItOursDialog`, `RemoveDialog`), since a confirmation has nothing to share as an address.
  - The choices sit under the one **Coven** section label every More has (`choicesFor`). More is drawn only with Make It Ours, which needs the delete permission too, so a role that may remove but not make it ours gets no More and no bin: the Annotate dialog holds its Remove. No role reaches that case today.
  - A write that takes the card away reads the list again, and the focus goes to the page's heading, More having gone with the card. Make It Ours goes to the new own entry's page. A Delete Ingredient, made in the Edit Ingredient dialog, says "{label} deleted" in the page's status region, which outlives the dialog and the card (`EntryDialog`). A refusal shows in the card and gives More the focus back.
- **The filter is `CompendiumListFilter audience="coven"`.** It adds Source, In Stock and Low Stock Only, submits to `/coven/<slug>/ingredients`, and suggests names from the coven's cupboard. A pick opens the entry's Quick Look over the list as shown, at `?entry=<id>` with the filter and the page kept.
- **With no entries it draws `EmptyCupboard`** (`empty.tsx`) instead of a list.

## The empty and no-match states

With no card to show, the list draws one of two `role="status"` regions. Each has its own name, so a test tells them apart by which one is present rather than by its words.

| Region           | When                                         | What it holds                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `No Ingredients` | No filter is in force: the cupboard is empty | "There are no ingredients here yet." and, when `canAdd`, an Add Ingredient button; otherwise "A practitioner or steward of this coven can add them." |
| `No Matches`     | A filter is in force and matched nothing     | "Change or clear the filter to see more." and, when the filter has a search, Search the Compendium                                                   |

- **Which state** is the search sentence's own test: `buildSearchSentence(...).hasFilter` (`IngredientSearch/sentence.ts`), with the filter normalised as the filter row normalises it. It counts the coven's own filters too (Source, In Stock, Low Stock Only), so an In Stock filter over a cupboard with nothing in stock reads as no match, and the region never disagrees with the sentence above it.
- **No Matches says only what to do.** The search sentence above the list already names every filter, so the region does not repeat it. Its one sentence is `NO_MATCH_HINT` from `src/lib/text.ts`, the advice that ends `noMatchText` on the admin lists; `noMatchText` itself is not used, because it always opens by naming the filters. The advice points at the filter row's own clears (Clear Name in the search box, the facet panel's Clear All), and the region draws no clear control of its own.
- **Add Ingredient** (`first-add.tsx`) builds no dialog. It writes `?new=<slug>` into the address with `openHref` from `AppShell/add-href.ts`, and the shell's Add Ingredient control opens its dialog for this coven ([`modal.md`](modal.md), "Add Ingredient"). The page passes `canAdd` from the same permission that control checks (`ingredient: create` and `inventory: update`), so an observer gets no button. An observer reads which roles can add instead, so the empty cupboard reads as not yet filled rather than as a fault ([`mb.121-workspace-ingredients-review.md`](../design-decisions/mb.121-workspace-ingredients-review.md)).
- **Search the Compendium** is a link styled as the filled primary button (`.btn .btn--solid`), going to `/compendium?query=<the search>`, since a search that found nothing in the cupboard may still be in the Compendium. A filter with no search, such as In Stock alone, has nothing to hand on, so the link is not drawn.
- **Layout** (`index.scss`): the empty cupboard's sentence and button are centred, set larger (the sentence at `heading-size(4)`, the button's label at body size) and set down `space(7)` from the filter row. No Matches stays at the list's left edge.

## The page

`src/app/(shell)/coven/[slug]/ingredients/page.tsx` draws it per request. It first calls `requireCovenMembership`, which sends a non-member to a 404, then reads through `reads.ts`. Nothing here is cached: a cupboard is workspace-scoped.

- **The `Membership` is the retired entry's proof.** The page hands the `Membership` that guard returns to `readCovenIngredients`, which passes it as `heldBy` to the forms and categories reads. Without the proof the services refuse a retired parent; with it a card for a retired compendium entry the coven still holds shows its forms and category chips. The proof admits a retired entry only where this coven's live cupboard row names it. The read is wrapped in React's `cache`, keyed by the coven id, the filter, the cursors and the `Membership` object itself, which is the same object all request because `requireCovenMembership` is cached per slug.
- **Last edit.** `readCovenIngredients` calls `lastEditsOf` (`src/modules/ingredients/services/last-edits.ts`) once for the page, and each card gets `lastEdit`: `{ by, at }`, or null for none. The edit is the newest `updated_at` of the coven's live jars of the entry (one batched `findStockOfIngredients` read under the `Membership`), of the entry's row too for the coven's own entry, and for a compendium entry of the coven's cupboard row of it (one batched `findCupboardEntriesOf` read; the annotation counts whatever it holds, since adding the entry is a coven edit). A compendium entry's own editor, a keeper or curator, is never shown, nor is another coven's annotation, so a compendium entry with no jar and no cupboard row of the coven reads as no edit. The editors' names are one `displayNamesOf` call for the page, so an editor the viewer cannot name has `by: null` and the card leaves out the "by" half ([`last-edit.md`](last-edit.md)).
- **Quick Look.** Every role, an observer included, gets the eye on each card. `?entry=<id>` opens the entry's details modal over the list ([`ingredient-entry.md`](ingredient-entry.md), "The details modal"), read by `readViewedEntry` in `reads.ts` with the same checks as `?edit`, so an id the coven cannot reach opens nothing. The page is its own `RouteModalProvider`, as the shell has none outside `/curator` and `/keepers`, so the eye, Edit and Annotate each open at once on a press ([`modal.md`](modal.md), "Opening at once"). The details and the edit and annotate dialogs are two places on the page, so an Edit inside the details opens over them and closes back to them.
- **The foot's controls come from one place.** The window's icons follow the card it was opened from, so `covenCardActions` makes both: Edit for the coven's own entry or Annotate for a compendium entry the coven holds, each only where the page gave its address, then `CardMore` when the role may do more. `covenCardName` (over `cardName` in `IngredientCard/card-name.ts`) names the entry "Name, Formal" for every control, the card's and the window's alike. It is a composer of the client controls, not a client file, so the page calls it once and hands the elements to the card and, with `leaveHref`, to the window. `leaveHref` is the list's address without `?entry`: a delete or a remove from the window goes there, replacing the history entry, which closes the window with the row gone, where a card reads the list again instead (`useDeleteEntry`'s `deletedHref`, `useCupboardWrites`' `removedHref`). The window has no plus, as the card has none.
- **Edit on the list.** A member whose role may update ingredients gets Edit on each card for the coven's own entry; a compendium entry in the cupboard and an observer get none. Edit's address keeps the filter and the page, so the entry page's edit dialog (`EntryDialog`, [`ingredient-entry.md`](ingredient-entry.md)) opens over the list as it was, and Close or a save comes back to it. `readEditedEntry` reads the entry `?edit` names as the signed-in member, with `entryValues` for the form. It returns nothing, and so opens no dialog, for an id the coven cannot reach or for a compendium entry, and the page reads nothing at all for an observer's `?edit`.
- **Annotate on the list.** The same member gets Annotate on each card for a compendium entry the coven holds, a retired one included, and none on the coven's own entries. `?annotate=<id>` opens the entry page's Annotate dialog over the list, through the one `AnnotateDialog` (`src/components/IngredientEntry/annotate-dialog.tsx`) the entry page draws too, and Close or a save comes back to the list as it was. The dialog's **More** menu, or its lone button when only one is allowed, offers Make It Ours, which goes to the new own entry's page, and Remove, which removes the entry from the cupboard and comes back to the same search without its card; each is offered on the member's permission ([`ingredient-entry.md`](ingredient-entry.md), "Cupboard buttons"). `heldCompendiumEntry` reads the entry under the coven's proof, as the entry page does; an own entry, one the coven does not hold, or an observer's `?annotate` opens nothing, and an observer's reads nothing.

## Not here

The coven itself has no card, so this list draws no Demo badge. The badge sits in the coven page's heading alone, not in the switcher ([`workspace-switcher.md`](workspace-switcher.md)), and the layout's notice says it on this list as on every page of the demo coven ([`notice.md`](notice.md)).
