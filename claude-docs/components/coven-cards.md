# CovenCards

`src/components/CovenCards/` is a coven's Ingredients list (M9.6): the search and its filters, one compact `IngredientCard` per entry of the coven's cupboard in an `IngredientCardList`, and the pager beneath. It is the coven's twin of [`CompendiumCards`](compendium-cards.md) and, like it, render-only: the page reads one page of the cupboard under the address's filter and hands it over. A page holds `CARD_PAGE_SIZE` (24) entries.

## Props

| Prop                                                    | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `covenSlug`, `workspaceId`                              | The coven's address segment, which builds every link, and its id, which the type-ahead and counts read                                                                                                                                             |
| `entries`                                               | One `CovenCardEntry` per card: the coven's label, formal name, forms, categories, kind, flags and jars, where Edit opens (`editHeading`), More's choices (`mayMakeOurs`, `mayRemove`), and `stock` (`forms` and `jars`, for the Edit Stock pencil) |
| `filter`, `facets`, `categories`, `vocabulary`, `total` | The filter state of [`compendium-list.md`](compendium-list.md), with Source, In Stock and Low Stock Only                                                                                                                                           |
| `previousHref`, `nextHref`, `position`                  | The pager's links and "Page X of Y"                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `canAdd`                                                | The viewer may add to this coven, so an empty cupboard offers the first add                                                                                                                                                                        |

## Contracts

- Each card is a plain link to `/coven/<slug>/ingredients/<entry id>`, with no modal. `label` is what the coven calls the entry (its cupboard row's label where one is set), so the card shows the coven's name, not the compendium's.
- `kind` is `own` for an entry the coven made, `cupboard` for a compendium entry its cupboard names. `retired` (the curators retired the entry) draws its mark on the card. The card shows neither Compendium nor Annotated (the owner's call, 2026-10-20), so `CovenCardEntry` carries no `annotated` and the list page does not work it out; the entry page and its head still show both ([`entry-marks.md`](entry-marks.md)).
- The list is `density="compact"`. The Compact switch that lets a member turn it off is [`card-density-switch.md`](card-density-switch.md); the coven filter row draws it (`CompendiumListFilter`, `audience="coven"`), inside the search landmark; the admin and public lists have none.
- The card's `children` hold the entry's jars as [`JarLines`](jar-lines.md), one line per jar, each line's stock badge centred on the same vertical line as the card's rightmost action icon below it ([`jar-lines.md`](jar-lines.md), "Where the badge sits"). The card draws **no forms line** (`IngredientCard`'s `forms={false}`, the owner's call, 2026-10-20): the jar lines name each form held, and the Quick Look window lists every form. The `actions` foot, built by `covenCardActions` (`actions.tsx`), starts with the Edit Stock pencil (below), then holds Edit, an `EditLink` named for the entry's label and formal name (two entries can share a label), when the entry carries an `editHref`, the list's own address with `?edit=<id>`, and an `editHeading`, the dialog's head shown at once over a skeleton while the form loads (M8.17). Beside it, a compendium entry the coven holds draws Annotate, an `AnnotateLink` named the same way, when the entry carries an `annotateHref`, the list's own address with `?annotate=<id>` (M8.17). Its icon, `AnnotateIcon` in `src/components/IconTip/icons.tsx`, is an open book with a pencil, the owner's choice, drawn at stroke width 1.25 rather than the set's 1.5 (the owner's call, 2026-10-19). Edit and Annotate wear the plain text colour (`icon-tip__edit icon-tip__edit--plain`, passed as `className` by `covenCardActions`; admin's uses keep the green), so the stock pencil is the foot's one green control (the owner's call, 2026-10-20). The Edit Stock pencil comes first, ahead of them, a [`StockDialog`](stock-dialog.md), when the entry carries `stock` (its forms and jars, given only to a role that may update stock): one dialog for all the entry's jars, in which a form is edited, added or removed (M9.9; the owner's call, 2026-10-20, replacing a pencil on each jar line). After them comes More (below). At the foot's left, in the card's `startActions`, the Quick Look eye (`DetailsLink`) opens the entry's details modal over the list at `?entry=<id>` (M12.5; the owner's call, 2026-10-20).
- **More** (`card-more.tsx`, `CardMore`; M8.17) is the card's further writes, as three vertical dots tipped "More" beside Edit or Annotate, a `MenuButton` with `icon` named "More for {label}", opening downward. It is drawn on a compendium entry the cupboard holds, offering Make It Ours (`mayMakeOurs`) and Remove (`mayRemove`), each on the permission its service checks. The coven's own entry has no More: its Delete Ingredient is in its Edit Ingredient dialog, and nowhere on the card or in its Quick Look (the owner's call in the MB.123 review; [`.claude/rules/components.md`](../../.claude/rules/components.md), "Destructive controls"). The dots are an icon on the card only, never words (the owner's call, 2026-10-20).
  - Each asks in place, in the dialogs the entry page shares (`MakeItOursDialog`, `RemoveDialog`), since a confirmation has nothing to share as an address (option A, the owner's call, 2026-10-20).
  - The choices sit under the one **Coven** section label every More has (`choicesFor`; the owner's call, 2026-10-20). More is drawn only with Make It Ours, which needs the delete permission too, so a role that may remove but not make it ours gets no More and no bin: the Annotate dialog holds its Remove. No role reaches that case today.
  - A write that takes the card away reads the list again, and the focus goes to the page's heading, More having gone with the card; Make It Ours goes to the new own entry's page. A Delete Ingredient, made in the Edit Ingredient dialog, says "{label} deleted" in the page's status region, which outlives the dialog and the card (`EntryDialog`, M9.10). A refusal shows in the card and gives More the focus back.
- The filter is `CompendiumListFilter audience="coven"`: it adds Source, In Stock and Low Stock Only, submits to `/coven/<slug>/ingredients`, suggests names from the coven's cupboard, and a pick opens the entry's Quick Look over the list as shown, at `?entry=<id>` with the filter and the page kept (the owner's rule of 2026-10-27, from the M10.11 review).
- With no entries it draws `EmptyCupboard` (`empty.tsx`) instead of a list, below.

## The empty and no-match states

With no card to show, the list draws one of two regions (M9.11). Each is a `role="status"` region, so a screen reader announces it, and each has its own name, so a test tells them apart by which one is present rather than by its words:

| Region           | When                                         | What it holds                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `No Ingredients` | No filter is in force: the cupboard is empty | "There are no ingredients here yet." and, when `canAdd`, an Add Ingredient button; otherwise "A member or owner of this coven can add them." |
| `No Matches`     | A filter is in force and matched nothing     | "Change or clear the filter to see more." and, when the filter has a search, Search the Compendium                                           |

- **Which state** is the search sentence's own test: `buildSearchSentence(...).hasFilter` (`IngredientSearch/sentence.ts`), with the filter normalised as the filter row normalises it. It counts the coven's own filters too (Source, In Stock, Low Stock Only), so an In Stock filter over a cupboard with nothing in stock reads as no match, not as an empty cupboard, and the region never disagrees with the sentence above it.
- **No Matches says only what to do.** The search sentence above the list already names every filter and says nothing matched ("No ingredients, matching Knitbone"), so the region does not repeat it. Its one sentence is `NO_MATCH_HINT` from `src/lib/text.ts`, the same advice that ends `noMatchText` on the admin lists; `noMatchText` itself is not used, because it always opens by naming the filters, which is exactly the repetition. The advice points at the filter row's own clears (Clear Name in the search box, the facet panel's Clear All), and the region draws no clear control of its own.
- **Add Ingredient** (`first-add.tsx`) does not build a dialog. It writes `?new=<slug>` into the address with `openHref` from `AppShell/add-href.ts`, and the shell's Add Ingredient control opens its dialog for this coven ([`modal.md`](modal.md), "Add Ingredient"). The page passes `canAdd` from the same permission that control checks (`ingredient: create` and `inventory: update`), so a viewer gets no button. In its place a viewer reads which roles can add, "A member or owner of this coven can add them.", so the empty cupboard reads as not yet filled rather than as a fault (MB.121, the owner's call of 2026-10-23; [`mb.121-workspace-ingredients-review.md`](../design-decisions/mb.121-workspace-ingredients-review.md)).
- **Search the Compendium** is a link styled as the filled primary button (`.btn .btn--solid`), going to `/compendium?query=<the search>`: a search that found nothing in the cupboard may still be in the Compendium. A filter with no search, such as In Stock alone, has nothing to hand on, so it is not drawn.
- **Layout** (`index.scss`, the owner's call): the empty cupboard's sentence and button are centred, set larger (the sentence at `heading-size(4)`, the button's label at body size) and set down `space(7)` from the filter row. No Matches stays at the list's left edge.

## The page

`src/app/(shell)/coven/[slug]/ingredients/page.tsx` draws it per request. It first calls `requireCovenMembership`, which sends a non-member to a 404, then reads through `reads.ts`. Nothing here is cached: a cupboard is workspace-scoped.

The page hands the `Membership` that guard returns to `readCovenIngredients`, which passes it as `heldBy` to the forms and categories reads (the entry page's rule, M12.24). That is what lets a card for a retired compendium entry the coven still holds show its forms and category chips: without the proof the services refuse a retired parent. The proof admits a retired entry only where this coven's live cupboard row names it. The read is wrapped in React's `cache`, keyed by the coven id, the filter, the cursors and the `Membership` object itself, which is the same object all request because `requireCovenMembership` is cached per slug.

**Last edit (M6.18).** `readCovenIngredients` also calls `lastEditsOf` (`src/modules/ingredients/services/last-edits.ts`) once for the page, and the page hands each card its answer as `lastEdit`: `{ by, at }`, or null for none. The edit is the newest `updated_at` of the coven's live jars of the entry (one batched `findStockOfIngredients` read under the coven's `Membership`) for the coven's own entry, of the entry's row too, and for a compendium entry, of the coven's cupboard row of it (one batched `findCupboardEntriesOf` read; the annotation counts whatever it holds, since adding the entry is a coven edit). A compendium entry's own editor, an admin or curator, is never shown, nor is another coven's annotation, so a compendium entry with no jar and no cupboard row of the coven reads as no edit. The editors' names are one `displayNamesOf` call for the whole page, so a card with an editor the viewer cannot name has `by: null`, and the card leaves out the "by" half ([`last-edit.md`](last-edit.md)). Tests: `tests/modules/ingredients/services/last-edits.test.ts`.

**Quick Look (M12.5).** Every member, a viewer included, gets the eye on each card. `?entry=<id>` opens the entry's details modal over the list ([`ingredient-entry.md`](ingredient-entry.md), "The details modal"), read by `readViewedEntry` in `reads.ts` with the same checks as `?edit`, so an id the coven cannot reach opens nothing. The page is its own `RouteModalProvider`, as the shell has none outside `/admin`, so the eye, Edit and Annotate each open at once on a press ([`modal.md`](modal.md), "Opening at once"). The details and the edit and annotate dialogs are two places on the page, so an Edit inside the details opens over them and closes back to them.

**The foot's controls in one place (the owner's call, 2026-10-20).** The window's icons follow the card it was opened from, so both are made by `covenCardActions` (`actions.tsx`): Edit for the coven's own entry or Annotate for a compendium entry the coven holds, each only where the page gave its address, then `CardMore` when the role may do more. `covenCardName` (over `cardName` in `IngredientCard/card-name.ts`) names the entry "Name, Formal" for every control, the card's and the window's alike. It is not a client file, only a composer of the client controls, so the page calls it once and hands the elements to the card and, with `leaveHref`, to the window. `leaveHref` is the list's address without `?entry`: a delete or a remove from the window goes there, replacing the history entry, which closes the window with the row gone, where a card reads the list again instead (`useDeleteEntry`'s `deletedHref`, `useCupboardWrites`' `removedHref`). The window has no plus, as the card has none.

**Edit on the list (M8.17).** A member whose role may update ingredients gets Edit on each card for the coven's own entry; a compendium entry in the cupboard and a viewer get none. Edit's address keeps the filter and the page, so the entry page's edit dialog (`EntryDialog`, [`ingredient-entry.md`](ingredient-entry.md)) opens over the list as it was, and Close or a save comes back to it. `readEditedEntry` in `reads.ts` reads the entry `?edit` names as the signed-in member, with `entryValues` for the form. It returns nothing, and so opens no dialog, for an id the coven cannot reach or for a compendium entry, and the page reads nothing at all for a viewer's `?edit`.

**Annotate on the list (M8.17).** The same member gets Annotate on each card for a compendium entry the coven holds, a retired one included, and none on the coven's own entries. `?annotate=<id>` opens the entry page's Annotate dialog over the list, through the one `AnnotateDialog` (`src/components/IngredientEntry/annotate-dialog.tsx`) the entry page draws too, and Close or a save comes back to the list as it was. The dialog's **More** menu (the owner's label, 2026-10-19), or its lone button when only one is allowed, offers Make It Ours, which goes to the new own entry's page, and Remove, which removes the entry from the cupboard and comes back to the same search without its card; each is offered on the member's permission ([`ingredient-entry.md`](ingredient-entry.md), "Cupboard buttons"). `heldCompendiumEntry` reads the entry under the coven's proof, as the entry page does; an own entry, one the coven does not hold, or a viewer's `?annotate` opens nothing, and a viewer's reads nothing.

## Stories and tests

Stories: `States`, drawn inside the app shell with the coven switcher, nav and Ingredients heading, with a control that chooses Default, For A Viewer, Middle Page, Empty, Empty For A Viewer and No Matches; and `WithStock` (the full member foot: Edit or Annotate, Edit Stock, and More on a held compendium entry; the coven's own entry has no More). Tests: `tests/components/CovenCards/index.test.tsx`, `card-more.test.tsx` beside it, and `tests/app/(shell)/coven/[slug]/ingredients/page.test.tsx`; the browser flow for Annotate on the list is in `tests/e2e/coven-ingredients.spec.ts`.

## The More menu on a card

A coven card's More is the vertical dots beside Annotate, opening a menu downward. On a compendium entry the cupboard holds, it offers Make It Ours and Remove, each asked in place in the dialogs the entry page shares. Choices sit under the one "Coven" label every More draws.

No card carries a bin. The coven's own entry's Delete Ingredient is in its Edit Ingredient dialog, and a lone Remove is in the Annotate dialog, so a card with nothing but a delete or a remove to offer draws none. A write that takes the card away reads the list again and focus goes to the page's heading; a refusal gives More the focus back.

## The demo coven

`CovenCards` is the Ingredients list, not a card for the coven itself, so it draws no Demo badge. The badge sits in the coven page's heading alone, not in the switcher ([`workspace-switcher.md`](workspace-switcher.md)), and the layout's notice says it on this list as on every page of the demo coven ([`notice.md`](notice.md)).
