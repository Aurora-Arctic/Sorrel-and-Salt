# CovenCards

`src/components/CovenCards/` is a coven's Ingredients list: the filter row, one compact `IngredientCard` per entry of the coven's cupboard, and the pager. It is the twin of [`CompendiumCards`](compendium-cards.md) and render-only: the page reads one page of the cupboard (`CARD_PAGE_SIZE`, 24) under the address's filter and hands it over. `filter`, `counts`, `categories`, `vocabulary` and `total` are the filter state of [`compendium-list.md`](compendium-list.md), with Source, In Stock and Low Stock Only added.

## Contracts

- **`label` is what the coven calls the entry**, so the card shows the coven's name, not the compendium's. `kind` is `own` for an entry the coven made and `cupboard` for a compendium entry its cupboard names; `retired` marks an entry the curators retired. The card shows neither Compendium nor Annotated, so the list page does not work out `annotated`; the entry page and its head still show both ([`entry-marks.md`](entry-marks.md)).
- **The list is compact.** The switch that turns it off is drawn by the filter row inside the search landmark ([`card-density-switch.md`](card-density-switch.md)); the admin and public lists have none.
- **The card draws no forms line** (`forms={false}`): the jar lines in its children name each form held, and Quick Look lists every form ([`JarLines`](jar-shelf.md#jar-lines)).
- **The foot is built by `covenCardActions`** (`actions.tsx`), in this order, each part only where the page gave its input:
  1. The Edit Stock pencil ([`StockDialog`](stock-dialog.md)), when the entry carries `stock`, which the page gives only to a role that may update stock. It is the foot's one green control: Edit and Annotate take the plain text colour (`icon-tip__edit--plain`), where the admin's keep the green.
  2. Edit, for the coven's own entry, when `editHref` and `editHeading` are given (the heading is shown at once over a skeleton while the form loads).
  3. Annotate, for a compendium entry the coven holds, when `annotateHref` is given.
  4. More.

  Every control is named "Name, Formal" by `covenCardName`, because two entries can share a label. Quick Look's eye sits at the foot's left, in `startActions`.

- **Edit and Annotate start the sortable list loading.** Their dialogs hold ordered lists, so the foot draws them through `CardEditLink` and `CardAnnotateLink` (`sortable-links.tsx`, client files, since a composer cannot hand a client component a function): the list loads once the page is idle and when the link is pointed at, focused or pressed, so the dialog opens with no loading state ([`../design-decisions/graphql.md`](../design-decisions/graphql.md), "The date field, the sortable list and the coven choice load apart").
- **`covenCardActions` is a composer, not a client file**, so the page calls it once and hands the elements to the card and, with `leaveHref`, to the Quick Look window. `leaveHref` is the list's address without `?entry`: a delete or a remove from the window goes there, replacing the history entry, which closes the window with the row gone, where a card reads the list again instead (`useDeleteEntry`'s `deletedHref`, `useCupboardWrites`' `removedHref`).
- **More** (`card-more.tsx`) is the card's further writes, for a compendium entry the cupboard holds: Make It Ours (`mayMakeOurs`) and Remove (`mayRemove`), each on the permission its service checks, under the one **Coven** section label (`choicesFor`).
  - The coven's own entry has no More. Its Delete Ingredient is in the Edit Ingredient dialog and nowhere on the card or in Quick Look ([`.claude/rules/components.md`](../../.claude/rules/components.md), "Destructive controls").
  - More is drawn only with Make It Ours, because Remove alone is already in the Annotate dialog. A role that may remove but not make it ours therefore gets no More; no role is in that case today.
  - Each choice asks in place, in the dialogs the entry page shares (`MakeItOursDialog`, `RemoveDialog`), since a confirmation has no address to share.
  - A write that takes the card away reads the list again and the focus goes to the page's heading, More having gone with the card. Make It Ours goes to the new own entry's page. A refusal shows in the card and gives More the focus back. A Delete Ingredient says "{label} deleted" in the page's status region, which outlives the dialog and the card (`EntryDialog`).
- **The filter is `CompendiumListFilter audience="coven"`.** It submits to `/coven/<slug>/ingredients` and suggests names from the coven's cupboard. A pick opens that entry's Quick Look over the list at `?entry=<id>`, with the filter and the page kept.

## The empty and no-match states

With no card to show, the list draws one of two `role="status"` regions. Each has its own name, so a test tells them apart by which is present, not by its words.

| Region           | When                                         | What it holds                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `No Ingredients` | No filter is in force: the cupboard is empty | "There are no ingredients here yet." and, when `canAdd`, an Add Ingredient button; otherwise "A practitioner or steward of this coven can add them." |
| `No Matches`     | A filter is in force and matched nothing     | "Change or clear the filter to see more." and, when the filter has a search, Search the Compendium                                                   |

- **Which state** is the search sentence's own test, `buildSearchSentence(...).hasFilter`. It counts the coven's own filters too (Source, In Stock, Low Stock Only), so an In Stock filter over a cupboard with nothing in stock reads as no match, and the region never disagrees with the sentence above it.
- **No Matches says only what to do.** The search sentence already names every filter, so the region uses `NO_MATCH_HINT` (`src/lib/text.ts`) and not `noMatchText`, which always opens by naming them. The advice points at the filter row's own clears; the region draws none.
- **Add Ingredient** builds no dialog. It writes `?new=ingredient-coven-<slug>` into the address with `openHref` (`AppShell/add-href.ts`) and the shell's control opens the dialog for this coven; the slug is always named, so it is this coven's even when the bar's default is another ([`modal.md`](modal.md), "Add Ingredient"). `canAdd` comes from the permission that control checks (`ingredient: create` and `inventory: update`), so an observer gets no button. An observer reads which roles can add instead, so the empty cupboard reads as not yet filled and not as a fault ([`grimoire.md`, "Design review of the workspace ingredients page"](../design-decisions/grimoire.md#design-review-of-the-workspace-ingredients-page)).
- **Search the Compendium** goes to `/compendium?query=<the search>`, since a search that found nothing in the cupboard may still be in the compendium. A filter with no search, such as In Stock alone, has nothing to hand on, so the link is not drawn.

## The page

`src/app/(shell)/coven/[slug]/ingredients/page.tsx` draws it per request. It calls `requireCovenMembership` (a non-member gets a 404) and reads through `reads.ts`. Nothing is cached across requests: a cupboard is workspace-scoped.

- **The `Membership` is the retired entry's proof.** The page hands the `Membership` that guard returns to `readCovenIngredients`, which passes it as `heldBy` to the forms and categories reads. Without it the services refuse a retired parent; with it a retired compendium entry the coven still holds shows its forms and category chips. It admits a retired entry only where this coven's live cupboard row names it. The read is in React's `cache`, keyed by the coven id, the filter, the cursors and the `Membership` object, which is one object per request because `requireCovenMembership` is cached per slug.
- **Last edit.** `lastEditsOf` (`src/modules/ingredients/services/last-edits.ts`) runs once per page and each card gets `lastEdit` (`{ by, at }` or null). The edit is the newest `updated_at` of the coven's live jars of the entry (one batched `findStockOfIngredients` read), of the entry's own row for a coven entry, and for a compendium entry of the coven's cupboard row of it (one batched `findCupboardEntriesOf`; the annotation counts whatever it holds, since adding the entry is a coven edit). A compendium entry's own editor, a keeper or curator, and another coven's annotation are never shown, so a compendium entry with no jar and no cupboard row of the coven reads as no edit. The editors' names are one `displayNamesOf` call, so an editor the viewer cannot name has `by: null` and the card leaves out the "by" half ([`last-edit.md`](last-edit.md)).
- **Quick Look.** Every role, an observer included, gets the eye. `?entry=<id>` opens the details modal ([`ingredient-entry.md`](ingredient-entry.md), "The details modal"), read by `readViewedEntry` with the same checks as `?edit`, so an id the coven cannot reach opens nothing. The page is its own `RouteModalProvider`, as the shell has none outside `/curator` and `/keepers`, so the eye, Edit and Annotate open at once on a press ([`modal.md`](modal.md), "Opening at once"). An Edit inside the details opens over them and closes back to them. The window has no plus, as the card has none.
- **Edit on the list.** `?edit=<id>` opens the entry page's edit dialog (`EntryDialog`, [`ingredient-entry.md`](ingredient-entry.md)) over the list, which keeps the filter and the page, so Close or a save comes back to it as it was. `readEditedEntry` reads as the signed-in member and returns nothing for an id the coven cannot reach or for a compendium entry; the page reads nothing at all for an observer's `?edit`.
- **Annotate on the list.** `?annotate=<id>` opens the one `AnnotateDialog` (`src/components/IngredientEntry/annotate-dialog.tsx`) the entry page draws too, for a compendium entry the coven holds, a retired one included. Its Make It Ours goes to the new own entry's page; its Remove removes the entry from the cupboard and comes back to the same search without its card, each on the member's permission ([`ingredient-entry.md`](ingredient-entry.md), "Cupboard buttons"). `heldCompendiumEntry` reads the entry under the coven's proof; an own entry, one the coven does not hold, or an observer's `?annotate` opens nothing, and an observer's reads nothing.

## Not here

The coven itself has no card, so this list draws no Demo badge. It sits in the coven page's heading alone, not in the switcher ([`workspace-switcher.md`](workspace-switcher.md)), and the layout's notice says it on every page of the demo coven ([`notice.md`](notice.md)).
