# MenuButton

`src/components/MenuButton/` is a button with a label and a chevron that opens a menu of choices. None of the choices is the button's own: pressing it only opens the menu. It follows the menu-button pattern of the ARIA Authoring Practices Guide, as the bar's Add Ingredient chevron does ([`app-shell.md`](app-shell.md)). That chevron keeps its own copy of the keys because it has no label and joins a split button. Props are in `types.ts`.

## Contracts

- **Props that carry a rule.** `label` is the button's text and accessible name, and the menu's name, in title case. `menuLabel` names the menu where it differs ("Add Ingredient To" under the bar's "Choose Where to Add"). `items` and `sections` are never both. `ref` is the button, so a caller can give the focus back after a dialog a choice opened. `state` is set as `data-state`, so a caller can style a passing state, such as the plus's check after an add. A `MenuButtonItem` is a link (`href`, and an optional `onClick` that runs first and may prevent the navigation) or an action (`onSelect`, with an optional `disabled` and `mark`); `destructive` and `primary` are optional on either.
- **Sections.** A menu that holds both the compendium's choices and the coven's draws two labelled groups, **Compendium** and **Coven**, the viewer's own side first: the compendium's on the public compendium and the admin pages, the coven's on a coven's page. The caller says which comes first by the order of `sections`; the menu reads no address. `sectionsFor(choices, viewing)` in `sections.ts` makes the two and leaves out a side with nothing. `choicesFor` is what every three-dots (More) menu is given: it always returns sections, so a menu with one kind of choice still draws a lone labelled **Coven** group. A menu is never a single choice: a lone Delete or Remove is its own bin, drawn by its caller (`.claude/rules/components.md`, "Destructive controls"). The bar's Add Ingredient menu keeps a plain list where it holds one kind of place: it makes its sections with `sectionsFor` and draws them itself. Each section is a `role="group"` named by its visible heading (`aria-labelledby`), with a `role="separator"` between sections and none before the first. The heading is small muted caption text in the item's side padding, takes no focus, and the arrows, Home and End run through every section's items as one list. A menu given `items` is drawn as a flat list.
- **The green item is first within its section.** The item marked `primary` is drawn first, whatever its place in `items`, and the rest keep the caller's order. It is done where the menu draws, so no caller can put it elsewhere, and the keys follow what is drawn. In a sectioned menu it leads its own section only, so green marks the one action of its group. No caller passes two green items; two would draw in the caller's order ahead of the rest, which is not a decided rule.
- **The keys.** Opening, by a press or ArrowDown on the button, puts the focus on the first choice. ArrowDown and ArrowUp move through the choices and wrap; Home and End go to the first and last. Escape closes the menu and gives the focus back to the button. Tab and a press outside close it.
- **Choosing closes first.** A choice closes the menu and then runs its action or follows its link. A choice that opens a dialog gives the focus back to the button through `ref` once the dialog closes, since the menu item has gone.
- **A disabled choice stays in the menu.** An action with `disabled` carries `aria-disabled`, not the `disabled` attribute, so the arrow keys still reach it and a screen reader reads it as present but unavailable; a press, Enter or Space runs nothing and leaves the menu open. Its `mark` is a green check after the label (the tick `HeldMark` draws on a compendium card), and the mark's words join the item's name after a comma, since the check is a glyph and says nothing: a `mark` of `in its cupboard` names the item "Fixture Coven Two, in its cupboard". It has no description. It takes the muted ink and the default cursor, no hover fill, and keeps the focus fill so the keyboard can see where it is. The compendium card's plus, on the card and the entry pages, uses it for a coven already holding the entry.
- **It fades in and out** over `$motion-short`. A closing menu stays drawn until its fade-out ends, inert and hidden from assistive technology, so nothing finds or focuses it on its way out. A browser that never reports the end still hides it after 400ms, and under reduced motion it goes at once. A closed menu stays in the page with the `hidden` attribute rather than being removed, so a dialog that one of its links opened can find that link when it closes and hand the focus to the menu's button (`RouteModal`, [modal.md](modal.md#giving-the-focus-back)).
- **An icon alone.** With `icon`, the button shows the icon and no chevron and keeps `label` as its accessible name in hidden text, so a list card's More (the vertical dots, [`coven-cards.md`](coven-cards.md)) sits in a row of other icons. `buttonType` makes the items take the button's own face, size, weight, top and bottom padding and height (`space(2)`, `$control-height`), with the sides one step less than the button's (`space(4)` for `space(5)`), instead of the lighter, shorter item; only the Annotate dialog's More sets it, since its two items are the dialog foot's actions. A destructive choice stays destructive.
- **Its tip is shut while it is open.** An icon button wearing an [`IconTip`](icon-tip.md) loses the tip as the menu opens and gets none until it has closed. `IconTip` does it by asking for a button in it with `aria-haspopup` and `aria-expanded="true"`, so `MenuButton` knows nothing of tips and every caller gets it.
- **Busy is shut, and keeps the focus.** While `busy`, the button carries `aria-disabled` and `aria-busy`, not `disabled`, and shows the spinner and `busyLabel` in place of the label and chevron. A press and ArrowDown open nothing. It is not `disabled` because a browser drops the focus of a control disabled under it to the page body, so a keyboard user who chose a coven would lose their place while the add ran. `.btn[aria-disabled="true"]` takes the disabled look, as `.btn:disabled` does.
- **Where it opens: the room decides.** The menu is placed by the Combobox's `useListPosition` (`src/components/Combobox/position.ts`), on Floating UI, with the same rules as a list's ([`combobox.md`](combobox.md), "Where the list opens"). By default it hangs from the button's end edge, 8px (`space(2)`) under it (`bottom-end`), and flips over it (`top-end`) when there is more room above. It is shifted to stay 8px inside the screen and clear of the phone's fixed bottom bar, is fixed rather than absolute so a scrolling dialog cannot clip it, follows a scroll or a resize while open, and keeps its own width. The caller names no side: every menu hangs from its button's end edge, because a card's More sits at the end of its card, in any column, and its menu belongs over that card. There is no `placement` or `opens` prop.
- **It fills a stretched box.** The button grows to fill the box a layout gives it, as a narrow form's stacked foot makes every button full width; otherwise it is its own width.
- **The fills are inset and rounded.** The panel carries `space(1)` of padding all round, so an item's hover, focus and chosen fill sits inside the panel's edge. Every item's fill is rounded on all four corners with `radius('control')`, one step tighter than the panel's `surface` so the corners nest. The panel does not clip (no `overflow: hidden`), and `buttonType` sets no radius of its own.
- **Look.** The menu is the bar's Add Ingredient menu: a card hung under or over the button's end edge, small text; hover, focus and the chosen flash as the menu fades take the grey quiet fill, `$fill-quiet`. Green marks a menu's one primary action alone: a `primary` choice takes `$fill-highlight` (the accent 12% into the card), as Edit Ingredient does on the ingredient page. Link to the Compendium, Annotate and Make It Ours are never green. A destructive choice is in the destructive colour and filled with it on hover and focus, as `.btn--destructive` is. Every ink on its fill holds 4.5:1 in both themes (`tests/scss/tokens.test.ts`).

## Styling

`index.scss`, on the `menu-button` block, tokens only. The panel (`.menu-button__menu`) is a floating card at `layer('floating')`, at least `$menu-min-width` wide, with `$shadow-floating`; when the panel itself takes the focus its ring is `focus-ring($placement: inset)`. An item (`.menu-button__item`) has the variants `--primary`, `--destructive` and `[aria-disabled='true']`. `.menu-button__mark` is HeldMark's check at the glyph size; `.menu-button__heading` and `.menu-button__rule` sit in the item's side padding. The chevron turns over while open (`chevron-flip`).

On a phone the panel has no rule of its own: Floating UI hangs it below or above, whichever has room. Under reduced motion it appears and goes without fading. In forced colours the panel keeps its 1px border and the check is `currentColor`; a pointed-at item takes a 1px inset `Highlight` outline (not while it has the focus ring), the chosen item a `Highlight` fill with `HighlightText` ink, and a disabled item `GrayText`.

## Used by

The ingredient entry page's three dots, **More** ([`ingredient-entry.md`](ingredient-entry.md), "Cupboard buttons"), the coven cards' foot ([`coven-cards.md`](coven-cards.md)), the compendium card's plus ([`compendium-cards.md`](compendium-cards.md)), the Annotate dialog's More, and the bar's Add Ingredient chevron in part. The entry pages always draw More; the cupboard's More holds Remove beside Make It Ours, since every role that may remove may also make the entry its own, so a menu of one appears only in a story or a test.
