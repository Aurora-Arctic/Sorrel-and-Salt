# MenuButton

`src/components/MenuButton/` is a button with a label and a chevron that opens a menu of choices. None of the choices is the button's own: pressing it only opens the menu. The ingredient entry page uses it as the three dots of its icon row, **More**, for the choices that are not an icon of their own ([`ingredient-entry.md`](ingredient-entry.md), "Cupboard buttons"; the owner's calls, 2026-10-19 and 2026-10-20), and the coven cards' foot does the same.

It follows the menu-button pattern of the ARIA Authoring Practices Guide, as the bar's Add Ingredient chevron does ([`app-shell.md`](app-shell.md)). That chevron keeps its own copy of the keys because it has no label and joins a split button.

## Props

`MenuButtonProps` (`types.ts`):

| Prop         | What it is                                                                                                                           |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `label`      | The button's text and accessible name, and the menu's accessible name, in title case: "Edit"                                         |
| `className`  | The button's classes: `btn btn--quiet btn--medium`                                                                                   |
| `items`      | The choices, in order (`MenuButtonItem`), or `sections` instead, never both                                                          |
| `sections`   | The choices in labelled groups (`MenuSection`: `key`, `label`, `items`), in the order to draw them                                   |
| `busy`       | Optional: one of the choices is in flight. The button shows the spinner and refuses presses, keeping the focus                       |
| `busyLabel`  | Optional: the label while busy, "Adding"                                                                                             |
| `ref`        | Optional: the button, so a caller can give the focus back to it after a dialog a choice opened                                       |
| `buttonType` | Optional: the items take the button's type and height, with one step less at the sides; off by default                               |
| `icon`       | Optional: an icon drawn in place of the words and the chevron; `label` stays the button's name, hidden                               |
| `menuLabel`  | Optional: the menu's name where it is not the button's, "Add Ingredient To" under the bar's "Choose Where to Add"; left out, `label` |
| `state`      | Optional: set as the button's `data-state`, so a caller can style a passing state, such as the plus's check after an add             |

A `MenuButtonItem` has a `key`, a title-case `label` and an optional `destructive` or `primary`. It is either a link, with `href` and an optional `onClick` that runs first and may prevent the navigation, or an action, with `onSelect` and, an action only, an optional `disabled` and `mark`.

## Contracts

- **Sections.** A menu that holds both the compendium's choices and the coven's draws them in two labelled groups, **Compendium** and **Coven**, the viewer's own side first: the compendium's on the public compendium and the admin pages, the coven's on a coven's page (the owner's call, 2026-10-20). The caller says which comes first, by the order of `sections`; the menu reads no address. `sectionsFor(choices, viewing)` in `sections.ts` makes the two, leaving out a side with nothing and so a group that would label nothing, and `choicesFor` is what every three-dots (More) menu is given: it always returns the sections, so a menu with one kind of choice still draws its labelled group, a lone **Coven** section, and an empty side is left out (the owner's call, 2026-10-20). A menu is never a single choice: a lone Delete or Remove is its own bin, so its callers draw the bin and do not come here. The bar's Add Ingredient menu is the exception that keeps a plain list where it holds one kind of place: it makes its sections with `sectionsFor` and draws them itself. It follows the APG menu pattern: each section is a `role="group"` named by its visible heading (`aria-labelledby`), with a `role="separator"` rule between sections and none before the first. The heading is text and not an item: it takes no focus, and the arrows, Home and End run through every section's items as one list. The heading is discreet, small muted caption text in the item's own side padding. `primary` goes first **within its own section**, not above the other section's items: green marks the one action of its group, and lifting it out would break the order the page asked for. A menu given `items` is drawn as it always was. Stories: `States`, states 'Sectioned On A Coven Page' and 'Sectioned On A Compendium Page'. Tests: `tests/components/MenuButton/index.test.tsx`, "MenuButton sections".
- **The green item is first.** The item marked `primary` is drawn first, whatever its place in `items`, and the rest keep the caller's order (the owner's call, 2026-10-20: "in any menu-button, the green item should always be first"). It is done where the menu draws, so no caller can put it elsewhere, and the keys follow what is drawn: Home and the first ArrowDown land on it. No caller passes two green items; with two, the stable partition would draw them in the caller's order, ahead of the rest, but that is not a decided rule.
- **The keys.** Opening, by a press or ArrowDown on the button, puts the focus on the first choice. ArrowDown and ArrowUp move through the choices and wrap at the ends; Home and End go to the first and the last. Escape closes the menu and gives the focus back to the button. Tab and a press outside close it.
- **Choosing closes first.** A choice closes the menu and then runs its action, or follows its link. A choice that opens a dialog gives the focus back to the button through `ref` once the dialog closes, since the menu item has gone.
- **A disabled choice stays in the menu.** An action with `disabled` carries `aria-disabled`, not the `disabled` attribute, so the arrow keys still reach it and a screen reader reads it as there but unavailable, as the WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices menu pattern has it; a press, Enter or Space on it runs nothing and leaves the menu open. Its `mark` is drawn as a green check after the label, the tick `HeldMark` draws on a compendium card, at the glyph size, and its words join the item's name after a comma: a `mark` of `in its cupboard` names the item Fixture Coven Two, in its cupboard, since the check is a glyph and says nothing (the owner's call, 2026-10-20, in place of a note under the label). The item has no description. It is drawn in the muted ink with the default cursor, takes no hover fill and keeps the focus fill so the keyboard can see where it is. The compendium card's plus, on the card and the entry pages alike, uses it for a coven already holding the entry, marked in its cupboard (the owner's calls, 2026-10-20; the words MB.120's). Story: `States`, state 'Marked Item'. Tests: `tests/components/MenuButton/index.test.tsx`, "a disabled item".
- **It fades in and out** over the short motion, `$motion-short` (the owner's call, 2026-10-19). A closing menu stays drawn until its fade-out ends, inert and hidden from assistive technology, so nothing finds or focuses it on its way out. A browser that never reports the end still hides it after 400ms, and under reduced motion it goes at once. Once closed, the menu stays in the page with the `hidden` attribute rather than being removed, so a dialog that one of its links opened can find that link when it closes and hand the focus to the menu's button (`RouteModal`, [modal.md](modal.md#giving-the-focus-back); M8.17).
- **An icon alone.** With `icon`, the button shows the icon and no chevron, and keeps `label` as its accessible name in hidden text, so a list card's More (the vertical dots, [`coven-cards.md`](coven-cards.md)) sits in a row of other icons (M8.17).
- **Its tip is shut while it is open.** An icon button wearing an [`IconTip`](icon-tip.md) loses the tip as the menu opens and gets none until it has closed (the owner's call, 2026-10-20). `IconTip` does it, by asking for a button in it with `aria-haspopup` and `aria-expanded="true"`, so `MenuButton` knows nothing of tips and every caller gets it.
- **Busy is shut, and keeps the focus.** While `busy`, the button carries `aria-disabled` and `aria-busy`, not the `disabled` attribute, and shows the spinner and `busyLabel` in place of the label and chevron. A press and ArrowDown open nothing. It is not `disabled` because a browser drops the focus of a control disabled under it to the page body, so a keyboard user who chose a coven would lose their place while the add ran (MB.120). `.btn[aria-disabled="true"]` takes the disabled look, as `.btn:disabled` does. Tests: `tests/components/MenuButton/index.test.tsx`, "while busy".
- **Where it opens: the room decides.** The menu is placed by the Combobox's own placement hook, `useListPosition` in `src/components/Combobox/position.ts`, on Floating UI, the same rules as a list's ([`combobox.md`](combobox.md), "Where the list opens"; MB.154, M8.17). By default it hangs from the button's end edge, 8px (`space(2)`) under it (`bottom-end`), and flips over it (`top-end`) when there is more room above, as at the foot of a dialog or of the window. It is shifted to stay 8px inside the screen and clear of the phone's fixed bottom bar, is fixed rather than absolute so a scrolling dialog cannot clip it, and follows a scroll or a resize while open. It keeps its own width, as wide as its items. The caller names no side, and every menu hangs from its button's end edge, a card's More included: it sits at the end of its card, in any column of the grid, and its menu belongs over that card. The ingredient page's Edit, now the pencil at the end of the formal name's line, does the same; the `placement` prop whose `auto` once hung that menu from the start edge, when a wrapped header dropped the button under the title to the start, is gone with nothing left using it (the owner's call, 2026-10-20). An `opens` prop that named a side is gone too.
- **It fills a stretched box.** The button grows to fill the box a layout gives it, as a narrow form's stacked foot makes every button full width (M8.17); otherwise the box is the button's own width.
- **The fills are inset and rounded.** The panel carries `space(1)` of padding all round, so an item's hover, focus and chosen fill, whatever its colour, sits inside the panel's edge rather than touching it, and every item's fill is rounded on all four corners with `radius('control')`, one step tighter than the panel's `surface` so the corners nest (the owner's call, 2026-10-20: "add some internal padding that doesn't get covered by the hover and round all corners of the hover"). The panel no longer clips (`overflow: hidden` is gone), since nothing reaches its corners. `buttonType` sets no radius of its own, so its items take the same one.
- **Look.** The menu is the bar's Add Ingredient menu: a card hung under or over the button's end edge, small text, and on hover, focus and the chosen flash as the menu fades, the grey quiet fill, `$fill-quiet`. Green marks a menu's one primary action alone (the owner's call, 2026-10-20): a `primary` choice takes the highlight, `$fill-highlight` (the accent 12% into the card), as Edit Ingredient does on the ingredient page. Link to the Compendium, Annotate and Make It Ours are never green. A destructive choice is in the destructive colour, and filled with it on hover and focus as `.btn--destructive` is (the owner's call, 2026-10-19). Every ink on its fill holds 4.5:1 in both themes (`tests/scss/tokens.test.ts`).
- **In the button's type.** With `buttonType` the items take the button's own face, size and weight, as `.btn` sets them, and its top and bottom padding and height (`space(2)`, `$control-height`), with the sides one step less than the button's, `space(4)` for its `space(5)` ("not so much left and right"), rather than the lighter, shorter item every other menu keeps. Only the Annotate dialog's More sets it, since its two items are the actions of the dialog's foot (the owner's call, 2026-10-20). A destructive choice stays destructive.

## Styling

`index.scss`, on the `menu-button` block, tokens only:

- **The panel** (`.menu-button__menu`) is a floating card at `layer('floating')`, at least `$menu-min-width` wide, with `space(1)` of padding all round and `$shadow-floating`. Its own focus ring, when the panel itself takes the focus, is `focus-ring($placement: inset)`, inside its edge (MB.120).
- **An item** (`.menu-button__item`) takes `$fill-quiet` on hover, focus and the chosen flash, rounded with `radius('control')`; `--primary` takes `$fill-highlight`, `--destructive` the destructive colour and its fill, and `[aria-disabled='true']` the muted ink with no hover fill.
- **A mark** (`.menu-button__mark`) is HeldMark's green check at the glyph size.
- **A section's heading and rule** (`.menu-button__heading`, `.menu-button__rule`) are small muted caption text and a hairline, both in the item's side padding.
- **The button** is the caller's (`className`), with the chevron turned over while open (`chevron-flip`). A busy `.btn` takes the disabled look from `.btn[aria-disabled='true']`.

**On a phone** the panel has no rule of its own: Floating UI hangs it below the button or above, whichever has room, and the button fills its box when a narrow form's stacked foot stretches it (M8.17). Under reduced motion the panel appears and goes without fading.

**Forced colours.** The panel keeps its 1px border, so its edge survives where the shadow is dropped, and the check mark is drawn in `currentColor`. An item's hover and chosen fill is a background colour, which forced colours repaints; its focus also draws the inset `focus-ring` outline, which survives. The forced-colours pass over the compendium section, M14.3, a Wave 14 task, checks it (minted from MB.120).

## The menu-button pattern

`MenuButton` is a labelled button with a chevron that opens a menu of choices, none of them the button's own. It follows the APG menu-button pattern, as the bar's Add Ingredient chevron does (`claude-docs/components/app-shell.md`). Opening moves the focus to the first item. The arrows, Home and End rove. Escape closes and hands the focus back. Tab and a press outside close it.

An item is a link or an action, and choosing either closes the menu first. An action may be disabled, which is shown and reached but not choosable, with a note saying why. The menu fades in and out over the short motion: a closing menu stays drawn, inert and hidden from assistive technology until its fade ends.

It is placed as a Combobox's list is, on Floating UI. It hangs from the button's end edge, below it or above it when there is more room there, shifted to stay on the screen clear of the phone's bottom bar. It is `fixed`, so a scrolling dialog cannot clip it, and follows a scroll or a resize while open.

## Menu sections

A menu that holds both the compendium's choices and the coven's draws two sections, the viewer's own side first. The compendium's side comes first on the public compendium and the admin pages, and the coven's on a coven's. The caller says where the viewer is (`viewing`), since the menu reads no address. A side with nothing to offer is left out, so a menu of one kind has one section. The code is generic over the item so both menus, with items of their own, share it.

A More menu always draws the labelled sections (`choicesFor`), viewer's side first, even where only one side has items. A card's dots, whose choices are all the coven's, still show a lone "Coven" section under its discreet label. The bar's Add Ingredient menu keeps a plain list for one kind of place and draws it flat.

A menu never holds a single choice. A caller whose one choice is a Remove or Delete draws the bin instead and does not come here.
