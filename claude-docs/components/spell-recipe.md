# SpellRecipe

A saved spell as a page at `/coven/[slug]/grimoire/[id]`, and the shared body the grimoire's Quick Look modal draws. `src/components/SpellRecipe/` holds the presentational parts; `src/app/(shell)/coven/[slug]/grimoire/[id]/` holds the page. Props are in `src/components/SpellRecipe/types.ts`.

## Contracts

### The page

- It calls `requireSession()` itself (the shell layout does not cover it) and `requireCovenMembership`, which gives a non-member a 404.
- It reads the spell with `readSpell(session, workspaceId, id)`. `NotFound` and `Forbidden` both become a 404, so another member's private spell and another coven's spell look like one that does not exist. Visibility is filtered in SQL.
- Steps (`stepsOfSpells`), assigned categories and the last editor's name come from services, never GraphQL. Nothing is cached. The page and the modal both read through `readRecipeOf` (`grimoire/recipe-read.ts`).
- The skeleton shape is `page` (`covenTarget` already returns it for this path).

### The body

- **Order and Recipe Card layout.** Top to bottom: a back link (the ingredient entry's `BackLink`, [`ingredient-entry.md`](ingredient-entry.md): the chevron, and the way back through history to the grimoire as it was, either tab counting as the list), `LastEdit` above the title (where the ingredient entry puts it), the title with Draft and Private badges centred on the action icons' middle line, then the shared body: the intent (muted), `CategoryChips` with `showAll` (every chip, no +N fold), a facts strip, the steps, and the note.
- **Facts strip.** A `dl` under a hairline with up to four cells: Jar (`jarDescription`), Seal Wax (`sealWaxColor`), Moon Phase (`moonPhase`) and Day (`dayOfWeek`), each `dt` in the overline style. A blank or null value draws no cell; none set draws no strip. The moon phase carries the builder's `MoonPhaseIcon` before its text, a third larger than its 1.25em line size, and draws nothing for a phase outside the builder's list. Four columns, two per row in a column narrower than `$narrow-column` (a container query on the strip's box).
- **No Ingredients section on screen.** The steps carry the amounts. The print layout adds the summary (see Print).
- **Steps** are an `ol` named Steps with no visible heading, in `position` order. The position column is `$step-position-width` (1.5rem), right-aligned: room for two digits, and wider read as an indent. The amount leads the name in weight 500, the name in 400. An instruction-only step's text is muted, at the ingredients' size, and fills the number's line. A unit with no quantity reads "— tsp", the dash muted. `StepRow` draws every step with no editing controls, so the page and the modal read steps as the builder draws them, and the body keeps no step renderer of its own. The ingredient's name reaches `StepRow` as markup (name, formal name, Deleted mark, Quick Look eye) and its marks as `marks`. The form is stored lower case and set in title case by the component. The form icon is hidden here, as the step view carries no form group.
- **A step's note** reads from `body`, or from `note` where `body` is empty (the `note` column is read only until it is dropped). The step is a grid: position, amount, name and marks across, the note beneath in the amount's and name's columns, ending where the name ends so it never runs under the marks.
- **Marks** sit right-aligned at the row's end: Toxic or Caution (`SafetyMark`, from the ingredient and the form laid) and, for a one-off, `OneOffBadge` (`src/components/StepRow/one-off-badge.tsx`), a button named One-Off at the default badge size whose tip and description read "Used in this spell only; not kept in your cupboard."
- **A one-off ingredient** shows its own name and form, the One-Off badge (`badge--muted`: grey in the light theme, pale in the dark), no eye and no marks.
- **The note** is a paragraph in the body ink (`$text-primary`, where the intent is muted) under its own hairline, drawn only when there is one. The strip and the list are capped at the paragraphs' `$measure`, so the two hairlines end together. A spell with no steps says so.
- **Formal name** shows in brackets only where two different ingredients in the spell share a label. The same ingredient in two forms does not get one.
- **Quick Look eye.** On the page a step carries none: the side column's In the Cupboard line carries it, and `SpellRecipe` hands the body its steps with `look` cleared. In the Quick Look modal, which has no side column, an eye sits beside the name, its glyph at seven eighths of the form's icon size in a 24px box (the WCAG 2.2 target). It opens the ingredient's details (`DetailsDialog`) over the spell without leaving it, at the tab's address with `?spell=<id>` kept and `&entry=<id>` added. The page and the tab read it with the Ingredients page's `readViewedEntry` and draw it with `viewedDetailsOf`, without the card's actions. An ingredient deleted since, or one the service withheld ("Unavailable Ingredient"), gets no eye.
- **Heading level.** The Steps heading and `SpellRecipeSummary`'s Ingredients heading take a `level` (2 or 3, default 2), one below the heading they sit under: 2 under the page's h1, 3 under a dialog's or pane's h2. The Quick Look and the Spell Preview pane pass `level={3}`. The styles that place or hide the headings match either level.
- **A spell search row's** `match` reuses `IngredientSearchMatch`, with `slug` set to the spell id.

### The actions row

- The `action` slot takes the page's controls in a `.spell-recipe__actions` row at the heading's end. They are icon buttons only, in this order: Record a Working (the cauldron, in the accent green), Edit (the pencil), Preview, and a three-dots menu. Edit and Preview are drawn in the text colour (`icon-tip__edit--plain`).
- Edit is an `EditLink` to `?edit=<id>` for a role that may update the spell; the page renders the spell builder's dialog over it, closing back to the page. Preview ([`spell-preview.md`](spell-preview.md)) is for every role.
- `SpellMoreMenu` (`more-menu.tsx`) holds Duplicate, Print (`window.print()`) and Delete (destructive), in that order. Print is for every role; Duplicate and Delete only for a role that may do them. Its items are bare verbs; the dialog titles carry the noun. A role that may do neither (an observer) gets Print as its own icon button (`print-button.tsx`, the dots' ink, tip "Print", named "Print <spell>") rather than a menu of one. The service refuses an observer's edit and delete by id.
- **Duplicate** (`useDuplicateSpell`, `duplicate-spell.tsx`) is for a role that may create a spell (`canDuplicate`, separate from the dialog's `canCreate`). It calls `duplicateSpell`, which makes a private draft titled with " (Copy)" added, then sends the browser to the grimoire's Drafts tab with the copy's builder open (`/grimoire/drafts?edit=<id>`, `copied-href.ts`). The dots are busy ("Duplicating", with the spinner) from the choice until the copy opens, so it cannot run twice. A refusal is the callout under the menu. It asks nothing first, so no dialog needs its focus returned.
- **Delete Spell** (`delete-spell.tsx`) asks in a `Modal` titled Delete Spell, as a coven's own ingredient's page asks. The question (`DeleteSpellWords`, which the builder's bin asks too) names the spell in bold, with no quotes, then says in one sentence what deleting does: "It leaves the grimoire for every member of the coven, and nothing in stock changes." for a shared spell, "It is gone for good, and nothing in stock changes." for a private one. A caller that passes no `visibility` gets the private line, which is true of either. A shared spell can be deleted: the visibility lock governs widening, not existence. Delete is `.btn--destructive` and busy "Deleting"; Cancel is quiet. It is not a `ConfirmQuestion`, which asks over a form, and the menu has none. Confirmed, `deleteSpell` runs and the page leaves, replacing its history entry so Back does not return to a 404, for the tab the spell was listed on (Spells, or Drafts for a draft) with `?deleted=<name>` (`deleted-href.ts`), which the tab says in a Notice ([`grimoire-list.md`](grimoire-list.md)). The Modal, gone with the page, gives the focus to the list's heading. A cancelled dialog returns the focus to the three dots. A refusal is the callout under the menu. The spell card uses the same menu with `canPrint={false}`, which returns nothing when empty.

### The jump menu

- The page carries the shared `PageNav` ("On This Page", [`page-nav.md`](page-nav.md)) in the ingredient entry page's grid. The article's children are the head (back link and Edited line), the summary, the lead (the intent and categories, `SpellRecipeLead`), the menu, then the columns (the rest of the body, `SpellRecipeRest`, then the aside, then Workings). `.spell-recipe--with-nav` takes the `entry-nav-grid` mixin on screen. The Quick Look modal has no menu.
- From a phone's width up the menu is the left column, sticky, spanning the lead's row and the columns' (`grid-row: 2 / span 2`) so it has room to stick. Below that it stacks above the sections, after the intent and categories and over the facts, in the source too, so the keyboard and a screen reader meet the chips before the menu (WCAG 2.2 SC 2.4.3). The columns and main wrappers dissolve into the article's stack at that width, each box taking `space(2)` over the stack's gap. From a tablet up the lead is padded on its end to keep clear of the side column.
- The page draws the body's two boxes apart (`body.tsx` exports both); Quick Look and the Preview pane draw them together through the default `SpellRecipeBody`. The article fills the coven column from its left edge, and the text is capped at `$recipe-column` (`$measure`, the gutter and the side column with the jar).
- Entries are Steps, a The Cupboard group (Category Match and In the Cupboard), and Workings, each present only where its section is drawn: Steps with at least one step, Workings with the `workings` slot, the group with the `aside` slot. The menu is left out when none is. The two jar entries are `narrowOnly`, and `PageNav` hides a group whose entries all are, label included: from a tablet up the jar stands beside the recipe; below it the jar stacks above Workings, as the menu lists it, and comes before Workings in the source too (SC 2.4.3). From a tablet up the columns place the rest and Workings in the text column by explicit row, with the jar spanning both, so no `order` rule moves a section.
- The ids are `sections.ts`'s constants, which the steps list, `SpellWorkings`, the aside and the two panels (their optional `id` prop) carry, each with a scroll margin under the shell's bar.
- Print hides the menu (`.page-nav` in `_print.scss`), and the nav column is a screen-only rule, so the printed sheet is unchanged.

### Print

- Print is the "Print" item of the page's `SpellMoreMenu`, or its own button (`print-button.tsx`) for a role whose menu would hold nothing else. `SpellRecipe` draws neither itself.
- `summary.tsx` (`SpellRecipeSummary`) draws an Ingredients list, one line per form in the order of its first step, between the intent and categories and the facts and steps. Each line has the total amount (`needOf`, in the first step's unit when the form's units are of one dimension, otherwise listed with "+"), the label and, where two ingredients share a label, the formal name. Its heading names the list (`aria-labelledby`). It is `display: none` on screen (`index.scss`) and shows in print and in the Preview pane.
- `src/scss/_print.scss`, the one print stylesheet, sits behind `:has(.spell-recipe)`, so no other page changes; a browser without `:has()` prints the screen layout. The recipe's sheet is A4, a named page (`@page recipe`) that only the recipe's body takes, so every other page prints on the reader's own paper. Every page keeps the 15mm margin (`@page`). Both are enhancements: a browser without them prints on its default sheet.
- The root is 11pt and the article is capped at the sheet's 180mm content width, so a print emulation on a wide window lays out as the paper does. The Preview pane takes the same width in its own rem (`$recipe-sheet-width`, [`spell-preview.md`](spell-preview.md)).
- Spacing: the grid's `space(2)` gap, `space(4)` under the head, `space(3)` between the categories and the facts strip's rule, `space(4)` between the strip and the Ingredients row, `space(3)` between that row and the note's rule.
- Print uses the light tokens whatever the theme, on white paper (the body has no ground), sets a step's line at the body size, still muted, and hides the shell's drawings (`.backdrop`), the shell's bar, the back link, the actions, the side column, Workings (`.spell-workings`), the Quick Look eyes, the Edited line and any dialog. The Draft and Private badges print beside the title, so a printed draft or private spell is not taken for a finished, shared one. The shell's context row stays as the page's head: the coven's name as plain text, with no box, chevron, section nav or line under it.
- The sheet is a grid of two columns under a head that runs across: the title and badges, the intent and categories, the four facts across the top over both columns, then the Ingredients summary in a `$recipe-summary-width` (17em) column, its lines flush with the heading, no indent and no ✦, beside the steps, then the note, uncapped so its rule runs the facts' width. The columns, main and rest wrappers are `display: contents`, so the article is the one grid and each part is placed by row and column.
- The grid is the `recipe-columns` mixin (`src/scss/_recipe-columns.scss`), shared with the Spell Preview pane, with the headings at `$recipe-heading-size`. `body.tsx` wraps the steps, or the "no steps yet" line, in a `spell-recipe__steps-section` with a "Steps" heading, the Ingredients heading's twin, which names the list (`aria-labelledby`). On screen it is visually hidden, there for a screen reader, and the mixin draws it.
- At phone width the summary is not on screen. Where it goes is open work on the print and phone layout.

### The side column

- Only the page draws it; the Quick Look modal does not. It is the builder's two Jar aside panels on the saved spell: Category Match (`SpellBuilder/comparison-panel.tsx`) then In Coven Inventory (`SpellBuilder/inventory.tsx`), the builder's order reversed. Both are read-only here, since the page hands them the saved steps. Each has an `h2` (a prop; the builder keeps `h5` under its Jar heading), styled as the entry page's side blocks head themselves: the h3 size and no rule beneath.
- `SpellRecipe` takes the column as its `aside` slot and wraps it in an `aside` named The Cupboard (a landmark inside an `article` needs the name). It stands beside the body in the ingredient entry page's columns (`entry-section` and `entry-side`: the same side width and gutter) and beneath it on a phone. The article is sized to the paragraphs' `$measure`, the gutter and the column, so the column starts one gutter after the text ends, and the head is capped to the body's width, so the Edit pencil ends where the text does.
- At tablet width and up the column starts level with the facts strip's rule, not the purpose: the page lays the body's two boxes straight into its grid (`display: contents` on `.spell-recipe__main`), so the column takes the second row. With no purpose and no chips it starts at the top.
- `aside.tsx` (`SpellRecipeAside`, client because the panels are) hands the panels their props and loads their stylesheet, `SpellBuilder/panels.scss`, itself, so the page loads none of the builder's other rules.
- `readRecipeAsideOf` (`[id]/recipe-aside.ts`) reads each linked step's categories (`categoriesOf`, then `categoriesOfVariants`, the union rule) and the coven's jars (`stockOf`), one batch each. It makes them the builder's `SpellStepValue` through `jarRowsOf` (`[id]/recipe-supply.ts`, shared with the Quick Look's marks) and the builder's `jarOf`, with the assigned categories as the intended set and the group colours from `readCategories` as entries (a Map does not cross to a client component). A step the service withholds has no categories and no jar. No form icon or safety is read, since the column draws neither.
- **Workings.** `SpellRecipe` takes a `workings` slot, drawn beneath the body in the main column and pinned to the text column's last row. The page fills it with `SpellWorkings` ([`spell-workings.md`](spell-workings.md)); the Quick Look modal leaves it empty.

### In the Cupboard

- One line per form, at its first step's place. The line says what all the form's steps need together (`needOf`) against what the coven's jar holds, as `supplyOf` judges it. A one-off keeps a line for each step; a step with an instruction only has none. Each line is a `JarLine` ([`jar-shelf.md`](jar-shelf.md#jar-line)), which the Complete Working preview uses too.
- **Verdict.** `VerdictMark` (`SpellBuilder/verdict-mark.tsx`) stands at the right end of the name's line, so the name is cut short before it is. Out, Not Enough, Units Differ or Low show as a symbol alone, in a button named by the verdict whose tip says why. One-Off and No Jar draw no mark; the line's words say them. The button (`.spell-inventory__mark`) is bare, so it takes the shared `focus-ring` itself.
- **Doors** (`doorsOf`, read in the same pass as `doors`), for a linked step only, serve the page alone; the builder's own inventory carries none. A Quick Look eye (the same `QuickLookLink` the modal's steps carry, to this page's `?entry=<id>`) stands in a column of its own at the line's right: a 28px box (halfway from `$icon-size` to `$icon-size-large`) round a mark of about 20px, its margin keeping the line's height (`SpellBuilder/panels.scss`). For a role that may update stock (`rolePermits(role, { inventory: ['update'] })`, the permission `saveStock` asserts), Edit Stock sits behind the jar and the name: a `StockJarButton` ([`stock-dialog.md`](stock-dialog.md)) named "Edit Stock, <name>", opening on the step's form, with the ingredient's forms (`variantsOf`, read only for such a role) and the coven's jars through `stockDialogOf`. The button is the name stretched over the line, as the shelf's jar button is, with the eye and the verdict raised above it. An observer gets the eye and no button; an ingredient or form deleted since gets neither.

### The Quick Look modal

- `look-dialog.tsx` (`SpellLookDialog`, client, because `RouteModal` takes its body as a function a server page cannot hand down) shows the shared body in a `RouteModal`: the title as the heading, `LastEdit` above it, Draft and Private badges (`SpellMarks`) in the head's `under` row where they apply. The body arrives as the page's rendered children. See Full Recipe is alone in the foot bar (`ingredient-details-foot`, shared with the ingredient's Quick Look and defined in `_primitives.scss`).
- Edit is a pencil (`EditLink`, named **Edit <title>**, only when given an `editHref`) in the heading's `formalActions` slot. It links to the list's address with `?edit=<id>` in place of `?spell=<id>`, which the builder's dialog answers, so one dialog shows at a time and closing the builder returns to the list rather than to this modal. The head sets the Modal's `actionsUnderClose` ([`modal.md`](modal.md), "Controls beneath the ×"): the pencil stands on the middle of the title's first line, so a wrapped title keeps it level, and runs to the dialog's right edge beneath the ×, since a spell has no formal name for it to ride on.
- It has no Delete: a spell has an edit dialog, and Delete lives there and in the card's and the page's More menus.
- `failed` draws a Notice that the spell could not be read, under the title **Spell Recipe**, the name of what the dialog would have shown.
- With no side column, each ingredient step carries its stock verdict in its own row. `readLookedSpell` reads it with `readStepSuppliesOf` (`[id]/recipe-supply.ts`), one `stockOf` call for the spell, judged by the same `supplyOf` as In the Cupboard, and sets it as the step's `supply`. `Marks` draws it as a `VerdictMark` after the safety mark, so the modal and the page give a step the same verdict. The page sets no `supply`, since its column already says it.

## Styling

Tokens only, in four stylesheets:

- `SpellRecipe/index.scss` lays the page out: the head (its back link in the shared `.back-bar` row, as the ingredient entry page's is), the body's boxes, the side column, Workings, and the Recipe Card look of each `StepRow`. It hides the Ingredients summary on screen and keeps each jump target clear of the shell's sticky bar.
- `src/scss/_recipe-columns.scss` is the printed layout shared with the Spell Preview pane.
- `SpellBuilder/panels.scss` styles the side column's two panels, which the builder's `index.scss` also takes.
- `src/scss/_print.scss` is the paper: what print hides, and the white ground.

## Testing

Component tests are in `tests/components/SpellRecipe/` (`index.test.tsx` checks that each jump link has a target). `tests/e2e/spell-recipe-layout.spec.ts` checks the column, a jump and print. `tests/e2e/spell-print.spec.ts` checks that an observer can choose Print from the menu, one printed page, the controls gone, the context row as plain text with no line under it, the intent and categories leading and the facts above the Ingredients column and the steps, the Ingredients column left of the steps with its heading level with the Steps heading and its lines flush with the heading, Workings and the Edited line absent, a draft private spell's Draft and Private badges in the head, and that the recipe prints on A4 (another page on Letter), on white, with no drawings, the note's rule the facts' width and a step's note at the body size.

Stories are in `src/components/SpellRecipe/index.stories.tsx`, titled "Grimoire / Spell Recipe": `States` (Complete, Draft, Private, Quick Look, Quick Look Draft, Quick Look Private, Quick Look Read Only, Quick Look Read Failed) and `Phone` (Complete at 375px).
