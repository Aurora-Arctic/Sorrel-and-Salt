# Skeleton

`src/components/Skeleton/` draws the grey outline of a page part that is on its way. `TabPanelLoading` and `ModalLoading` are thin users of it. Props are in `types.ts`.

## Contracts

- **Shapes and their options.** `shape` is `table`, `cards`, `form`, `page`, `contents`, `recipe`, `detail`, `document`, `requests` or `line`. `filter` (a filter row above the list), `title` (the page's heading row, a heading bar laid out by `.page-header`) and `count` (rows, cards, fields, blocks or lines: 6, 6, 4, 3 and 1 by default; not for a `page` with a `menu`, whose sections are fixed) apply to the shapes that name them. `table` also takes `columns` (default three, `--skeleton-columns`) and `fields` (the filter row's controls, default two). `title` also takes `action` (a button-sized block at the heading's end), `tabs` (a tab track between heading and action), and with `tabs`, `tabsBelow` (the track on its own line beneath the heading, as wide as six pills, as `TabbedPage`'s `tabsBelow` lays them). `cards` takes `density` (`compact` draws the compact cards the coven lists open at, unless a stored Regular choice sits on `<html>`) and `card` (`spell` draws the Grimoire's spell-card placeholder: title, edited line, action row). `detail` takes `side`, the settings column, drawn for a keeper and left out for a curator. `page` takes `menu` and `head`. `head: 'eyebrow'` draws an eyebrow bar above the title and `'title'` a title alone with no line, each bar at the real head's height (an h1's size, a two-line lede) so nothing below jumps on arrival.
- **A `page` with a side menu** is drawn when its frame is `with-nav` (the coven home, the narrow column) or `entry` (an ingredient's page, the wide column: a Compendium entry in the `main` region, a coven's own entry in the `coven` region). The menu column sits beside the title, lede and sections, laid out by the page's own `.page-with-nav` and `.page-nav`. Below the head the sections are bare bars with no card surface, as the real pages have none. The coven home (`head: eyebrow`) draws Contents (a heading and two linked entries each with a line), Your Membership and Delete This Coven (a heading and a button) in `.page-frame__section`. The account (`head: title`) draws four `.page-frame__section.panel` panels of a heading, a line, a field and a button. The `skeleton__block` card survives only on a `page` with no menu.
- **An `entry` frame** fills the wide column beside the menu in the entry's own `.ingredient-entry--with-nav` grid (`entry-nav-grid`), with bare bars (no card surface) in the order the page lays them: Details (a heading, three text bars, the side column's blocks in `entry-section` and `entry-side`), a Stock part (a heading and three placeholder jars on a plank, sized from the jar shelf's tokens), then the forms ([`ingredient-entry.md`](ingredient-entry.md), "Layout"). `count` does not apply.
- **`requests`** draws the account's Support Requests tab inside the tab layout so the tabs stay put: a head line (a short muted-line bar at the left, a button-sized box at the right, the small button's width at the compact control height, 2rem), then three rows (`count`) of the list's own shape, each a subject bar over a meta line of a badge-sized pill and three short bars, at the row's padding (`space(3)` by `space(4)`) and `space(3)` apart. The tab sets `shape: 'requests'` in `ACCOUNT_TABS`; the page has no `loading.tsx` of its own.
- **`document`** stands in for a site document's editor (`DocumentEditor`) in the editor's own column (`.document-editor-column`, so it is as wide as the real page): a title bar, a lede line, two button-sized blocks at the start of a row, then a box 24rem tall, the text box's minimum height. With `title`, `tabs` and `tabsBelow` it draws the page's heading and tab row first. Each of the four documents' tabs sets `shape: 'document'` in `ADMIN_PAGES.documents`, and `adminTarget('/keepers/documents/privacy')` draws the whole page the same way.
- **`detail`** stands in for an opened support request in the route modal's body, in the `support-detail` grid the real detail uses: the facts, the Thread and the reply in the main column, and with `side` the settings column. `SupportRequests` passes it to `RouteModal` as `loadingBody`. The admin Support list is a `table` with `title`, `tabs`, `tabsBelow`, `filter`, `fields: 3` and `columns: 9` (`adminTarget('/keepers/support')`, and the view tabs' own `columns` and `fields`); it does not use `requests`, which is the account's tab.
- **`recipe`** stands in for a spell's own page (`covenTarget` returns it for an id beneath `grimoire`, bar `drafts`): the back link, the Edited line, the title with its row of action icons, the Workings menu in the left column, then the main and side columns in the recipe's grid (`entry-nav-grid`, `entry-section`, `entry-side`). From a phone's width down the title bar is two lines, the menu takes four rows and the side column stands over the main one. A spell's lead and facts are not drawn, as a spell may have neither.
- **`cards`** stands in for a card list: the public and admin Compendium draw it at regular density; the coven's Ingredients list (`CovenCards`) and the grimoire (`GrimoireList`) draw it compact. Inside an element that says `data-density="compact"`, unless a stored Regular choice sits on `<html>` (`data-card-density="regular"`), the column, gap and padding follow the compact cards (`IngredientCard/_compact.scss`), so the list does not jump when it arrives. The two coven lists put that attribute on a wrapper round their search. Skeletons drawn outside it pass `density: 'compact'`, which the skeleton writes on its own root: a navigation's from the section's target (`covenTarget` in `src/lib/coven-path.ts`), a tab switch's from the pressed tab. Stacked tables show a card-shaped row; the skeleton is a size container, so the table's rows turn to cards under the width the real table stacks at.
- **The filter row sits where the loaded page's does, at every width.** The heading row has no bottom margin in the skeleton, because the column's gap already spaces it from the filter row. Under the page header's narrow width the heading bar takes its own line, the action follows and the tab track goes last at full width, as the pages reflow. `tests/e2e/grimoire-skeleton.spec.ts` measures the row's top in the wait and on the loaded page, at 1280 and 375, for the Grimoire (both tabs), coven Ingredients, the Compendium and the admin lists. A list head with an eyebrow and a lede (`head: 'eyebrow'`, the public Compendium) draws them above the heading row, the lede on three lines on a phone. The Grimoire draws the New Spell block for an observer too, who has none.
- **A compact `cards` filter row ends with the Compact switch**: a label bar over a track bar (beside it under the forms' column, where the Filters button then shares the second line). The Ingredients and Grimoire lists both draw it.
- **Exactly one `<output>`, "Loading"**, visually hidden, so a screen reader hears one announcement whatever the shape. The drawn shapes are `aria-hidden`.
- **It shows at once**, with the short `$motion-short` fade (`arrived`): the press has already taken the old content away, and holding the skeleton back would leave a blank panel. Only the spinner waits for `$loading-delay`. It leaves by fading ([Skeleton fade](#skeleton-fade)); its own fade-in is off in the overlay.
- **Shimmer through `skeleton-shape`**, which uses `loading-motion`: a band of `$shimmer-duration` (1.4s), or under reduced motion the shared `loading-pulse` of `$pulse-duration` (2s).

## Styling

`index.scss`, tokens and shared mixins only. Every bar is a `span` inside `.skeleton__shapes`, drawn by the `skeleton-shape` mixin (`_mixins.scss`): the quiet fill, the band and the reduced-motion pulse. The block classes name the part each bar stands in for: `skeleton__header`, `__title`, `__tabs`, `__action` (heading row); `__filter`, `__field`, `__count` (filter row); `__head`, `__row`, `__pager` (table); `__cards`, `__card`; `__intro`, `__eyebrow`, `__lede`, `__menu` and `__entry-*` (pages). The cards' column is `$ingredient-card-min`, or `$ingredient-card-min-compact` under the compact selector; the table turns to cards under `$table-stack-width`. `.skeleton-frame` is the class `MainBody` adds to a `main` wait, which draws it from the top rather than centred.

`swap-panel.tsx` (`SwapPanel`) swaps a panel for the skeleton while a transition runs (`ListFrame`, `TabbedPage`).

## Skeleton fade

When a wait ends, the shell's bodies (`MainBody`, `SectionBody`) keep the last skeleton over the arrived page and it fades out over 200ms (`$arrive-duration`, the time `arrived` fades content in) while the page shows through. The skeleton fades rather than the page fading in because wrapping the arriving page in a box to run `arrived` would put a box round it that the section layouts' flex columns would feel. It is a client part, since the timer is the browser's. The same keyframe (`fade-in`) runs backwards.

- **Parts.** `useLeavingSkeleton(shown)` takes the skeleton while a wait is on and `null` when not, and returns the skeleton to keep for 200ms after the wait ends. `skeletonFadeHost(leaving)` is the class of the box the overlay is placed against: `position: relative`, and `overflow: clip` only while fading. `SkeletonOverlay` is the kept skeleton: `aria-hidden`, `pointer-events: none`, absolute at the host's top-left, out of layout, on the page's own ground. `heldPage(waiting, className?)` gives the page's box, while waiting, the `skeleton-held` class (`visibility: hidden`), `inert` and `aria-hidden`. `SkeletonCover` is the skeleton during the wait: a `skeleton-cover` box holding a pin that sticks at `var(--sticky-top, 0)`, the top of the window under the bar (the shell sets `--sticky-top`; `PublicFrame` sets `0px`). `skeletonCoverHost(waiting)` is the class of the box holding the page and cover (`position: relative`); while waiting it is a one-cell grid with both in the same cell, so a skeleton taller than the old page pushes the footer down rather than painting over it. `MainBody` puts it on its host; `SectionBody` on a box around its frame.
- **The page is held, not swapped.** During a wait the old page stays mounted, hidden, at its full height, with the skeleton over it. Swapping it for a shorter skeleton made the document shorter than the reader's scroll position, so the browser clamped the scroll, and Chrome saved the clamped position into the history entry when Next pushed the address: Back returned to the wrong place. `visibility: hidden` keeps the layout; `inert` and `aria-hidden` take the page out of reach of keyboard, pointer and screen reader. `display: none` and React's `<Activity>` collapse the height, which is the bug. The page is a child of the same element in both states, so React never remounts it, and a section frame keeps the page as its direct children, so its flex column and gap are untouched.
- **Out of layout and out of the way.** The overlay is absolute, so it adds no height; the host clips it while it fades, so a skeleton taller than the arrived page cannot lengthen the document. It takes no pointer events and unmounts when the fade is done, so it never covers a focused control after it.
- **One heading, one status.** The overlay carries no hidden `h1` and is `aria-hidden`; the arrived page has its own heading and landmark. The hidden `h1` exists only while the wait is on.
- **A new wait mid-fade** takes the skeleton back at once; the old timer is cleared.
- **No skeleton, no fade.** A navigation with no skeleton target (a refresh) leaves the page standing and nothing fades.
- **Reduced motion** shows the new content at once: the overlay is `display: none`.
