# TabbedPage

## What a tabbed page is

`src/components/TabbedPage/` draws a page divided into tabs, each tab at its own address: the page's `h1`, a row of pill links, one per tab, the open tab's action at the line's end, and the open tab's content beneath. Each keeper list shares a page with the group list that organises it (Categories and Groups at `/curator/categories` and `/curator/category-groups`). Every admin page but the compendium is one ([`admin-nav.md`](admin-nav.md) tables them). Other users: the coven's People group, the grimoire's Spells and Drafts, and the account (`framed`). Props are in `types.ts`.

`tab-switch.tsx` holds the client parts the row, the action and the content share. `Skeleton/swap-panel.tsx` is the keyed swap of content for skeleton that `TabPanel` and a list's `ListBody` share. `index.scss` is imported by the component because a list reuses the tab skeleton, with no filter row, while a filter or pager press is on its way ([`list-frame.md`](list-frame.md)). The heading is [`page-header.md`](page-header.md)'s, the skeleton's shapes are [`skeleton.md`](skeleton.md)'s.

## Contracts

- **Every tab keeps its own address.** A tab is a link to its own route, so it can be bookmarked, and its `?new`, `?edit=` and cursor links keep working.
- **Pressing a tab does not change the page.** On the admin pages a route group holds the tabs, and its `layout.tsx` renders this component through `tabbedLayout(key)` (`src/app/(shell)/curator/tabbed-layout.tsx`, over `ADMIN_PAGES` in `src/components/AdminNav/sections.ts`). A layout stays mounted while the address moves between its child routes, so only the action and the content swap. The open tab's `ADMIN_PAGES` entry sets each tab's `filtered`.
- **The action belongs to the tab.** Add Category, Add Group, Add Form, Add Deity, Add Tradition, Add Celestial Body, Add Zodiac Sign, Pause Keeper Changes on Users, New Invitation on Invitations, and nothing on Privilege Changes. The pills and the action are handed to `PageHeader` together as its trailing children, so the header needs no slot of its own.
- **The action comes from an `@action` parallel slot** (`node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/03-api-reference/03-file-conventions/parallel-routes.md`): `src/app/(shell)/curator/(categories)/@action/categories/page.tsx` renders Add Category beside `categories/page.tsx`'s list, one slot page per tab. Next renders a slot page again on every client-side navigation that changes its address (a tab switch, `?new`, `?edit=` or a cursor), since a page segment's key carries its search parameters. Every tab has a slot page, Privilege Changes one that renders nothing, because on a client-side navigation Next keeps a slot's last page where the new address has none. Each slot's `default.tsx` is the shared `src/app/(shell)/no-action.tsx` (the coven's People group uses it too). A slot page calls the guard as every admin page does. `ValueAction` and `GroupAction`, beside `ValuePage` and `GroupPage`, read the same address the same way, so the Add keeps the list's filter and cursor. Pause Keeper Changes reads the pause through a per-request cache the Users page shares (`src/app/(shell)/keepers/(people)/pause-state.ts`). New Invitation is a link to `?invite`, which opens its form over the list ([`admin-invitations.md`](admin-invitations.md)).
- **A direct request renders the whole page on the server.** A typed address, a bookmark, a refresh or an outside link gets the heading, tabs and content in the first response, with nothing streamed ahead. So the route groups have no `loading.tsx`, and the tabbed pages add no other `<Suspense>` boundary ([`../loading.md`](../loading.md)). Without JavaScript each tab is a plain link.
- **The group's layout reads nothing.** No session, no guard, no data. A layout does not re-run on a tab press, so a guard there would add nothing; the guard stays in `src/app/(shell)/keepers/layout.tsx` and every page ([`auth/admin-guard.md`](../auth/admin-guard.md)).
- **Each tab names itself** in its `page.tsx` `metadata.title` ("Category Groups"); `src/app/(shell)/keepers/layout.tsx` sets `title.template`, so the tab shows "Category Groups | Admin | Sorrel & Salt".

### Pressing a tab

- **A pressed tab greys the row and shows the loading state.** A plain left click on another tab navigates through `usePendingNavigation` (`src/lib/navigation/`), which runs `router.push` inside a React transition the row and the content area share. Next runs its navigation inside the caller's transition, so `pending` stays true until the new tab has rendered. A transition is used rather than a route group's `loading.tsx`, so a direct request streams nothing ahead. A provider already above (the workshop's) is kept rather than shadowed, and the context is exported so the workshop, whose stub router goes nowhere, can draw a tab on its way. A navigation that fails or is abandoned ends the transition, and the pill slides back.
- **While a tab is on its way** the row and the pressed tab are `aria-busy` (the row rather than the panel, whose status a screen reader should hear as it arrives); `aria-current="page"` stays on the open tab, since it names the page shown, not the press; the open tab's action is `inert`, so it can be neither pressed nor focused until the new action replaces it in the same render; the content area shows the pressed tab's skeleton beneath the heading and tabs.
- **Only a plain press is the row's.** A Ctrl, Cmd, Shift or Alt click and a middle click keep the browser's behaviour, by the pager's rule (`opensElsewhere`, `src/lib/opens-elsewhere.ts`). The open tab is left to Next's link and shows no loading state.
- **A filter and the pager show the same loading state, within the tab.** Each list's [`ListFrame`](list-frame.md) puts this skeleton, without its filter row, in place of the list under the filter, and greys the filter row as this greys the tab row; the tab row stays live. A modal's `?edit=` or `?new` link navigates within the tab with neither, so the list stays behind it; the modal shows its own skeleton ([`modal.md`](modal.md), "Opening at once").

### The row

- **A nav of links, not a `role="tablist"`.** Each tab navigates to another address, and a tablist promises arrow keys between panels shown in place. The open tab is found from `usePathname()` by `isAtPath` (`src/lib/at-path.ts`): the tab's own address or one beneath it, the longest winning, so on the grimoire's Drafts (beneath Spells) Drafts is open. A page with a single tab draws no row, since one pill to navigate between is noise.
- **Tabs told apart by query.** The admin support page's views share one path, so `open` (the open tab's `href`) replaces the pathname match. That page also sets `tabsBelow`, so its row sits on a line of its own beneath the heading at every width, by the forced break described under "The header at narrow widths".
- **A tab's loading shape** is set on the tab. `shape` is `table` by default; the keeper's four Documents tabs set `document`; the Grimoire's Spells and Drafts tabs set `cards` with `density: 'compact'` and `card: 'spell'`, the compact spell-sized cards the list opens at. `sections` is for the account's tabs, whose content is a jump menu beside stacked panels: the skeleton draws the menu and `sections` panels (3 on Account, 4 on Sign-In; the Support Requests tab is a `table`) in the page's own `.page-with-nav` grid, with no head of its own. `columns` and `fields` size the table skeleton when the table has more than three columns or the filter more than two controls; the admin Support views set both, with `shape: 'table'`.

## Styling

Tokens named here are in `src/scss/`. The heading line is the `.page-header` primitive ([`styling.md`](../styling.md), "Buttons"): the action is pushed to the end by an auto margin, and the pills are centred on the line, not brought down to the heading's letters as an action is.

### The sliding pill

The pill is one element beneath the links, placed by `tab-links.tsx` under the open tab, or the pressed one while it is on its way. It is placed before the slide is switched on, so the first placing does not slide in from the row's start, and placed again whenever its tab or the track changes size. The server draws the pill on the open link itself, so it is there before the script runs; once the sliding pill is placed, the row's `data-sliding` hands the drawing over. It is shown by `inherit`, not `visible`, because a page held hidden under a navigation's skeleton (`.skeleton-held`, see [`skeleton.md`](skeleton.md)) hides it by inheritance. It is not a View Transition: Next's (`node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/02-guides/view-transitions.md`) rides on an experimental flag and animates whole-page snapshots, and a tab press should move one pill.

### The track and the pills

- **The track's wash** (`$segment-track`) is the muted ink at 10%, fainter than `$fill-quiet`'s 18% so the open pill stands apart; a green wash sat too close. The row inside carries no padding of its own.
- **The open tab** is the accent 18% into the card, edged 1px in the accent; the fill alone sits under WCAG 1.4.11's 3:1 against the track, so the edge carries it. The mixes are local to the stylesheet. `tests/scss/tokens.test.ts` holds the open label and edge, and the muted labels on the track, to their floors in both themes.
- **Each hit area is a control tall.** A transparent `::before` pads each link back to `$control-height`. The row inside the track is itself a control tall, centred and overflowing the track equally, and its scrollport is where the padding lives: a scrolling box clips what sticks out of it, so the padding sits inside the row.
- **Focus** is the accent ring drawn 2px inside the pill, where the track would clip it: the shared ring's 66.6% in dark, full strength in light, where any fade takes it under 3:1 (2.75 even at 75%; [`styling.md`](../styling.md), "The focus ring").
- **Pressed**, while a pointer is down, the pill's fill shows under the label and it sinks a pixel, so a press answers before the pill slides over. A tap marks it through `pressFeedback` (`src/lib/press-feedback.ts`; [`styling.md`](../styling.md), "Buttons"), since a phone applies `:active` late or not at all.
- **A long row scrolls sideways** inside the track rather than wrapping or squeezing; the label cut off at the edge says there is more. People's three tabs need about 290px and fit at 375, so this is a safeguard. `tab-links.tsx` centres its tab by setting the track's `scrollLeft`, never `scrollIntoView()`, which would scroll the page too.
- **While a tab is on its way** every tab takes the disabled look ([`styling.md`](../styling.md), "Disabled is dashed and faded"). The pill fades with them, colour taken out, so it reads as on its way rather than arrived; its edge then falls under 3:1 in light while every tab is disabled. The action fades the same way so a solid button greys as the tabs do.
- **The loading skeleton** shows the list it stands in for, so the page does not snap from one tab to the next: the filter row where the tab has one (search box and a narrower select, edge to edge, no Add, which sits beside the heading), a table's heading and six rows, then the pager's two ends; or the grid of cards. One skeleton serves every tab and every list's filter and pager press; a tab with no filter, and a list whose filter row stays, leave the row out. It is a size container: under `$table-stack-width` of its own width the heading goes and each row is a card, as `.data-table--stack`; under 28rem the filter's shapes stack full width. Shapes, fill and shimmer live in the shared [`Skeleton`](skeleton.md).
- **Motion.** The skeleton fades in over 150ms as the press takes the old content away, and the new content fades in over 200ms (`data-arrived` on the content area, set only after a tab switch, so a direct request's content shows at once). The skeleton is shown at once, not held back: the press has already removed the old content, so a held-back skeleton would leave the panel blank. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the shimmer becomes the loading standard's one slow opacity pulse, never still shapes ([`../loading.md`](../loading.md)).
- **What a screen reader hears.** The shapes are `aria-hidden`; the one status is a visually hidden `<output>` saying the tab is loading.

## The header at narrow widths

Widths are the header's own (a container query on `page-header`). All thresholds are tokens: `$narrow-column` (28rem), `$tabbed-plus-width` (680px), `$tabbed-reflow-width` (536px), `$tabbed-reflow-width-labelled` (700px), `$tabbed-action-below-width` (400px), `$add-below-width` (350px).

- **At `$narrow-column` and under** the pills drop under the heading at the same size, not stretched to the line as an action is. The selector is doubled to outweigh the primitive's narrow rules ([`page-header.md`](page-header.md#styling)). The track shrinks below its pills' width rather than overflow, so the row scrolls.
- **The Add** is a plus beside the heading from `$tabbed-plus-width` down. The pills stay on the line beside it until `$tabbed-reflow-width`, a separate threshold on purpose. There the heading takes the line less the plus, and the pills, first in the markup, are `order`ed after the action so they drop beneath. Between the two thresholds the heading yields first: a zero basis keeps the pills and plus on the line and a long heading wraps its own words rather than sending the plus to a line of its own. At `$add-below-width` and under the Add is the labelled button again, beneath the heading, with the pills on a line of their own below it.
- **Any other action** (Pause Keeper Changes, a locked control) keeps the heading's line at its end. From `$tabbed-reflow-width-labelled` down the pills drop beneath the two at their own size. A flex box cannot otherwise give an item a line of its own at its own width, so a line break is forced by a pseudo-element the width of the line and no height, ordered between the action and the pills. The empty line it makes would take a row gap above and below, so the pills give one back with a negative top margin. The two keep the line through the forms' column until `$tabbed-action-below-width`, where the action goes beneath the heading at its natural width and the line's start. An action that is not the Add never wraps to the line's end.
- **A `framed` page** (the account) differs at every width. Its tabs hold a `.page-with-nav`, which centres a menu and a column of sections, so the head stands over the sections' column, up to `$narrow-column` wide; its start is offset by the grid's arithmetic. Its pills sit on their own line beneath the heading at every width, by the same forced break. Phone widths need no offset, as the page-with-nav is then a single column. A framed page is its own frame (no `.page-column` wraps it), so `.tabbed-page--framed` carries the frames' side gutter at every width.
