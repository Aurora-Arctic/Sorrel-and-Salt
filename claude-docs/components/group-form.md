# GroupForm

`src/components/GroupForm/` is the admin's form for a group (M5.6b). One component serves every group vocabulary, `kind: 'category' | 'form' | 'tradition'`: MB.132's deity traditions take it as the third. It has a name and a description. A category group adds its chip's two colours, each a [`ChipColorField`](chip-color-field.md). The form saves through `createCategoryGroup` and `updateCategoryGroup`, the form-group pair, or `createDeityTradition` and `updateDeityTradition`. On an existing group it offers Delete Group (Delete Tradition), which moves the group's rows before deleting it. It holds no modal of its own: the page puts it in one.

It is [`GroupedValueForm`](grouped-value-form.md)'s shape, and everything that page says about field errors, required marks, save rules and title-case buttons holds here. What follows is what a group adds.

## Props

`GroupFormProps` (`types.ts`):

| Prop          | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `kind`        | `'category'`, `'form'` or `'tradition'`: which mutations, whether to ask for colours, and the nouns for the group and the rows it holds                                                                                            |
| `group`       | The group to edit, as `{ id, name, description, colorDark, colorLight }`, the colours `''` for a form group or a tradition                                                                                                         |
| `groups`      | Every live group of the kind, `{ id, name }` and for a category group `colorDark` and `colorLight`, alphabetical (MB.35); the move offers every one but `group`, and each colour picker warns of a colour too near another group's |
| `memberCount` | How many live categories, forms or deities `group` holds, which a delete must move; 0 by default                                                                                                                                   |
| `onDone`      | Called after a save or a delete, and on Cancel, after Discard Changes if anything changed; the owner closes it                                                                                                                     |

Without `group`, the form adds one.

## Kinds

Everything that tells one kind from another is its entry in `index.tsx`'s `KINDS`, a `GroupFormKind` (`types.ts`): `group` and `title`, what the copy calls a group, lower-case and title case ("group" and "Group", or a tradition's "tradition" and "Tradition"); `one` and `many`, the rows it holds ("category", "forms", "deities"); `moved`, what the move's confirmation says the rows keep; `describe`, the Description's placeholder, a one-line prompt; its resolver; and its `save` and `remove` requests. A tradition is a form group's shape under its own nouns, so a new group vocabulary is an entry there, its three mutation documents, and nothing else. The `group` noun reaches every sentence that names a group: the move picker's placeholder ("Choose a group", "Choose a tradition"), the no-other-group message, and the buttons (Save Group, Save Tradition; Delete Group, Delete Tradition).

## Contracts

- **The shared schema runs first, the contrast floor included.** react-hook-form runs `CategoryGroupInput`, `IngredientFormGroupInput` or `DeityTraditionInput` (`src/modules/vocabulary/validation/`) through the Zod resolver. A category group's colour under 4.5:1 on its own ground is refused beside its own picker before any request, in the service's words: "The dark theme colour reads 2.16:1 on the dark card — it needs at least 4.5:1" (MB.36, MB.43; [`../styling.md`](../styling.md), "Chips, badges and the solid-fill rule"). The form group's and the tradition's schemas drop the two empty colours, so their mutations never receive them. A `VALIDATION` error's `fieldErrors` land beside `name`, `description`, `colorDark` or `colorLight`. Anything else renders above the fields in a `role="alert"` notice.
- **A group holding rows is deleted only once they have somewhere to go.** This is the owner's call ([`m5.6b-admin-groups.md`](../design-decisions/m5.6b-admin-groups.md)). The delete runs in two steps, each a question asked over the form, which is dimmed and held until it is answered (MB.115, the owner's call; [`modal.md`](modal.md), "Asking over the form"):
  1. Delete Group asks with a `ComboboxSelect` labelled, in title case as every label is, "Move Its N Categories To" (or "Forms", or "Deities"; "Move Its 1 Category To" for one), offering every other live group, beside Continue and Keep It; the picker's label names the question. Continue stays disabled until a group is chosen. Keep It takes the focus.
  2. Continue asks the admin to confirm the move it names: `Move N categories to "<target>" and delete "<group>"? Each category keeps its name, its address and the entries filed under it.` For forms the sentence ends: `Each form keeps its name and takes its new group's address.` For deities: `Each deity keeps its name and takes its new tradition's address.` A category's slug is its name alone, so a moved category keeps its address. A form's slug carries its group's name and a deity's its tradition's, so each moved row is re-slugged ([`mb.132-admin-deities.md`](../design-decisions/mb.132-admin-deities.md)). Move and Delete sends the delete with `moveTo`. Back, which takes the focus, returns to the choice, still chosen. Each step is its own question, keyed by its step, so each takes the focus on its own safe answer; Keep It puts the delete away and the focus back on Delete Group.

  If the group holds no rows, Delete Group asks plainly instead (`Delete "<group>"? No categories are filed under it.`, or `No forms…`, `No deities…`) and sends no `moveTo`. If the group holds rows and no other group exists, the form says so (`"<group>" holds N categories, and there's no other group to move them to. Add another group first, then delete this one.`, or "tradition" for a tradition) and offers only Keep It.

- **A refused move lands beside the picker.** A `VALIDATION` issue at `['moveTo']`, such as a moved form or deity colliding with one already in the target, returns the admin to the choice with the service's sentence under the picker. Any other refusal of the delete shows in the alert notice, and the form stays open with the delete withdrawn.
- **The sample chip carries the name as typed**, or "Sample" before there is one, so the admin sees the chip they are making.
- **Save is offered only when there is something to save**, by `isDirty`. From the press to the answer it is busy and says so, as a [`BusyButton`](busy-button.md): "Saving Group" or "Saving Tradition" behind the shared `.spinner`. The delete's final button is one too, busy as "Deleting".
- **Unsaved changes are asked about** (MB.115, the owner's call). While the form has changes, its `formState.isDirty`, the flag that turns Save on, Cancel asks "You have unsaved changes. Discard them?" over the form, ahead of a delete step it replaces until answered, beside Discard Changes, which calls `onDone`, and Keep Editing, which goes back to the form as it was. The modal's Close button, Escape and a click outside ask the same, through the form's guard (`useDiscardGuard`, [`modal.md`](modal.md), "Unsaved changes"). With nothing changed, Cancel calls `onDone` at once; a save or a delete never asks.
- **Buttons are title case**: Save Group, Cancel, Delete Group, Continue, Keep It, Move and Delete, Back, Delete, Discard Changes, Keep Editing; a tradition's Save Tradition and Delete Tradition. Save and Continue are solid. Cancel, Keep It, Back and Keep Editing are quiet. Delete Group (Delete Tradition), Move and Delete, Delete and Discard Changes are destructive.

## The pair

A category group's two colours are one family, the owner's call ([`m5.6b-admin-groups.md`](../design-decisions/m5.6b-admin-groups.md), "A category group's pair keeps one hue"):

- **The colour chosen first fills its partner.** Once one colour is a whole hex and the other is empty, the form sets the other to `pairedColor` (`src/lib/group-colors.ts`): the same hue, the saturation scaled as the seeds scale it, and the lightness nearest the partner's ground that clears 4.6:1 there. A grey fills a grey.
- **The partner follows until the admin sets it.** The form remembers the value it filled, and refills while the partner still holds it, so a picker dragged across the wheel takes its partner along. Typing or picking the partner stops it. An existing group's pair is never refilled, since neither colour starts empty.
- **A colour too near another group's is warned of.** Each picker compares its colour with every other group's in the same theme, from `groups`, the edited group left out, and names the nearest within reach ([`chip-color-field.md`](chip-color-field.md)). It is a warning, not a refusal: Save stays enabled.
- **The schema holds the rule either way.** `CategoryGroupInput` refuses a pair more than 10° apart in hue, or a grey beside a colour, beside the light-theme picker, under the pair: "The two colours are 135° apart in hue — keep them within 10° of each other". It speaks only once both colours have passed their own checks.

## On the admin pages

`/admin/category-groups`, `/admin/form-groups` and `/admin/deity-traditions` are one page, `src/app/admin/group-page.tsx`'s `GroupPage`, each route's `page.tsx` naming its kind and keeping its tab title. It opens the form in a `RouteModal` ([`modal.md`](modal.md)) from the address, as `/admin/categories` does ([`grouped-value-form.md`](grouped-value-form.md), "On the admin pages"). `?new` opens an empty form. `?edit=<slug>` opens the group at that slug, read through `getCategoryGroupBySlug`, `getIngredientFormGroupBySlug` or `getDeityTraditionBySlug`, with `memberCount` counted by `countCategories` or `countIngredientFormValues` under `{ groupId }`, or `countDeities` under `{ traditionId }`. `src/app/admin/group-dialog.tsx` is the client edge between the server page and the form, since a server component cannot hand `RouteModal` the function its contents take: closing, saving or deleting fades the modal out, then `router.replace`s the page it opened over, cursor kept (`groupsHref`), and calls `router.refresh()`. An `?edit=` that names no live group opens nothing and says so above the list in the shared `MissingNotice`: "No group has that address. It may have been renamed or deleted, so search for it in the list.", or "No tradition has…". Renaming a tradition re-slugs its deities, as renaming a form group re-slugs its forms, so it moves their addresses on `/admin/deities` as well as its own.

## Styling

The form has no stylesheet of its own: it is built from the shared pieces and the form primitives ([`../styling.md`](../styling.md), "Form fields").

- **Name and Description are [`TextField`](field.md)s** in a `FormProvider`, each with its label row, required mark and a short placeholder, "Add a name" and the kind's `describe`, a one-line prompt such as "What the categories in this group share" ([`field.md`](field.md), "Contracts"). The description's textarea takes the primitive's height rather than three rows of its own.
- **Each colour is a `FieldShell` around a [`ChipColorField`](chip-color-field.md).** The shell draws the label, its mark and its hint, the info tip saying which theme the colour is for, the hex form, its 4.5:1 floor and the partner it fills, and names the hex box, whose placeholder is "Add hex"; it is handed no error, since ChipColorField draws the refusal itself, between its ratio and its warning.
- **The move picker is a `FieldShell` around the `ComboboxSelect`**, its error beneath it as any field's is.
- **The buttons are `.form__actions`**, which wraps, with Delete Group pushed to the far end by `.form__aside`. They stay where they are while a question is asked: the question is a `ConfirmOverlay` card over the form, with its own `.modal__actions` row ([`modal.md`](modal.md), "Asking over the form"). Under 28rem of the form's own width, every row stacks, full width, the card's too.

The alert above the fields is a `Notice` ([`notice.md`](notice.md)). A
failed request is read by the shared `readGraphqlIssues`
(`src/lib/graphql-failure.ts`, MB.116), which gives every field error of a
`VALIDATION` refusal at once, where `readGraphqlFailure` answers only the
first field it finds. The form places each issue under its field and joins
the rest into the alert. Anything that is not a `VALIDATION` refusal comes
back as one issue carrying the server's sentence, or `GENERIC_FAILURE` when
there is none.

## Stories

[`index.stories.tsx`](../../src/components/GroupForm/index.stories.tsx) has seven stories: `AddingACategoryGroup`, `EditingACategoryGroup` (three categories held, so Delete Group shows the move), `EditingAFormGroup` (none held), `AddingATradition`, `EditingATradition` (two deities held, so Delete Tradition shows the move to the other invented tradition), and `PhoneDark` and `PhoneLight`, the category group's edit at 375px in each theme. Each sits in the modal and its own iframe.

## Testing

`tests/components/GroupForm/index.test.tsx` answers the mutations with MSW in the route's own shape. It covers the kinds' fields, create and update with their variables, the contrast refusal before the request, a server field error placed, the two-step move with its variables, Back and Keep It, each step asked over the form in a modal (the form inert, the focus on Keep It, then Back, then Keep It, then back on Delete Group), no move offered when there is no other group, a `moveTo` refusal beside the picker, the plain delete, and its unsaved-changes wiring, the form in a modal (`tests/support/discard-guard.tsx`): Cancel and Escape asking after a change, and Discard Changes closing. `describe('GroupForm, for a deity tradition')` holds the tradition's own: no colours asked for and a create from the name and description alone, and its deities moved on a confirmed delete. The pages' half is `tests/app/admin/group-page.test.tsx`, run on the category groups, the form groups and the traditions getting only their path and their services: the guard refusing before any read, the first page of 25 and its "Page X of Y" counted from its first row, every group read for the move, the cursor kept, the traditions' `?query=` reaching their service and kept on every link while a kind without a search reads none, `?new` opening the modal of the page's kind — with the colours or without — and `?edit=` reading the group by its slug, filling the modal and counting what its delete must move, a slug no group holds an alert and any other failure thrown, and the way back to the page it opened over. How the form behaves once open is this file's. Its fields differ from `GroupedValueForm`'s — no Group picker, two colours — so it is not a row of that form's shared table. The shared pieces it is built from, `TextField`, `FieldShell` and `BusyButton`, have no test file of their own: this file covers them as it uses them.
