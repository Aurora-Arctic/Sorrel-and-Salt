# GroupForm

`src/components/GroupForm/` is the admin's form for a group. One component serves every group vocabulary, `kind: 'category' | 'form' | 'tradition'`. It has a name and a description. A category group adds its chip's two colours, each a [`ChipColorField`](chip-color-field.md). A form group adds its icon: a labelled radio group (Icon) of the six icons, then None last, as a compact strip (`RadioGroupField`'s `layout="strip"`, [`field.md`](field.md)) of icon-only segments. Each segment draws its [`FormIcon`](form-icon.md) with its name in a tooltip, and the chosen name is printed beneath. None is the generic salt spoon and is sent as `icon: null`, which clears the icon. `GroupValues.icon` is `''` for None and for the other kinds, whose schemas drop it; a form group's schema turns the `''` into `null` before the request. The form saves through `createCategoryGroup` and `updateCategoryGroup`, the form-group pair, or `createDeityTradition` and `updateDeityTradition`. On an existing group it offers Delete Group (Delete Tradition), which moves the group's rows before deleting it. It holds no modal of its own: the page puts it in one. Props are in `types.ts`.

It has [`GroupedValueForm`](grouped-value-form.md)'s shape, and that doc's rules on field errors, required marks, save rules and title-case buttons hold here. What follows is what a group adds.

## Contracts

- **Props that carry a rule.** Without `group`, the form adds one. `groups` is every live group of the kind, alphabetical: the move offers every one but `group`, and each colour picker warns of a colour too near another group's. `memberCount` is how many live categories, forms or deities `group` holds, which a delete must move (0 by default). `onDone` is called after a save or a delete, and on Cancel (after Discard Changes if anything changed); the owner closes the form.
- **Everything that tells one kind from another is its entry in `KINDS`** in `index.tsx` (`GroupFormKind`, `types.ts`). It holds what only the form says: `moved` (what the move's confirmation says the rows keep), the resolver, and the `save` and `remove` requests. The nouns come from the list's kind tables (`GroupList/kinds.ts`, `GroupedValueList/kinds.ts`). A tradition is a form group's shape under its own nouns, so a new group vocabulary is an entry in each table, its three mutation documents, and nothing else. The group noun reaches every sentence that names a group: the move picker's placeholder ("Choose a group", "Choose a tradition"), the no-other-group message and the buttons.
- **The shared schema runs first, the contrast floor included.** The Zod resolver runs `CategoryGroupInput`, `IngredientFormGroupInput` or `DeityTraditionInput` (`src/modules/vocabulary/validation/`). A category group's colour under 4.5:1 on its own ground is refused beside its own picker before any request, in the service's words: "The dark theme colour reads 2.16:1 on the dark card — it needs at least 4.5:1" ([`../styling.md`](../styling.md), "Chips, badges and the solid-fill rule"). The form group's and the tradition's schemas drop the two empty colours, so their mutations never receive them. A `VALIDATION` error's `fieldErrors` land beside `name`, `description`, `color` or `icon`. Anything else renders above the fields in a `role="alert"` `Notice` ([`notice.md`](notice.md)). A refused save that marks no field moves the focus to it; one that marks a field leaves the focus on the error summary.
- **Server failures are read whole.** The form reads a failed request with `readGraphqlIssues` (`src/lib/graphql-failure.ts`), which gives every field error of a `VALIDATION` refusal at once, where `readGraphqlFailure` answers only the first. `placeServerIssues` puts each issue under its field and joins the rest into the alert. Anything that is not a `VALIDATION` refusal comes back as one issue carrying the server's sentence, or `GENERIC_FAILURE` when there is none.
- **A group holding rows is deleted only once they have somewhere to go** ([`ingredients.md`, "The admin group pages: where a deleted group's rows go"](../design-decisions/ingredients.md#the-admin-group-pages-where-a-deleted-groups-rows-go)). The delete runs in two steps. Each is a question asked over the form, which is dimmed and held until it is answered ([`modal.md`](modal.md), "Asking over the form"):
  1. Delete Group asks with a `ComboboxSelect` labelled "Move Its N Categories To" (or "Forms", or "Deities"; "Move Its 1 Category To" for one), offering every other live group, beside Continue and Cancel. For forms each choice draws its group's icon before its name, the generic spoon where the group has none, as the chosen value does; the other kinds draw none. Continue stays disabled until a group is chosen. Cancel takes the focus.
  2. Continue asks the admin to confirm the move it names: `Move N categories to "<target>" and delete "<group>"? Each category keeps its name, its address and the entries filed under it.` For forms the sentence ends: `Each form keeps its name and takes its new group's address.` For deities: `Each deity keeps its name and takes its new tradition's address.` A category's slug is its name alone, so a moved category keeps its address. A form's slug carries its group's name and a deity's its tradition's, so each moved row is re-slugged ([`ingredients.md`, "The admin deity pages: the slug, the tradition's delete, one form"](../design-decisions/ingredients.md#the-admin-deity-pages-the-slug-the-traditions-delete-one-form)). Move and Delete sends the delete with `moveTo`. Back, which takes the focus, returns to the choice, still chosen. Each step is its own question, keyed by its step, so each takes the focus on its own safe answer. Cancel puts the delete away and the focus back on Delete Group.

  A group that holds no rows is asked about plainly (`Delete "<group>"? No categories are filed under it.`, or `No forms…`, `No deities…`) and the delete sends no `moveTo`. A group that holds rows with no other group to receive them gets a message (`"<group>" holds N categories, and there's no other group to move them to. Add another group first, then delete this one.`, or "tradition" for a tradition) and only Cancel.

- **A refused move lands beside the picker.** A `VALIDATION` issue at `['moveTo']`, such as a moved form or deity colliding with one already in the target, returns the admin to the choice with the service's sentence under the picker. Any other refusal of the delete shows in the alert notice, and the form stays open with the delete withdrawn. The focus goes to the alert (`settle('result')` from `useEntityForm`, [`notice.md`](notice.md), "Result focus"), which scrolls it into view, after the withdrawn question has handed the focus back to the delete button, and even when a field is still marked from an earlier save.
- **The sample chip carries the name as typed**, or "Sample" before there is one, so the keeper sees the chip they are making.
- **Save is offered only when there is something to save**, by `isDirty`. From the press to the answer it is a [`BusyButton`](busy-button.md): "Saving Group" or "Saving Tradition". The delete's final button is one too, busy as "Deleting".
- **Unsaved changes are asked about.** The form hands `formState.isDirty` to the shared guard ([`modal.md`](modal.md#unsaved-changes)). Its question comes ahead of a delete step and replaces it until answered; Discard Changes calls `onDone`.
- **Buttons are title case**; a tradition's are Save Tradition and Delete Tradition. Delete Group (Delete Tradition), Move and Delete, Delete and Discard Changes are destructive.
- **Undo and redo** ([`field.md`](field.md#undo-and-redo)). The form gets it from `useEntityForm` (Field), which also holds the unsaved-changes guard, the save and where a refused save is said, and GroupedValueForm and HelpArticleForm refuse a delete the same way, in the alert; the history attaches to its `<form>` and provides it to `FormHistoryButtons`; the hook listens for its chords on the form itself. It is off while the unsaved-changes question or either delete step is up. Undoing a colour redraws its hex box, picker and sample chip from the restored value, since `ChipColorField` re-derives them from its `value`. A colour and the partner it filled are one step.

## The pair

A category group's two colours are one family ([`ingredients.md`, "The admin group pages: where a deleted group's rows go"](../design-decisions/ingredients.md#the-admin-group-pages-where-a-deleted-groups-rows-go), "A category group's pair keeps one hue").

- **The colour chosen first fills its partner.** Once one colour is a whole hex and the other is empty, the form sets the other to `pairedColor` (`src/lib/group-colors.ts`): the same hue, the saturation scaled as the seeds scale it, and the lightness nearest the partner's ground that clears 4.6:1 there. A grey fills a grey.
- **The partner follows until the keeper sets it.** The form remembers the value it filled and refills while the partner still holds it, so a picker dragged across the wheel takes its partner along. Typing or picking the partner stops it. An existing group's pair is never refilled, since neither colour starts empty.
- **A colour too near another group's is warned of.** Each picker compares its colour with every other group's in the same theme, from `groups`, the edited group left out, and names the nearest within reach ([`chip-color-field.md`](chip-color-field.md)). It is a warning, not a refusal: Save stays enabled.
- **The schema holds the rule either way.** `CategoryGroupInput` refuses a pair more than 10° apart in hue, or a grey beside a colour, beside the light-theme picker: "The two colours are 135° apart in hue — keep them within 10° of each other". It speaks only once both colours have passed their own checks.

## Used by

`/curator/category-groups`, `/curator/form-groups` and `/curator/deity-traditions` are one page, `GroupPage` in `src/app/(shell)/curator/group-page.tsx`; each route's `page.tsx` names its kind and its tab title. It opens the form in a `RouteModal` ([`modal.md`](modal.md)) from the address, or at once from a press on an Edit or Add ([`modal.md`](modal.md), "Opening at once"), as `/curator/categories` does ([`grouped-value-form.md`](grouped-value-form.md), "On the admin pages").

- `?new` opens an empty form.
- `?edit=<slug>` opens the group at that slug, with its saved name as the title ([`modal.md`](modal.md), "An edit dialog's head").
- An `?edit=` that names no live group opens nothing and says so above the list in the shared `MissingNotice`.
- `src/app/(shell)/curator/group-dialog.tsx` is the client edge between the server page and the form, since a server component cannot hand `RouteModal` the function its contents take. The page always renders it, with `opened` (`{ heading, group, memberCount }`) only when the address opens a modal, and the form is keyed by the group's id.
- Closing, saving or deleting fades the modal out, then `router.replace`s the page it opened over, cursor kept (`groupsHref`), and calls `router.refresh()`.
- Renaming a tradition re-slugs its deities, as renaming a form group re-slugs its forms, so it moves their addresses on `/curator/deities` as well as its own.

## Not here

The form has no stylesheet of its own: it is built from the shared pieces and the form primitives ([`../styling.md`](../styling.md), "Form fields"). Each colour's `FieldShell` is handed no error, since `ChipColorField` draws the refusal itself. The confirming step is the shared `ConfirmQuestion` ([`modal.md`](modal.md), "`ConfirmQuestion`").
