# SiteFooter

`src/components/SiteFooter/` — the footer, drawn once by the root layout (`src/app/layout.tsx`) under every page: the shell, the public frame, the home page and the error screens. It is a column in the wide column (`.site-footer__inner`) holding the row (`.site-footer__row`) and the tagline beneath it. The footer sits under a hairline that spans the whole window (its own top border). In the row, at the left is the site name (`SITE_NAME`) as a link to `/`, in the heading face at the h4 size (`heading-size(4)`, 1.5rem), and at the right the footer nav, in the body face at the small size. The two are centre-aligned and wrap; at phone width the row (not the tagline) stacks, name above links, left-aligned. The nav carries a Support link and nothing else; later footer links are added to it here. Under the row, on a line of its own, is one small muted line in the caption size (`type-size('caption')`, 0.8125rem; `SITE_TAGLINE` in `src/lib/site.ts`, at most 66ch wide): what the site is, and that its reference is not advice.

The link is a [`SupportLink`](support-link.md) drawn as the support icon (as in [`ReportThis`](report-this.md)), bare: `.site-footer__support`, the `icon-button(2rem, 1.5rem)` mixin, with no border and no fill at rest and the quiet button's fill (`$fill-highlight-field`) on hover and keyboard focus. `--icon-ground` is the footer's surface at rest and the fill on hover, so the bubble's cut-out matches inside an `IconTip` with the tip "Support", and still named "Support" by a `visually-hidden` span. It opens the Support dialog over the current page. The dialog is mounted beside the footer in the root layout, so every page has one. Before the address is known, the link goes to `/support`.

## At the foot of the window

The layout wraps the page and the footer in `.site-frame`, a column at least one window tall (`100dvh` less the top inset). The footer's top margin is `auto`, so on a short page the spare room opens between the page and the footer and the footer sits at the foot of the window; on a long page it follows the content. The step of air above it (`space(8)`) is the bottom margin of whatever precedes it, so the gap is never smaller. A centred page frame (`.page-frame`) is shorter by the footer's height (`$footer-space`, `$footer-space-phone`) so the page and the footer together fit one window and do not scroll for it. The one exception is the home page: its frame (`.page-frame.welcome-page`) takes the whole window height, so the footer always follows below the fold, and `.site-frame > .welcome-page:has(+ .site-footer)` drops the step of air (`margin-block-end: 0`), so the footer's top edge sits at the window's bottom edge and one footer-height of scroll reveals it. The frame keeps no bottom padding either. The footer has a fixed minimum height (`$footer-height`, `$footer-height-phone`) so those numbers hold. Wide: 2rem of row padding, the 2rem icon button (taller than the 1.8rem brand line), a 0.5rem gap and one 1.21875rem tagline line (0.8125rem x 1.5), 5.71875rem in all. Phone: 2rem padding, the 1.8rem brand line (1.5rem x 1.2), 0.5rem, the 2rem button, 0.5rem and two tagline lines (2 x 1.21875rem), 9.2375rem in all; the tagline wrapping to a third line on a very narrow phone would exceed it.

## The salt stays above it

The footer is opaque (`$surface-page`) and full width, so in a browser without scroll-driven animations the fixed backdrop images do not show through it. Where `animation-timeline` is supported, the footer declares a view timeline (`view-timeline: --site-footer`, scoped on `body` with `timeline-scope`), and the salt image runs an animation on it (`salt-rise` in `Backdrop/index.scss`) that lifts its `bottom` by the footer's height as the footer enters the window. The footer therefore never scrolls over the salt; it pushes it up, as the sorrel is held below the bar. Both are inside `@supports` and marked "not yet Baseline" for the browser floor. On an entry page, where the images are absolute in a band at the page's foot, the animation is off.

On a shell page at phone width the section nav is a bar fixed along the bottom, so the footer's bottom padding adds `$bottom-bar-height` (the bar's own token, plus the device's bottom inset) to keep the link above it, and the salt's resting place is already above the bar.
