# ReceivedInvitations

`src/components/ReceivedInvitations/` lists the coven invitations addressed to the signed-in account, one row each: coven, role, who sent it and what they wrote, expiry, with an Accept and a Decline on each and the pager beneath. Its host is the account page's Invitations section (`id="account-invitations"`, `src/app/(shell)/account/page.tsx`), always shown, with the table's empty state when none wait. The `/coven` landing does not render it: it tells the account how many wait and links to that section, and the account menu shows the same count ([`mb.57-post-sign-in-landing.md`](../design-decisions/mb.57-post-sign-in-landing.md), [`m10.32-accept-from-the-landing.md`](../design-decisions/m10.32-accept-from-the-landing.md)). The props and `ReceivedInvitationEntry` are in `types.ts`; the page maps the service's `ReceivedInvitation` straight onto an entry.

## Contracts

- **The page pages the list** with `?after=` and `?before=`, read by `readPageCursors`, and ends the pager links with `#account-invitations`, so another page keeps the reader at the section rather than the top of the account page.
- **No permission check lives here.** The page reads through `listReceivedInvitations`, which filters in SQL by the session's own verified address, workspace tier only. An unverified account cannot reach the account page (`requireSession()` sends it to the email page), and the service reads nothing for one in any case.
- **No token or hash** is a prop, and none can be: the service never reads one for this list.
- **The answer column.** Its heading is "Answer", and the cell is `.received-invitations__answer`, a flex row holding Accept and Decline side by side, wrapping if the card is narrow. A refusal floats under its button, out of the flow, so the row holds nothing else.
- **Accept** (`accept-control.tsx`) is a client island. It sends `acceptInvitationById` with the row's id and stays busy until the router has opened `/coven/<slug>`. While it sends it is `aria-disabled`, not `disabled`, so the focus stays and a second press sends nothing. A refusal is said in the service's words in a callout under Accept, which keeps the focus, with a badge to bring the callout back ([`click-feedback.md`](click-feedback.md)); the button is usable again. A success announces nothing, since it opens the coven. Each button is named "Accept the invitation to <coven>", so rows are told apart by name. After `router.push` it calls `router.refresh()`: a navigation keeps the shell's layout, so without it the account menu's count would stay at its old figure.
- **Decline** (`decline-control.tsx`) is the second client island, a `ConfirmedAction` as the steward's Revoke is. The button is "Decline", `btn btn--destructive btn--small`, named "Decline the invitation to <coven>". It opens a "Decline Invitation" dialog that names the coven and says the steward will see that the invitation was declined and its link will stop working. Confirming sends `declineInvitationById` with the row's id. On success `router.refresh()` reads the page and shell again, so the row and the menu's count go, and the status region says "The invitation to <coven> is declined.". A refusal shows the service's words in a callout under Decline, which has the focus back. Nothing is granted or seated, and the invitation cannot be accepted afterwards by link or by id. Declining is final; to join, they ask the steward to invite them again.
- **The inviter** shows as "from <name>" only when known; a deleted account shows nothing. The sender's note sits on its own line beneath (`received-invitations__note`, a block).
- **No region of its own.** It is a `div.received-invitations`, not a labelled `<section>`: the account page's Invitations section is the region, named by its heading, so a second landmark with the same name would only repeat it. The table keeps its own name, "Your Invitations", rather than taking the heading's through `aria-labelledby`, because the heading already names the region, the lede sits between the two, and the same words twice in a row is what this avoids.
- **The empty line is live.** The section is drawn whether or not anything waits, so "There are no invitations yet." shows when the list is empty.
- **It lives in a `ListFrame`**, since the soft pager ends navigate through it, as `PendingInvitations` does.

## Styling

Tokens and the `.data-table` primitives, plus `index.scss`. The account page's card is narrow, so the table is always stacked there. The primitives draw a stacked row as a card of its own on the panel's colour, whose padding read as an inset with nothing to explain it, so in this table:

- **Rows run the card's full width**, with a `$hairline` between them and `space(5)` of room either side of it, so two invitations never read as one. The first row has no padding above it.
- **Three tracks per row.** The coven (with who sent it) and the answer buttons share the first line, Accept and Decline at the top right (the Answer column is `action: 'corner'`). Role and Expires sit side by side beneath, Role as wide as its word and Expires running on under the buttons, so a date keeps to one line at a phone's width. Each cell is placed by its column's class (`received-invitations__coven`, `__role`, `__expires`).

A refusal's callout is `ClickFeedback`'s, styled there.
