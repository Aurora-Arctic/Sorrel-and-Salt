# Admin Invitations

`src/components/AdminInvitations/` is the People page's Invitations tab, `/keepers/invitations`: New Invitation, a filter by address and by expiry within 24 hours, a page of the pending invitations the filter leaves (address, site role granted, reason, expiry, Revoke), the pager, and the invite form that `?invite` opens over them. It is render-only. The page reads the filter and the cursor off its address (`invitations/filter.ts`), reads one page of the list and its count through `listPendingAdminInvitations` and `countPendingAdminInvitations`, reads whether this keeper is held by the pause (`invitations/lock.ts`), and hands them down. The rules are in [`auth/admin-users.md`](../auth/admin-users.md), "Inviting a keeper". Props are in `types.ts`.

## Contracts

- **A region named "Admin Invitations", with no heading of its own.** The page's `h1` and open tab already say what it holds ([`tabbed-page.md`](tabbed-page.md)). The name keeps the sent notice and the rows findable as one.
- **Props that carry a rule.** `locked` is the pause's reason (`ADMIN_CHANGES_PAUSED_REFUSAL`) while keeper changes are paused and this keeper is not the primary one, and absent otherwise. The page sets it from `adminRoleChangePauseState` (paused and not `canToggle`), and sets `inviting` only while not locked. `InviteAdmin` takes `locked` and the `filter` and `cursor` its link keeps.
- **The filter is the shared [`FilterForm`](filter-form.md)**, named "Filter Invitations", inside the list's [`ListFrame`](list-frame.md), as the user list's is (`filter.tsx`).
  - **Email**, a search box with the placeholder "Search emails", narrows to addresses holding what is typed, in any case. **Expires in 24 Hrs**, a `SwitchField`, narrows to invitations lapsing within the next 24 hours. Both are filtered in the repository's SQL against the database's clock ([`db/invitations.md`](../db/invitations.md)).
  - The address carries them as `?email=fern&expiring`. `expiring` is a bare flag read by its presence, so a native submit's `expiring=` reads the same. `invitationsHref(filter, cursor, invite)` in `href.ts` builds every address the tab links to, and `INVITATIONS_PATH` is the form's action and Clear's.
  - The email applies from its magnifier or Enter and the switch the moment it changes, each carrying the other and opening the first page, since they name no cursor ([`filter-form.md`](filter-form.md#contracts)). Clear is offered only while a filter is shown and opens `/keepers/invitations`.
- **25 to a page, with the shared [`Pager`](pager.md)**, newest first, as the Users list does (CLAUDE.md rule 8). Prev and Next carry the filter and a cursor (`?after=` or `?before=`), with "Page X of Y" between them. They are plain anchors rather than soft links, for the reason [`pager.md`](pager.md) gives. The page builds the hrefs through `pagerHrefs` in `src/lib/list-href.ts`. A cursor that names no row of this list (one copied from another list) reads the first page, through `readNumberedPage`. A list of one page shows no pager.
- **New Invitation is the tab's action, beside the page's heading**, exported as `InviteAdmin` and drawn by `src/app/(shell)/keepers/(people)/@action/invitations/page.tsx`. The heading line and the list render apart, so the button cannot hold the form's state. It is a link to `/keepers/invitations?invite` (`href.ts`), and the page opens the form over the list by that address, as a value page's `?new` does.
  - A plain press opens the modal at once through the modal provider ([`modal.md`](modal.md), "Opening at once"). The link stays for a new tab or a press before the script loads. `InviteForm` takes `open: boolean` and is rendered always.
  - Both keep the filter and the page shown (`?email=fern&expiring&after=…&invite`), and the form closes back to the tab at that page.
  - While locked it is the locked control instead, and `?invite` opens nothing.
- **Revoke asks first**, through `UserList/confirmed-action.tsx`'s `ConfirmedAction`, in a modal titled "Revoke Invitation": "Revoke the invitation to <address>? Its link will say it was withdrawn." Once revoked, the page's status region says "The invitation to <address> is revoked." A refusal is said in a callout under Revoke, which keeps the focus ([`click-feedback.md`](click-feedback.md)). The row's Revoke, live or locked, is `btn btn--quiet btn--small`; the red is the confirm's own.
- **A locked control** is `UserList/locked-control.tsx`'s `LockedControl`: `aria-disabled` with the reason in a tip, and said again as an alert on a click. Each of these user-list parts brings its own rules (`UserList/controls.scss`), so the section stands on its own in the workshop without the list's stylesheet.
- **The invite form** (`invite-form.tsx`) is a `RouteModal` titled "New Invitation", the link's own name ([`modal.md`](modal.md)). Its fields:
  - **Email Address**, required, with the shared red asterisk.
  - **Role**, the shared `RadioGroupField` ([`field.md`](field.md)) offering Curator and Admin, each with the line Change Role shows beside it (`SITE_ROLE_ACCESS` in `UserList/roles.ts`). Curator is chosen to begin with, so that granting the People page is a choice the keeper makes.
  - **Reason**, labelled plainly, because a field is optional unless it carries the required mark and no label says "(Optional)". Its hint, "Kept with the invitation, and in the record of who changed what once it is accepted.", sits behind an `InfoTip` beside the label. It is the shared `ReasonField` and takes `.user-list__note` (a step of room above and `space(6)` below), as the user list's reason does.
- **The form's actions.** The row is the shared `FormActions` with its submit renamed ([`field.md`](field.md#form-actions)). Send Invitation stays disabled until there is an address and is a `BusyButton` ([`busy-button.md`](busy-button.md)), "Sending" while the request is out. Cancel is quiet, waits while the request is out, and closes back to the tab. With anything typed in either field, or Admin chosen, Cancel asks first ([`modal.md`](modal.md#unsaved-changes)), and Discard Changes closes. The fields are `InviteFields` in `invite-form.tsx`, rendered inside the modal so the guard reaches the modal's ways out.
- **A refused send.** `readGraphqlFailure` is given `email` as the one field the form draws an error under, so a `VALIDATION` refusal on `email` is said beside the field and any other refusal in a `Notice` alert in the modal ([`notice.md`](notice.md)).
- **A sent invitation** closes the modal back to the tab at the page shown, refreshes it so the re-read lists the invitation on the first page, and says "Invitation sent to …" in a `Notice` above the list (`admin-invitations__sent`). The address is this component's state, set by the form before it closes, so the notice outlasts `?invite` without the address riding in the URL. Opening the form again clears it. No link is ever shown: the mutation answers none.
- **No invitation waiting is a sentence**: "There are no keeper invitations yet." Under a filter it names the filters in use through `noMatchText` (`src/lib/text.ts`): "No invitations match “fern” and Expires in 24 Hrs. Change or clear the filter to see more."
- **Five columns**: Email, Role (Curator or Admin), Reason, Expires and Revoke, drawn by [`StackTable`](data-table.md), which gives each cell its heading as its card label and writes the table's roles out so it can stack into cards at phone width. Revoke is a `'foot'` action, across the card's foot.
- **The expiry** is [`LocalTime`](local-time.md) with `long` (`28 August 2026, 12:00 am`), in the viewer's own zone: a date and a time, since a link lasts seven days to the minute.

## Styling

The table is [`StackTable`](data-table.md): the `.data-table` primitive with the `--stack` modifier in its `.data-table-frame` ([`styling.md`](../styling.md), "Tables at phone width"). At desktop width no cell wraps and the table scrolls in its frame, as the user list's does, through `data-table--nowrap`. Under `$table-stack-width` each invitation is a card, its cells wrapping and each led by its column's name, with Revoke across the foot with no label. The filter row is the `.filter-row` primitive, which stacks at phone width ([`filter-form.md`](filter-form.md), "Styling").

The component's own stylesheet centres each cell on its row, holds the tab's column with the sent notice and the list's frame `space(5)` apart, styles the muted "None given", and sets the sent notice's margin and full line. That rule is `.notice.admin-invitations__sent`, compounded with its primitive, which the app scopes as `body .notice`.
