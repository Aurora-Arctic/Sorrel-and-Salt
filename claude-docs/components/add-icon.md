# AddIcon

`src/components/AddIcon/` is the plus: **Add to My Ingredients** as an icon button. It stands at a compendium card's bottom right ([`compendium-cards.md`](compendium-cards.md), "The plus"), just left of the pencil on the entry pages, and beside the pencil in the Quick Look window ([`ingredient-entry.md`](ingredient-entry.md), "The plus"). It is a client component of its own because a press needs the browser and the cards stay a server component. Its props are in `types.ts`: `NamedAddIconProps` is `AddIconProps` plus the entry's name, kept apart because a page reads the covens (`CupboardPlus`) and the name from different places.

## Contracts

- **One coven adds at once; two or more are a menu.** With one writable coven, a press sends `addCupboardEntry` there with no quantity and reads the page again. With two or more, the plus is always a [`MenuButton`](menu-button.md) of every one by name, even when only one lacks the entry. Choosing one adds there at once. A coven that holds the entry is a disabled item with a green check, named **<coven>, in its cupboard**.
- **It adds the entry and nothing else.** The plus puts the entry in the coven's cupboard and writes no stock. A form and a quantity are given afterwards in Edit Stock, which the card's three dots offer first once the coven holds the entry ([`compendium-cards.md`](compendium-cards.md), "The menu"), and which the coven card's own button also opens ([`stock-dialog.md`](stock-dialog.md)).
- **Its name and its tip are the words and then the entry:** **Add to My Ingredients: Testwort, Formalis testis**. The words on screen are therefore part of the accessible name (WCAG 2.5.3), and a list of buttons tells one card's plus from the next. The colon is there because a formal name holds a comma of its own. The menu takes the same name. The reasoning is in [`compendium-cards.md`](compendium-cards.md), "Names".
- **Busy keeps the focus.** From the press until the page has been read again, the plus shows the shared spinner and carries `aria-busy` and `aria-disabled`, never `disabled`, so the focus stays on it (the menu's button does the same in `MenuButton`). A press while busy does nothing.
- **The words are held once**, in `src/components/AddIcon/wording.ts` (`ADD`, `HELD_ALL_TIP`, `HELD_TIP`, `HELD_MARK`, `HELD_IN_COVENS_TITLE`), imported by the plus, both `HeldMark`s and the entry page's summary. The e2e specs keep their own copy in `tests/support/cupboard-wording.ts`, so a changed word fails them.
- **The plus and the menu share one wrapper:** `control` is the `MenuButton` or the button, and the component returns it inside one `IconTip` beside the feedback.
- **After an add**, the spinner turns into the check, drawn along its stroke. The check holds for `ADDED_HOLD_MS` (1s) and until the page has been read again, then cross-fades back to the plus. `AddIcon` sets the button's `data-state` (`added`, then `returning` during the fade) and times the hold. `useFade` keeps the check drawn while it fades and drops the fade under reduced motion, when the check and then the plus simply appear. The success is said once, "Added to <coven>.", through the page's status region.
- **When the add left every coven holding the entry, the check stays** until the page draws `HeldMark` in its place, so nothing jumps. While the check shows, the plus wears the held mark's look (no wash, default cursor).
- **A refusal** is said in a callout under the plus ([`click-feedback.md`](click-feedback.md)), whose badge is named Show the error for <the plus's name>, and the focus goes back to the plus, since the chosen menu item went with the menu.
- **The held check**, `HeldMark` in `src/components/CompendiumCards/held-mark.tsx`, takes the plus's place once every writable coven holds the entry. It is tipped and named **In Every Cupboard** and then the entry: **In Every Cupboard: Testwort, Formalis testis**. Its look lives here, in `.add-icon--held`, since it stands where the plus stood.

## Styling

`index.scss`, on the `add-icon` block (IconTip's stylesheet does not hold it, since IconTip only wraps the plus):

- **`.add-icon`**: the `icon-button` mixin at `$icon-size-large` around `$glyph-size-large`, in Edit's green (`icon-button-edit`), since adding is constructive. The spinner takes the glyph's size.
- **`.add-icon--held`**: the held check, the same box and green but the default cursor and no wash under the pointer, since it acts on nothing. It is worn alone, without `.add-icon`, so the plus's wash on focus never reaches it.
- **`.add-icon[data-state]`**: while the check shows, no wash and the default cursor, the held check's look, so nothing moves when the page swaps one for the other.
- **`.add-icon__glyphs`, `__check`, `__plus`**: the check and the plus stacked in one grid cell. The check is drawn along its stroke (a dash of 15, run to nothing) over `$arrive-duration` and fades out the same way; under reduced motion nothing is drawn or faded.
- **`.add-icon__eye`**: the Quick Look eye arriving in the plus's place, as `__plus` does. The ingredient form's duplicate warning stacks it under the check, whose + turns into the eye after an add rather than back into a plus ([`ingredient-form.md`](ingredient-form.md), "The + in the duplicate warning").
- **On a phone** the plus and the check have no rule of their own: each is a `$icon-size-large` (2rem) target, above WCAG 2.5.8's 24px.
- **Forced colours.** The glyphs are drawn in `currentColor`, so they take the system's ink. The plus has no border, so its hover and open states come from the `icon-button` and `icon-button-edit` mixins: a 1px inset `Highlight` outline under the pointer (not while it has the focus ring), and a `Highlight` fill with `HighlightText` ink while its menu is open. The held check, which has no hover, and the plus while it shows the check (`data-state`), which has neither state, undo those two. Focus is the shared ring, an outline, which forced colours keeps.
