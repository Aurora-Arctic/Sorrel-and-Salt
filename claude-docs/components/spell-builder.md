# SpellBuilder

`src/components/SpellBuilder/` is the form that writes a spell: its title, intent, jar description, seal wax colour, moon phase, day of the week and note, the categories it is assigned, and its jar of ingredients (the steps). It is a client component, drawn in a modal over the grimoire's list at the default column and laid out as Add Ingredient is (`SpellDialog`, `spell-dialog.tsx`; [`design-decisions/m10.12-spell-builder-modal.md`](../design-decisions/m10.12-spell-builder-modal.md)). There is no page of its own and no `instructions` field: the ritual is the spell's steps.

Props are `SpellBuilderProps` in `types.ts`. `deletedHrefOf` and `copiedHrefOf` take effect only once the spell exists.

Tests are in `tests/components/SpellBuilder/`, the gate specs `tests/e2e/spell-builder-autosave.spec.ts`, `tests/e2e/spell-visibility.spec.ts` and `tests/e2e/spell-dialog-size.spec.ts`, and the grimoire stories in `tests/e2e/stories/06-grimoire.spec.ts`. The stories are `index.stories.tsx` ("Grimoire / Spell Builder", `States`), each state answering its requests through `useMockHandlers`.

## The spell dialog and its address

- `?new=spell-<slug>` draws the builder empty, from New Spell at the Grimoire's heading, which only a steward or a practitioner is shown. `?new=spell` is the same for the grimoire the page is on. `?new=spell-<slug>` on any other page is sent to that coven's grimoire with a replace (`NewSpellElsewhere`).
- `?edit=<id>` draws a saved spell, from a spell card's pencil, the recipe page's Edit (the dialog opens over the recipe page and closes back to it) and Quick Look's Edit. Only a role that may update a spell is offered either control. An observer's `?edit` opens nothing, and on the recipe page it opens only when it names that page's own spell.
- New Spell's press writes `?new=spell-<slug>` in the browser with no server request, and the dialog draws the empty builder from the address itself. It opens at full size at once with no skeleton: a skeleton the server's form replaced grew the centred dialog and moved it under the pointer ([`modal.md`](modal.md), "A form the browser can draw skips the skeleton"). Any other `?new` is Add Ingredient's, and holds a saved spell's dialog back until it closes.
- The head names the coven the spell lands in ("New Spell to Whitethorn Coven", `ModalHeading.destination`, [`modal.md`](modal.md)). A saved spell's stays "Edit Spell" over its title.
- The dialog is a client part because the builder's handlers are functions, which a server page cannot hand down.
- **The first save moves the address.** A create replaces `?new=spell-<slug>` with `?edit=<id>` using `router.replace`, so Back goes to the grimoire and a reload opens the saved draft. The builder reports it through `onCreated`, whichever save made it. The dialog keeps the same builder under the new address, so nothing typed is lost. A create made by Close skips the move and closes.
- **A saved spell's steps are read after it opens.** The grimoire page's server read for `?edit=<id>` (`readEditedSpell`) gives the fields alone. The dialog reads the steps through `/api/graphql` (`SpellBuilderSteps`, `steps/documents.ts`), afresh each time. Until they land the builder holds them as `stepsRead`: the jar shows its loading state, Save is off and nothing is sent, because an update's steps are the whole recipe and one sent now would delete the steps not yet shown. A Close pressed meanwhile saves once they land, then closes. Fields typed meanwhile are kept. If the read fails the jar says so and opens its section, nothing can be saved, Close closes without saving, and a reload tries again. A draft the dialog created needs no read: its save's answer holds its steps.

## Delete

On a saved spell, `SpellDialog` given `canDelete` (the grimoire list and the recipe page's edit dialog both give it) passes the builder a `deletedHrefOf(title)`. The builder then draws Delete Spell as the shared bin icon (`BinButton`, accessible name "Delete Spell <name>") in `FormActions`' `aside` slot. It opens a `ConfirmQuestion` with the recipe page's words (`DeleteSpellWords`, `SpellRecipe/delete-spell.tsx`): the name in bold, then one sentence read from the saved visibility. A second `Modal` cannot stack over this one, so the question stays `ConfirmQuestion` over the form.

Confirmed, `useDeleteSpell` (the same write the recipe page runs) sends `deleteSpell` and replaces the address with the list plus `?deleted=<name>` (from `deletedFromHref` when given, else `closeHref`; `deletedHrefOf` is in `SpellRecipe/deleted-href.ts`, shared with the card and Quick Look). A refusal shows in the `Notice`. The hook's `pending` is `removed`, and turns off the autosave (timers, focus-out and page-hide flushes) and the save on close, so nothing writes to the deleted spell. A refusal lifts it.

## Saving

**It autosaves** through the shared engine ([`autosave.md`](../autosave.md)): three seconds after the last change, thirty at most while typing, with `SaveStatus` beside Save. Every way of saving (autosave, Save, Close, Mark Complete, Share) goes through one function, `persist` in `index.tsx`, which checks the form, calls `saveSpell` (`autosave.ts`) and rebases the form on the answer. A refusal skips the engine's retries, so the status stops at "Could not save" with Retry. The autosave is held back while a step's unit is refused or a saved spell's steps are still being read.

- **Save Spell** is `FormActions` ([`field.md`](field.md)) with `noun="Spell"`. It is disabled until a field reads differently from the last save (blank and spaces alike read as none, as `SpellInput` reads them), flushes at once, and is busy as Saving Spell. There is no Cancel: the modal's Close leaves, saving first. A changed category pick or visibility turns Save on like a changed field.
- **No field is required.** A draft saves with any one field. Only marking a spell complete needs a title (the `spells_complete_has_title` CHECK), so no label is marked required.
- **Created once, then updated.** With no `spell`, the first save calls `createSpell`. The builder then holds the new id and revision, and every later save calls `updateSpell` with `expectedRevision` set to the revision the last save answered. After a save the form starts again from the answer. Typing in flight is kept: the answer is applied with `reset(..., { keepValues: true })`, so what was typed meanwhile reads as the next change.
- **What it sends.** The seven fields, trimmed, null for a blank one (so an emptied field is cleared), `visibility`, and every step. The steps are the whole recipe: on an update the service keeps the steps sent by id, adds the new ones and removes the rest, so a step the builder does not send is deleted, and a saved instruction step is read in and sent back unchanged. `categoryIds` go only when the picks differ from the last save, as a set in any order. Left out, the service keeps the set, and an empty list clears it ([`../db/grimoire.md`](../db/grimoire.md), "Saving its categories").
- **The autosave never sends a blank instruction.** A new blank instruction step (no saved id) is left out of what the autosave sends and of what makes the form unsaved, so the rest saves and the blank row stays with no error, until it is written, removed, or Save or Close checks the whole form. A saved instruction whose text has been cleared is not left out, since that would delete it: the autosave is held back for it, as for a refused unit, and Save or Close shows the error beside it.
- **Mark Complete** flushes first. A missing title refuses inline on the title field.
- **The adapter** (`autosave.ts`) takes a target: `{ kind: 'new' }` calls `createSpell` once, and `{ kind: 'spell', id, revision }` calls `updateSpell` with that `expectedRevision`. The builder never sends a null revision (the unconditional write): after a conflict it names the revision the refusal reported. A later edit to a complete spell can name another target when spell history arrives. `readLatestSpell` reads the spell as the server has it now (`SpellBuilderReload`).
- **The browser copy.** Every change is mirrored to localStorage under `autosave:spell:<id|new>:<userId>` until the server confirms. If a copy newer than the spell's `updatedAt` exists when the builder opens, a warning banner (`RecoveryNotice`, [`SaveStatus`](../autosave.md#save-status)) offers Keep (load the copy into the form, unsaved; the one solid button) and Discard (quiet). In the dialog it sits in the modal's header note ([`modal.md`](modal.md), "A control in the header") with the conflict notice, so it is in view however far the form has scrolled. Outside a modal it is above the fields. It is a wrapping flex row with a `space(2)` gap, so at a narrow width the buttons drop to their own line. An older copy is removed without a word.
- **A saved step's selection is written out three times, not shared as a fragment.** The steps read and both save mutations each list a step's fields in full. Through a fragment spread, the jar's `stock { variant { id } }` and the form's `formChoice { group { icon } }` are 8 levels deep, past the API's limit of 7 (DESIGN.md §7; [`graphql/protections.md`](../graphql/protections.md)), and every save is refused: graphql-armor counts a spread as a level. Written out they are 6. Each answer is typed `SpellBuilderStep` (`types.ts`), so a copy missing a field fails the type check, and `tests/guards/document-depth.test.ts` holds every document to the limit.

### The builder's sections and autosave

See "Sections", "Saving" and "Closing". Moon Phase and Day suggest a fixed list (`constants.ts`) and still take what is typed.

### The save module and its conflicts

The call saves `input` to `target`: `createSpell` for a new spell, otherwise `updateSpell` against the revision the target names. Today both targets are the spell's one row, draft or complete. The builder never sends a null revision, so a third save between a refusal and Keep My Version is met as a conflict too. "Two people, one spell" sets out Reload Latest and Keep My Version.

## Two people, one spell

Each save names the revision it was read at. The second of two saves against the same revision is refused with `StaleWrite`, which reaches the builder as a `STALE_WRITE` answer carrying the spell's revision now ([`autosave.md`](../autosave.md), "The server's half"). Nothing of the refused save lands (`tests/modules/grimoire/`). A draft and a complete spell meet it alike. The builder:

- **Pauses the autosave** and shows "This spell was changed elsewhere." with Reload Latest and Keep My Version (`SaveStatus`'s `conflict` state). It sits in the modal's header note through `conflictInto`, not beside Save, where it went unnoticed under a long form; the saved line stays beside Save. Typing goes on, but nothing is sent until a button is pressed.
- **Reload Latest** reads the spell as the server has it now (`readLatestSpell`: fields, categories and the whole recipe) and puts it in the form in place of what was typed, discarding that and the browser copy. The form starts clean, so Save goes off, and the engine carries the revision the refusal reported. A save someone else made between the refusal and the read is met as the next conflict. If the read fails, the conflict and the typing stay and the form says "The latest version couldn't be loaded. Try again." above the fields.
- **Keep My Version** sends the form again naming the reported revision as `expectedRevision`, so it writes over the other person's save. Their open builder meets the conflict on its next save. The version written over is kept nowhere.

There is no merge, diff or history. A conflict is settled by a button, never a page reload: the engine's `resolveConflict` moves the status on.

## Visibility

`SpellVisibilityField` (`visibility-field.tsx`) is drawn in the footer's row after Mark Complete and before the save status, so it is in reach with every section shut. It wraps `VisibilitySwitch` (shared with the Record a Working dialog, [`field.md`](field.md)) in its `brief` form, labelled **Shared** (`VISIBILITY_LABELS.workspace` in `GrimoireList/params.ts`, the one constant the tests read). On is the coven (`workspace`), off is private (`private`). A line under the label says what the state means ("Only you can see it." or "The coven can see it."), and an InfoTip holds the rest and is in the switch's accessible description. The toggle comes first, label and line to its right, a control's height in all (`index.scss`, `.spell-builder .form__actions > .switch`). Below 28rem the footer stacks and the switch takes its own line at the start.

The rule is DESIGN.md §5's: a complete spell's visibility only widens, and a draft's moves either way ([`m10.20-draft-visibility.md`](../design-decisions/m10.20-draft-visibility.md)).

- **A new spell** opens private: the autosave saves it 3 s after the first keystroke and a shared spell never goes back. The database default stays `workspace`, since every write sends the value. It may be turned either way and the choice goes out with the first save. A visibility other than the last save's makes the form dirty, so Save enables, Close saves it, and the browser asks before the tab closes.
- **A saved draft** keeps the switch open both ways with no question. Shared, its tip says "Once the spell is complete, it stays shared."
- **A shared draft** can be switched off as a pending change. The lock binds only once the stored status is complete.
- **A saved complete private spell** keeps the switch open. Turning it on asks "Share **<name>**? Every member of the coven will see it, it cannot be made private again, and your other changes are saved with it." (`ConfirmQuestion`, Share or Keep It Private). Share saves the whole form with `visibility: workspace` at once. A refusal lands in the `Notice` and the spell stays private.
- **A complete shared spell** shows the switch on and disabled, with an InfoTip: "It cannot be made private again. Duplicate makes a private copy."

Which state shows is read from the form's starting values (the last save's status and visibility) and whether the spell has been created. Every save carries the visibility the form holds, so a save from a stale page, after the spell was shared elsewhere, says `private` and, if the spell is complete, is refused by the service (`assertWidens`). The refusal's words explain the rule and name Delete, and show in the `Notice`, never a bare "Forbidden".

## Draft and complete

### Status and visibility controls

`SpellStatusField` (`status-field.tsx`) is in the form's action row beside Save Spell, outside every section. A spell is a `draft` until marked `complete`, and a new spell is a draft (`valuesOf` reads a missing status as one).

- A draft shows one button, Mark Complete, with a visually hidden line as its description. A click saves the whole form with status `complete` at once, so the status is never a pick left pending. It flushes the autosave first, so one save carries the status and every unsaved change.
- A complete spell shows no Mark Complete and no sentence, and there is no Reopen: its workings are recorded against it and its visibility lock has started, so reopening would undo both. The service refuses complete to draft as well, and `updateSpell` refuses a complete spell with an explaining error.
- **Duplicate** takes Mark Complete's place for a member who may create a spell. It flushes the autosave, calls `duplicateSpell` and opens the private draft copy (`?edit=<id>`) in the builder. It is busy as Duplicating until the copy opens. A change to a complete spell goes through that copy.
- **A shared draft** asks first, because completing it keeps it shared for good. Shared means the visibility the form holds, saved or not. The question is "Mark **<name>** Complete? It stays shared with every member of the coven for good, and your other changes are saved with it." (`ConfirmQuestion`: Mark Complete, busy "Marking Complete", or Keep It a Draft, which sends nothing; the name is the form's title, or Untitled Spell). A private draft completes with no question. If the title is blank, the question closes before the title takes the focus.
- **A blank title** is refused before anything is sent: the form holds the asked-for status while it validates, so the schema shows `COMPLETE_NEEDS_TITLE` (`validation/spell.ts`) on the title field and focus goes there.
- Stock check refusals are shown by the stock check itself, not by this control.

## Two error surfaces

The shared schema runs as the form's resolver, so a field it refuses shows its error beneath that field before the save. A `VALIDATION` answer's `fieldErrors` for one of the seven fields, for a pick at `['categoryIds', index]`, or for a step's quantity or unit (`steps.<i>.quantity`, `steps.<i>.unit`) show beneath that field after, through the same element (`aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby`). Anything else, such as an observer's `FORBIDDEN`, goes to the `Notice` above the fields in the server's words. `placeServerIssues` does the placing and `openErred` opens a section holding a refused field. `SaveStatus` says the save failed ([`autosave.md`](../autosave.md#save-status)).

## Closing

### Closing the modal

Close, Escape and a click outside flush the autosave, then close, and ask nothing (`useSaveOnClose`, `save-on-close.ts`). This is the builder's exception to the Discard Changes question: a spell's draft is kept, never thrown away. The close waits for the save. A refused save keeps the dialog open with the refusal in the `Notice`. With nothing unsaved, Close closes at once. Closing the tab or reloading still asks the browser's own question, which no page can answer by saving; `useAutosave` saves as the tab hides.

Undo and Redo are the shared `FormHistoryButtons`. Undo writes the `steps` array back whole with `setValue`, which gives every row a new field-array id, so the list keys its rows, drag ids and open row by each step's `rowKey` (carried in the step, stable across an Undo). Every path that writes stored steps back goes through `restoredSteps` (`steps/values.ts`): Undo and Redo (`prepare`) and a recovered draft (`keepRecovered`). It sets a saved id the server's current steps do not hold (`live`, the ids as last saved) to none, so the next save adds the step as new and is never refused for a dead id, and it gives a step with no `rowKey` one. A save's answer setting a new step's `stepId` is bookkeeping, not a history step (`skip`).

## Sections

The fields sit in four `CollapsibleSection`s at `h3` ([`collapsible-section.md`](collapsible-section.md)) inside `.collapsible-sections`, as Add Ingredient's do.

| Section         | Fields                                                                            |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Purpose         | Title, Intent, Categories                                                         |
| Jar             | The jar details (Seal Wax, Jar Description), What Goes In (steps), Category Match |
| When to Work It | Moon Phase, Day                                                                   |
| Notes           | Note                                                                              |

A new spell opens with every section open. A saved spell opens with every section shut. Shut, a section's line names the values it holds on one line, joined by a middle dot ("Waxing Moon · Monday"), or reads "Nothing yet". Categories read by name once `useCategoryIndex` has them, so the line never shows an id. Steps read as a count of ingredients ("3 ingredients", instructions not counted). The visibility switch is the footer's, so Notes reads its note alone. `SECTIONS` holds each section's values and flags the one holding an error; `lineOfField` reads a value for the line. A save refused on a field opens that field's section, flagged "Has an error to fix". What is typed in a shut section is kept. Open All / Close All (`FoldAll`) sits in the dialog's head before Undo and Redo, reading Close All while any section is open.

## The fields

Each field has a one- or two-word placeholder and no info tip ([`info-tip.md`](info-tip.md), "When a field gets a tip"). Every value is stored as typed: the columns are free text ([`../db/grimoire.md`](../db/grimoire.md)).

- **Moon Phase and Day** are the recipe's timing, when to work the spell, not a record of when it was worked (a working's own day and phase follow from its `workedOn`). Both are comboboxes ([`combobox.md`](combobox.md)), `SpellSuggestField` in `suggest-field.tsx`, suggesting a fixed list from `constants.ts` narrowed to what is typed. The phases are the eight of Wikipedia's "Lunar phase", New Moon to Waning Crescent, then Dark Moon (the span around the new moon when no crescent shows), Waxing Moon and Waning Moon (the two halves of the cycle). The days are Monday first, with no planet beside them. A pick fills the box with the name and anything else typed is kept, so a value saved before the lists existed ("Full") opens unchanged. They are constants rather than vocabulary tables: each set is closed and adding a name is a one-line change, unlike the celestial bodies, which keep growing.
- **Moon phase drawings** (`MoonPhaseIcon`, `src/components/MoonPhaseIcon/`) show beside the name in the open list and the closed box, hidden from screen readers. They follow the northern hemisphere (waxing lit on the right, waning on the left), the lit part is always the paler of the two (`--moon-lit`, `--moon-shadow`, `--moon-mare`), and the maria are placed by an orthographic projection whose table, sources and formula are in `icons.tsx`. New Moon is the shadowed disc, Dark Moon the outline alone, and Waxing Moon and Waning Moon stack their half's three lit parts (`HALVES`). A phase typed outside the list shows its words with no drawing.
- **Categories** (`categories-field.tsx`, after Intent) are what the spell is meant to do: the categories its author assigns, never the ones its ingredients bring (DESIGN.md, "Two kinds of spell category"). It is the ingredient form's `CategoryPicker` ([`category-picker.md`](category-picker.md)) on the same `PickerCategories` query (`CategoriesDocument`, from `IngredientForm/categories.tsx`): a box named Category in a group headed Categories, each pick a chip in its group's colour with a × to remove it. A pick the service refuses (a category deleted since the builder loaded) is marked invalid and the message names it. A saved spell opens with its categories picked: `readEditedSpell` loads them with `assignedCategoriesOf`.
- **Jar Description and Seal Wax** are plain text boxes with no list. **Intent and Note** are multi-line. Title, Jar Description and Seal Wax are `autoComplete="off"`.

## The suggest field

`SpellSuggestField` suggests from a fixed list (`constants.ts`). The ingredient form's SuggestField is bound to that form's values and asks a server lookup, so this is the builder's own.

## Steps

The Jar section is one column at every width (the default dialog is 32rem of content; `tests/e2e/spell-dialog-size.spec.ts`). In order: the jar details, "What Goes In" (`steps/`), then the comparison (Category Match). "What Goes In" holds a summary line (how many steps and how many ingredients), the list, then one add row beneath it: the picker and Add Instruction. The add row sits below the list because a new step lands at the end: a pick is followed by a short move to the new step, and Shift+Tab from the picker reaches the new step's last field. With no steps the list takes no space. While a saved spell's steps are being read the column is a skeleton, and if the read fails, an error.

- **Add to Jar** (`steps/picker.tsx`) is one combobox over `ingredientSuggestions`: the coven's own ingredients under Your Ingredients and the compendium's under Compendium, one row per form, since a step lays a form and not a whole ingredient. A row reads the cupboard's label (or the ingredient's name) with the form in brackets, the form's icon, and a second line: the formal name in italics (`.spell-steps__formal`), then " · " and the form's categories (else the ingredient's) as `CategoryChips`. The box is a `FieldShell` field ([`field.md`](field.md)): label "Add to Jar", hint "Type to search your ingredients and the compendium." behind the ⓘ and as the description, placeholder "Add an ingredient". A retired ingredient is not offered. A form already in the jar is listed and can be picked again, for another step. A pick appends the step and clears the search, and focus stays in the search: the append passes `shouldFocus: false`, because react-hook-form would otherwise move focus to the new step's Quantity a render later and close the list.
- **A one-off ingredient** is the combobox's first row, `Add "<typed>" as a one-off ingredient` (the Combobox's `action`). It makes no ingredients row: the step saves its `name` with no ids, is marked One-Off, and its Form is typed in the open row. A name already in the jar as a one-off, trimmed and ignoring case, is refused beneath the box.
- **What a step saves** (`stepInputOf` in `values.ts`). A picked step sends its `ingredientId` and `variantId`, never the cupboard entry or jar it was found through. A one-off sends its `name` and `form`. A saved step sends its `id`. Quantity goes as a number, and a blank quantity or unit is left out.
- **Each row** (`steps/step-row.tsx`'s `BuilderStepRow`) is [`StepRow`](step-row.md), an open or closed card. Open, it adds Name (one-offs only, required and editable: a blank is refused as "Give the ingredient a name" and a repeat as the picker does; a linked name is read-only), Form (one-offs only), Quantity and Unit on one line, and Instruction full width beneath. One row is open at a time: a new step opens, opening another closes the first, and a step whose unit breaks the rule is forced open so its error is seen. An open row also closes when a click, tap or focus lands outside it; a new step stays open while focus is in Add to Jar. What was typed in a closed row is kept. An instruction-only step is the same card: closed, its text on one line (a muted "Add an instruction" when empty); opened, a text area in place of the text, and the pencil becomes the Close chevron with Remove hidden.
- **One name per row.** The handle, pencil and Remove name a step as the drag messages do: an ingredient with its form ("Move Fixture Rosewort (Dried Leaf)", "Edit …", "Remove …"), an instruction by its opening words, and "New Instruction" while blank.
- **Add Instruction** is on the add row after the picker: a `.btn` on wide, and on narrow the dashed numbered last line beneath the picker. It appends an instruction-only step, opens it and focuses its Instruction field. The Instruction field is a two-row text area with the placeholder "Add an instruction". A step with an ingredient needs no instruction: a cleared one is left out of the save. An instruction-only step with a blank one is refused at its field, "Write the instruction, or remove it" (`stepInputOf` keeps the blank body on that kind so the schema says it). `body` is the one text on a step; there is no step `note` ([`pr1308-step-note-retired.md`](../design-decisions/pr1308-step-note-retired.md)).
- **After a Remove** focus goes to the handle of the row now in that place (the one before it for the last), and to Add Instruction when the list is empty.
- **The starting unit.** A form the coven keeps a jar of is added with that jar's unit already in Unit (`newStep` reads `jarUnit`, which the picker's lookup returns in `stock`). A form with no jar, or a jar with no unit, starts blank. It is set once, as the step is added, so it never replaces a unit the member chose.
- **The unit rule** (`unitIssues`) is the service's, from `src/modules/grimoire/validation/step-unit.ts`: when the coven holds a jar of that form, the step's unit must be one the jar can be measured in (the same unit, or weight with weight, volume with volume; a count matches only itself). It is checked as the unit is chosen, the error shows on the step's Unit, and Save Spell stays disabled while any step breaks it. A step with no unit, a form with no jar, and a saved step whose unit is unchanged all pass, as on the server.
- **Safety.** A step carries the ingredient's and the form's `toxic` and `safetyNotes` and draws `SafetyMark` beside its name and `SafetyNote` (heading level 5) beneath it: Toxic, else Caution, else nothing. Only the form laid is shown. Adding a step announces "Warning: Toxic." or "Caution: has safety notes." in the live region. The warning never blocks the add or the save. A one-off or an instruction has no safety value (null) and draws nothing; it is not called safe either.
- **The Quick Look eye** stands in each ingredient step after the badges and safety mark, before Edit, in one tight group with Edit and Remove (three fixed slots, so badges line up), closed and open, and on no one-off or instruction step. It is `QuickLookButton` (`size="small"`) named "Quick Look <name>", opening `DuplicateQuickLook` over the builder so nothing typed is lost to a navigation. It needs the coven's id and URL segment, so a builder given no `covenSlug` draws no eye. `StepQuickLook` (`step-quick-look.tsx`) holds the modal and the focus return. While the entry loads the head reads only the step's name with a one-line skeleton for the formal name (the step carries none, so `DuplicateQuickLook` draws the skeleton when the entry has no `canonicalName`).

### Add to Jar: categories on the rows and the filter

- **Folded categories.** A row draws its categories with `CategoryChips` (compact, [`category-chips.md`](category-chips.md)) with `limit` `ROW_CATEGORY_CAP` (1): the first in its group colour, the rest behind the `CategoryMore` "+N" button and floating panel. The cap is a fixed count because measuring needs the row's laid-out width. The panel is `position: fixed` so the list's scrolling does not clip it. A category whose colours have not landed is neutral grey. The chips swallow the option's click and press, so they never pick the row or close the list.
- **In Jar.** A row whose form is already a step shows a compact `badge--muted` "In Jar" flush right on its first line (`margin-inline-start: auto`), and the option's name gains visually hidden "already in the jar". It is information only: the row stays pickable. The set comes from the watched `steps` by `variantId`.
- **Order.** Three runs: the assigned categories Category Match counts Missing (from `compareJar` in `comparison-panel.tsx`), then the other assigned ones (Carried), then the rest in the server's order.
- **Filter.** When the spell has categories, a tray sits inside the box's border: one frame (`.spell-steps__frame`) wraps the search input and the tray with a hairline between, and draws the field's one focus ring (the Combobox box inside has none). The first line is the muted caption "Filter By Category" with the hint "Show only ingredients in any of the pressed categories." beneath, and a quiet "Clear" at the right, present only while a chip is pressed. The second is the chip group (`role=group`, named by the caption). A chip is a toggle (`aria-pressed`): at rest a hairline border, primary ink and an 8px dot in its group colour; pressed, a solid group-colour fill, card-coloured ink and a check (`CheckIcon`) taking the dot's place in the same 12px slot (`.spell-steps__filter-chip svg`), so the chip keeps its width and the state is not carried by colour alone. The pressed chip is not bold, and the fill and border ease over 150ms, off under reduced motion. None pressed is no filter; one or more keeps ingredients carrying any. A pressed category the spell no longer has drops out. While any are pressed the list's heading names them ("Your Ingredients · In Protection", joined with ", "). The filter goes as `categoryIds` on `ingredientSuggestions`, not applied after fetching, because that query is paged at ten rows and a client-side filter would miss matches beyond the page. Add Instruction sits in the box's row with its top edge level with the top of the frame (`align-self: start`, offset below the label row by that row's height), so the tray grows below it without moving it. It is drawn flat.

## The jar details

`jar-details.tsx` (`JarDetails`) is the block at the top of the Jar section, a `group` named Jar Details. Its first line is the jar drawing at the left, spanning the whole Seal Wax field (label and input together), then Seal Wax taking the remaining width. The row stretches its items, so the jar's top lines up with the label's top and its bottom with the input's, and the drawing keeps its aspect ratio. The Jar Description is a plain full-width field beneath (`space(5)` below). DOM order is the jar, Seal Wax, Jar Description. The drawing is `JarDrawing` and `pickJarShape` from the jar shelf ([`jar-shelf.md`](jar-shelf.md)), tinted by the seal wax.

**The jar fills as ingredients are added.** `JarDrawing` takes `bands`: one per ingredient step (one-offs count, instructions do not), each the one group colour of the step's first category (or null). The colour is the first category's in listed order (the ingredient's, then the form's), skipping any whose group is not loaded: one colour for each layer, never stripes. The group colours are on the group row, so they reach the drawing as the custom properties `--band-dark` and `--band-light`, never as tokens. The colour is mixed 55% in the light theme and 65% in the dark with `color-mix`, picked by `light-dark()`. A step with no categories, or none whose group is loaded, takes the jar's ink (`$text-primary`) in two alternating tints, 8% and 18% opacity.

- Colours come from `useCategoryIndex()`, the categories Category Match reads, so the jar adds no request.
- Bands are the same height, stacked from the base and clipped to the vessel's body. Eight fill the jar (`BANDS_FULL`); past eight they shrink to fit.
- No line is drawn between layers. Each boundary is a smooth wave drawn once and shared as the upper band's bottom and the lower band's top, so bands tile with no gap and the top layer keeps its full thickness; the waves stay under the jar's full line and are identical on every render. The lowest band is flat on the base. The highest band's top edge is the level line of a held jar (`$accent`, 1.2 wide), lifted 0.6 so the whole stroke sits above the fill. The wave shape is in `JarShelf/index.tsx`.
- A band moves by a `transform` transition, none under reduced motion. The drawing stays `aria-hidden`, and an empty jar draws no band.

**The builder shows no stock.** It draws no In the Cupboard panel and no step row carries a verdict: whether the coven holds enough shows on the spell's page, its Quick Look and in workings. `inventory.tsx`, `verdict-mark.tsx` and `steps/supply.ts` stay in `SpellBuilder/` because the recipe page and its Quick Look render them ([`spell-recipe.md`](spell-recipe.md)); nothing in the builder does. The starting unit presets a unit and shows nothing.

## The comparison panel

`comparison-panel.tsx` sets what the spell intends beside what its jar carries, so a mismatch shows while the spell is built. It is a region named Category Match in the Jar section beneath the steps, rows of a `dl` (Carried, Missing, Extra) whose values are `CategoryChips` ([`category-chips.md`](category-chips.md)) in each category's group colours, read by id through `useCategoryIndex` (a category whose colours have not landed is left out until they do). It reads the form's values as they stand, saved or not. The comparison is `compareSpellCategories` over `summarizeSpellCategories`, run in the browser on the steps' categories; a one-off or an instruction brings none. It also lists Derived Categories, the union of the steps' categories, once each, sorted by name.

- **Carried** is each assigned category the jar carries. **Missing** is each assigned category nothing in the jar carries. **Extra** is each category the jar carries that was not assigned. A row is its chips and nothing else.
- The row's label alone says Carried, Missing or Extra. Missing draws its chips hollow (`CategoryChips`' `absent`: no fill, a dashed border and text both `$text-muted`).
- Every chip has a tip through `tipOf`: "From <the ingredients carrying it, in the jar's order>" on Carried and Extra, and "Assigned to this spell, and nothing in the jar carries it" on Missing. It opens by hover, focus and tap and is the chip's accessible description.
- Carried and Missing draw every chip (`showAll`), each being bounded by the assigned categories. Extra draws its first eight (`EXTRA_CHIP_LIMIT`) and folds the rest into `CategoryChips`' own +N panel, as on the ingredient card. The component is shared, so the builder, the spell page's side column and Quick Look fold alike (`tests/components/SpellBuilder/comparison-panel.test.tsx`).
- The chips are the compact size, the row labels the 5.5rem caption column, and rows `space(2)` apart.
- With none of the three it reads "Intent and ingredients match." With no category assigned it does not list every ingredient as an extra: it asks for categories first. With nothing assigned and nothing in the jar it reads "Nothing to compare yet."

## The categories, read once

`useCategoryIndex()` (`category-index.ts`) is the builder's one read of the `categories` query: the list the picker's filter offers, each id's name, each id's group colours, and the query's pending and failed states. The picker, the jar details, Category Match, the Categories field and the sections' closed lines all call it, and the query cache makes every call one request.

## Reordering

Each step is moved by its handle, a grip button named "Move" and the step's name (`stepNameOf` in `values.ts`: "Fixture Rosewort (Dried Leaf)", a one-off's name, or an instruction's text cut to 40 characters). Linked, one-off and instruction steps move alike.

- **The list** (`steps/sortable.tsx`, `SortableSteps`) is a column on dnd-kit's `verticalListSortingStrategy`. The sensors, keyboard target, handle instructions and wording are shared with the combobox from [`src/lib/sortable`](../../src/lib/sortable/index.ts); only the layout and keys are the list's own.
- **Keyboard.** Space or Enter lifts the focused handle's step, Up and Down move it a place, Home and End take it to either end, Space or Enter puts it down, Escape puts it back. Left and Right do nothing in a column. Focus stays on the moved step's handle.
- **Pointer.** The handle drags once the pointer has moved 4px, and the step drops on the place whose centre is nearest.
- **Touch.** The handle drags once a finger has rested on it for `TOUCH_DRAG_DELAY_MS` (250ms, moving no more than 5px), a constant in `src/lib/sortable` the combobox shares.
- **Every move, Add and Remove is said.** A move in dnd-kit's live region in the combobox's wording ("Picked up Testwort, at position 2 of 3."); an Add or Remove in the list's own `@dnd-kit/accessibility` `LiveRegion` ("Added Testwort, at position 4 of 4.").
- **A move is a change to save.** The field array's `move` reorders the steps, the dirty check compares them in order, and Save sends them in their new order, each saved one by id. The service writes `position` from that order, moving live rows in place under the `(spell_id, position)` partial unique index through a negative scratch place, never by removing a step and adding it again.

## Step values, documents and totals

- **Stored values.** `restoredSteps` is described under "Closing the modal".
- **GraphQL documents.** The picker's lookup and the saved steps' read are in `steps/documents.ts`; the full selection is written out as "Saving" sets out.
- **The saved steps read** returns each step as the builder opens it: what it lays and how much, the form's icon, the categories it adds to the derived ones, and the coven's jar units for the coven `$workspaceId` names. A step's unit is checked against those.
- **The supply total** (`steps/supply.ts`) is what a form's steps need together, for its one line in the recipe page's In the Cupboard panel ("3 tsp"). The builder draws no supply. One amount stays as typed. Amounts in one unit add as they are (a count too, since nothing is converted). Amounts in two units of one dimension add in the jar's unit through `unitConvert`. Anything else is listed ("1 tsp + 2 pinch"), never guessed. A blank amount adds nothing, and no amount at all is null.

## Why Save Spell is off

While Save Spell is off, a tip on the button says why. `FormActions` takes the reason as `saveReason`: the tip opens on hover (after the wait), focus and tap through `IconTip`, and the same words are the button's description. The button is `aria-disabled` rather than `disabled`, so it keeps focus and the tip has something to open from; it looks off and its press does nothing. In this order: "Fix the unit in step N to save", "Waiting for the jar to load", "Nothing to save yet". It is left out while a save is on its way. "Nothing to save yet" shows only while the spell has not been edited (autosave status idle), so it does not return after every autosave.

## Styling

The `<form className="form">` sits straight in the body of a `RouteModal` of `size` unset: the default `$dialog-column` (32rem of content), not `wide` ([`modal.md`](modal.md)). Values are in `rem`, so they are the same at 1280px and 390px. `index.scss` holds the visibility switch's place in the footer row and the Jar section. Binding rules:

- Spacing: the step list's gap is `space(4)`, the add row sits `space(5)` below the list, and an open section's fields end `space(6)` above the next section, set under `.spell-builder` so IngredientForm's `space(5)` stays. A bare `section`'s margin is zeroed under `.spell-builder` so the sections' gap is not doubled.
- The list is a column spaced by its `gap` alone, with no row margin and no room while empty. It is the size container a row stacks by (`spell-steps`), declared in the one `.spell-steps__list` rule.
- The lifted step (`.step-row.spell-step.is-dragging`) takes `$shadow-lifted` and an `$accent` border. Every colour is a token, so both themes follow.
- Category Match's and In the Cupboard's rules are `panels.scss`, which `index.scss` takes with `@use` and the recipe page's side column imports on its own.

The row's own look is in [`step-row.md`](step-row.md), "The card".
