# SignInMethods

`src/components/SignInMethods/` is the list inside the account page's Sign-In Methods section: every way into the account, one row per roster provider, linked or addable, and removable while another method is left. The server component `src/app/(shell)/account/page.tsx` reads the session, `linkedAccounts()`, `sessionIsFresh()`, `?error=` and `?provider=`, and hands the results down as props. This component renders the list and makes the two Better Auth calls. Props are in `types.ts`. Why a second provider is added this way and never at sign-in: [`auth/admin-bootstrap.md`](../auth/admin-bootstrap.md), "Linking a second provider".

The section's `<h2>` "Sign-In Methods" and its intro are the page's, not the component's. The page writes all three sections' heads the same way, so each is a region named by its heading ([`auth/admin-bootstrap.md`](../auth/admin-bootstrap.md), "The account page").

## Contracts

- **Props that carry a rule.** `linked` is `{ id, providerId }` per provider account: `id` is the `accounts` row's own id, which `/unlink-account` takes. `error` is a failed link as `{ providerId?, message }`; `message` is a readable sentence from `linkErrorMessage()` (`src/lib/sign-in.ts`), never a raw `?error=` code, and `providerId` is set when the callback named a provider on the roster. `fresh` is optional and `true` when left out, since Better Auth's refusal is the rule either way; `false` withholds every Remove and offers Sign In Again. `configured` lists the providers this environment has credentials for; the rest cannot be added.
- **A provider row has one of three states, told apart by its control and its words, never by colour alone** (`ProviderRow`, `provider-row.tsx`):
  - `linked`: the provider's mark, its name, and a small quiet "Remove" (`.btn--quiet.btn--small`). With one method left the Remove is greyed (`aria-disabled`, still in the tab order) and the line "Your only sign-in method" is its description, so the reason sits under the control it explains. From a stale session there is no Remove, and the line above the list says why.
  - `addable`: the mark, the name and a small quiet "Add".
  - `unavailable`: the mark, faded with its button, the name, and "Add" greyed with `aria-disabled`, over the shared unavailable note.
- **No row carries a status word such as "Linked".** The linked row is the one with Remove or its reason. Each button shows one word, since "Remove Microsoft" fell onto a line of its own at 375px; the provider's name is in the accessible name as an `aria-label` beginning with the visible word ("Add Microsoft"), and tests locate the button by it.
- **`ProviderRow`'s `removal`** (`ProviderRemoval`) is `offered` while another method is left and the session is fresh, `last` for the only method, and `withheld` from a stale session. `pending` is whether a removal is in flight anywhere in the list. `busy` is whether this row's own action is in flight: its button is a `BusyButton` ("Removing", "Adding"). A link that starts the redirect calls `reportLeaving()`, so the page bar stays up until the browser leaves. `onAdd` and `onRemove` answer the sentence a failed press should say, or nothing. The list works out each row's state; the row's one state of its own is its click feedback, named for its control: "Show the error for Remove Discord" ([`click-feedback.md`](click-feedback.md)).
- **Password and Passkeys are `SectionRow`s** (`section-row.tsx`) after the providers. "Password" shows Set or Not Set, with a link "Manage Password" or "Set Password" to `#account-password`. "Passkeys" shows None Added or a count, with "Manage Passkeys" to `#account-passkeys`. They hold no control, and they are not providers, so they never count toward whether a provider is the last one.
- **Adding one leaves the page.** "Add <Provider>" calls `linkSocial` from `src/lib/auth-client.ts` with `ACCOUNT_SIGN_IN_PATH` (`/account/sign-in`) as `callbackURL` and `/account/sign-in?provider=<id>` as `errorCallbackURL`. Better Auth's client assigns `window.location.href` to the provider, and the callback lands back here. A failed link adds `?error=` to the error callback, so the page gets both the code and the provider, and the sentence shows in that provider's row. A failure before any redirect (a network error, an unregistered provider) says `GENERIC_LINK_ERROR` in a callout under that row's Add, the focus left on it.
- **An unconfigured provider's Add** is `aria-disabled`, names the "This sign-in method isn't available right now." note (`ProviderUnavailableNote`, [`provider-mark.md`](provider-mark.md)) in its `aria-describedby`, and starts nothing. It is not `disabled`, so it stays in the tab order, as on `/sign-in`. A provider that is linked but no longer configured still has its Remove, since removal needs no credentials.
- **Remove is offered only while two or more are linked and the session is fresh.** Better Auth refuses the last one anyway (`allowUnlinkingAll` is pinned off), so the rule keeps the page honest.
- **Removing one answers in place.** Remove asks first, in a modal titled Remove Sign-In Method: "Remove **<Provider>** from your sign-in methods? You can no longer sign in with it until you add it again.", the provider in bold with no quotes, over a red Remove and Cancel. Cancel sends nothing and returns the focus to the row's Remove. The dialog's Remove calls `unlinkAccount({ accountId })` and stays open, busy, until the answer, then closes and sends the focus to the row's control (the Add that replaced the Remove, on a success). On success the row leaves the component's own copy of the list and the provider's Add returns; the page's status region says "<Provider> was removed from your sign-in methods." (`announce`, [`click-feedback.md`](click-feedback.md)). There is no reload, so a list changed in another tab shows only after one.
- **A refusal is mapped by its `code`** through `unlinkErrorMessage()`: `FAILED_TO_UNLINK_LAST_ACCOUNT` (two tabs racing), `SESSION_NOT_FRESH` (the session went stale while the page was open: sign in again), or the generic sentence, which a dropped connection also takes. It shows as a callout under the Remove that stayed, with the focus back on it.
- **While a removal is in flight every other Remove is `disabled`**, so two cannot race to leave none. The pressed one is `aria-disabled` instead, so it keeps the focus, and refuses a second press itself. `.btn` greys out under `disabled` as under `aria-disabled`, so the component adds no styling for it.
- **A stale session signs in again.** Better Auth's `/unlink-account` refuses a session older than its `freshAge` (a day, pinned in `tests/lib/auth.test.ts`) with `SESSION_NOT_FRESH`. A session lasts seven days, so most visits after the first day would meet that refusal on every Remove. The page pre-empts it:
  1. `stateFromHeaders` in `src/lib/request-session.ts` keeps the session's `createdAt` and works out `sessionFresh` against Better Auth's resolved `freshAge` (`auth.$context`'s `sessionConfig`; 0 means always fresh). `sessionIsFresh()` reads it from the same per-request lookup as `getSession()`. It sits beside `emailVerified` on `SessionState`, off the service-level `Session`, since a page needs it and no service does.
  2. With `fresh` false and two or more methods linked, no row offers Remove. Above the list a plain `Notice` says "Removing a sign-in method needs a recent sign-in.", and a `.btn` button, Sign In Again, names it in its `aria-describedby`.
  3. Sign In Again calls `signOut()` from `src/lib/auth-client.ts`, busy as "Signing Out" ([`account-menu.md`](account-menu.md), "Contracts"). Success is a full load of `signInPath('/account')` (`/sign-in?next=%2Faccount`), so the sign-in lands back here with a fresh session. While it runs the button is `aria-disabled`, not `disabled`, so it keeps the focus. A failure is `GENERIC_FAILURE` in a callout under the button, which is offered again.

  The refusal stays mapped all the same: freshness is read when the page renders, so a page left open past the day can still press a Remove that Better Auth refuses. With one method left nothing changes, since there is nothing to remove.

- **Where a message shows.** A press's failure (Add before the redirect, a refused or unreachable removal, a failed Sign In Again) is a click-feedback callout under the control pressed, the focus left on it, with a badge that brings it back once dismissed; the next press of that control clears it ([`click-feedback.md`](click-feedback.md)). A failed link's callback, which the server seeds from `?error=` and no press here raised, is an error `Notice` inside that provider's row beneath its controls; one with no `?provider=`, or naming a provider not on the roster, is an error `Notice` at the head of the list. Any press here clears it. A removal's success is said in the status region and seen as the row turning to Add; there is no success `Notice`. `Notice` ([`notice.md`](notice.md)) gives a callback error its role, and no `Notice` element exists in the DOM without a message.
- **Each row draws its provider's mark** (`ProviderMark`, [`provider-mark.md`](provider-mark.md)), `aria-hidden`: the name beside it is what a screen reader reads.

## Used by

The account page (`src/app/(shell)/account/page.tsx`).

## Not here

The section's frame is the page's: `.page-frame--narrow` gives its width, `.page-frame__section` its column, `.panel` its ground, and `.header` and `.lede` its head ([`../styling.md`](../styling.md), "The page frame"). Each section is on its own panel, so no rule is drawn between them. `index.scss` styles only the list:

- The rows clear `_typography.scss`'s ✦ marker with a local reset, since this is a column of rows, not a bulleted list.
- An unavailable row's mark fades by the same 0.55 a disabled `.btn` does, so the row reads as off as a whole.

Stories: `src/components/SignInMethods/index.stories.tsx`, each inside the page's real frame. `FailedRemove` fails because the workshop serves no `/api/auth`; `WholePage` composes `/account` from fixture props, since the page is a server component.

Tests: `tests/components/SignInMethods/index.test.tsx` (role and label queries, `@/lib/auth-client` mocked as in SignInPanel's test), `tests/app/(shell)/account/page.test.tsx`, `tests/e2e/account.spec.ts` (axe in both themes, 375px without sideways scroll, and a callback error in Microsoft's row), and `tests/db/account-linking.test.ts` for the link and unlink flows against Better Auth's endpoints.
