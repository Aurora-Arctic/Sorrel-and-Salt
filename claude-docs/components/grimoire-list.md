# GrimoireList

`src/components/GrimoireList/` draws one tab of a coven's Grimoire: a title search and five facets (Categories, Moon Phases, Days of the Week, Coven Members and Visibility) above the coven's spells, one `SpellCard` each ([`spell-card.md`](spell-card.md)), with the search sentence and the pager. It is built on the ingredients page's search stack ([`ingredient-search.md`](ingredient-search.md)). `index.tsx` has no `'use client'`: the page reads the spells on the server and renders the list there, and only the filter row (`filter.tsx`) is a client component. Props are in `types.ts`.

## Contracts

- **Two tabs, two addresses.** The Grimoire is a `TabbedPage` ([`tabbed-page.md`](tabbed-page.md)) titled Grimoire. Spells is `/coven/[slug]/grimoire` (the `grimoire` query, `status: complete`); Drafts is `/coven/[slug]/grimoire/drafts` (`status: draft`). Drafts have their own tab because a draft may have no title, and the complete list is the one a member goes back to. The tabs replace a status facet, so the panel offers none, there is no status parameter, and the tab's status wins over any `status` in the address. The order is newest edit first (`UPDATED_DESC`); while a search is active the best match comes first and that order breaks a tie.
- **The route group** `src/app/(shell)/coven/[slug]/grimoire/(list)/` holds them. Its `layout.tsx` runs the membership guard (`requireCovenMembership`, which calls `requireSession()`) and draws the tab row, with New Spell at the heading's end for a steward or a practitioner (the roles that may create a spell; an observer sees none), opening the builder in a wide modal over the open tab at `?new=spell`, tab, filter and page kept ([`spell-builder.md`](spell-builder.md)). `page.tsx` and `drafts/page.tsx` set their titles and each call `grimoireTab()` (`grimoire-tab.tsx`) with its status. `grimoireTab` is an async function the page returns, not a component, so a page test can await the page whole. It reads the address through `GRIMOIRE_PARAMS`, the page through `readNumberedPage` at `CARD_PAGE_SIZE` (24), each card's assigned categories in one batch, and the Categories counts in one grouped read beside the page (`reads.ts`, `readGrimoireCategoryCounts`). The editors are named in one batch: the page reads through the services rather than GraphQL, so it calls `displayNamesOf` (`@/modules/identity`) once with every spell's `updatedBy`, and each card's `lastEdit` is that name, or null for an editor the viewer cannot name, with `updatedAt`.
- **A delete leaves for its tab.** A delete on a spell's page lands on the tab the spell was listed on with `?deleted=<name>`, drawn as a success Notice above the list ("**Hearth Warding** was deleted."), a status region so it is announced. It is read once: the next address the tab builds (a filter, a page, a dialog's close) drops it.
- **`GRIMOIRE_PARAMS`** (`params.ts`, `defineSearchParams`) holds `query`, `categories`, `categoryMode`, `moonPhases` (`moonPhase`, repeated), `daysOfWeek` (`dayOfWeek`, repeated), `visibility` and `makers` (`maker=<user id>`). It reads the address and writes the GraphQL variables, so a search and its facets live in the address and combine. The filter row and pager write the address as every other list's ([`ingredient-search.md`](ingredient-search.md), "The parameter table"). Moon Phases and Days of the Week are free text, a spell's own words, so an uncurated value stays valid. Visibility is one of a fixed pair (`VISIBILITIES`, labelled by `VISIBILITY_LABELS`: Private and Shared). Coven Members holds member ids; each is checked as an id (a non-uuid is dropped) and a repeat is dropped.
- **The title search** is `IngredientSearch`'s box, labelled Title, with the same submit behaviour as the ingredients page. It matches title and intent from two characters. Its suggestions come from the coven's spells on this tab, by title. Picking one opens the spell's Quick Look over the list, at the address the card's eye opens: `?spell=<id>` added to the tab's address with its filter and page (the `cursor` prop) kept and the typed-but-unsearched text not applied. It goes through `IngredientSearch`'s `pickHref`, `pickHeading` and `pickPlace`, the row's `match` holding the spell id, as a pick on the coven's Ingredients page opens the entry's Quick Look.
- **The Categories panel** is `FacetShell` holding the shared `CategoryLine` ([`facet-panel.md`](facet-panel.md)): the category picker and its Match chip on the first line, the four facets below. Each category shows how many spells on this tab, under the search, the viewer can read in it; one holding none is not offered (`hideZeros`). The counts are `countGrimoireCategories`'s, read with the page under the filter the address holds, not the one held in the open panel, so unlike the compendium's they do not follow a pick until it is applied. Under Any the chosen categories are left out of the count; under All they stay in force ([`db/grimoire.md`](../db/grimoire.md), "The Categories filter's counts"). A pick is held while the panel is open and applied to the address, as a slug, when it shuts.
- **The four more facets.** `FacetShell` holds Moon Phases, Days of the Week and Coven Members, each a `FacetCombobox` taking several picks ([facet-combobox.md](facet-combobox.md)), and last Visibility. Within a facet the picks are alternatives; facets are ANDed with each other, with Categories and with the search.
  - **Moon Phases and Days of the Week** are typeaheads over the values written on spells the viewer can read, compared trimmed and ignoring case. Typed text matching no suggestion picks nothing.
  - **Coven Members** picks by the spell's maker (`created_by`). Makers are named in one `displayNamesOf` batch, a former member included. A maker the viewer cannot name (a seed author, say) is still offered, reading A former member.
  - **Visibility** is a [`ChoiceChip`](../../src/components/FacetPanel/choice-chip.tsx), the control the compendium filter draws Toxic with, in a `facet-panel__quick` wrapper after Coven Members on the panel's last line. Its states are Any (no filter, the default), Private and Shared; Private and Shared show their count, zero included. It takes one value (`visibility=private|workspace`, left out for Any). With either count at zero every spell shares one visibility, so the chip rests on Any and is disabled, as Toxic's is. Private counts only the viewer's own.
  - The counts are `countGrimoireFacets`'s ([db/grimoire.md](../db/grimoire.md)), read in `reads.ts` under the address with each facet's own picks dropped, never cached. A picked value with no count shows at 0.
- **The list** is the shared `IngredientCardList` at compact density, named after its tab (Spells or Drafts). Its items are unnamed and each card (`article`) is named by its spell's title, so a screen reader hears the title once and a test finds a spell by `getByRole('article', { name: 'Hearth Warding Jar' })`.
- **Two empty states**, each a named status region so a test or a screen reader can tell them apart ([`coven-cards.md`](coven-cards.md)). **No Spells** ("There are no complete spells here yet.") or **No Drafts** ("There are no drafts here yet.") shows when the tab holds nothing and no filter is in force; a viewer who may not write a spell (`canCreate` false) is also told "A practitioner or steward of this coven can write them." **No Matches** shows when a filter is in force and matched nothing; the search sentence already names the filter, so it says only what to do. Which one shows is the search sentence's own test (`hasFilter`), so they never disagree. Neither offers a first spell: New Spell at the heading does.
- **Each card links to its spell's recipe page**, built from `covenSlug` by `recipe-href.ts` ([`spell-recipe.md`](spell-recipe.md)).
- **The loading shape** is `PARTS_OF_SECTION.grimoire` in `src/lib/coven-path.ts`. Drafts draws the same; any other path beneath `grimoire` draws as a spell's own page.
- **Quick Look.** `?spell=<id>` on either tab opens `SpellLookDialog` over the list, for every role. The tab reads it with `readLookedSpell` (`reads.ts`): `readSpell` for the signed-in member, which keeps another member's private spell and another coven's spell as a NotFound, so the tab opens nothing and says nothing of the spell; any other failure draws the modal's own Notice. The steps, assigned categories and editor come from `readRecipeOf` (`grimoire/recipe-read.ts`), the function the recipe page calls, with the editor named in one `displayNamesOf` call. Each ingredient step carries its stock mark (`supply`) from one `readStepSuppliesOf` call (`grimoire/[id]/recipe-supply.ts`), since the modal has no In the Cupboard column ([spell-recipe.md](spell-recipe.md), "Quick Look"). A malformed id reads nothing, and `?new` or `?edit` hold the modal back so one dialog shows at a time. The modal's Edit shows for the same `rolePermits(role, { spell: ['update'] })` check as the card's pencil and links to `?edit=<id>` in place of `?spell=<id>`. Delete Spell's bin, with the same confirmation dialog, sits beside it in the heading and on each card as an item of its More menu, for the roles that may delete (`rolePermits(role, { spell: ['delete'] })`: steward and practitioner). A confirmed delete lands on this tab's address, filter and page kept and `?spell` dropped, with `deleted=<name>`. The confirmation stacks over the Quick Look. An ingredient step's Quick Look eye opens the ingredient's details (`DetailsDialog`) over the spell's modal at the same address plus `&entry=<id>`, read with the Ingredients page's `readViewedEntry` and drawn with its `viewedDetailsOf` (`ingredients/viewed-details.tsx`), without the card's actions; closing returns to the spell's modal. `?entry` with no spell open opens nothing.
- **The Compact switch.** The filter row ends with `CardDensitySwitch` ([card-density-switch.md](card-density-switch.md)), a view setting inside the search landmark that submits nothing. The list carries `data-density="compact"` (`IngredientCardList`'s `density`), as does the wrapper the loading skeleton draws inside ([skeleton.md](skeleton.md)); the stored choice (`data-card-density="regular"` on `<html>`) turns the compact look off in CSS, so one choice serves this list and the Ingredients list and works before hydration. `SpellCard`'s compact look is in [spell-card.md](spell-card.md), "Compact".
- **One `SpellCardFilters` before the list.** It holds the SVG filter the cards' burn front uses ([spell-card.md](spell-card.md), "The smouldering edge"); an id must be unique on the page, so the list renders it once rather than per card. The list needs no stacking context of its own: the card's layers stack by markup order, with no `z-index`.

## Styling

`index.scss` makes the filter row the positioned ancestor the facet panel opens under, and sets the two empty regions as the coven's empty cupboard is (`CovenCards/index.scss`).
