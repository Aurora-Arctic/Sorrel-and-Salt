## The plants from R to Z (MB.246), part 1

**This pull request is part 1 of the shard.** It seeds **30 entries with 77 forms** beneath them, from the 98 rows [`candidates.md`](candidates.md) gives `plants-r-z`: the 27 rows from Radish to Southernwood (Sarsaparilla excepted) and the three from Yew to Zedoary. Yarrow, also on this shard's list, was seeded by the pilot ([`pilot.md`](pilot.md)) and is not researched again. Sarsaparilla is left out (below). **The remaining 66 rows, Spanish Moss to Yellow Flag and Yerba Mate to Yerba Santa, follow in a later pull request on the same task**, which completes this doc.

The literal is `src/db/seed/compendium/plants-r-z.ts`, registered in `COMPENDIUM` after `resins-woods-and-other`; it is the source of truth, and this doc records where each entry's values come from and what was left out. **Every page and revision cited was read during the task, on 2026-10-11.** No value was filled from memory.

### How the research was done

The lane researched four entries itself (Radish, Yew, Yucca, Zedoary) and fanned the rest out to helper agents of three to six entries each, under the README's rules. Each helper returned its entries with an evidence file naming, for every value, the book file and line or the saved Wikipedia or Grieve text and line it rests on. The lane opened a sample in every batch (Rhubarb's Kynes, AHPA and Cunningham lines and its Wikipedia lead; Rue's and Sage's Kynes and Cunningham correspondences; Snowdrop's Kynes entry; Self-Heal's Wikipedia lead) and found each said what the evidence claimed, ran the guard over every batch, and confirmed through the Wikipedia API that all 30 cited revisions exist under the titles cited.

**Plants of the World Online was tried once**, on 2026-10-11 at 07:06 UTC (`powo.science.kew.org/results?q=Ruta%20graveolens`): HTTP 403, Cloudflare's challenge page, as for MB.240 and the pilot. Each formal name was checked instead against the taxobox of the Wikipedia revision cited for it and, where AHPA has a monograph, the binomial AHPA heads it with. **No formal name on the candidate list changed** (the name checks are below).

**What it cost.** A helper used 139,000 to 226,000 tokens for three to six entries, about 32,000 to 46,000 an entry (the agents' own usage reports; an estimate per entry, since the reports are per agent), and 7 to 14 minutes of wall clock per batch. The lane's own four entries took 8 to 17 minutes each.

**Substitutes.** No source read for these thirty names a substitute, so the literal holds none.

- **The toxic flag** (M14.1) is on where a source says in so many words that the plant, a part of it or a form is poisonous or toxic to people, pets or animals, as the literal's safety note records; its source line names the source with "the toxic flag". Cunningham's health code X (not for internal use), a safety class, a drug interaction or a pregnancy caution sets no flag.
