### A jar and its coven's own version

**M12.8 — A jar follows a local that hides its compendium entry** · 3h

_Story:_ As a coven member, I want the jars we hold of a compendium entry to follow our own version of it when we write one, so that adding our own Rosemary never hides the Rosemary already on our shelf.

The rule that a coven's own entry beats the compendium entry it matches (DESIGN.md §5, "Local beats compendium") decides which entry the coven's list shows: a local entry hides a compendium entry with the same formal name, or with the same label when the local entry declares no formal name. When a local entry is created, relabelled or given a formal name so that it now hides a compendium entry the coven holds jars of, each of those jars moves to the local entry's variant of the same form. Forms match folded, the way §5's variant index compares them, so `Oil` and `oil` are one. Where the local entry has no variant of that form, one is created. The jar keeps its id, quantity, unit and threshold; its `ingredient_id` and `variant_id` change to the local entry's. The moves and any new variants are written in the same `withAudit` transaction as the local entry's save, so no read sees a jar in between and a save that fails moves nothing. `createStock` on a variant of a compendium entry the coven's own version hides stores the jar on the local entry's variant the same way, creating the variant if it is absent, so M9.12's add never makes a jar no read can reach. Where the local entry already holds a jar of that form, which only a relabel or a new formal name can bring about, the two jars merge into the local entry's, on the owner's call (2026-10-16). With one unit the quantities add and the moving jar's row is soft-deleted; the threshold is the local jar's. With two units the service never guesses: it refuses the save with an explaining result that names both jars, in the shape `unitConvert` refuses with, and the dialog that made the save (M8.17) asks the member how to merge them and saves again with the quantity and unit they chose. Re-estimated from 2h for the merge. M9.4's recorded decision is superseded, and `claude-docs/db/stock.md`'s note "A jar a local entry hides stays where it is" is rewritten in this task's PR.

_Acceptance criteria:_

- Creating a local entry that hides a compendium entry the coven holds jars of moves each jar to the local entry's variant of the same folded form, asserted on the rows: the same id, quantity, unit and threshold, now naming the local entry
- A variant of that form the local entry lacks is created; one it has, under any casing, is reused
- Relabelling a local entry, or giving it a formal name, so that it newly hides a compendium entry moves the coven's jars the same way
- The save and the moves are one transaction: a save that fails leaves every jar where it was

### Undo and redo

**M12.9 — Undo and Redo in the Admin Forms** · 11h

_Story:_ As a site admin editing a category group or a curated value, I want to undo my last change with Ctrl+Z or an Undo button and redo it again, so that a wrong pick, a removed chip or a mistyped colour is one keystroke from fixed rather than a Cancel that throws away everything.

Today the browser undoes typing inside one text box and nothing else. This task builds the one undo mechanism every react-hook-form based form will share, in `src/components/Field/`, and wires it into the two admin forms (`GroupForm`, `GroupedValueForm`). One history per saved thing, newest first: Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on a Mac) and the Undo button both undo the most recent change wherever focus is, as every editor does; redo is Ctrl+Shift+Z, Cmd+Shift+Z and Ctrl+Y (Cmd+Y is not bound, since it opens the browser's history). The form owns the chord everywhere, including inside text boxes, so the browser's own per-keystroke undo never fights it; typing in one field is grouped into one step per pause of about half a second or until focus leaves the field. A step records the fields one action changed and is applied back with `setValue`, so Save disables again when undo reaches the starting values and the discard guard follows. Undo takes you to what it changed: focus moves to the undone field and a labelled live region names it. Two small quiet icon buttons, Undo and Redo, disabled when there is nothing to do, sit in the modal header slot. A save resets the history. The store is React's `useReducer` and a context; react-hook-form stays the one store of values and no dependency is added. Focused-field-first undo and per-subform stacks were considered and set aside as unpredictable (the owner's call, 2026-10-16); `claude-docs/design-decisions/m12.9-plan.md` records it.

_Acceptance criteria:_

- In `GroupedValueForm`, undoing a select pick restores the previous value and disables Save; a typed change undone and redone leaves the redone value
- In `GroupForm`, undoing a colour change redraws the colour field's hex and sample from the restored value
- Ctrl+Z, Cmd+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z, Cmd+Shift+Z and Ctrl+Y act; Cmd+Y, Alt+Z and a keypress during text composition do not; a chord inside an open combobox list is left to it
- Typing within the pause in one field is one step; a pause, a blur or a different field starts another; a select pick never merges with typing
- A change that triggers another in the same tick undoes as one step
- After an undo, focus is on the undone field; after undoing a row removal, inside that list's fieldset; with nothing left to focus, on the Undo button, never the page body
- A labelled live region holds text after each undo, redo and empty undo, and two identical announcements both read
- The Undo and Redo buttons are disabled when their stack is empty, and a click acts as the chord does
- Nothing is undone while the discard question or a delete confirmation is showing
- A form's own reset clears the history; after it, undo does nothing and the buttons are disabled
- Before the first submit a restore shows no errors; after it, a restore to an invalid value shows the field's error, and `submitCount` is unchanged
- Under StrictMode one keypress undoes one step
- A pure reducer in `src/components/Field/form-history.ts` is tested directly; hook, buttons and both forms have component tests under `tests/components/`
- `claude-docs/components/field.md` gains an "Undo and redo" section; `group-form.md`, `grouped-value-form.md` and `modal.md` are corrected; the plan is copied to `claude-docs/design-decisions/m12.9-plan.md`

**M12.10 — The Ingredient Form Adopts Undo and Redo** · 7h

_Story:_ As a coven member or curator editing an ingredient, I want to undo a removed chip, a reordered list, a deleted variant or a changed classification and have the form show me what came back, so that a slip in a long form never costs me the rest of my edits.

`IngredientForm` takes M12.9's hook: the chord handler on the form, the Undo and Redo buttons beside Open All in the modal header, and the history cleared after both of its own resets (Save & Add Another, and an edit's save). Because its sections and variant cards fold, a restore first opens whatever holds the changed field, mapping names to sections the way the form already maps error paths, and reconciles the variant folds when a variant is restored or removed. The reference panel is a form of its own that saves itself, so it runs its own history with its own buttons, and the outer form ignores chords inside it.

_Acceptance criteria:_

- Undoing a classification pick restores the formal name it emptied in the same step, and focus lands on the classification field
- Undoing a change in a closed section opens that section; undoing a change inside a closed variant card opens the card
- Undoing a chip removal, a list row removal and a keyboard reorder each restores the list; redo re-applies each
- Undoing a variant removal restores the variant with its folds consistent with the array
- After Save & Add Another, and after an edit's save, undo does nothing
- Ctrl+Z inside a reference panel undoes the panel's own change and leaves the outer form's history untouched
- Axe reports nothing on the ingredient modal at 375px
