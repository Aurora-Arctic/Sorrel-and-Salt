### Spell steps

**M13.1 — Spell steps: the recipe is one ordered list of steps** · 4h

_Story 69:_ As a workspace member, I want a spell's steps recorded in one order, each an instruction, an ingredient, or both, so that the ritual is kept beside what goes in the jar.

Table task, the first of the spell steps and workings plan (2026-10-16, the owner's call; the plan is copied to [`design-decisions/m13.1-plan.md`](../design-decisions/m13.1-plan.md) in this task's PR). A spell's recipe becomes one ordered list of steps, where a step is an instruction, an ingredient, or both. The step is a row in the existing ordered table, not a second table beside it, by MB.40's own argument that one ordered list cannot span two tables ([`design-decisions/mb.40-custom-spell-ingredients.md`](../design-decisions/mb.40-custom-spell-ingredients.md), "Why not the alternatives"). "Layer" survives only as the word for a step that lays an ingredient.

One expand migration, written with `drizzle-kit generate --custom` and a hand-edited snapshot as `0067_celestial-bodies-expand.sql` was ([`db/expand-contract.md`](../db/expand-contract.md)): `spell_ingredients` is renamed `spell_steps`, its indexes, CHECKs and foreign keys renamed with it, and a `spell_ingredients` view left under the old name for the deploy still running (MB.252), listed in `tests/db/schema-drift.test.ts`'s `PENDING_DROPS` as a view. `position integer` is added nullable and backfilled from `layer_order`, with its own live unique index on `(spell_id, position)`; the code writes both columns until M13.9 drops the old one, per the column-rename rule in `db/expand-contract.md`. `body text` is added, nullable and non-blank: the instruction. The exactly-one CHECK `spell_ingredients_ingredient_or_name` is replaced by two, `num_nonnulls(ingredient_id, name) <= 1` and `num_nonnulls(ingredient_id, name, body) >= 1`: at most one identity, never an empty step. A new CHECK keeps `quantity`, `unit` and `note` null unless the step carries `ingredient_id` or `name`. `spells.note text` is added and backfilled from `instructions`, the single free-text note on a spell; the code writes both until M13.9. Everything else stays: the variant unique index, the custom-name unique index, the variant composite foreign key, the form and variant CHECKs, the `updated_at` trigger. The `.ack.md` sidecar (MB.48) follows `0067`'s format: the `Destructive DDL acknowledged:` line, then the findings table naming each RENAME and the DROP CONSTRAINT.

The schema file becomes `src/modules/grimoire/schema/spell-steps.ts`, exporting `spellSteps`. The repository renames `findManyOfSpellIngredientsIncludingSoftDeleted` to say steps; `findManyInSpell` and `existsIn` keep their shape. Seed types (`SeedLayer` becomes `SeedStep`, with `body`) and the demo scenario change, the demo gaining an instruction step or two between its ingredient steps. The test support (`tests/support/db/table-metadata.ts`, `insert-spell.ts`, `tests/support/fixtures/spell.ts`, `fixtures/types.ts`) and every test naming the old table, column, index or CHECK are updated: `tests/modules/grimoire/schema/spell-ingredients-schema.test.ts`, `spells-schema.test.ts`, `tests/db/partial-unique-indexes.test.ts`, `schema-drift.test.ts`, `repository/spells.test.ts`, `repository/finders.test.ts`, `spell-visibility.test.ts`, `workspace-isolation.test.ts`, and the two ingredient-service tests that select from the table. `tests/db/migrations/0063-variant-children.test.ts` runs 0063's SQL against the live schema and breaks on the rename: it is retired, as MB.226 retired the earlier shipped-migration tests.

Carries `claude-docs/design-decisions/m13.1-spell-steps.md`: why one table, why the rename now (nothing above the schema is built), and the two-step column renames. Corrects [`DESIGN.md`](../DESIGN.md) §5 (`spells`, and `spell_ingredients` becomes `spell_steps`), §7's `Spell` and `SpellIngredient` sketch (`Spell.steps`, `type SpellStep`), §10 (story 69), §13 (a working pins the spell revision it was made from) and [`db/grimoire.md`](../db/grimoire.md). The later tasks of the plan, M13.2 to M13.9, build on this row shape.

**M13.2 — Step units must convert to the jar's** · 1.5h

_Story 66 (part):_ As a workspace member, I want a step's unit to be one the jar can be measured against, so that making the spell can take it out of stock.

A new rule in the spell service's save path, the `createSpell` and `updateSpell` that M10.10 builds, and not in the Zod schema, which cannot see stock. A step linking a variant the coven holds a stock row for must carry a unit that `unitConvert` (M9.5) accepts into that row's unit: the same dimension, and for a count unit the identical unit, since `unitConvert` refuses every count. Anything else is refused as a `ValidationError` at `steps.<i>.unit`, which MB.43's `fieldErrors` shows inline in the builder. A custom step, and a step whose variant has no stock row, are unchecked. A stock row whose unit changes after the spell was saved does not invalidate the spell: that drift is reported as a warning on the working (M13.5), which saves anyway and deducts nothing for that step. Plan: [`design-decisions/m13.1-plan.md`](../design-decisions/m13.1-plan.md), "Comparable units at save time".

_Acceptance criteria:_

- A weight step against a volume jar, and a `pinch` step against a `piece` jar, are refused at the step's path and never written
- A `tsp` step against a `ml` jar, and an identical count unit, save
- A step whose variant has no stock row saves with any unit
- A stock row whose unit changes after the save does not invalidate the spell; the working's warning reports it (asserted in M13.5)
- The authorization precondition is proved: the check runs under the caller's proof against that coven's stock only
- Docs corrected: the spell service's rules in `claude-docs/` and `DESIGN.md` §10's story 66

### Workings

A spell is the recipe; a working is the record of one time it was made. A working is created as a draft, which deducts nothing, and completing it takes the spell's ingredients out of the coven's stock.

**M13.4 — `spell_workings`, participants and deductions tables** · 2h

_Story 65:_ As a workspace member, I want to record that we made a spell, when and with whom, so that the grimoire shows how many times it has been worked.

Table task: the tables only, with the service in M13.5.

- `spell_workings`: `id`, `spellId`, `status` (`draft` or `complete`, a new `working_status` enum), `workedOn` (the date it was made), `completedAt` (null while a draft), `note` (nullable, non-blank), `visibility` (the `spell_visibility` enum, default `workspace`), full `...auditColumns`, soft-deleted. `createdBy` is who recorded it. Index `(spell_id, worked_on DESC) WHERE deleted_at IS NULL`.
- `spell_working_participants`: `id`, `workingId`, `userId` (nullable, foreign key to `users`), `name` (nullable, non-blank), `position` (the order entered), full audit, soft-deleted. `CHECK (num_nonnulls(user_id, name) = 1)`, MB.40's one-of idiom: a member or a typed name, never both or neither. Partial unique indexes on `(working_id, user_id)` and `(working_id, lower(name))`.
- `spell_working_deductions`: `id`, `workingId`, `spellStepId`, `inventoryItemId`, `quantity`, `unit` (the stock row's unit), `shortfall` (0 when fully met), stamp-only audit columns, written once at completion and never edited. Index on `working_id`.

Each audited table gets its `updated_at` trigger in this migration (M1.18). Carries `claude-docs/design-decisions/m13.4-spell-workings.md`: the noun, the owner's decisions of 2026-10-16 (plan: [`design-decisions/m13.1-plan.md`](../design-decisions/m13.1-plan.md)), why deductions carry stamp columns only, and the doc pass replacing the phrase "a spell is a record of a working" with the recipe and record distinction across `DESIGN.md`, `claude-docs/db/`, the task entries and the schema comments. Adds stories 65–68 to §10 and the three tables to §5.

_Acceptance criteria:_

- Catalogue test for columns, enums, checks, indexes and triggers; a participant holds a member or a name, never both or neither; a repeated member or repeated name on one working is refused
- A working of a deleted spell is unreachable through the finders (the spell's filter, not a cascade)
- `check:destructive-ddl` passes with no sidecar (expand only)
- The "record of a working" phrase is gone from the live docs and schema comments

**M13.5 — Working service: record, complete and deduct, update, delete** · 3.5h

_Stories 65–68:_ As a workspace member, I want to record a working, complete it and have the stock it used deducted, note how it went, and keep it private or share it with the coven, so that the grimoire tracks what was made and the stock stays true.

- `recordWorking(session, workspaceId, spellId, input)`: `assertMembership` with a write permission, so owners and members record and viewers are refused; the spell must be visible to the caller and `complete`; the working's visibility is capped at the spell's, enforced here because a CHECK cannot read the parent row; participants are written in order, the caller first by default; the drift warnings are computed and returned.
- `completeWorking`: in one `withAudit`, for each live step with a linked ingredient, find the coven's stock row for its variant, convert the step's quantity into the row's unit with `unitConvert` (M9.5), subtract through M9.3's stock writer floored at zero, and write a deduction row with the amount taken and the shortfall. A step that is custom, unstocked, has no quantity, or whose conversion is refused (drift since the spell was saved) writes no deduction and comes back as a warning naming the step and the reason. Returns the working and a per-step report.
- `updateWorking`: date, note and participants; visibility widens only, under `resolveSpellVisibility`'s rule and its explaining error.
- `deleteWorking`: a soft delete by its recorder; stock is untouched.
- Finders in `findManyInSpell`'s shape, ANDing the working's own visibility: a `private` working reaches `created_by = membership.userId` or a live participant row with that user id. A count finder over a batch of spell ids counts the visible complete workings.

_Acceptance criteria:_

- Direct-id refusals, each with its precondition proved: D (non-member) on every call; C (viewer) recording; B reading A's private working unless named on it; recording against a draft spell; recording against a private spell B cannot see
- Completing deducts exactly the converted amount per stocked step, floors at zero and records the shortfall; a draft deducts nothing; completing twice is refused
- A custom step, an unstocked step, a step with no quantity, and a refused conversion each write no deduction and are named in the report with their reason, at save and at completion
- One transaction: a failing deduction leaves no `completedAt` and no deduction rows
- Narrowing visibility is refused with the explaining error; a working cannot be wider than its spell
- A removed member stays a participant, read under their last name (MB.10)
