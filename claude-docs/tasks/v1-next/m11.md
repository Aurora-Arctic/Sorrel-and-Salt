### Launch readiness

**M11.16 — License or create the site's images** · 3h

_Story:_ As the site owner, I want every image the site ships to be one we made or hold a licence for, and to be able to say which, so that no public page shows a picture whose terms nobody checked.

Minted on request during Wave 6. M11.16 rather than M11.15, which is a retired id.

The site ships no images today: no favicon or app icons, and every page is type on a surface. The social-share image is not this task's: it is generated, as a card drawn from the entry and the site's typefaces, by M12.34 in Wave 14, which lands first. Decide what imagery the site needs and source each piece by licensing it or by making it. The likely set: favicon and app icons plus the web manifest; a mark for the front door (`/`, MB.57); and the illustrations the empty and error states want — M2.8's invite-only explanation, M5.4's not-authorized page, M11.9's skeletons, M11.10's 404/500 pages. The set is the task's first output, not its input: an inventory of which pages need what, agreed before anything is bought or drawn.

Sized at 3h rather than the usual 1–2h because sourcing is not coding: reading licence terms, commissioning or drawing, and exporting formats each take their own sitting, and an inventory-then-source pair is one decision rather than two tasks.

**Every image pulled in goes on one list, `images.md`:** the file; the page(s) that use it; where it came from — licensed from whom under which licence, commissioned from whom, or made in-house by whom and with what; the licence's terms in a clause (attribution required or not, modification allowed or not, commercial use allowed or not); the attribution wording if any; and where a copy of the licence text or the receipt is kept. Nothing generated by a model goes on a page without a licence position recorded on the same list. Licences must permit commercial use even though the Vercel Hobby plan is non-commercial (DESIGN.md §4): a licence that covers only today's plan is a relicensing task the day the site earns anything. Follows the M0.6 precedent for typefaces — chosen, licensed for the intended use, recorded.

**Two placeholders are already shipping and must go.** The masks behind the `Backdrop` component on every page are derived from photographs used without a licence, on the understanding that this task replaces them: `src/components/Backdrop/salt-spoon.webp` from `salt-spoon-slate.jpg` on the Salt Association's website (`saltassociation.co.uk`, marked "© Salt Association"), and `src/components/Backdrop/sorrel-plate.webp` from a Feast Magazine editorial image (`feastmagazine.com`, served from `bloximages.newyork1.vip.townnews.com`). Each replacement is a photograph of the same subject on a dark plain ground — a bowl and a spoon of coarse salt, top-down and right-heavy, for the bottom-right corner; sorrel leaves on a plate, left-heavy, for the top-left — either licensed or shot in-house, run through `scripts/backdrop-masks.mjs` ([`components/backdrop.md`](../../components/backdrop.md), "The image is tone and shape"), and recorded in the ledger. The placeholder files are then deleted, and the ledger notes that they existed and when they left.

_Acceptance criteria:_

- An inventory names each image the site needs and the page that needs it, agreed before sourcing begins
- Every image shipped is licensed or created, and appears in `images.md` with its source, its licence and terms, attribution wording where required, and where the licence text or receipt is kept
- No image file in the repo is missing from that list, asserted by a guard test in `tests/guards/` that walks the shipped image files against it
- Every licence permits commercial use, modification and web distribution
- Attribution a licence requires is rendered where the licence says it must be
- Favicon, app icons and the web manifest exist and validate; the social-share image is the generated card M12.34 builds, which is already in place, so this task adds none of its own and its ledger notes the card and the fonts it is set in
- Every image is served through `next/image` or as inline SVG, carries alt text or is marked decorative
- Every image it adds has alt text that says what the image shows, or is marked decorative with empty alt
- An `@axe-core/playwright` scan of every page it adds an image to passes in both themes, asserted in Playwright; this task checks its own images because M11.8's sweep and MB.124's design review run before it
- Its images meet what those two would have checked: text and meaningful graphics hold 4.5:1 contrast against their surface in both themes (MB.124's criterion, M11.8's zero violations), nothing scrolls sideways at 375px, and an image that carries meaning stays visible under forced colours, with a decorative one left out of the accessibility tree (M14.3's pass over the compendium section is the reference for what forced colours removes)
- Raster sources are committed at the size the page needs, not the original, and the repo's size change is recorded in the PR body
- Both placeholders behind `Backdrop` — the Salt Association salt bowl and the Feast Magazine sorrel plate — are replaced by licensed or in-house photographs of the same subjects, given the same mask treatment by `scripts/backdrop-masks.mjs`, and deleted from the repo; the ledger records that they were there
