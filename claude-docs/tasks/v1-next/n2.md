**N2.1 — Remove the Entries Unlawful to Grow, and the Alcohol, Cannabis and Tobacco Entries, From the Seed** · 3h

_Story:_ As the owner, I want the compendium to hold no entry for a plant, fungus or substance that is unlawful to grow or possess, and none for alcohol, cannabis or tobacco, so that a public site carries no such information.

The rule and its test are in [`ingredients.md`, "No controlled substances in the compendium"](../../design-decisions/ingredients.md#no-controlled-substances-in-the-compendium). A plant that is lawful to grow stays, even where a preparation of it would be a controlled substance. The candidates in that record are unverified until this task reads each entry's sources.

_Acceptance criteria:_

- Each candidate is checked against 21 CFR Part 1308 and RCW 69.50 by its own sources, and the result is recorded per entry; iboga is decided from a source and the reason is recorded
- Every plant lawful to grow stays; each removal is written to an ethics file with its citation, as the endangered removals were (`compendium/ethics/endangered-removals.md`)
- Each kept candidate has no sentence that instructs preparation or dosing, and carries a sourced `safetyNotes` line (and `toxic` where its source says so) saying what harm a person without the knowledge could come to
- The removed entry files and the source files only they cite are deleted, and each is listed in `compendium/left-out.md`
- The seed README's "No controlled substances" section is in place beside "No endangered species"
- `documents/code-of-ethics.md`'s sourcing section gains the rule in the same PR, and a keeper republishes it
- The seed's existing guards pass

Tests are written first:

- the seed guard test over the compendium files fails on a fixture entry that names a removed key, and passes once the key is gone

**N2.2 — Retire the Removed Entries From Seeded Databases** · 2h

- A variant a coven holds is kept

- It follows N2.1 and names the same keys

Tests are written first: the `tests/db/` test above.

**N2.4 — Add the `ingredient_closed_practices` Table** · 1h

_Story:_ As a developer, I want one table that holds an entry's closed-practice marks, so that the warning has somewhere to be read from.

A table task only: no service uses it yet.

_Acceptance criteria:_

- One migration adds `ingredient_closed_practices` with `id`, `ingredient_id`, `tradition_id` (to `deity_traditions`), `kind` (`closed` or `asked-not-to-use`, as an enum), `statement` text, `seed_key`, and `...auditColumns` with the `updated_at` trigger
- A partial unique index on (`ingredient_id`, `tradition_id`) where `deleted_at IS NULL`
- `reference_links.ingredient_closed_practice_id` is a seventh sourced target, and the `reference_links_one_row` CHECK is widened to count it
- The migration is forward-only and expand-only; the catalogue test in `tests/db/` passes

Tests are written first: the catalogue test in `tests/db/` naming the table, its partial unique index and the widened CHECK.

**N2.5 — A Curator Sets, Changes and Clears an Entry's Closed-Practice Mark** · 3h

_Story:_ As a curator, I want to mark an entry as belonging to a closed practice, so that the site says so.

_Acceptance criteria:_

Tests are written first: the service tests above, then a component test of the form section by role and label.
