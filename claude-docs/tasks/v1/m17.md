_Acceptance criteria:_

- Create, update and delete run under `assertSiteAdmin` alone, through `withAudit`; a curator, a practitioner and a signed-out caller are each refused, asserted by direct id as well as by list, with the keeper accepted as the precondition
- The slug is always `slugify(title)` (`src/lib/slugify.ts`), derived on create and on every title change; the input and the patch hold no slug. A title that makes no address, one of the reserved addresses `glossary`, `staff` and `search`, or an address a live row of the same kind holds is refused on `title` with an explaining error naming the title, for every kind, on create and on rename; the same address on a row of another kind is accepted

- An article's input and patch take an optional `childIds`, applied in the same `withAudit` transaction as the parent's own fields: each listed article becomes a child, positioned 1, 2, 3… in list order and moved from any other parent, and each live child left out goes to the top level, positioned after the last top-level article. A non-article, a missing or deleted row, the article itself, an id listed twice, an article with live children of its own, an article wider than the parent, and `childIds` on a row that is not a top-level article are each refused on `childIds`, and a refused save writes nothing. - `shown` hides a row from every reader, the keeper included: every reader finder (the FAQ, the index, the glossary, one article by slug, children, the staff list, the sitemap paths and both arms of the search) filters `shown` in SQL, and a child whose live parent is hidden is hidden with it, its own flag kept.
