**M9.5 — unitConvert() within a single dimension** · 2h

_Story 24 — As a workspace member, I want quantities compared across units of the same kind, so that a recipe in teaspoons can be checked against a jar measured in millilitres._

Pure function converting only within one dimension: weight to weight, volume to volume. Cross-dimension conversion is refused outright rather than attempted — grams to teaspoons depends on what is being measured, and a wrong answer here silently doubles or halves an ingredient in a working. Count converts to nothing at all; three pinches is not a quantity of millilitres. The refusal is an explicit result the caller must handle, never a null or a best guess.

**Amended by MB.215 (2026-10-11):** the stock and layer quantities it compares each name a variant, so a comparison is always of one form: teaspoons of rosemary oil in a jar are checked against millilitres of the oil, never against grams of the herb ([`design-decisions/mb.215-ingredient-variants.md`](../design-decisions/mb.215-ingredient-variants.md)).

**Amended 2026-10-13 after the Wave 11 review:** the task named a function but not its home, its factors or its result. (1) It lives in `src/modules/ingredients/units/convert.ts` and is exported from `@/modules/ingredients` as `unitConvert`. It imports only `schema/units.ts` and `schema/quantities.ts`, which import nothing, never the database layer ([`db/stock.md`](../db/stock.md), "One module owns the units"). (2) The factors. Weight: 1 oz is 28.349523125 g and 1 lb is 453.59237 g. Volume: 1 tsp is 5 ml, 1 tbsp is 15 ml, 1 cup is 250 ml, 1 `fl_oz_uk` is 28.4130625 ml and 1 `fl_oz_us` is 29.5735295625 ml. The owner chose these on 2026-10-13: metric kitchen spoons and cups, as a British kitchen measures them, and both fluid ounces, since the UK and US ones differ by about four per cent and a recipe's origin decides which it means. (3) A value is converted through a base unit for its dimension, milligrams for weight and millilitres for volume, and rounded to three decimals only at the end, the scale of the column's `numeric(12,3)`. Rounding at each step would let the error grow. (4) The result is a union the caller must test: `{ ok: true, value }` or `{ ok: false, reason: 'dimension' | 'count' }`. (5) It depends on M11.17, which renames the stored unit `fl_oz` to `fl_oz_uk` and adds `fl_oz_us`. Its consumers are M9.3's default threshold and M10.19's comparison of a recipe against a jar.

_Acceptance criteria:_

- Weight to weight and volume to volume convert exactly, to a defined precision
- Every factor above is tested, each pair of units within a dimension, including both fluid ounces
- The defined precision is three decimals, applied once at the end and not at each step
- Weight to volume is refused, in both directions
- Any conversion involving count is refused
- Refusal is a distinguishable result the caller must handle, not null or NaN
- Round-tripping a value returns the original within the defined precision
- No density table exists anywhere in the codebase
- Unit tested across every pair within each dimension, and a sample of refused pairs

**M9.17 — E2E specs tag the stories they walk** · 2h

> **Superseded by M9.1 (2026-10-16).** Stories are now proved by browser flows in `tests/e2e/stories/`, so the ` · browser` column and this task's scan of `tests/e2e/**` are gone; the tag alone decides a story's status.

The column reports a tagged spec on disk, not a Playwright run’s result: the Playwright job’s own summary lists the stories its run walked and whether they passed. A tag goes only on a test whose own steps are the story’s flow; visiting a story’s page is not walking it. The existing specs are tagged in the same task, since the column proves nothing until they are, and M11.1 to M11.7 each gain the criterion for the flows they add. No test of the harness and no guard: MB.224 retired the harness’s tests, and review holds the tags as it holds the describe names.

Lands after M6.7, early in the wave, so the specs the wave adds tag their stories as they land, and before Wave 15 aims its seven flows.

- A tag naming a number §10 does not list is reported under "Not a v1 story" with its file
- The Playwright CI summary lists the stories its run walked, and a story is failing when any spec tagged with it failed
- Every existing test whose steps are a story’s flow carries its tag, and no test is tagged for a page it merely visits
