**M10.26 — Invited by on the coven members table** · 3h

_Story 9 — As a workspace member, I want to see who's in a workspace and their roles._ This adds who invited each one.

The members table shows, for each member, who invited them. The database already holds the inviter, but only on the invitation: `invitations.created_by` is the sender and `accepted_by` the person who accepted. The membership row has no link back, and its own `created_by` is the person who accepted, since acceptance runs under their session. Working the inviter out at read time, by joining on `workspace_id` and `accepted_by`, cannot tell which accepted invitation actually seated someone. A person removed and invited back has several, and `accepted_at` (the database clock) and the revived `joined_at` (the app clock) are not guaranteed to order the same way. An invitation accepted by someone already seated is stamped all the same, and duplicate invitations to one address are allowed.
