M6's UI half: its schema (M6.2) landed in Wave 3 and its authorization (M6.3, M6.6) in Wave 5. M6.8 precedes M6.16, whose remedy needs the reason M6.8's refusal carries, and M6.11 and M6.12 land before M6.13 because the members page composes both. **MB.7 and MB.122 follow M6.10**, pulled forward from Wave 12 on 2026-10-11 at the owner's call. **MB.240 to MB.249 open the wave**, ahead of M6.1: minted into Wave 8 on 2026-10-11 and moved here on 2026-10-12, both the owner's call, to seed the compendium with 614 researched, sourced entries rather than leave an admin to type them, which reverses Wave 8's opening line ([wave-08.md](wave-08.md)) ([`design-decisions/mb.240-plan.md`](../design-decisions/mb.240-plan.md); [`design-decisions/mb.240-compendium-seed.md`](../design-decisions/mb.240-compendium-seed.md)).
