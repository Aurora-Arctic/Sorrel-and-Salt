# Wave 13 — Grimoire

M10's tables landed in Wave 3 and its visibility rule in Wave 5, so this is the service and UI half. **M10.6's visibility rules block M10.9, M10.11 and M10.13**, since every list, search and page filters in SQL under rule 7, and M10.7 and M10.8, the pure comparison functions, block M10.17's comparison panel. **MB.8's Zod schema precedes M10.10**, which needs it; M4.5 covered ingredient and category only. **MB.6's recipe view precedes M10.22**, which had no page to attach print styles to. **MB.123 closes the wave**, M5.4's design review of the grimoire once its print layout lands. MW.13 is retired (MB.31).

**M13.1 follows M10.1** (added 2026-10-16): the spell steps and workings plan makes a recipe one ordered list of steps, an instruction, an ingredient or both, in the existing table renamed `spell_steps`; it lands before M10.5 so every service and UI task builds on the step row rather than retrofitting it.

**The rest of the plan follows its prerequisites** (added 2026-10-16). M13.2, the rule that a step's unit converts to the stock row's, follows M10.10's save path. M13.3, step text in the builder, follows M10.16's reorder. M13.4 to M13.8, the workings tables, service, GraphQL and the recipe page's dialog and section, follow M10.22, since they need MB.6's recipe page and Wave 11's stock writers (M9.3) and `unitConvert` (M9.5). M13.9, the contract dropping the `spell_ingredients` view and the old columns, closes the wave after MB.123, once M13.1 has reached production.

**The Wave 13 review** (added 2026-10-23). The new grimoire stories are renumbered from 65–69 to 76–80, because 65 was taken by MB.176 (bug reports) and 70–75 by M16 (more login options): 76 is recording a working, 77 step units converting, 78 completing a working and deducting stock, 79 workings on the recipe page and the card's count, and 80 steps. M13.4 carries the `DESIGN.md` §10 list, the rows in `tests/support/story-sections.ts` and the v1 story-count statements. Two tasks are added because the grimoire had no way to change or remove a saved spell: **M13.10 (edit a saved spell)** and **M13.11 (delete a spell)**. Both need MB.6's recipe page to hang their controls on and M10.10's save path (`updateSpell`) and service, and neither needs the workings tables, so they sit right after MB.6 and before M10.22 and the workings tasks. M13.11 soft-deletes the spell and its steps; M13.5 extends the same writer to the workings once their tables exist. M13.5 also adds a `working` permission statement and a single decrement writer in the repository, and M13.4, M13.5 and M13.7 are re-estimated to 3h, 4.5h and 4.5h.

**M13.22 follows M10.16** (minted 2026-10-27, the owner's request): four facets on the grimoire filter, Moon Phase, Day of Week, Visibility and Coven Member. Its one prerequisite is M10.11's filter and counts, but it goes after the builder run M10.12 to M10.16 rather than inside it: a spell gets a moon phase and a day only from M10.12's fields and a visibility only from M10.13's control, so before them the facets would have nothing to suggest in a real coven, and the builder tasks stay one run. It comes before M13.3, which touches the builder, not the list.

**M13.21 follows M13.10** (minted 2026-10-27, the owner's request): the spell Quick Look opens a spell over the grimoire list, as M12.5's does an ingredient. Its modal shares MB.6's recipe body, draws the steps with M13.3's `StepRow` and links to M13.10's edit route, so it sits right after M13.10. It comes before M13.18 and M13.19, which change only the edit route, and none of its neighbours had started.

**M13.12 and M13.13 follow M13.8 and precede MB.123** (minted 2026-10-23, the owner's call): the Preview pane opens from the recipe page MB.6 builds (M13.12), and the screen wake lock lives in that pane (M13.13). They sit before the grimoire design review so it signs off the pane.
