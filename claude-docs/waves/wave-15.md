# Wave 15 — Launch

Every other wave is complete first. This one is mostly verification, but not purely: M11.9 (cold-start skeletons) and M11.10 (error boundaries and 404/500 pages) build net-new UI, so do not schedule it as if it were a checklist pass. **M11.16** (the site's images) was minted on request during Wave 6 and lands before M11.8, whose axe sweep scans its alt text, and before M11.9 and M11.10, whose pages use the illustrations it sources. Nothing in it depends on the wave, so it can be pulled earlier if the front door wants a mark sooner. M11.8's axe sweep depends on every page existing. **MB.124 follows M11.10**, the last of M5.4's design reviews, once the error pages and M11.16's images exist and ahead of the checks that close the release. M11.13 (full story checklist green) gates M11.14 (release).

**MW.15 closes the project.** It is the one close-out pass that survives, since MB.31 retired MW.3 through MW.14 and MW.1 and MW.2 already ran, and it is sized at 3h because it carries the v1 close-out M11.15 held ([`tasks/mw.md`](../tasks/mw.md)).

**M7.A.1 is unscheduled by design**: trigger-based, not wave-based, and a prerequisite for nothing; its trigger is in [`tasks/m7a.md`](../tasks/m7a.md). It sits on this wave's milestone, last in its row, because that is the latest point it could still fire before v1, and every open task needs a milestone and a place on the board. The trigger, not the milestone or the position, is what starts it.
