# Wave 15 — Launch

Every other wave is complete first. This one is mostly verification, but not purely: M11.9 (cold-start skeletons) and M11.10 (error boundaries and 404/500 pages) build net-new UI, so do not schedule it as if it were a checklist pass. **M11.16** (the site's images) was minted on request during Wave 6 and lands before M11.8, whose axe sweep scans its alt text, and before M11.9 and M11.10, whose pages use the illustrations it sources. Nothing in it depends on the wave, so it can be pulled earlier if the front door wants a mark sooner. M11.8's axe sweep depends on every page existing. **MB.124 follows M11.10**, the last of M5.4's design reviews, once the error pages and M11.16's images exist and ahead of the checks that close the release. **MB.148 and MB.149 follow MB.124**, minted during MB.140 for the public launch: MB.148 puts a read made inside a write on the write's own transaction, which closes a save-against-migration deadlock no one can hit until there is traffic and which the deferred policies assume; MB.149 is the general guard, a lock timeout and a retry on the migrate job. Both change code and CI, so they precede M11.11's verification passes, and neither depends on the wave, so either can be pulled earlier. M11.13 (full story checklist green) gates M11.14 (release). **MB.176 follows M11.16 and precedes M11.8**, minted during MB.154 on the owner's call: bug reports, filed by any signed-in account, tracked in the app and turned into a GitHub issue by an admin, in v1 so testers can report from the first deploy. It is the scoping record and mints the build's four tasks, which follow it in this wave; they come before M11.8 because its axe sweep must scan their pages, and before M11.10 because the 500 page links the report form.

**MW.15 closes the project.** It is the one close-out pass that survives, since MB.31 retired MW.3 through MW.14 and MW.1 and MW.2 already ran, and it is sized at 3h because it carries the v1 close-out M11.15 held ([`tasks/mw.md`](../tasks/mw.md)).

**M7.A.1 is unscheduled by design**: trigger-based, not wave-based, and a prerequisite for nothing; its trigger is in [`tasks/m7a.md`](../tasks/m7a.md). It sits on this wave's milestone, last in its row, because that is the latest point it could still fire before v1, and every open task needs a milestone and a place on the board. The trigger, not the milestone or the position, is what starts it.

**MB.163 is unscheduled by design too, and post-launch**, the owner's call: it moves a curated rename's rewrite of compendium entries into a background job, and is a prerequisite for nothing, since M5.6a, MB.95 and MB.132 rewrite in the rename's own transaction until it lands. Its trigger is a rename's rewrite coming near the function's time limit as the compendium grows after launch; its entry is in [`tasks/mb.md`](../tasks/mb.md). It sits last in this wave's row for the reason M7.A.1 does.

**MB.78 is unscheduled by design and post-launch too**, the owner's call: the OAuth proxy that lets a hotfix preview complete a real sign-in. It was minted into Wave 8 ahead of MB.53, but hotfixes are not needed until after launch, and the hotfix path needs infrastructure fixes before its branches work properly. It is a prerequisite for nothing before launch. Its trigger is the first hotfix after launch that needs a sign-in to check; its entry is in [`tasks/mb.md`](../tasks/mb.md). It sits last in this wave's row for the reason M7.A.1 does.

**MB.175 is unscheduled by design and post-launch too**, the owner's call: FAQ and help articles on how the site is used, decided at minting as rows an admin edits and anyone reads (DESIGN.md §13), and a prerequisite for nothing. Its trigger is v2 opening, or the first question the FAQ would have answered; it is the scoping record and mints the build's five tasks, and its entry is in [`tasks/mb.md`](../tasks/mb.md). It sits last in this wave's row for the reason M7.A.1 does.
