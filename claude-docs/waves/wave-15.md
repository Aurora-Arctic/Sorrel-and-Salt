# Wave 15 — Launch

Every other wave is complete first. This one is mostly verification, but not purely: M11.9 (cold-start skeletons) builds net-new UI, so do not schedule it as if it were a checklist pass. M11.10 (error boundaries and 404/500 pages) moved to Wave 11 on the owner's call, 2026-10-16 ([Wave 11](wave-11.md)). **M11.16** (the site's images) was minted on request during Wave 6 and lands before M11.8, whose axe sweep scans its alt text, and before M11.9, whose pages use the illustrations it sources. Nothing in it depends on the wave, so it can be pulled earlier if the front door wants a mark sooner. M11.8's axe sweep depends on every page existing. **MB.124 follows M11.9**, the last of M5.4's design reviews, once the error pages and M11.16's images exist and ahead of the checks that close the release. **MB.148 and MB.149 follow MB.124**, minted during MB.140 for the public launch: MB.148 puts a read made inside a write on the write's own transaction, which closes a save-against-migration deadlock no one can hit until there is traffic and which the deferred policies assume; MB.149 is the general guard, a lock timeout and a retry on the migrate job. Both change code and CI, so they precede M11.11's verification passes, and neither depends on the wave, so either can be pulled earlier. M11.13 (full story checklist green) gates M11.14 (release). **MB.176 follows M11.16 and precedes M11.8**, minted during MB.154 on the owner's call: bug reports, filed by any signed-in account, tracked in the app and turned into a GitHub issue by an admin, in v1 so testers can report from the first deploy. It is the scoping record and mints the build's four tasks, which follow it in this wave; they come before M11.8 because its axe sweep must scan their pages. M11.10's 500 page now lands first, so the build adds the report link to it.

**M15.5 follows M15.4**, minted on 2026-10-19 on the owner's call: tooltips on the admin Users page's and a coven's Members page's roles, saying what each role may do. The pages' own waves are closed, and it lands before M11.8, whose axe sweep scans the open tooltips. It is a prerequisite for nothing.

**M15.9 → M15.16 follow M15.5**, minted on 2026-10-30 on the owner's call: the demo coven, one seeded coven every account is seated in as a viewer at sign-up, with admins as its owners ([`tasks/m15.md`](../tasks/m15.md), "The demo coven"). They add a column, a seed, a sign-up write, service refusals, screens and a story, so they precede M11.8, whose axe sweep scans their pages, and MB.124, the last design review. The eight run in their numbered order, except that M15.13, a refactor, depends on nothing and runs beside M15.10.

**M15.17 → M15.20 follow M15.16**, minted on 2026-10-30 on the owner's call: printable invitation cards, each carrying a QR code that admits any verified account once and grants only the right to create a coven ([`tasks/m15.md`](../tasks/m15.md)). They are a third shape of the `invitations` row, bound to no address, minted by admins with a chosen expiry and downloaded once. A table task, the acceptance, the admin page, then story 88, in that order; they precede M11.8, whose axe sweep scans the admin page, and MB.124, the last design review.

**M15.6 and M15.7 follow MB.149**, minted on 2026-10-20 on the owner's call from a profile of a production build with the processor slowed 6×. M15.6 swaps the theme toggle's hundreds of colour fades for one cross-fade, and M15.7 makes taps, dialogs and search respond sooner on a slow device. Both change code, so they precede M11.11's verification passes, M15.6 first because it is the larger freeze. Neither depends on the wave, so either can be pulled earlier. **M15.8 follows M15.7**, minted on 2026-10-21 on the owner's call: the browser downloads every GraphQL document in the app with any one of them, and codegen's compiler plugin cuts each page to its own. It changes the build, so it also precedes M11.11, and it can be pulled earlier.

**MW.15 closes the project.** It is the one close-out pass that survives, since MB.31 retired MW.3 through MW.14 and MW.1 and MW.2 already ran, and it is sized at 3h because it carries the v1 close-out M11.15 held ([`tasks/mw.md`](../tasks/mw.md)).

**M7.A.1 is unscheduled by design**: trigger-based, not wave-based, and a prerequisite for nothing; its trigger is in [`tasks/m7a.md`](../tasks/m7a.md). **MB.163 is unscheduled by design too, and post-launch**, the owner's call: it moves a curated rename's rewrite of compendium entries into a background job, and is a prerequisite for nothing, since M5.6a, MB.95 and MB.132 rewrite in the rename's own transaction until it lands. Its trigger is a rename's rewrite coming near the function's time limit as the compendium grows after launch; its entry is in [`tasks/mb.md`](../tasks/mb.md). It sits last in this wave's row for the reason M7.A.1 does.

It sits last in this wave's row for the reason M7.A.1 does.
