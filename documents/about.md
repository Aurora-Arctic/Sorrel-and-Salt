Sorrel & Salt is a grimoire for covens: somewhere to keep what they know, what they have, and the spells they make. Each coven is run by its own members.

Beside the covens sits the Compendium, a public reference on ingredients that the site's keepers and curators write. A coven keeps its own notes; the Compendium shows everyone where the lore came from.

## Why It Exists

Sorrel & Salt began with the owner's own practice, and each of its four parts answers a problem found there. Ingredients keeps the inventory, so that tracking stock does not pull attention from the ritual. The Compendium is where to look an ingredient up and see where its information came from. The Grimoire holds the workings and how they turned out. The Coven keeps all of it shared with the people you practise with and with no one else.

## Who It Is For

Witches, folk practitioners, the curious, and the sceptical are all welcome, whatever their race, nation, gender, or sexuality, and whether or not they follow a faith. Transgender, non-binary, and queer practitioners are welcome here as themselves. Bigotry of any kind is not, and the [Code of Ethics](/code-of-ethics) says so in full.

## The Compendium and Its Sources

The Compendium is written and checked by the site's keepers and curators. Every entry records where its information came from, and an entry with no source stays on the curators' list of work to do.

It records what traditions, books, and other sources say about an ingredient. It is the keepers' and curators' reading of those sources, not an authority on any tradition or practice: where a page cites a source, the source is the authority, not the page. A safety note is what a cited source said, never the whole story. Read the [Disclaimer](/disclaimer) before you act on anything here.

## Who Runs It

The site is run by the owner, its keepers, and its curators, and the [Code of Ethics](/code-of-ethics) binds whoever holds each role.

## What We Promise

- **Community over profit.** No advertising. No data sold. Nothing written here is used to train anything, and analytics run only on the site's own servers. The site will charge fairly: enough to cover its costs and to pay the people who work on it a fair, living wage. We would rather it stay small and honest than grow by exploiting anyone.
- **Nothing changes quietly.** If any of that could ever change, the owner emails every account to say so before it does.
- **Built for everyone.** The site's pages are checked against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, and an accessibility bug is a bug we fix first.

The [Terms of Use](/terms) and the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) set out the rest in plain words.
